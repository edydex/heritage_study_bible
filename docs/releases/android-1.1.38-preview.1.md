Fixes the first jump from a saved note or reference search so the selected verse is brought into view immediately. Search recognizes unique book-name prefixes such as `Luk` and `Joh`, and shows clickable choices for ambiguous abbreviations such as `Ma` and `Jo`. Invalid chapter choices explain why they are unavailable.

This development update also accompanies Community's new Other slide editor: movable text, pictures, braces, circles, and selected-text highlighting. Use SyncShow 1.4.0-preview.30 or newer for these new slide objects.
