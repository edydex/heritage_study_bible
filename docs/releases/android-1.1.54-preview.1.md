# Heritage 1.1.54 preview 1

- Reader font controls and verse scroll mode are available through the Settings button. Books and sermons support sentence reading with tap-to-seek and audio-following scroll where matched audio timings are available.
- Book readers support bookmarks at a selected word and a black reading background in dark mode.
- Bible verse numbers are easier to read and toggle favorites when tapped. Compact stars sit behind the numbers, with a faint gray fill and a dimmer appearance in dark mode. Verse stacking is on by default for readers without a saved preference.
- Restored missing Greek word colors for BSB passages including Philippians 4:12 and Romans 1:3 and 1:12. LSV now supports partial Greek word links; words without a reliable mapping remain uncolored.
- Community member-book sign-in is easier to find from the reader.

This development preview includes the latest reader updates. Automated Android acceptance checks cover packaged reading, storage, sync and audio behavior. Physical-device review of the new reader controls remains to be completed.
