UKRK-R now has reviewed Greek word links throughout Colossians, 1 John, James, 1 Peter, and 2 Peter.

- The Android reader includes Greek word-link review for 3,856 Ukrainian New Testament verses across 121 chapters. Tap a linked word in Parallel Bible to compare the wording, or hold it to open Word Study.
- When the original and Ukrainian verse numbers differ, you can jump to the Greek verse where the linked words appear. Try James 4:9 or 1 Peter 2:7.
- Double brackets mark confirmed Ukrainian wording absent from the Greek edition Heritage displays. In 2 Peter 3:10, the additional “в ночі” is marked and the distinct Greek and Ukrainian closing verbs remain visible in their own columns.
- Includes the Android Auto play/pause and seek fixes from 1.1.57-preview.1.
