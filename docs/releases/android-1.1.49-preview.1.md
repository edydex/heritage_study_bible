Simplifies the reading and audio screens:

- The audio library respects Android's actual system-bar/cutout insets and groups Bible recordings under one translation entry, such as **BSB Audio Bible**, with books and chapters inside.
- The quick settings menu contains Bible/commentary text sizes, Dark mode and **More settings**. More Settings contains **B&W** and **Volume Scroll** toggles, Audio Settings, Sync and other settings. Verse Stacking defaults on for new preferences and moves under **Other settings**; saved choices are retained.
- Hold Play/Pause in either reader to open player controls with a seek timeline, −10/+10 seconds, speed and track controls. A normal tap still plays/pauses. Right-click or Shift+Enter also opens the player. Playing audiobooks have a floating −10 button above the chapter bar.
- All ten bundled audiobooks (384 recordings) now have sentence highlighting derived from retained recognized word timestamps. The source text, recording identities, existing paragraph navigation and listening progress are preserved. Unmatched narration is left unmarked; this is automatic alignment, not a claim of human-verified accuracy.

The previous Android native verse-boundary fix remains in place. No Bible verse timestamps are shifted. Book sentence boundaries use the same native scheduling mechanism.

Update through **Settings → More settings → Check for update**. The APK retains the existing application ID and signer, notes, reading progress and downloaded recordings.
