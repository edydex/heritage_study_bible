# Heritage 1.1.52 preview 1

- Books now combines the built-in library with Community books and refreshes church listings automatically. Community Home has a Books shortcut; pull down or press Refresh to update the list.
- Members can download a Community book with its narration and timings, resume interrupted downloads, and remove downloaded copies in Internal Storage.
- Community books use the regular book reader and audio library, including chapter navigation, bookmarks, saved progress, player controls and Android background playback. Private recordings are verified against their timestamps and remain tied to the signed-in church account.
- Audio follows sentences rather than individual words. Book auto-scroll moves only enough to keep the active sentence visible.

No private book text, recordings or credentials are bundled in this release. Keep your church sign-in on the device for offline access. Browser storage can be cleared by the browser; download again if needed.
