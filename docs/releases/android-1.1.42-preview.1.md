**Go to nearby text** now covers all 383 recordings across the nine audiobooks
with text in Heritage, including Wars of the Jews, Antiquities, City of God and
Institutes of the Christian Religion.

The player now opens the named translation actually being read for Polycarp
(Kirsopp Lake, 1912) and Tertullian (Charles Dodgson, 1842). The complete John
Allen Institutes text includes both printed volumes and all four books; the
previous reading file contained only volume one. Existing reading editions and
their bookmarks remain available separately. Recording IDs, downloads and saved
listening positions are preserved. Switching books also waits for the saved
reading position before writing progress, preventing a chapter from the previous
book from overwriting it.

Text navigation uses checked automatic phrase-to-paragraph matches. It does not
promise word-perfect synchronization or invent destinations in long unmatched
passages. Failed timing downloads leave the audio player and normal book text
available. Maximus still opens its external source and has no internal text map.

Reader and browser checks cover exact source paragraphs, recording identities,
first-click navigation, reloads, and bookmarks in both old and new editions.
The Android workflow also checks native playback/storage and update signing.
Physical phone, car and listening tests remain separate.
