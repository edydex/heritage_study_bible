# Heritage 1.1.53 preview 1

- Community audiobook sentence highlighting follows the spoken order, including recordings that read a printed Bible reference before its quotation.
- Shared timing ranges are merged without losing the beginning of a sentence or producing overlapping highlights.
- Audiobook auto-scroll advances when a sentence reaches the bottom quarter of the screen and places its beginning near 15% from the top, clear of the reader bars. Long sentences stay anchored at their beginning.

No private books or recordings are bundled. Reopen an updated Community book while online to get its current narration and timings. If you previously downloaded an older recording, use Remove download and then Download book & audio to replace the offline copy; your reading and listening positions are kept.
