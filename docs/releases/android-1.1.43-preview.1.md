BSB verse timing has been rebuilt against the complete text for all 1,189
chapters. 29,630 of 31,102 verse entries now have accepted automatic timings
(95.27% coverage, not an accuracy claim), up from 14,912. Romans 8 has 38 of 39,
including the previously missing verses 2–3 and inconsistent verses 6–8.
Low-confidence, numeric and intentionally blank entries remain unhighlighted.
Independent large-v3 recognition sampled 60 passages, including all of Romans 8;
timing and wording limitations are recorded in the Bible audio documentation.

Bible audio controls now fit into the existing reader navigation: Play/Pause is
beside the book and chapter name, and the extra audio card and bottom player
have been removed. Settings → Audio Settings holds playback position/speed,
chapter browsing and downloads. Bible and audiobook text following are on by
default and can be disabled there. Tap a timed verse while listening to start
from its beginning. Whole-BSB downloads on Android show their size before
confirmation, retain finished chapters after interruption and can resume.

Audiobooks follow the current paragraph and advance the open text across chapter
boundaries when a matching edition is available. Saved audio positions, existing
downloads, reading editions and bookmarks are retained.

In parallel view, tap a word to find its other occurrences; hold it (or press
Shift+Enter) to show its checked translation match. Greek word searches use the
Nestle 1904 source lemmas, including inflected forms. Other translations use
explicitly labelled word-form searches. Source-to-translation links remain
limited to the checked BSB/Greek links in Romans. Light color tints are the
default; Settings → B&W word links switches to patterns, including distinct
diagonal lines. Parallel word actions do not open verse notes.

Automatic audio timings are estimates, not a human listening review. Unmatched
or rejected passages remain unhighlighted and do not prevent playback. Physical
phone, car, listening and e-ink checks remain separate from automated tests.
