## Notable decisions

- 7:1 γυναικὸς μὴ ἅπτεσθαι joined = "to abstain from sexual relations" (the
  euphemism "touch a woman" with its negative); ἀνθρώπῳ `s`.
- 7:8 ἐὰν μείνωσιν joined = "to remain" (conditional recast as infinitive).
- 7:9, 7:17: EVIDENCE shows BSB's Greek differs by one word; the same verb
  in another form (probably γαμῆσαι for γαμεῖν, ἐμέρισεν for μεμέρικεν), so
  linked with a note. 7:13: BSB follows a reading with "if" (probably εἴ τις
  for ἥτις); ἥτις `s`, "if" `t`.
- 7:14 ἀδελφῷ = "believing husband" (the "brother" sense); the first
  "believing" (wife) is `t`.
- 7:17 ἑκάστῳ = "each one + to him" (same person, the dative's own role);
  ἕκαστον = "him" kept; ὡς = "that", οὕτως = "the life" kept.
- 7:20 κλῆσις = "situation"; ᾗ = the closing "in"; resumptive ἐν ταύτῃ `s`.
- 7:22 ὁμοίως = "Conversely" kept (a connective rendered with another).
- 7:26 second καλόν and ὅτι `s` (BSB merges the two clauses).
- Words of different meaning kept `s` with the English `t`: 7:27 γυναικός /
  "commitment"; 7:36 οὕτως … γίνεσθαι / "to marry"; 7:37 τηρεῖν / "not to
  marry".
- 7:34 ἵνα = "how" (BSB parallels the purpose clause with "how he can
  please"); καί = "or".
- 7:36 παρθένον = "betrothed"; 7:37-38 παρθένον = "virgin"; 7:38 γαμίζων =
  "marries": linked as BSB renders them, without notes on the
  interpretation.

## Doubts for the peer

- 7:17: the double link ἑκάστῳ = "each one + to him". Alternative: "to him"
  `t`.
- 7:9: οὐκ = "cannot" (one English word; the "can" has no Greek owner).
  Alternative: join οὐκ ἐγκρατεύονται.
- 7:28: καί `s` while γαμήσῃς keeps the emphatic "do marry". The emphatic
  "do" could instead link to καί ("indeed").
- 7:37: ἔχων = "under" and ἔχει = "with" (verbs of having rendered as
  prepositions) are kept linked; τηρεῖν `s` / "not to marry" `t` could be
  read as a same-meaning recast and linked.
- 7:10, 7:11: "her husband"/"his wife" kept on the anarthrous nouns
  (publisher rows); the possessive could be `t`.
- 7:3 "marital duty" kept on ὀφειλήν (publisher row).
