Verses reviewed: all 28 (14:1-28). No KEEP blocks in this chapter. Final
check: `fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 28, "problems": 0}`.

89 of the 401 publisher `g` rows changed, in 26 verses (14:2 and 14:7 kept
every publisher row). Some rows only gained a token, with the English
unchanged: 14:1 κατὰ τὸ αὐτό = as usual; 14:3 τῷ μαρτυροῦντι = who

## Publisher rows corrected

English words with no Greek counterpart that had been folded into a Greek
word (now `t`):

- Supplied names: 14:3 and 14:18 "Paul and Barnabas" (verb ending only),
  14:23 "Paul and Barnabas" (participle), 14:10 "Paul" and "the man".
  14:1 αὐτούς = Paul and Barnabas stays linked: there the Greek has a
  pronoun.
- Participle "and": 14:6 "and fled", 14:14 "and rushed", 14:20 "and went".
- Supplied objects: 14:6 "about it", 14:14 "about this" (as Matthew 14:13),
  14:19 "him" (ἔσυρον has no object), 14:22 "them".
- Other additions: 14:3 and 14:28 "there" (was on χρόνον); 14:8 "was"
  (supplied copula, was on χωλός); 14:13 "temple" (was on τοῦ ὄντος; the
  Greek speaks of Zeus himself as before the city) and "city" in "city
  gates" (πυλῶνας is only "gateways"); 14:15 "only" (was on ἐσμεν); 14:20
  "back"; 14:21 "Then" (was on ὑπέστρεψαν); 14:23 "as" (was on παρέθεντο);
  14:26 "just" (was on ἐπλήρωσαν); 14:22 "they said" (was on ὅτι, now `s`
  as a quotation marker, as in Acts 2 and 11).

Words on the wrong Greek word:

- Lone articles carrying a preposition: 14:4 τῆς "of the"; 14:6 τήν "to
  the"; 14:22 τῶν "of the" and τῇ "in the"; 14:23 τῷ "to the"; 14:26 τῇ "to
  the"; 14:27 τοῖς "to the"; 14:3 τῆς "of"; 14:8 τοῖς "in his". The
  articles keep "the"/"his"; the preposition goes on the noun (πόλεως = of +
  city, μαθητῶν = of + disciples, πίστει = in + faith, Κυρίῳ = to + Lord,
  χάριτι = to + grace, ἔθνεσιν = to + Gentiles, χάριτος = of + grace, ποσίν
  = in + feet). In 14:6 the second "to" is εἰς understood again (εἰς = to +
  to, as Acts 6:11).
- τε in τε … καί carried English: 14:1 τε "of" (now on Ἰουδαίων = of Jews)
  and 14:5 τε "together" (now σύν = together with).
- "When/after" on a connective or article: 14:11 "When" from τε to ἰδόντες;
  14:14 "when" from οἱ to ἀκούσαντες; 14:20 "after" from δέ to κυκλωσάντων;
  14:24 "After" from καί to διελθόντες; 14:25 "when" from καί to
  λαλήσαντες; 14:27 "When" from δέ to Παραγενόμενοι.
- 14:3 χειρῶν ("hands") carried "them": αὐτῶν = them (the pronoun becomes
  the object of enabling); διὰ τῶν χειρῶν is `s`.
- 14:8 ὅς carried "and": ὅς = who (BSB moves the relative before lame);
  "and" is `t`. Negative split: οὐδέποτε = never, περιεπάτησεν = had +
  walked. Also 14:17 οὐκ = not, ἀφῆκεν = He has + left.
- 14:9 λαλοῦντος had "the words of": "of" moved to Παύλου (of Paul).
- 14:10 φωνῇ = In a + voice, μεγάλῃ = loud (as Acts 7:57, 7:60).
- 14:11 ὁμοιωθέντες had "in human form": ἀνθρώποις = human, ὁμοιωθέντες =
  in … form.
- 14:12 Ἑρμῆν had "they called Hermes": the repeated "they called" links to
  ἐκάλουν (understood again).
- 14:15 ὑμῖν had "like you": "like" belongs to ὁμοιοπαθεῖς (= human like);
  εὐαγγελιζόμενοι had "you" (now on ὑμᾶς); τά had "everything" (now on
  πάντα, τά `s`).
- 14:16 the article ταῖς had "own": αὐτῶν = their own (majority
  convention), ταῖς `s`.
- 14:18 ταῦτα had "with": ταῦτα = these, λέγοντες = with + words;
  κατέπαυσαν = could + stop, μόλις = hardly.
- 14:21 μαθητεύσαντες had "many": ἱκανούς = many.
- 14:27 ἐκκλησίαν had "together": the "together" renders the συν- of
  συναγαγόντες (= they gathered + together).

- 14:14 ἑαυτῶν: a different form of the pronoun (probably αὐτῶν); "their"

- 14:21 εὐαγγελιζόμενοί: a different form of the verb (probably aorist

## Notable decisions

- Split pairs avoided with a shifted καί: 14:19 καί (between πείσαντες and
  λιθάσαντες) = the "and" before dragged; 14:22 καί (before the ὅτι clause)
  = the "and" between strengthening and encouraging; 14:27 καί (between the
  two participles) = the "and" before reported. Each has a note.
- 14:1 Ἐγένετο δέ `s` (BSB has no opener); καί = where kept with a note.
- 14:15 καὶ λέγοντες `s`: "shouting" (14:14) introduces the speech.
- 14:15 kept "that you should turn" on ἐπιστρέφειν; ὑμᾶς = you (object of
  bringing good news).
- 14:21 the repeated εἰς before Iconium and Antioch is `s` (BSB says "to"
  once); the first καί of the list is a comma.
- 14:22 διά = endure (publisher row kept, with a note); εἰς `s` (absorbed
  into enter).
- 14:23 μετά = and ("praying and fasting") kept with a note.
- 14:26 κἀκεῖθεν = From Attalia kept with a note.
- Kept publisher rows: 14:2 τάς = their (possessive article), 14:8
  ἐκάθητο = there sat, 14:13 πρό = just outside, 14:15 ζῶντα = the living
  (as 1 Thessalonians 1:9), 14:17 ἀγαθουργῶν = to His goodness.

## Doubts for the peer

- 14:19, 14:22 and 14:27: the shifted-καί links to "and"; the alternative
  is καί `s` with the participle "and" as `t`. 14:22 is the weakest, as
  the καί there joins the two things urged, not the two participles.
- 14:22 ὅτι `s` + "they said" `t`, rather than the publisher's link.
- 14:13 "city" (city gates) and 14:26 "just" as `t`.
- 14:12 λόγου = speaker (note), and ὁ ἡγούμενος joined as "the chief".
- 14:11 ὁμοιωθέντες = in … form split from ἀνθρώποις = human; the
  alternative is one joined group "in human form".
