## Publisher rows corrected

"When" or "Then" was on the wrong word:

- 2:3: "When" moves from the opening καί to the genitive absolute ὑστερήσαντος
  (When + ran out). The καί is `s`.
- 2:13: BSB turns the first of two clauses joined by καί into a When-clause.
  "When" now goes on the καί between the clauses; the opening Καί is `s`.
- 2:9: the article ὁ (before the repeated ἀρχιτρίκλινος) carried "Then". Now
  ὁ ἀρχιτρίκλινος → he, and the ὡς ("when") clause, which BSB makes a main
  sentence, carries "Then".

A supplied "and" joining a participle to the main verb is now `t`: 2:15 ("and
drove", ποιήσας → made), 2:23 ("and believed", θεωροῦντες → saw).

English-only words that a Greek word had claimed (now `t`):

- Subject: "the guests" (2:10, μεθυσθῶσιν → are drunk).
- Objects: "some" and "it" (2:8), "them" in "them all" (2:24, πάντας "all
  people").
- Other: "at their tables" (2:14, καθημένους → seated); "But" in 2:10, where
  the Greek has no connective; "again" in "raise it up again" (2:19); "to prove
  Your authority" (2:18, see below).

Negatives split off verbs: 2:4 οὔπω → not yet, ἥκει → has + come; 2:9 οὐκ →
not, ᾔδει → He did + know; 2:24 οὐκ → not, ἐπίστευεν → did + entrust.

Other wrong anchors:

- 2:2: ἐκλήθη carried "also". Now καί → also, ἐκλήθη → had + been invited.
- 2:5: λέγει carried "to". Now λέγει → said and τοῖς → to the.
- 2:6: the article τῶν (with Ἰουδαίων → Jewish) carried "the"; it belongs to
  τὸν καθαρισμόν. χωροῦσαι carried "Each"; distributive ἀνά ("apiece") now →
  Each. μετρητὰς δύο ἢ τρεῖς is one group → "from twenty to thirty gallons"
  (a converted measure), instead of δύο alone carrying it and ἀνά → "from".
- 2:8: ἤνεγκαν carried "They". In οἱ δέ the article now → They, and ἤνεγκαν →
  did so.
- 2:9: ἐστίν carried "from"; πόθεν now → where + from. νυμφίον carried
  "aside"; it now goes with φωνεῖ (called + aside).
- 2:11: καί carried "He thus". Now καί → thus and ἐφανέρωσεν → He + revealed.
- 2:12: κατέβη carried "He". The explicit αὐτός now → He.
- 2:16: οἶκον carried "into". Now οἶκον ἐμπορίου → into a marketplace.
  μή carried "How dare you"; now μή → How dare and ποιεῖτε → you + turn.
- 2:17: the lone article τοῦ carried "for". Now τοῦ is `s` and οἴκου → for +
  house.
- 2:18: ὅτι carried "to prove" and ποιεῖς carried "Your authority to do". Now
  ποιεῖς → to do, ὅτι is `s`, and "to prove Your authority" is `t`.
- 2:19, 2:20: the verb carried "it"; αὐτόν now → it. In 2:20 ναός carried
  "took"; οἰκοδομήθη now → took + to build.
- 2:21: αὐτοῦ carried "of His". Now σώματος → of + body and αὐτοῦ → His.
- 2:23: αὐτοῦ carried "the". Now τά → the, and αὐτοῦ is `s` (BSB's "He was
  doing" shows whose signs they were).
- 2:25: εἶχεν carried "He did" and χρείαν "need". The idiom χρείαν εἶχεν is
  now one group → He did + need.

Joined units (rule 3): Ὅ τι ἄν → whatever (2:5); article + participle (2:9 οἱ
ἠντληκότες, 2:14 τοὺς πωλοῦντας, 2:16 τοῖς … πωλοῦσιν); repeated ὁ
ἀρχιτρίκλινος → he (2:9); Πᾶς ἄνθρωπος → Everyone (2:10); οὐ πολλάς → a few
(2:12); answered/said pairs ἀπεκρίθησαν καὶ εἶπαν → demanded (2:18) and
ἀπεκρίθη καὶ εἶπεν → answered (2:19); Τεσσεράκοντα καὶ ἕξ → forty-six (2:20);
διὰ τό + infinitive → for (2:24).

## Notable decisions

- 2:4: Τί ἐμοὶ καὶ σοί is one idiom group → why + us, with heads τί → why and
  ἐμοὶ καὶ σοί → us. "does this concern" is `t`, following Mark 1:24 and 5:7.
- 2:3: "no more" stays on οὐκ, as BSB's idiomatic negative ("they have no more
  wine"); "more" was not split off.
- 2:10: "and then" stays on καί and "cheap wine" on ἐλάσσω (the noun wine is
  understood in the Greek).
- 2:12: EVIDENCE shows one extra word in BSB's Greek (probably αὐτοῦ after
  ἀδελφοί). BSB's single "His" covers mother and brothers, so no link changes.
- 2:4: EVIDENCE says the Greek differs but lists 0 and 0 unmatched words, so no
  different reading is claimed.
- 2:23: the second ἐν and τῇ are `s`: BSB merges "at the Passover, at the
  feast" into "at the Passover Feast"; ἑορτῇ → Feast.
- 2:24: the intensive αὐτός in αὐτὸς … Ἰησοῦς ("Jesus himself") is `s`.
- 2:25: the opening καὶ ὅτι ("and because"), which continues the reason from
  2:24, is `s`. ἵνα is `s`, because BSB turns the ἵνα clause into the noun
  phrase "any testimony".

## Doubts for the peer

- 2:9: ὡς → "Then". BSB changes "When he tasted …, he called" into "… tasted.
  … Then he called", so ὡς is linked to "Then" to avoid a split pair. The other
  option is ὡς `s` and "Then" `t`.
- 2:18: ὅτι is `s` and "to prove Your authority" is `t`. The other option is
  ὅτι → "to prove", if the peer reads the causal ὅτι ("seeing that") as what
  BSB renders.
- 2:16: μή → "How dare". BSB turns the prohibition "do not make" into a
  rebuke. The other option is μὴ ποιεῖτε joined → "How dare you turn".
- 2:11: καί → "thus". "thus" could instead be `t`, with καί `s`.
- 2:19: "again" is `t`. It could stay with ἐγερῶ as part of BSB's "raise it up
  again".
