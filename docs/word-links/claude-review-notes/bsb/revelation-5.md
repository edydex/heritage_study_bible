# BSB Revelation 5: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: all 14 (5:1-14). No KEEP blocks in this chapter. Final
check: `fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 14, "problems": 0}`.

50 of the 300 publisher `g` rows changed, in 12 verses. 5:3 and 5:14 keep
every publisher row, and only their TODO tokens are resolved. I checked the
unchanged rows too. No "of the"/"to the" now sits on a lone article or on
a number. No supplied copula ("is", "be", "to be") sits on a Greek word. No
person word sits on a participle. Every "a" goes with its noun.

Readings: EVIDENCE shows a word difference from N1904 only in 5:4, 5:8,
5:12 and 5:14 (one word each). 5:11 shows 0/0 and I claim nothing there.
5:9 and 5:10 equal N1904 (no object after ἠγόρασας; αὐτούς and
βασιλεύσουσιν), so no reading is claimed there: "those" in 5:9 is a
supplied object (`t`), and "them" and "they will reign" in 5:10 link to
αὐτούς and βασιλεύσουσιν.
- 5:4: the one unlinked English word was "I". It renders the person of
  ἔκλαιον, so it is joined to that verb. The note says BSB follows a reading
  with an extra word here (probably ἐγώ).
- 5:8 and 5:14: the unlinked word was the number "four" on τέσσερα. It is
  linked with "Same number, spelled differently in BSB's Greek text"
  (probably τέσσαρα).
- 5:12: the unlinked word was "Worthy" on Ἄξιός. It is linked with a note
  that BSB's Greek has another form of this adjective with the same meaning
  (probably the neuter ἄξιον, agreeing with ἀρνίον).
No cross-verse links.

Conventions followed:
- Revelation 4:3, 4:9 and 4:10, Revelation 1:5 and Matthew 23:22 for article
  + participle units, with the case word inside the unit: 5:1 and 5:7 τοῦ
  καθημένου = "of the One seated", 5:13 Τῷ καθημένῳ = "To Him who sits",
  5:12 τὸ ἐσφαγμένον = "who was slain". "on" stays on ἐπί and "the" on the

- Revelation 1:6 and 4:9 for εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας = "forever" and τῶν αἰώνων =

- Revelation 4:4 and 4:10 for εἴκοσι τέσσαρες = "twenty-four" joined (5:8).
- Revelation 4:11 for Ἄξιος εἶ = "Worthy" + "are You" (5:9). The copula is
  the Greek εἶ there, and ἐστιν in 5:12, so both stay linked. In 5:2 Τίς
  ἄξιος has no verb, so "is" is `t`.
- Revelation 1:10 and 1:13 for "a" with its noun: 5:2 "a#0 + angel", "a#1 +
  voice"; 5:9 "a + song"; 5:12 "In a + voice".
- Luke 7:13 for Μὴ κλαῖε: Μή = "not", κλαῖε = "Do + weep" (5:5).
- Colossians 1:20 for a nominalizing article linked to the English that
  stands for it (5:13 τά = "that").
- Revelation 9:16 (Codex) for numbers: 5:11 ἦν is a real verb, so "was"
  stays on it; "of" goes on each genitive noun (μυριάδων, χιλιάδων).

## Notable decisions

- 5:6 ἐν μέσῳ τοῦ θρόνου καὶ τῶν τεσσάρων ζῴων καὶ ἐν μέσῳ τῶν
  πρεσβυτέρων: BSB has "in the center of the throne, encircled by the four
  living creatures and the elders". The first ἐν μέσῳ = "in the#0 center".
  The second ἐν μέσῳ (11 12) = "encircled by", with a note that BSB applies
  it to both groups. καί (10) = "and#0" (it leads to the elders). The first
  καί (6), joining the throne and the creatures, is `s`.
- 5:13 ὃ … ἐστίν ("which is", with the creature) is `s`. The "is" in "all
  that is in them" belongs to the other phrase (τὰ ἐν αὐτοῖς), so it is a
  supplied copula (`t`), not a link to that ἐστίν.
- 5:13 the doxology has no English "the", so all four articles (35 38 41
  44) are `s`; each καί keeps its own "and". In 5:12 the one article τήν
  (10) is `s` for the same reason.
- 5:11 ὁ ἀριθμὸς αὐτῶν: αὐτῶν = "their", so ὁ is `s`. The TODO tail (κύκλῳ
  τοῦ θρόνου, the articles, πρεσβυτέρων) is linked as "encircling the#1
  throne" and "elders"; each καί keeps its own "and".
- 5:5 the second article ὁ (before ἐκ τῆς φυλῆς) and τάς (25) are `s`;
  αὐτοῦ = "its".
- 5:8, 5:10 opening καί `s` (BSB starts with "When" / a new sentence).
  5:9 λέγοντες `s` (BSB uses a colon).

## Doubts for the peer

- 5:6 "The Lamb" is `t`. You could read it as Ἀρνίον "understood again"
  (link "Lamb#1" to 15 with "a Lamb"), but BSB adds it only to give the
  participle ἔχων a subject, so I treated it like a supplied name.
- 5:6 "encircled by" on the second ἐν μέσῳ. The other choice is `t` for
  "encircled by" and `s` for the second ἐν μέσῳ, but that is a split pair
  if "encircled by" is read as BSB's rendering of "in the midst of".
- 5:10 "to#1 serve our God": I put "to" on Θεῷ (the dative) and "serve"
  `t`. The other choice is "to serve" both `t`, with Θεῷ = "God" only.
- 5:3 and 5:4 "look inside" stays on βλέπειν (kept from the publisher, like
  "fell down" for ἔπεσαν). A stricter reading would make "inside" an added
  adverb (`t`).
- 5:13 τά = "that" (kept from the publisher minus "is"). If you read
  "that" as an added relative, τά would be `s` and "that" `t`.
- 5:11 φωνήν = "the#0 voices" is kept (singular noun, plural English, no
  Greek article).
