70 of the 419 publisher `g` rows changed, in 24 verses (in 1:25 and 1:26
only TODO tokens were settled). About 25 of them only gained

## Publisher rows corrected

English words with no Greek counterpart that had been folded into a Greek
word (now `t`):

- Supplied names: "Jesus" (1:7 εἶπεν, 1:22 ἀνελήμφθη).
- Supplied objects or complements: "us" (1:24 Show), "discuss" (1:4 μου,
  now "Me" only), "to heaven" (1:2 ἀνελήμφθη), "here" (1:11 ἑστήκατε).
- 1:1 "In" off the article (the article is now "my", used as a possessive).
- 1:13 "son" twice (James son of Alphaeus, Judas son of James), as in
  Luke 6:15-16: the bare genitive has no word for "son".
- 1:15 "and" before "said" had been put on the article τό of ἐπὶ τὸ αὐτό;
  it joins the participle ἀναστάς to εἶπεν, so `t`.
- 1:18 "and burst open": "and" joins γενόμενος to ἐλάκησεν (`t`); "there"
  had been put on καί and is now `t`, with καί `s`.
- 1:21 "to choose" and "one" (one of the men) are BSB additions; 1:22
  "For" and "must" are `t` (see doubts).

Words on the wrong Greek word:

- Lone articles carrying a noun or preposition: 1:4 τήν "the gift" (gift
  now with ἐπαγγελίαν: gift + promised), 1:8 τοῦ "when the", 1:11 ὁ "same"
  (now `s`; "This same" on οὗτος), 1:18 τῆς "for his" (his only; for goes
  to ἀδικίας), 1:19 τῇ "in" (now with διαλέκτῳ), 1:21 τῶν "one of the",
  1:22 τῆς "of" (now with ἀναστάσεως). 1:6 τῷ Ἰσραήλ: "to" moved onto the
  article (indeclinable name).
- When/As/After moved to the participle or genitive absolute: 1:8 "when"
  to ἐπελθόντος; 1:9 "After" from the opening καί to εἰπών; 1:10 "as" from
  αὐτοῦ to πορευομένου.
- Auxiliaries and optatives put on the wrong word: 1:6 "will You" was on
  ἐν (now with ἀποκαθιστάνεις); 1:20 "May" was on αὐτοῦ and ἕτερος (now
  May + be, May + take on the imperatives).
- 1:3 "of" moved from τεσσεράκοντα to "over a span of" (διά).
- 1:5 "in a few" had been put on πολλάς alone: now οὐ … πολλάς = "a few"
  (litotes), μετά = "in", ταύτας `s`.
- 1:11 "in the same way" had been put on the relative ὅν alone: now οὕτως …
  ὃν τρόπον together. οἳ = "they", εἶπαν = "said".
- 1:12 "of" had been put on καλουμένου: now τοῦ καλουμένου `s` (BSB has no
  "called"), "of Olives" on Ἐλαιῶνος.
- 1:17 "in this": "in" belongs to "shared in" (ἔλαχεν τὸν κλῆρον).
- 1:24 "know everyone's heart" was all on καρδιογνῶστα: everyone's now goes
  to πάντων (was TODO).

Negatives split off verbs: 1:4 μή (Do + leave / not), 1:20 μή (no / let
there be).

## Notable decisions

- 1:1 ὦ Θεόφιλε: the explicit ὦ keeps "O"; Θεόφιλε = Theophilus. τε … καί:
  τε `s`, καί = and (also 1:8, 1:13).
- 1:4 συναλιζόμενος is singular (Jesus) but BSB has "while they were
  gathered together"; kept as one group with a note. ἀπό absorbed into
  "leave" (`s`), as εἰς in Matthew 24:38.
- 1:6/1:18 μὲν οὖν: οὖν = So/Now, μέν `s` (Hebrews 7:11, 8:4).
- 1:10 ὡς clause made the main clause: ὡς `s`, "when" on the καί before
  ἰδού (the When-on-καί convention), "as" on the genitive absolute.
- 1:15 ὄχλος … ἐπὶ τὸ αὐτό = "a gathering" (one group); ὀνομάτων = "of";
  ἦν and τε `s` (BSB's parenthesis). ἑκατὸν / εἴκοσι kept split as the
  publisher had them (each English number word shows its Greek word).
- 1:25 τὸν τόπον τῆς ("the place of") `s`: BSB says "take up this
  ministry". The second τόπον is "place"; the first τόν is "his"
  (possessive article), ἴδιον = "rightful". In 1:7 the publisher's "His own"
  on ἰδίᾳ was kept (τῇ `s`); both are acceptable and were not changed for
  style.
- 1:24 Σὺ Κύριε: Σύ is an explicit pronoun and keeps "You" on its own;
  Κύριε = Lord.

## Doubts for the peer

- 1:21-22: δεῖ is "it is necessary" in 1:21; BSB repeats it as "must" in
  1:22 with γενέσθαι. A cross-verse link was not used (no verse-division
  difference), so "must" is `t` with a reason naming δεῖ. Likewise "one"
  in 1:21 anticipates ἕνα in 1:22 (`t`).
- 1:11 "will come back" kept on ἐλεύσεται (phrasal verb), while "here" in
  "stand here" was made `t`. 1:12 "away" kept on ἔχον (publisher row).
- 1:15 joining ὄχλος with ἐπὶ τὸ αὐτό under "a gathering" (alternative:
  ὄχλος = a gathering, ἐπὶ τὸ αὐτό `s`).
- 1:9 "watched as" kept on βλεπόντων (genitive absolute rendered as the
  main clause, "as" introducing ἐπήρθη).
