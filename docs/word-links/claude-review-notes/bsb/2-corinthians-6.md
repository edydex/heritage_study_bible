## Publisher rows corrected

Connectives and prepositions carrying words they do not mean:

- 6:1 δέ "As" → "then" (the connective BSB renders as then); καί "then" →
  `s` (unrendered "also"); "As" joins the participle: Συνεργοῦντες "God's
  fellow workers" → "As + fellow workers"; "God's" `t` (the Greek names no
  God here).
- 6:4 ἐν (15) "and" → `s`; "and" `t`. 6:6 ἐν (6) "and" → `s`, ἐν (11) "and
  in" → "in"; both "and" `t`. 6:7 ἐν (3) "and in" → "in"; "and" `t`.
- 6:5 ἀκαταστασίαις "and riots" → "riots", νηστείαις "and hunger" →
  "hunger"; both list "and" `t` (no καί).
- 6:12 δέ "It is" → "but"; ἐν (6) "but" → `s`. "It is … that" `t` (cleft);
  στενοχωρεῖσθε (4) "that is restrained" → "is restrained".
- 6:13 δέ "As" → `s`; "As a … exchange" goes on the adverbial accusative
  ἀντιμισθίαν; αὐτήν "a fair" → "fair".

Lone article: 6:7 τῶν "in the" → "the"; "in + right hand" on δεξιῶν.

Supplied copulas, verbs, nouns, possessives and subjects (`t`):

- 6:2 καιρός "is the time", ἡμέρα "is the day" → "the time", "the day";
  both "is" `t`.
- 6:3 διδόντες "We put" → "put", 6:4 συνιστάνοντες "we commend" →
  "commend"; "We/we" `t` (participles). 6:3 μηδενί "anyone's way" →
  "anyone's", "way" `t` (English idiom); μή "no one" → "no", "one" `t`
  (BSB supplies a subject when it turns the passive μωμηθῇ active).
- 6:8 ὡς "viewed as" → "as"; "viewed" `t`.
- 6:9 ζῶμεν "we live on" → "we live"; "on" `t` (added adverb).
- 6:13 τέκνοις "my children" → "children", "my" `t`; ὑμεῖς "your hearts"
  → "your", "hearts" `t`; "you" (object of ask) `t`.
- 6:14 μετοχή "partnership can" → "partnership"; καί "have with" →
  "with"; φωτί "does light have" → "light"; "can", "have", "does", "have"
  `t` (no verb in the Greek questions).
- 6:15 Χριστοῦ "is there between Christ" → "Christ", "is there" `t`;
  πρός "and" → "between + and"; πιστῷ "does a believer" → "a believer",
  "does" `t`.
- 6:16 μετά "can exist between" → "between + and"; εἰδώλων "and idols" →

Other splits and joins:

- 6:9 θανατούμενοι "not killed" → "killed"; μή → "not" (negative split).
- 6:10 πολλούς "making many" → "many"; πλουτίζοντες "rich" → "making +

- 6:11 ἀνέῳγεν "have spoken freely" → στόμα + ἀνέῳγεν joined as the idiom

- 6:14 Μή "Do not" → "not"; γίνεσθε "be" → "Do + be".
- 6:16 αὐτοῖς "them" → "them + them" (understood again with "walk

- 6:17 αὐτῶν "among them" → "them"; μέσου ("midst") gets "among".
- 6:18 ἔσομαι + εἰς and ἔσεσθε + εἰς joined as "I will be" / "will be".

## Notable decisions

- The catalogues 6:4-7: each ἐν links to the "in" BSB prints; where BSB
  lets one "in" (or one "through" in 6:8) cover several nouns, the others
  are `s`, and the "and" BSB uses to close each list is `t`.
- The ὡς … καί/δέ pairs 6:8-10: ὡς links "as" where BSB prints it (6:8 and
  the start of 6:9); the later ὡς are `s`. Each καί or δέ links the "yet"
  or "and yet" that renders it; ἰδού in 6:9 is `s`.
- 6:12: BSB's cleft states "restrained" once; it goes on the second
  στενοχωρεῖσθε (the clause about "yours"), and the first is `s`.
  "affection" stays with σπλάγχνοις although English places it with "our".
- 6:15-16: "between … and" goes on πρός / μετά, the word that relates the
  two nouns (publisher had "between" on Χριστοῦ in one verse and on μετά in
  the other).
- 6:16 ἐνπεριπατήσω (22) was not linked by the publisher; its EVIDENCE line
  shows one word differing, probably the spelling ἐμπεριπατήσω. Linked to
  "walk among" with a neutral note.
- 6:18 εἰς: joined with the verb ("I will be", "will be"), not `s`.

## Doubts for the peer

- 6:13 αὐτήν ("the same") → "fair": linked as a shifted rendering (the
  same in return = a fair exchange). An `s` + `t` split is the alternative.
- 6:13 λέγω ("I speak") → "I ask": linked as the same speech act.
- 6:9 "on" in "we live on" made `t` as an added adverb; keeping "live on"
  whole with ζῶμεν is defensible.
- 6:3 "way" `t` in "in anyone's way", while 6:4 keeps "every way" on παντί
  (1 Corinthians 1:5); and "one" `t` in "no one".
- 6:12 which στενοχωρεῖσθε carries "is restrained".
