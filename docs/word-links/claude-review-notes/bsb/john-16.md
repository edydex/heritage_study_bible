# BSB John 16: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: all 33 (16:1-33). No KEEP blocks in this chapter. Final
check: `fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 33, "problems": 0}`; a `totext --all`
round trip of the output gives the same decision lines.

No Synoptic parallel. Conventions followed from the reviewed John 1-9 files
(and the sibling John 12-15 drafts for consistency): ἀμὴν ἀμὴν λέγω ὑμῖν ὅτι
with the ὅτι `s` (John 5:25, 8:34); "in My name" = ἐν → in, τῷ `s`,
ὀνόματι → name, μου → My (John 5:43, 14:13-14, 14:26); Παράκλητος →
Advocate with its article → the (14:16, 14:26, 15:26); οὐ + δύναμαι joined as
cannot (7:34, 7:36); διὰ τοῦτο = That + why with the copula `t` (9:23);
τὰ ἴδια → his + own (1:11); πρὸς / μετ’ ἀλλήλων after asked `s` (4:33).

## Publisher rows corrected

Negatives split off verbs (οὐ/μή/οὐ μή → not; the verb keeps will/do/have/am):

- 16:1 μή; 16:3 οὐκ (they have + known); 16:4 οὐκ (I did + tell); 16:7 οὐ μή → not, ἔλθῃ = will + come; 16:9 οὐ (they do + believe); 16:13 οὐ (He will + speak); 16:17, 16:19 οὐ (you will + see); 16:18 οὐκ (We do + understand); 16:24 οὐκ (you have + asked); 16:26 οὐ (I am + saying).
- οὐκέτι split as no longer / no more: 16:10 (you will + see), 16:25 (I will + speak).

English-only words folded into Greek words (now `t`):

- 16:12 βαστάζειν "bear to hear it" → bear; `t to hear it`.
- 16:14 "disclosing it", 16:15 "disclose it" → `t it`.
- 16:15 λαμβάνει "the Spirit will take" → will take; `t the Spirit` (BSB names the subject).
- 16:15 διὰ τοῦτο "That is why" (τοῦτο unlinked) → That + why; `t is`.
- 16:32 ἐλήλυθεν "has already come" → has + come; `t already`.

Joins, moves and wrong owners:

- 16:2 ὁ ἀποκτείνας, 16:5 τὸν πέμψαντα, 16:13 τὰ ἐρχόμενα: article + participle joined (who kills / Him who sent / what is to come).
- 16:7 ἐὰν … μή joined as Unless (μή was unlinked); συμφέρει "it is for your benefit" → it is + benefit, ὑμῖν = for your.
- 16:8 καί "And when" → And; "when He comes" belongs to the participle ἐλθών.
- 16:11 τούτου "of this" → this; κόσμου = of + world (as the 12:31 peer file).
- 16:12 "I still have" → Ἔτι = still, ἔχω = I + have.
- 16:13 ἐκεῖνος linked to He (main clause); ὁδηγήσει = will guide.
- 16:14, 16:15 τοῦ ἐμοῦ: the lone article carried "what is" → joined as what is Mine. 16:14 ὅτι = by, λήμψεται = taking (was "by taking").
- 16:15 ὁ "to the" → the; ἔχει = belongs to.
- 16:17, 16:18 ἐστιν "is" removed: BSB's "Why is He telling/saying" recasts "What is this that He says"; ἐστιν τοῦτο ὅ `s`, the auxiliary "is" goes with λέγει.
- 16:18 τὸ μικρόν: the article carried "a" → `s`; μικρόν = a little while.
- 16:19 "Jesus said" → Ἰησοῦς = Jesus, εἶπεν = said; Περί "why" → Περὶ τούτου = why.
- 16:21 ὅταν τίκτῃ = in childbirth (ὅταν was unlinked); οὐκέτι μνημονεύει = she forgets (one English verb).
- 16:23 ἄν τι = whatever; οὐκ = no longer, ἐρωτήσετε = you will + ask.
- 16:25 ἐν παροιμίαις = this way (ἐν was unlinked).
- 16:26 ὑμῶν "your behalf" → your; περί = on + behalf.
- 16:28 ἐλήλυθα εἰς = entered; 16:29 ἐν παρρησίᾳ = plainly.
- 16:30 καί "and that" → and; ὅτι = that + that (English repeats it before the second clause).
- 16:31 Ἄρτι = finally (split from "Do you finally believe").
- 16:32 κἀμέ = and + Me (the καί was on ἀφῆτε); ἀφῆτε = you will leave.

## Notable decisions

- μικρόν: each occurrence links as one unit to "In a little while" / "after a little while" (16:16, 16:17, 16:19) or "a little while" (16:18). The καί after each μικρόν is `s` (as 14:19 draft); πάλιν = then (16:16, 16:17, 16:19).
- Παράκλητος (16:7) = Advocate, ὁ = the; no note.
- "in My name" (16:23, 16:24, 16:26): ἐν = in, ὀνόματι = name, μου = My, τῷ `s`; no note on what the phrase attaches to.
- 16:8 περί before righteousness and judgment `s` (BSB does not repeat "in regard to"); 16:9 μέν and 16:10 δέ `s`.
- 16:13 is the only verse with a reading difference that touches links. The English "into all truth" and "hears" match the N1904 words, so εἰς / ἀλήθειαν / πᾶσαν and ἀκούει are linked with a same-sense note (probably a different preposition phrase and a future form of the verb in BSB's Greek).
- 16:19: the EVIDENCE line shows one extra publisher word (probably an article with Ἰησοῦς); nothing to link or note.
- 16:22 ἀφ’ ὑμῶν: ἀφ’ = away (publisher), ὑμῶν `s`.

## Doubts for the peer

- 16:13 ἐκεῖνος → He. The pronoun is the subject of ἔλθῃ; BSB names that subject as the Spirit of truth and puts "He" on the main verb. `s 3` with "He" on ὁδηγήσει is the alternative.
- 16:17, 16:18: unlinking ἐστιν from BSB's "is" (auxiliary of telling/saying). The publisher linked it.
- 16:19 Περὶ τούτου = why, with the following ὅτι `s`. Linking ὅτι too, or `t why`, are alternatives.
- 16:14 ὅτι = by (causal "because" shifted to means) rather than `s` + `t by`.
- 16:23 οὐκ = no longer, with a note; "longer" has no Greek word. `g no` + `t longer` is the alternative.
- 16:30 one ὅτι linked to both "that"s.
