# BSB 1 Corinthians 11: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: all 34 (11:1-34). No KEEP blocks in this chapter. Final
check: `fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 34, "problems": 0}`.

102 of the 448 publisher `g` rows changed, in 32 of the 34 verses. 11:7 and
11:14 keep every publisher row and only gained `s` lines for μέν, γάρ and
articles. 17 of the 102 only join a TODO token into a unit, with the English
unchanged (listed under "Joined units"). Unchanged rows were checked too. No
"of the"/"to the"/"for the"/"with the"/"about the" now sits on a lone
article, no copula sits on a lone article, and no first- or second-person
word, modal or negative sits on a participle or infinitive.

Readings: EVIDENCE shows the publisher's Greek equals N1904 in every verse,
so no reading is claimed. No cross-verse links.

Words of institution (11:23-26) are linked word by word with grammar notes
only. Where the Greek matches the reviewed Gospel accounts, the links match
them: Luke 22:19 for τοῦτο ποιεῖτε εἰς τὴν ἐμὴν ἀνάμνησιν (this / do / in /
τήν `s` / ἐμήν = "of Me" / remembrance) and for "it" after broke as `t`;
Luke 22:20 for ὡσαύτως = "In the same way", τό = "the", τὸ δειπνῆσαι =
"supper", "He took" `t`, Τοῦτο τὸ ποτήριον ἡ καινὴ διαθήκη (τό `s`, ἡ =
"the"), ἐν τῷ … αἵματι (τῷ `s`); Matthew 26:26 for Τοῦτό … ἐστιν τὸ σῶμα
(This / is / τό `s` / body). Here 11:25 has its own ἐστίν, so "is" links to
it (in Luke 22:20 it is `t`).

Other conventions followed: John 6:54 and John 5:24 for one article over two
participles (ὁ ἐσθίων = "anyone who eats", πίνων separate, 11:29); README
rule 3 for τοὺς … ἔχοντας (11:22); Luke 21:24 for ἄχρι οὗ = "until"; Luke
9:24 and John 4:14 for ὃς ἄν = "whoever"; Luke 2:6 and Acts 11:15 for ἐν τῷ
with the infinitive = "as" (11:21); 1 Corinthians 8:10 for εἰς τό with the
infinitive = "to" (11:22, 11:33); 1 Thessalonians 3:5 and 1 John 4:5 for διὰ
τοῦτο as one unit; John 11:21 and Hebrews 8:4 for ἄν = "would" (11:31); 1
Corinthians 2:3 for κἀγώ = "I"; 1 Peter 5:2 and 1 Corinthians 2:12 for an
article rendered "which" with the copula `t` (11:19, 11:24); Mark 4:38, Luke
12:57 and 1 Corinthians 6:16 for "Don’t"/"Doesn’t" on the negative.

## Notable decisions

- Link terms are word by word: κεφαλή = "head" throughout; κατακαλύπτεται /
  κατακαλυπτέσθω = "cover her head" (the middle "veil oneself" carries the
  head), κατακαλύπτεσθαι = "to cover" with τὴν κεφαλήν = "his head";
  ἀκατακαλύπτῳ = "uncovered", ἀκατακάλυπτον = "with her head uncovered"
  (11:13, no κεφαλή in that verse); δόξα = "glory"; ἐξουσίαν = "authority"
  only; σχίσματα = "divisions"; αἱρέσεις = "differences"; κυριακόν = "the
  Lord’s", δεῖπνον = "Supper"; ἀνάμνησιν = "remembrance"; διαθήκη =
  "covenant"; ἀναξίως = "in an unworthy manner"; δοκιμαζέτω = "must examine".
- 11:16 τοιαύτην ("such") is `s` and "other" `t`: BSB's "no other practice"
  uses a word of different meaning, so it is not linked (split-pair rule).
- 11:4 κατὰ κεφαλῆς ἔχων keeps the publisher split (κατά = "covered", ἔχων =
  "with") with notes; only "his#0" was taken off the noun.
- 11:7 ὑπάρχων "since he is" and 11:24 εὐχαριστήσας "when He had given
  thanks" keep a third-person subject on the participle, as reviewed
  chapters do (Luke 18:36, Ephesians 1:20); only "I/we/you" were moved.
- 11:20 ἐπὶ τὸ αὐτό is `s`: the publisher keeps "come together" on
  Συνερχομένων, and BSB has one "together".
- 11:26 τό = "this" before cup (article with demonstrative force, matching
  "this bread"); τοῦτον stays only with bread.
- 11:28 καὶ οὕτως ("and so") = "before" as one unit: BSB states the same
  order of examining and then eating.

## Doubts for the peer

- 11:16 τοιαύτην `s` / "other" `t` versus linking with a note. I chose the
  split because "other" and "such" differ in meaning here.
- 11:16 "nor do the churches": "do" `t`, not linked to ἔχομεν as understood
  again.
- 11:19 the publisher's γάρ = "indeed", καί = "And" is kept; the reverse
  (γάρ = "And", καί = "indeed") is also defensible.
- 11:22 ἐπαινῶ = "I will" (present verb, praise elided) and "No" `t`.
- 11:34 "result in" on εἰς and "when#0" `t` in BSB's recast purpose clause;
  "about" on λοιπά rather than on the verb.
- 11:5 τῇ ἐξυρημένῃ = "her head were shaved" keeps "head" inside the unit.
- 11:17 οὐκ … ἀλλά = "than" in "more harm than good".
