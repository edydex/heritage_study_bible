# BSB Revelation 7: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: all 17 (7:1-17). No KEEP blocks in this chapter. Final
check: `fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 17, "problems": 0}`.

75 of the 320 publisher `g` rows changed, spread over all 17 verses. About
a third of these are joins: a TODO token added to a publisher row, with the
English left as it was. I checked the unchanged rows too. No "of the"/"to
the"/"be to" now sits on a lone article. No supplied copula, verb, object
or possessive sits on a Greek word. No person word ("They", "he") sits on a
participle. Every "will" sits on a finite verb.

Readings: EVIDENCE shows a word difference from N1904 only in 7:6, 7:7
and 7:8. In each verse it is one tribe name that BSB renders anyway, so
each is linked with "Same name, spelled differently in BSB's Greek text":
- 7:6 Naphtali (probably Νεφθαλίμ).
- 7:7 Levi (probably Λευί).
- 7:8 Benjamin (probably Βενιαμίν).
7:3 shows 0/0 word differences, so no reading is claimed there. There are
no cross-verse links.

Conventions followed (Revelation 1 and the brief):
- "forever and ever": εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας = "forever", τῶν αἰώνων = "and#6
  ever" (7:12, as 1:6 and 1:18).
- Article + participle titles joined: 7:4 τῶν ἐσφραγισμένων, 7:10 τῷ
  καθημένῳ, 7:13 οἱ περιβεβλημένοι, 7:14 οἱ ἐρχόμενοι, 7:15 ὁ καθήμενος.
- "a" goes with its noun, and so does a "the" that has no Greek article:
  7:2 and 7:10 φωνῇ = "in a + voice", μεγάλῃ = "loud" (as 1:10 and Acts);
  7:2 Θεοῦ = "of the + God"; 7:4 φυλῆς = "the#1 tribes".
- Negatives split from verbs: 7:3 Μή = "not", ἀδικήσητε = "Do + harm"
  (as 1:17).
- Tribe list (7:5-8), done the same way in all twelve lines, as in Luke
  2:36 ἐκ φυλῆς Ἀσήρ: ἐκ = "From/from", φυλῆς = "the tribe" (with "the"
  on the noun), and the name in the genitive = "of <Name>". δώδεκα χιλιάδες
  = "12,000" joined. The participle ἐσφραγισμένοι = "were sealed", rendered
  as a finite verb as in 1:16 ἔχων = "held". The "were" belongs to that
  rendering, not to a supplied copula. Only 7:5 renders the participle.
  7:4 (after 144,000) and 7:8 (end of the list) do not repeat "sealed",
  so those are `s`. None of the names has an article, so the case word
  "of" goes on the name.

## Notable decisions

- 7:9 BSB's "a multitude too large to count" replaces ὃν ἀριθμῆσαι αὐτὸν
  οὐδεὶς ἐδύνατο ("which no one could count"). Linked as follows:
  ἀριθμῆσαι = "to count", πολύς = "large", and οὐδεὶς ἐδύνατο together =
  "too", because "too … to" is how BSB expresses that no one could count
  it. ὅν and the resumptive αὐτόν are `s` (BSB's infinitive has no
  object). This BSB text has no "no one could", so a word-for-word
  negative split is not possible here.
- 7:3 αὐτῶν = "of#0". BSB recasts "seal the servants … on their
  foreheads" as "sealed the foreheads of the servants". The "of" carries
  exactly the possessive relation of αὐτῶν, with the servants named in
  place of "them". I linked it so it is not left as a split pair. δούλους
  keeps plain "servants".
- 7:14 Κύριέ μου joined as "Sir": a polite address, not "Sir" + an
  untranslated "my".
- 7:5 "were sealed" stays on the participle, as noted above. The
  alternative ("sealed" on the participle and "were" `t`) would treat
  the line as a verbless sentence, as in Revelation 9:16 "was".
- Kept from the publisher: 7:2 ἔχοντα = "with" (note added); 7:9 ἰδού =
  "saw" (note added) and εἶδον = "I looked"; 7:13 ἀπεκρίθη = "addressed"
  (note added); 7:14 εἴρηκα = "I answered" (note added); 7:17 ἀνά =
  "in", μέσον = "the#1 center" (not joined, since each word has a clear
  owner).

## Doubts for the peer

- 7:9 οὐδεὶς ἐδύνατο = "too". The other choice is "too large" on πολύς
  (the publisher row) with οὐδεὶς ἐδύνατο `s`. I think "too" renders the
  inability, not πολύς.
- 7:3 αὐτῶν = "of#0". The other choice is αὐτῶν `s` and "of#0" `t`
  ("English makes the servants the owners of the foreheads"), as in 1:20,
  where "of#1" is `t`.
- 7:5 "were sealed" on ἐσφραγισμένοι versus "were" `t`; see above.
- 7:16 οὐδὲ … ἔτι joined as "and never". The other choice is οὐδέ = "and
  never" with the second ἔτι `s` as a repeat BSB leaves out.
- 7:2 "of the + God" / ζῶντος = "living". Revelation 1:15 kept "of many"
  on the adjective πολλῶν. I moved "of the" here because the "the" has no
  article, and like "a" it goes with the noun.
