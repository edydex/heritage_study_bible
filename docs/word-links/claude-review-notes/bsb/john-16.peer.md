# BSB John 16: independent peer review (Claude)

Checked: 33 of 33 verses (16:1-33), every `g`/`s`/`t` line, against N1904,
BSB's English and the publisher record in
`public/data/original-languages/bsb-word-links/john.json` (only 16:13 and
16:19 have a `partial` record). Final check: `fromtext` →
`{"versesOk": 33, "problems": 0}`.

## Changes (2 verses)

- 16:19: `g 9 10 = why` + `s 14` (ὅτι) → `g 9 10 14 = why` with the note
  "Περὶ τούτου … ὅτι ("about this … that") is rendered why." BSB's "why I
  said" collapses "about this, that I said"; "why" stands where ὅτι
  introduces the clause, so ὅτι should not be `s` as having no English
  word. Joined like the reviewed John 5:16 διὰ τοῦτο … ὅτι → because and
  14:22 τί γέγονεν ὅτι → why.
- 16:23: οὐκ = "no longer" (note) → οὐκ = "no", plus `t longer` ("English
  adds longer to contrast that day with now; the Greek has a plain οὐκ
  ("not"), not οὐκέτι."). The EVIDENCE line shows publisher Greek = N1904,
  so "longer" has no Greek word. Linking it to οὐκ would look like the
  οὐκέτι = "no longer" of 16:10 and 16:25. Same treatment as the reviewed
  Hebrews 8:11 (οὐ μή → "No", `t longer`).

## Answers to the author's doubts

- 16:13 ἐκεῖνος → He: **agree**. Precedent: the reviewed John 11:31
  (Μαριάμ → "she" in the next clause) and 5:16 (Ἰησοῦν → "Him", with "Jesus"
  as `t`). There BSB moves an explicit Greek word into another clause and
  the link follows it. This chapter does the same in 16:8 and 16:14, where
  ἐκεῖνος = "He" of the main verb. In the "comes" clause BSB uses only the
  apposition "the Spirit of truth", so `s 3` would leave a word BSB does
  render without a link.
- 16:17, 16:18 ἐστιν unlinked: **agree**. "Why is He telling/saying" is a
  present progressive of λέγει, the same as "what He is saying" for λαλεῖ
  in 16:18, where there is no ἐστιν. Τί/τί = Why is a real lexical link
  (τί often means "why"). ἐστιν τοῦτο ὅ (16:17) and Τοῦτο ἐστιν ὅ (16:18)
  are `s` with a recast reason, as in the reviewed Luke 22:64 and Mark
  5:14. Each "is" has one owner, and no Greek word is dropped without a
  reason. Joining the whole frame to "Why", as 14:22 does, would also be
  acceptable. That is a matter of style, so I did not change it.
- 16:19 Περὶ τούτου = why with ὅτι `s`: changed (see above).
- 16:14 ὅτι = by: **agree**, kept with its note. The reviewed files link a
  recast ὅτι to the English connective BSB gives it ("so", "After all",
  "when", "how" in 11:31). "by" is BSB's rendering of the ὅτι clause, here
  turned from cause into means. This is a meaning shift, not an unrelated
  word, so `s` + `t by` would be a split pair.
- 16:23 οὐκ = no longer: changed (see above).
- 16:30 one ὅτι → that#0 + that#1: **agree**. The same pattern is in the
  reviewed Hebrews 11:6, Matthew 27:24 and others.

## Other points checked

- 16:14-15 τοῦ ἐμοῦ = "what is Mine": an article and a substantival
  adjective joined as one unit, like τὰ ἐμά → "My sheep" in the reviewed
  John 10:14. No lone article carries "is". In 16:15 the separate ἐστιν =
  is#0 and the supplied is#1 (`t`, "That is why", as 9:23) each have one
  owner.
- 16:13 reading notes: these agree with the EVIDENCE line (5 N1904 words
  unmatched, 5 publisher words not in N1904). The tokens without a
  publisher link that fit the count are εἰς τὴν ἀλήθειαν πᾶσαν and ἀκούει.
  ἐκεῖνος, τῆς and οὐ are blank publisher rows of the usual kind. The two
  notes ("a slightly different phrase here with the same sense"; "another
  form of this verb") do not quote the publisher's Greek. "into all truth"
  and "hears" render the N1904 words.
- 16:19: the extra publisher word has no English counterpart. There is no
  note and nothing is linked to it. Correct.
- Παράκλητος (16:7) = Advocate, μικρόν = "(In/after) a little while", and
  "in My name" (16:23, 24, 26) are linked word by word with no interpretive
  notes. The only μικρόν note (16:16) is a plain gloss.
- The splits that split negatives off verbs (16:1, 3, 4, 7, 9, 13, 17, 18,
  19, 24, 26) and the `t` lines (16:12 "to hear it", 16:14/15 "it", 16:15
  "the Spirit", 16:32 "already") are correct.

## Still uncertain

- 16:22 ἀφ’ = "away" (from the publisher's row) with ὑμῶν `s`. The
  alternative is αἴρει = "will take away" with ἀφ’ ὑμῶν `s`. Both are
  defensible, so I kept the publisher's row.
- 16:17/18: whether to join Τί ἐστιν τοῦτο ὅ → "Why" (the 14:22 style)
  instead of Τί = Why with the rest `s`. Both are accurate.
