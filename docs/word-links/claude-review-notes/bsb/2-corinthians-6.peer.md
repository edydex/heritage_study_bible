# BSB 2 Corinthians 6: independent peer review (Claude)

Checked: 18 of 18 verses (6:1-18). I read every `g`, `s` and `t` line of
the author's final file against the Greek and BSB's exact wording, and
compared each verse with the publisher's rows in the base draft and with
the EVIDENCE line. No KEEP blocks in this chapter. Final check:
`fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 18, "problems": 0}`.

Changes: 3 verses (6:3, 6:7, 6:13), all link changes. The other 15
verses are unchanged.

## Changes

- 6:3: μηδενί = "anyone's", "way" `t` → μηδενί = "anyone's way" (the
  publisher's row, with the author's note extended). BSB's "put no
  obstacle in anyone's way" is the same English idiom as Romans 14:13,
  where the reviewed file links "in + brother's way" to the dative
  ἀδελφῷ. Here "in" is on ἐν, and "way" belongs to the rendering of the
  person, not to an added noun. This also matches 6:4 "every way" on
  παντί (1 Corinthians 1:5) and Philippians 1:28 (ἐν μηδενί = "in any
  way"). "one" in "no one" stays `t`; see below.
- 6:7: τῶν (11) = "the#2" and ἀριστερῶν = "in#3 the left" → τῶν =
  "the#2 + the#3" and ἀριστερῶν = "in#3 + left". In τῶν δεξιῶν καὶ
  ἀριστερῶν one article covers both adjectives, and BSB repeats "the". So
  the second "the" belongs to that article, as in Luke 14:3 and 14:21 (one
  τούς linked to each "the"). Before, tapping "the" in "the right hand"
  showed τῶν, but tapping "the" in "the left" showed ἀριστερῶν.
- 6:13: τήν `s`, αὐτήν = "fair", ἀντιμισθίαν = "As a + exchange" →
  τὴν αὐτὴν ἀντιμισθίαν joined as "As a fair exchange", with a note.
  "fair" does not mean "the same", so αὐτήν = "fair" was a cross-wired
  link. But the phrase as a whole, an adverbial accusative ("as the same
  recompense", a like-for-like return), is what "As a fair exchange"
  says. A fair exchange is one where each side gives the same. Reviewed
  files join such phrases the same way: Matthew 27:44 τὸ αὐτό = "In the
  same way", Luke 6:23 κατὰ τὰ αὐτά = "in the same way". This is not 2
  Corinthians 3:18, where BSB's "His image" replaces "the same image" with
  a different idea and τὴν αὐτήν stays `s`. Here the unit is a faithful
  paraphrase, so `s` + `t` would hide a real rendering. δέ stays `s`.

## Answers to the author's doubts

- 6:13 αὐτήν → "fair": changed (see above). Neither the single-word link
  nor the `s` + `t` split; the three Greek words become one group.
- 6:13 λέγω → "I ask": agreed. λέγω is the speech act that introduces the
  appeal (πλατύνθητε), and "I ask" names the same act. The person goes on
  the finite verb. "you" (object of ask), "my" (1 Timothy 1:2) and
  "hearts" (supplied noun from 6:11) are correctly `t`. ὑμεῖς = "your" is a
  shifted rendering of the explicit pronoun, which is fine.
- 6:9 "on" `t` in "we live on": agreed. "on" is an added adverb that
  shows the life goes on. ζῶμεν means only "we live".
- 6:3 "way" `t`: changed (see above). "one" `t` in "no one": agreed. BSB
  turns the passive μὴ μωμηθῇ ("may not be faulted") into an active verb
  and supplies a subject. μή = "no" (as in 1 Corinthians 1:29 and 16:2),
  and "can" stays with μωμηθῇ.
- 6:12, which στενοχωρεῖσθε carries "is restrained": agreed, the second.
  BSB states "restrained" once, for the clause it affirms ("but yours ...
  that is restrained"). The first, negated στενοχωρεῖσθε is a Greek repeat
  BSB does not say, so it is `s`, and its οὐ keeps "not". The cleft "It is
  ... that" is `t` (1 Corinthians 1:30). δέ = "but", both ἐν `s`, ἡμῖν =
  "our", σπλάγχνοις = "affection" with its note: all confirmed.

## Other points checked

- 6:4-10 catalogue: consistent. Each ἐν that BSB prints as "in" is
  linked. An ἐν that BSB does not repeat is `s` (6:4 ×2, 6:5 ×4, 6:6 ×3).
  Each list "and" with no καί is `t` (6:4, 6:5 ×2, 6:6 ×2, 6:7 "and#0"),
  and no ἐν carries an "and". The extra διά in 6:8 is `s`. ὡς is linked
  only where BSB prints "as" (6:8 and the start of 6:9); the others are `s`
  (6:9 ×2, 6:10 ×3). καί/δέ carry "yet" / "and yet", and ἰδού in 6:9 is
  `s`. 6:8 "viewed" `t` (ὡς means only "as") and 6:9 "on" `t` follow the
  same rule for added English. In 6:9 μή = "not", split from θανατούμενοι
  (a plain negative, not a question). In 6:10 πλουτίζοντες = "making +
  rich".
- 6:3/6:4: "We"/"we" `t` on the participles διδόντες and συνιστάνοντες
  is right; a participle has no person.
- 6:11: στόμα + ἀνέῳγεν = "have spoken freely" as one idiom: confirmed.
  Acts 10:34 likewise joins ἀνοίξας τὸ στόμα as one group ("began").
  ἡμῶν = "We" is a shifted possessive, and τό stays `s`.
- 6:14-16: "can", "have", "does", "have" (6:14), "is there", "does"
  (6:15) and "can exist" (6:16) are `t`. The Greek questions have no verb
  (Hebrews 5:11 for a supplied "have" with a dative). "is there" is not an
  existential that renders a Greek verb, since there is none.
  "between + and" on πρός (6:15) and μετά (6:16) matches John 3:25 (ἐκ …
  μετά = "between … and"). καί = "with#1" in 6:14 is the joining word
  rendered by "with". μερίς = "have in common" renders "share" as a unit.
  In 6:14 μή = "not" and γίνεσθε = "Do + be" (a prohibition, 1
  Corinthians 10:7).
- 6:16: αὐτοῖς = "them + them" is the one pronoun serving both verbs,
  which BSB repeats after "walk among" (Acts 5:27, 26:11 and many
  others). In 6:18, ἔσομαι + εἰς = "I will be" and ἔσεσθε + εἰς = "will
  be" are joined as in Hebrews 1:5 and 8:10.
- Readings: in 6:16 the EVIDENCE line shows 1 N1904 word unmatched and 1
  publisher word not in N1904. ἐνπεριπατήσω is the only word the publisher
  left unlinked, so the neutral note "Same word, spelled differently in
  BSB's Greek text" is supported. It does not name the publisher's form.
  6:15 is word for word N1904, and the Βελιάρ note speaks only of the name
  (Βελιάρ, a form of Belial), not of the publisher's spelling. 6:9 and
  6:13 have `partial` records, but all Greek words match (14/14 and
  10/10). Correctly, no reading is claimed there.
- Old Testament quotations, checked word by word:
  - 6:2 (Isaiah 49:8): Καιρῷ = "In the time", δεκτῷ = "of favor", the
    supplied "is" ×2 `t`, the second ἰδού `s`.
  - 6:16-18 (Leviticus 26:12; Isaiah 52:11; 2 Samuel 7:14): ἐκ = "from"
    and μέσου = "among"; ἀκαθάρτου = "unclean thing"; κἀγώ = "and I";
    Κύριος Παντοκράτωρ = "the Lord" + "Almighty".
  All confirmed.
- Articles: no "of the" / "to the" / "in the" sits on a lone article.
  6:7 τῶν (7) = "the#1", τῶν (11) now = "the#2 + the#3", τῆς `s`.

## Still uncertain (left as is)

- 6:16 ζῶντος = "of#1 the living", with Θεοῦ = "God#1". Reviewed files are
  split: 1 Timothy 3:15 and Romans 9:26 do the same, while 2 Corinthians
  3:3, Hebrews 10:31 and 12:22 put "of the" with Θεοῦ. Either is
  defensible, so the publisher row stands.
- 6:11 τό `s`: Acts 10:34 puts the article inside its idiom group. Leaving
  it out of the group is not wrong, so it was not changed.
- 6:8 "viewed" `t`: "viewed as" on ὡς would also be defensible, since ὡς
  here means "as (regarded)". I kept the author's `t`, which matches 6:9
  "on".
- 6:7 "hand" is kept with δεξιῶν ("right-hand"), following Acts 2:33. BSB
  does not repeat "hand" for "left".
