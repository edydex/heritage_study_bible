# BSB John 17: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: all 26 (17:1-26). No verse was already reviewed, so none is
kept. 17:16 had no TODO and its publisher rows are kept as they are.
Final check: `{"versesOk": 26, "problems": 0}`.
40 of the 447 publisher `g` rows were corrected (changed, split or joined).
5 more only gained a note, and 2 only gained a TODO token (17:12 εἰ μή,
17:23 εἰς ἕν).

John 17 has no Synoptic parallel. The conventions follow reviewed John 1-9,
11 and 18 and 1 John: a relative pronoun BSB leaves out is `s` (John 5:20,
6:2); a supplied copula is `t`; κἀγώ is linked to "and I" / "I"; ἵνα with an
English infinitive is "to" (John 4:34, 6:38); a preposition rendered "in …
presence" stays on the preposition (1 John 3:19); an emphatic σύ with a command is `s`
(Luke 10:37).

## Readings (only where EVIDENCE shows a difference)

- 17:21, 17:24, 17:25: the `partial` record shows exactly one N1904 word
  unmatched and one publisher word extra, and the publisher's own English is
  fully matched. The unmatched word is Πατήρ each time, so BSB's Greek has
  another form of the same word (probably the vocative Πάτερ). Πατήρ is
  linked to "Father" with the note "Same word, in a different form in BSB's
  Greek text." No other reading is claimed.

## Publisher rows corrected

- 17:1: "When Jesus" was on Ἰησοῦς and "and said" on εἶπεν. "When" now goes
  on the καί between the two clauses (BSB makes the first one a When-clause),
  Ἰησοῦς = Jesus, εἶπεν = said, and "and" is `t` (it only joins the
  participle ἐπάρας to the main verb).
- 17:2: "to all" was on πᾶν. The resumptive αὐτοῖς ("to them") now carries
  "to"; πᾶν = all.
- 17:5: σεαυτῷ carried "Your presence" and the lone article τῇ carried
  "with the". Now παρά = in + presence, σεαυτῷ = Your, τῇ = the, δόξῃ =
  with + glory (the dative). Emphatic σύ is `s`.
- 17:6: the lone article τοῖς carried "to those". Now τοῖς ἀνθρώποις =
  "to those" (note); οὕς is `s`.
- 17:8: "them" (after received) was on ἔλαβον; it is now `t` (English object).
- 17:9: "their behalf" was on αὐτῶν. Now περί = on + behalf, αὐτῶν = their,
  matching περί = "on behalf of" twice in the same verse. "I do not ask" is
  split: οὐ = not, ἐρωτῶ = I do + ask.
- 17:10: ἐμά carried "is Mine" and the article τά carried "all". Now ἐστιν =
  is + is (understood again), ἐμά = Mine, τά `s`, and the second "all" is
  `t` (the Greek has πάντα only in the first half).
- 17:11: "I will no longer be" split: οὐκέτι = no longer, εἰμί = I will +
  be. "We are one" was on ἡμεῖς; now ἡμεῖς = We and "are one" is `t` (the
  Greek says only καθὼς ἡμεῖς).
- 17:13: "while I am in" was on ἐν. Now ἐν = in and "while I am" is `t`.
- 17:15 and 17:20: "I am not asking" split: οὐ(κ) = not, ἐρωτῶ = I am +
  asking.
- 17:18: κἀγώ carried "I have also". Now κἀγώ = I + also and ἀπέστειλα =
  have + sent.
- 17:20: περί = "on behalf of" (was "on behalf" with "of them" on τούτων),
  τούτων = them; τῶν πιστευόντων joined as "those who will believe" (rule 3).
- 17:21: ἐν carried "are in" and κἀγώ "and I am". The copulas "are" and
  "am" are now `t`. ἵνα and ὦσιν are joined as "May + be" (BSB turns this
  ἵνα clause into a wish), matching "may be" / "may believe" on the verbs
  elsewhere in the verse.
- 17:22: ἕν carried "are one". Now ἕν = one and "are" is `t`.
- 17:24: ἐμήν carried "the" (wrong). Now τήν = the, the second τήν `s`,
  ἐμήν `s` (BSB leaves "My" out). ὦσιν carried "to be"; now ἵνα = to and
  ὦσιν = be (as 17:4 ἵνα ποιήσω = to do). "those" moved from ὅ to
  κἀκεῖνοι (see decisions); ὅ is `s`.
- 17:25: "has not" was on οὐκ. Now οὐκ = not, ἔγνω = has + known.
- 17:26: "known to them" was on αὐτοῖς. Now ἐγνώρισα = I have made + known,
  αὐτοῖς = to them. "it" (from "make it known") is `t`. The relative ἥν
  carried "You have for"; that now goes on ἠγάπησας (note), and ἥν is `s`.

## Notable decisions

- Vocatives are linked word by word: Πάτερ = Father (17:1, 5), Πάτερ ἅγιε =
  Father + Holy (17:11), Πατὴρ δίκαιε = Father + Righteous (17:25).
- Relative pronouns that BSB leaves out (ὅ, ᾗ, οὕς, ὅσα, ἅ, ἥν) are `s`.
  Where BSB repeats the noun instead (17:11, 17:12 "the name You gave Me"),
  ᾧ keeps the publisher's link to "the name", as John 4:18 and 7:25 do
  with ὅν = "the man".
- "in Me / in You / in them / in Us": every ἐν = in and every pronoun = its
  English pronoun; English "are/am" in 17:21 and "are" in 17:11, 17:22 are
  supplied copulas (`t`), as in John 14:20.
- 17:23 εἰς ἕν = "united" (joined), τετελειωμένοι = "perfectly".
- 17:25 καί keeps "although"; δέ is `s` (BSB expresses the contrast once).
- 17:2 καθώς = "For" and 17:3 δέ = "Now" are kept.

## Doubts for the peer

- 17:24 "those": the publisher linked it to ὅ. I moved it to κἀκεῖνοι
  ("those also"), which is the subject of "to be with Me" and literally means
  "those"; ὅ is then the relative BSB leaves out. If the peer prefers ὅ =
  those, κἀκεῖνοι would be `s` as a resumptive.
- 17:21 ἵνα + ὦσιν joined as "May + be". The alternative is the publisher's
  ἵνα = May, ὦσιν = be.
- 17:13 "while I am" `t` (ἐν = in only). The alternative is to treat it as
  BSB's rendering of ἐν.
- 17:10 second "all" `t`. One could argue πάντα is understood again in the
  second half and link both "All" and "all" to it.
- 17:2 αὐτοῖς = "to". The alternative is `s` for the resumptive pronoun,
  but then "to" would have to stay on πᾶν, which has no dative.
- 17:26 ἠγάπησας = "You have for", με = "Me". "for" could instead go with
  με ("for Me").
