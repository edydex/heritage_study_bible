# BSB John 11: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: all 57 (11:1-57). No verse was already reviewed, so none is
kept. 11:36 and 11:46 had no TODO and their publisher rows are kept as they are.
Final check: `{"versesOk": 57, "problems": 0}`.
98 of the 791 publisher `g` rows were changed (corrected, split or joined).
Another 20 only gained a Greek token from the TODO list.

John 11 has no Synoptic parallel. The conventions follow John 3-5 (article +
participle joined, `αὐτῷ` after a speech verb `s`, a name BSB supplies `t`).

## Readings (only where EVIDENCE shows a difference)

- 11:8 Ῥαββεί → Rabbi and 11:16 συνμαθηταῖς → fellow disciples: spelling only
  (probably Ῥαββί, συμμαθηταῖς).
- 11:22 "But" is `t`: BSB's Greek has an extra word (probably ἀλλά before καὶ
  νῦν). καί → even, νῦν → now.
- 11:29 ἐγείρεται → got up: BSB's Greek has another form of the same verb
  (probably ἠγέρθη).
- 11:45 ὃ → what: BSB's Greek has another form of the same pronoun (probably
  the plural ἅ).
- 11:18 and 11:21: EVIDENCE shows one extra word in BSB's Greek, but no English
  word is left for it (probably an article before Βηθανία and before Ἰησοῦν).
  No reading is claimed.

## Publisher rows corrected

"When"/"After" moved to the participle (δέ/οὖν/καί now `s`): 11:4 (When +
heard; "this" `t`), 11:17 (When + arrived), 11:28 (After + had said; "Martha"
`t`), 11:31 (When + saw), 11:43 (After + had said; "Jesus" `t`).

Article + participle joined (rule 3): 11:2 ἡ ἀλείψασα, 11:16 ὁ λεγόμενος,
11:25 ὁ πιστεύων, 11:26 ὁ ζῶν, 11:27 ὁ … ἐρχόμενος, 11:31 οἱ ὄντες, 11:37 ὁ
ἀνοίξας, 11:39 τοῦ τετελευτηκότος, 11:42 τὸν περιεστῶτα, 11:44 ὁ τεθνηκώς,
11:45 οἱ ἐλθόντες, 11:52 τὰ διεσκορπισμένα.

Lone articles carrying a preposition: 11:2 ταῖς carried "with" (now θριξίν →
with + hair); 11:7 τοῖς carried "to" (μαθηταῖς → to + disciples); 11:9 τῆς
carried "of" (ἡμέρας → of daylight) and τό carried "by" (φῶς → by + light);
11:16 τοῖς carried "to his" (now τοῖς → his, συνμαθηταῖς → to + fellow
disciples); 11:37 τοῦ carried "of" (τυφλοῦ → of + blind); 11:44 τούς carried
"with his" (δεδεμένος → with + bound, τάς → his, τούς `s`). 11:9 τούτου
carried "of" (κόσμου → of + world).

Negatives split off verbs: 11:9 (he will + stumble), 11:26 (οὐ μή … εἰς τὸν
αἰῶνα → never, ἀποθάνῃ → will + die, as in John 4:14), 11:21 (οὐκ → not,
ἄν → would), 11:30, 11:40, 11:50
(οὐδέ → not), 11:51. 11:49 οὐκ … οὐδέν → nothing at all.

English-only words a Greek word had claimed (now `t`): "named" (11:1, 11:49,
as in earlier reviewed chapters); "He" (11:1); "it is" (11:4); "Lazarus"
(11:6, ἀσθενεῖ); "there" (11:11); "Jesus" (11:13, λέγει; see below); "that"

Other wrong anchors:

- 11:6: δύο carried "for". Now ἡμέρας → for + days. ἐν ᾧ … τόπῳ → where.
- 11:11: μετὰ τοῦτο → After; the καί between the clauses is `s`.
  ἐξυπνίσω → wake + up, αὐτόν → him.

- 11:17: ἔχοντα carried "already". Now ἤδη → already, ἔχοντα → had + spent.
- 11:18: ὡς σταδίων δεκαπέντε → a little less than two miles (one unit).
- 11:22: τὸν Θεόν → of Him ("of" moved from the verb).
- 11:23: λέγει carried "her". Now αὐτῇ → her.
- 11:29: ἤκουσεν carried "Mary heard this". Now ἤκουσεν → heard; ἐκείνη → she

- 11:30: οὔπω carried "had". Now οὔπω → not yet, ἐληλύθει εἰς → had + entered.
- 11:31: "Mary" was on αὐτῆς (in μετ’ αὐτῆς, "with her", which BSB leaves out).

- 11:32: "and" was on the participles ἰδοῦσα and λέγουσα. Now `t`. ὅπου ἦν →

- 11:37: ἵνα … μή → from (kept … from), ἀποθάνῃ → dying.
- 11:42: ἀκούεις carried "always". Now πάντοτε → always; εἶπον → I say.

- 11:50: ἵνα → that + that (BSB repeats it); καὶ μή → than.

- 11:53: ἡμέρας carried "on". Now ἀπό → from + on.
- 11:54: ἔμεινεν carried "And". Now κἀκεῖ → And + there. ἀπῆλθεν ἐκεῖθεν →
  withdrew.
- 11:56: δοκεῖ ὑμῖν → do + think / you; οὐ μή → at all; ὅτι `s`.

## Notable decisions

- 11:1: δέ keeps "At this time" (publisher row; a narrative opener). ἀπό →
  lived in; ἐκ (in the apposition "the village of") is `s`.
- 11:2: ἡ ἀλείψασα → to anoint, because BSB says she "was to anoint" (the
  anointing is told in 12:3).
- 11:3: λέγουσαι ("saying") keeps "word" (sent word), publisher row.
- 11:7: ἔπειτα μετὰ τοῦτο → and then (one unit).
- 11:16: Ἄγωμεν → Let + go and ἡμεῖς → us (the publisher had put "Let us" on
  ἡμεῖς and only "go" on the verb).
- 11:19: περί keeps "in the loss of" (publisher row, with a note).
- 11:38: ἐν ἑαυτῷ ("in himself") is `s`; καί → with; αὐτῷ → the entrance.
- 11:39: τεταρταῖος → four days, ἐστιν → It has + been, γάρ `s`.
- 11:41, 11:44, 11:48: the article as possessive → His / his / our (majority
  convention).

## Doubts for the peer

- 11:12: οἱ → His with αὐτῷ `s`. The other option is the publisher's αὐτῷ →
  His (same person, but "to him", not "his").
- 11:13: Ἰησοῦς → He with "Jesus" `t` (John 5:16 precedent). The other
  option is the lexical link Ἰησοῦς → Jesus across the reversed clauses.
- 11:31: Μαριάμ → she (the object of saw, which BSB makes the subject of got
  up) and αὐτήν → Mary. One could argue Μαριάμ → Mary instead.
- 11:50: ἵνα → that + that and καὶ μή → than. The other option is ἵνα → that
  once, with the second "that" in the καὶ μή group.
- 11:52: ὑπέρ → for + for. The other option is the second "for" `t`.
- 11:3: λέγουσαι → word could instead be `s`, with "word" `t`.
- 11:38: ἐν ἑαυτῷ could instead join ἐμβριμώμενος → deeply moved.
