# BSB Matthew 22: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: 46 of 46 (no verse was kept from an earlier reviewer).

Check: `fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 46, "problems": 0}`.

54 publisher rows in 32 verses were changed because they were wrong or
over-claimed. Another 22 rows were reshaped only because a neighbouring
correction freed a word or an article joined its participle, and 14 rows only

## Publisher rows corrected

- 22:1: Καί = "Once" was wrong → πάλιν = Once again; καί explained.
- 22:4: λέγων = "and said" → said, `t and`. Ἰδού = "that" was wrong → Ἰδού
  explained, `t that` (BSB recasts the message as indirect speech).
  πάντα = "everything is" → everything, `t is`.
- 22:5: "they paid no attention" → οἱ = they (article as pronoun), verb = paid
  no attention. "and went away" → went away, `t and`.
- 22:6: "mistreated them", "killed them" → the verbs only; both "them" are `t`
  (τοὺς δούλους αὐτοῦ serves all three verbs).
- 22:10, 22:13, 22:18, 22:22: "and gathered", "and throw him", "and said",
  "and went away" → the verb only, `t and` (participle rendered as a finite
  verb or command). 22:13: αὐτόν = him#1.
- 22:11: δέ = "But when" → But; when + came in. οὐκ = "who was not" → not;
  ἐνδεδυμένον = who was + dressed in. γάμου = "in wedding" → wedding.
- 22:12: "the man was speechless" → ὁ = the man (article as pronoun), verb =
  was speechless.
- 22:14: ὀλίγοι = "few are" → few; the one εἰσιν = are + are.
- 22:15: "and conspired" on ἔλαβον → συμβούλιον ἔλαβον = conspired, `t and`.
  "to trap" → ὅπως = to, verb = trap. "His words" → words, `t His`.
- 22:16: καί = "and that" → and, `t that#1`. "You pay no attention" → οὐ =
  no, verb = You pay + attention.
- 22:19: κήνσου = "the tax" → tax; τοῦ = used for the. "they brought" →
  οἱ = they, verb = brought.
- 22:20: αὕτη = "is this" → this, `t is`. The second ἡ = "whose" was wrong →
  article explained; Τίνος = Whose + whose.
- 22:21: "Jesus told" → told, `t Jesus`. τά = "what is" (twice; a lone article
  cannot carry is) → what, `t is`.
- 22:22: "when they heard this" → `t this`.
- 22:23: τῇ = "same" was wrong → article explained, `t same`.
- 22:24: "declared that" → declared, `t that`.
- 22:25: "first one" → first, `t one`. καί = "and" (married and died) was the
  wrong conjunction → that καί = So; ἀφῆκεν = he left; `t and`.
- 22:26: ὁμοίως = "The same thing happened to" → The same thing,
  `t happened to`. "third brothers" → third, `t brothers`.
- 22:29: "You are mistaken because" / "you do not know" → μή = not, εἰδότες =
  because you do + know.
- 22:30: "people will neither marry" → οὔτε = neither, verb = people will +
  marry.
- 22:31: "have you not read" → οὐκ = not, verb = have you + read.
- 22:33: καί = "When" was wrong → καί explained; When + heard; `t this`.
- 22:34: δέ = "And when" → And; when + heard. "Jesus had silenced" →
  had silenced, `t Jesus`.
- 22:36: "is the greatest" → the greatest, `t is`. 22:39: "is like" → like,
  `t is`.
- 22:37: ἔφη = "Jesus declared" → ὁ = Jesus (article as pronoun), ἔφη =
  declared.
- 22:41: δέ = "While" was wrong → δέ explained; While + were assembled
  (genitive absolute).
- 22:42: ὑμῖν = "do you" → you; δοκεῖ = do + think.
- 22:43: "Jesus said" → said, `t Jesus`. "does David" → David; καλεῖ = does +
  call. "For he says" → he says, `t For`.
- 22:46: τις = "no one" (the negation comes from οὐδέ) → οὐδὲ … τις = and +
  no one.

## Notable decisions

- Joint units: ἀποκριθεὶς … εἶπεν = spoke / answered (22:1, 22:29);
  οὐκ ἤθελον = they refused (22:3); ὃς μέν / ὃς δέ = one / another (22:5);
  διεξόδους τῶν ὁδῶν = crossroads and ὅσους ἐάν = as many as (22:9);
  μὴ ἔχων = without (22:12, where English has no "having"); συμβούλιον ἔλαβον =
  conspired (22:15); οὐ … οὐδενός = no one and πρόσωπον ἀνθρώπων = external
  appearance (22:16); τῷ ἀδελφῷ αὐτοῦ → τῷ = for, ἀδελφῷ αὐτοῦ = him (22:24);
  ἐπὶ τὸ αὐτό = together (22:34); Τοῦ Δαυείδ = David’s (22:42); ἕως ἄν =
  until (22:44); οὐδὲ … τις (22:46).
- Article + participle joined: those he had invited (22:3), those who have
  been invited (22:4), those I invited (22:8), what … said (22:31).
- Genitive and dative articles carry the English case word where there is no
  English "the": τοῦ = of in "of God" (22:16, 22:29), τῷ = to in "to God"
  (22:21).
- One Greek word, two English copies: εἰσιν = are + are (22:14), Τίνος =
  Whose + whose (22:20).
- Textual differences: 22:39 "And" is `t` (N1904 has no δέ; other editions read
  δευτέρα δέ). 22:37 "Jesus" is linked to the pronominal ὁ; the Byzantine text
  reads ὁ δὲ Ἰησοῦς εἶπεν, but BSB's "declared" matches N1904's ἔφη. BSB follows
  N1904 in 22:7 (no ἀκούσας), 22:13 (no ἄρατε αὐτὸν καί), 22:30 (no τοῦ Θεοῦ
  after angels), 22:32 (one Θεός) and 22:44 (ὑποκάτω, "under"), so no other
  reading notes were needed.
- Supplied English (`t`): them ×2 (22:6), that and is (22:4), His (22:15),
  that#1 (22:16), Jesus (22:21, 22:34, 22:43), is (22:20, 22:21 ×2, 22:36,
  22:39), this (22:22, 22:33), same (22:23), that (22:24), one (22:25),
  happened to and brothers (22:26), For (22:43), plus the participle "and" in
  22:4, 5, 10, 13, 15, 18, 22, 25.
- Left unexpressed (`s`): ἀνθρώπῳ in ἀνθρώπῳ βασιλεῖ (22:2, as in 18:23);
  Ἰδού (22:4); ἐκεῖνοι (22:10); the relative οὕς (22:10); ἐκεῖ (22:11);
  τότε (22:21, BSB's So is οὖν); ἐν (22:23); αὐτοῦ in "the widow" (22:24);
  ὑπὸ τοῦ in the passive turned active (22:31); λέγων / λέγοντος before
  quotations (22:1, 22:31, 22:42); addressee datives.
- Kept from the publisher on purpose: ἐκεῖ = where there (22:13, as in the
  reviewed 8:12 and 13:42); ἀπώλεσεν = to destroy (22:7, finite verb rendered
  as purpose); ἐπηρώτησεν = with a question (22:35); μέλει = seek favor and
  περί = from (22:16); αὐτόν / αὐτῷ / αὐτοῦ rendered with a name (22:15, 22:23,
  22:45); modal "can" / "could" with εὕρητε / εὗρον (22:9, 22:10).

## Doubts for the peer

- 22:25: I gave the καί before μὴ ἔχων … ἀφῆκεν to "So" and made the "and" in
  "married and died" `t`. The publisher's reading (καί = and, verb = So he left)
  is the alternative.
- 22:3, 22:8: the agent "he" / "I" stays inside the passive-participle group
  (those he had invited, those I invited). `t he` / `t I` is the alternative.
- 22:34: "they themselves gathered" stays on συνήχθησαν; "themselves" may be
  better as `t` (English emphasis) than as the middle/passive voice.
- 22:19: τοῦ = "used for the" mirrors τοῦ = "of the" elsewhere; check whether
  "used for" should rather be `t` with τοῦ = the.
- 22:16: οὐ joined to οὐδενός as one English negative; `s` for οὐ is the
  alternative.
