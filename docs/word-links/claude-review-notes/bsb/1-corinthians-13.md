# BSB 1 Corinthians 13: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: all 13 (13:1-13). No KEEP blocks in this chapter. Final
check: `fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 13, "problems": 0}`.

32 of the 160 publisher `g` rows changed (2 of them, 13:3 g 5 and 13:10
g 7, were re-keyed into joined units), in 10 of the 13 verses (13:2, 13:7
and 13:9 keep every publisher row; 13:2 only gained `s` rows). Unchanged
rows were checked too. All TODO
tokens are resolved; no English TODO remains.

Readings: EVIDENCE shows the publisher's Greek differs from N1904 only in
13:3 (one word each way). BSB's "exult" follows the "boast" reading
(probably καυχήσωμαι for N1904 καυθήσομαι, "I will be burned"). The meaning
differs, so καυθήσομαι is `s` and "exult in" is `t`, both with the reading
reason. No other verse claims a reading.

Conventions followed: 1 Corinthians 8:1 for the article of an abstract
noun (ἡ ἀγάπη = "Love", article `s`). 1 Thessalonians 5:14 and 2 Peter 3:9
for μακροθυμέω = "is/be patient" with "is" on the verb; the same holds for
χρηστεύεται "is kind", ἀσχημονεῖ "is rude", ζητεῖ "is … seeking",
παροξύνεται "is … angered" and φυσιοῦται "is … proud", because each "is"
renders a Greek verb (no supplied copula). James 5:15 and John 8:55 for
ἐάν/κἄν = "If" as a connective, with "I" on the finite verb. 1 Corinthians
2:11 for a pronoun article τά ("the things") carrying its English noun.
1 Corinthians 4:15 and John 15:4 for a verb understood again with its
person. 1 Corinthians 1:7 for ἐν `s` when BSB makes the noun a direct
object. 1 Corinthians 5:7 and 1 Peter 5:8 for "a … noun" split around an
adjective. 1 Peter 3:7 for an expanded noun ("the gracious gift") kept on
the noun. 1 Corinthians 3 and 4 for an added "only" (`t`).

## Publisher rows corrected

Lone articles carrying a case word:

- 13:1 ταῖς "in the" → "the"; γλώσσαις "tongues" → "in + tongues".
- 13:6 τῇ "in#1 the" → "the"; ἀληθείᾳ "truth" → "in#1 + truth".

Supplied words moved off Greek words (`t`):

- 13:1 ἠχῶν "only a ringing" → "ringing"; "only" `t`; "a#0" joins χαλκός
  ("a#0 + gong"). ἀλαλάζον "a#1 clanging" → "clanging"; "a#1" joins
  κύμβαλον ("a#1 + cymbal"). A participle keeps no article it lacks.
- 13:3 ὑπάρχοντα "possess to the poor" → joined with τά as "possess"; "to
  the poor" `t` (ψωμίζω has no recipient word). παραδῶ "in the surrender" →
  "the#1 surrender"; "in" goes with "exult" (`t`, reading). "of" moved from
  μου to σῶμα ("of + body"); μου = "my". The case word marks σῶμα as the
  object of "surrender", so it belongs on the noun.
- 13:5 παροξύνεται "it#1 is not easily angered" → "it#1 is + angered";
  "easily" `t` (added adverb).
- 13:6 χαίρει "Love takes no pleasure" → "takes + pleasure"; "Love" `t`
  (English names the subject; the Greek has only the verb ending, as with a
  supplied "Jesus"). οὐ (TODO) = "no".
- 13:8 προφητεῖαι, γλῶσσαι, γνῶσις "there are/is X" → the noun only; each
  "there are/is" `t` (no Greek verb in these clauses).
- 13:12 δι’ "as#0 in" → "in#0"; "as#0" `t` (comparison English adds).
  αἰνίγματι "but a dim reflection" → "a#0 dim reflection"; "but" ("only")
  `t`. τότε "then#0 we shall see" → "then#0"; "we#1" and "see#1" join
  βλέπομεν as the verb understood again; "shall#0" `t` (tense English adds).
- 13:13 ἀγάπη "and love" → "love#0"; "and" `t` (no καί before the last list
  item). τούτων "of these is" → "of these"; "is" `t` (supplied copula).

Negatives split from verbs:

- 13:4 ζηλοῖ "does#0 not envy" → "It does + envy" (person on the finite
  verb); περπερεύεται "it#0 does not boast" → "does#1 + boast"; φυσιοῦται
  "it#1 is not proud" → "it#1 is + proud". οὐ (TODO, 3×) = "not".
- 13:5 ἀσχημονεῖ "It is not rude" → "It is + rude"; ζητεῖ "it#0 is not
  self-seeking" → "it#0 is + seeking"; λογίζεται "it#2 keeps no account" →
  "it#2 keeps + account". οὐ/οὐκ (TODO, 4×) = "not"/"no".

Mispositioned link:

- 13:4 the third ἀγάπη (before οὐ περπερεύεται) "It" → "it#0". The publisher
  had put it on the "It" of "It does not envy" (which belongs to ζηλοῖ's
  ending) and folded the real "it#0" into περπερεύεται.

Units and split words:

- 13:10 τὸ ἐκ μέρους: ἐκ (TODO) joins μέρους as "partial".
- 13:11 τὰ τοῦ νηπίου "childish ways" (all on νηπίου) → τά = "ways" (pronoun

- 13:5 "self-seeking" is two English words: "self" = ἑαυτῆς, "seeking" on

## Notable decisions

- ἀγάπη is linked in every occurrence: "love" in 13:1, 2, 3, 8, 13 (2×) and
  the first two in 13:4; the third in 13:4 is BSB's "it#0", its actual
  rendering. In 13:6 BSB's "Love" has no Greek word (subject only in the
  verb), so it is `t`.
- 13:2 προφητείαν = "the gift of prophecy" kept (publisher row; the
  expansion renders the one noun, as 1 Peter 3:7 "of the gracious gift").
  εἰδῶ = "can fathom" (modal on the finite verb, no "I" in BSB's English).
- 13:3 μου = "I#1" in "all I possess" (the possessor becomes the subject).
- 13:8 εἴτε = "where" (a connective BSB renders with a different connective
  is linked).
- 13:12 ἐπιγνώσομαι/ἐπεγνώσθην keep "fully" (it renders the ἐπι- compound,
  not an added adverb).

## Doubts for the peer

- 13:3: "the#1" and "of" around "surrender" — "the" is on παραδῶ (English
  article of the noun rendering), "of" on σῶμα. Putting "of" on παραδῶ
  ("the surrender of") would also be defensible.
- 13:12: "as#0" `t` vs. keeping the publisher's "as in" on δι’. I read "as"
  as English marking the image as a comparison, not part of διά.
- 13:5: τά `s` vs. joining τὰ ἑαυτῆς as one unit on "self" (Philippians
  2:4 joins τὰ ἑαυτῶν, but there BSB has "interests"; "self-" renders only
  the reflexive).
- 13:2: "the gift of" could instead be `t` if the peer reads "gift" as a
  supplied noun rather than an expansion of προφητεία.
