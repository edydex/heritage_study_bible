No parallel Gospel chapter. Conventions followed from reviewed Acts 1-11:
ὅτι before a quotation `s` with a supplied verb of saying `t` (Acts 9:20);
"that" before an infinitive clause `t` (Acts 11:28); a relative pronoun
rendered as a personal pronoun is linked, with an added "and" `t`; the
μέν / μὲν οὖν wording; καθ’ ἡμέραν = every + day (Acts 3:2). The litotes
οὐκ ὀλίγ- has no earlier reviewed precedent.

Words on the wrong Greek word:

- Lone articles carrying a preposition: 17:4 τῶν "of" (now Ἑλλήνων = of +
  Greeks, τῶν σεβομένων = God-fearing); 17:5 τῶν "from the" (now τῶν =
  the, ἀγοραίων = from + marketplace); 17:11 τῶν "than the" (joined with ἐν
  Θεσσαλονίκῃ); 17:17 τοῖς "with the" (now Ἰουδαίοις = with + Jews); 17:22
  τοῦ "of the" (now Ἀρείου Πάγου = of + Areopagus); 17:28 τῶν "own" (now
  καθ’ ὑμᾶς = your own, ποιητῶν = of + poets, τῶν `s`); 17:30 τούς "of"
  (now χρόνους = of earlier times).
- When/While/because moved off the connective onto the participle or
  genitive absolute: 17:1 "When" (δέ → Διοδεύσαντες), 17:6 "when" (δέ, which
  keeps "But", → εὑρόντες), 17:16 "While" (δέ → ἐκδεχομένου), 17:25
  "because" (αὐτός → διδούς), 17:32 "When" (δέ → ἀκούσαντες).
- Negatives split off: 17:6 μή = not (verb = when they could + find); 17:24
  οὐκ = not (verb = does + live; "and" `t`).
- 17:1 "a" moved from Ἰουδαίων ("a Jewish") to συναγωγή (a + synagogue).
- 17:5 ἐθορύβουν had "and sent the city into an uproar": now sent + into
  an uproar, τὴν πόλιν = the city, καί (between the participles) = and.
  ἐζήτουν = in search of + hoping, αὐτούς = Paul and Silas + them,
  προαγαγεῖν = to bring + out ("hoping" and "them" had been on
  προαγαγεῖν).
- 17:6 Οἱ … ἀναστατώσαντες = who have turned + upside down (rule 3); τήν =
  the, οἰκουμένην = world ("upside down" had been on the noun).
- 17:7 Ἰάσων had "and Jason", ὑποδέδεκται had "them", πάντες had "are
  all", Ἰησοῦν had "named Jesus": now οὕς = them, πάντες = all, ἀπέναντι …
  πράσσουσιν = are + defying, Ἰησοῦν = Jesus.
- 17:10 "away" from εἰς to ἐξέπεμψαν (sent + away); "they" from ἀπῄεσαν to
  the relative οἵτινες (new sentence).
- 17:13 "also" from κατηγγέλη to καί (verb = was + proclaiming).
- 17:14 "to" from πορεύεσθαι (now `s`) to ἕως ἐπί.
- 17:16 "he" from παρωξύνετο to αὐτῷ (BSB makes Paul, not his spirit, the
  subject).
- 17:18 "is" from οὗτος to θέλοι (is + trying); "said" after Others now
  links to ἔλεγον understood again (asked + said); "Others" alone on οἱ.
- 17:19 "that" from σοῦ to the article ἡ joined with λαλουμένη (that + are

- 17:23 "even" from εὗρον to καί.
- 17:26 "the" (the whole earth) from παντός to τῆς; "of" from αὐτῶν to

- 17:27 εἰ ἄρα γε jointly = perhaps ("and" `t`); αὐτόν = Him + Him (object

- 17:29 "by" from ἀνθρώπου to τέχνης (by + skill).
- 17:4 and 17:12 οὐκ ὀλίγ- jointly = quite a few (οὐκ had carried "quite").

## Readings

Only where the EVIDENCE line shows a difference:

- 17:7: one word of ἀπέναντι … πράσσουσιν differs in form (probably
  πράσσουσι without the movable ν, not certain); same meaning, linked to
  "are defying" with a note.
- 17:18 Ἐπικουρίων and 17:34 Ἀρεοπαγείτης: spelled differently (probably
  -είων and -ίτης); linked to "Epicurean" and "Areopagite" with a note.
- 17:19: the publisher's Greek has a different connective (probably τε for
  δέ); "So" renders either, so δέ = So with a note.
- 17:22: one extra publisher word with no English counterpart (probably ὁ
  before Παῦλος); no reading claimed.
- 17:30: the publisher's Greek has a different verb (probably παραγγέλλει,
  "commands"). N1904's ἀπαγγέλλει means "declares/announces", so it is
  not linked to "commands": ἀπαγγέλλει `s`, "commands" `t`, both with the
  reading reason; "He" `t`; τὰ νῦν = now.

## Notable decisions

- Scene openers: 17:11 and 17:21 δέ = Now, 17:22 δέ = Then, 17:17 οὖν = So,
  17:33 οὕτως = At that; 17:10 and 17:14 δέ `s` (εὐθέως carries "As soon
  as"/"immediately").
- Connectives rendered with another connective are linked: 17:5 καί = So,
  17:14 τε = but, 17:20 οὖν = and, 17:21 ἤ = and. 17:4 τε … τε: first τε =
  along with (kept), second = and.
- Pronouns rendered by their referent are linked with a note: 17:4 αὐτῶν =
  the Jews, 17:11 οὗτοι = the Bereans, 17:14 ἐκεῖ = in Berea, 17:19 αὐτοῦ =
  Paul, 17:2 πρὸς αὐτούς = the synagogue, 17:33 ἐκ μέσου αὐτῶν = the
  Areopagus.
- 17:27: Θεόν stays on "Him" (object of seek, as the publisher had it);
  "God" in BSB's supplied frame "God intended that" is a new role, so `t`.
- 17:28 Τοῦ = His (the article as a pronoun in the quoted line); γάρ and
  καί of the quotation `s`.
- 17:30 μέν keeps the publisher's "Although" (μέν sets up the contrast with
  τὰ νῦν); οὖν `s`.
- Kept publisher rows: "a large" on πολύ (17:4, as Acts 11), "some other"
  (17:6), "Some of them" (17:18), "have now come" and "now proclaim" (17:6,
  17:23), "proof of this" (17:31), "with" for λαβόντες (17:15), "to see if"
  for εἰ (17:11).

## Doubts for the peer

- 17:30 ἀπαγγέλλει vs "commands": treated as a different meaning (not
  linked). If the peer reads ἀπαγγέλλω + infinitive as "command" (cf. Acts
  26:20, BSB "declared that they should"), it could be linked with a note.
- 17:30 "Although" on μέν rather than on the participle ὑπεριδών.
- 17:5 double links: ἐζήτουν = in search of + hoping and αὐτούς = Paul and
  Silas + them; an alternative is "hoping" and "them" `t`.
- 17:5 καί (between the participles) = the "and" of BSB's series before
  "sent"; an alternative is καί `s` and "and" `t`.
- 17:33 ἐκ μέσου αὐτῶν jointly = the Areopagus; an alternative is ἐκ μέσου
  joined to "left" and αὐτῶν = the Areopagus.
- 17:2 πρὸς αὐτούς = the synagogue (publisher link kept, πρός added); an
  alternative is `s` for πρὸς αὐτούς and `t` for "the synagogue".
- 17:4 / 17:12 οὐκ ὀλίγ- joined (changes the publisher's οὐκ = quite).
