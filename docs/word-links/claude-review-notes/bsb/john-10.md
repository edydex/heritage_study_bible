# BSB John 10: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: 42 (10:1-42). No verse was kept from an earlier review (the
draft had no KEEP blocks). Final check: `fromtext` →
`{"versesOk": 42, "problems": 0}`.

489 of the 580 publisher `g` rows are unchanged. 14 more only gained Greek
tokens from the TODO list, with the English unchanged (αὐλὴν τῶν προβάτων =
sheepfold 10:1; τίνα ἦν ἅ = what 10:6; οὐκ … εἰ μή = only 10:10; οὐ μέλει
αὐτῷ = is unconcerned 10:13; τὰ ἐμά = My sheep ×2 10:14; διὰ τοῦτο = The
reason 10:17; Ἕως πότε = How long 10:24; περὶ ἐμοῦ = on My behalf 10:25; οὐ
δύναται = cannot 10:35; ἐξῆλθεν ἐκ = He escaped 10:39; τὸ πρῶτον = first
10:40; μέν … δέ = Although and πάντα … ὅσα = everything 10:41). The other 77
rows, in 28 verses, were corrected, split, joined or moved.

No Synoptic parallel. Conventions followed from John 6-9: ἐγώ εἰμι = I + am
word by word (6:35, 6:48, 8:12, 8:58); supplied "the" before an anarthrous
noun is `t` (John 3, 7, 9); recitative ὅτι `s`; οὐ μή … εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα =
never (8:52); a pronoun rendered "Jesus" is linked with a note (7:3); the
same-meaning variant note "Same verb, in another form in BSB's Greek text"
(7:19, 7:24, 9:26).

## ἐγώ εἰμι and 10:30

- 10:7, 10:9, 10:11, 10:14: ἐγώ = I, εἰμι = am, article = the, noun = gate /
  shepherd, as the publisher had them. No notes.
- 10:30 ἐγὼ καὶ ὁ Πατὴρ ἕν ἐσμεν: ἐγώ = I, καί = and, ὁ = the, Πατήρ =
  Father, ἕν = one, ἐσμεν = are. All six publisher rows kept, word for word,
  no notes.

## Publisher rows corrected

## Readings

- Only the EVIDENCE lines of 10:7, 10:16, 10:18, 10:24, 10:31, 10:33, 10:40
  and 10:41 show publisher Greek different from N1904. 10:33 differs only in
  order (0 + 0 words), so no reading is claimed there.
- 10:7: `t to them` with the reading reason (one extra publisher word,
  probably αὐτοῖς).
- 10:31: `t At this` with the reading reason (one extra publisher word,
  probably οὖν).
- 10:16: γενήσεται = there will be, noted as another form of the same verb
  (probably γενήσονται).
- 10:18: ἦρεν = takes, noted as another form of the same verb (probably
  present αἴρει); N1904's aorist means "took".
- 10:24: εἰπόν = tell, noted as another form (probably εἰπέ).
- 10:40: Ἰωάνης = John (spelling note, probably Ἰωάννης); ἔμενεν = He stayed,
  noted as another form (probably ἔμεινεν).
- 10:41: Ἰωάνης = John and Ἰωάνης = he (BSB names John earlier in the
  verse), both with the spelling note.

## Notable decisions

- 10:1: εἰς `s` (enter takes the sheepfold as object); the resumptive
  ἐκεῖνος `s`.
- 10:6: τίνα ἦν ἅ joined = what (condensed indirect question).
- 10:7: Ἰησοῦς = He, noted; ὁ `s`.
- 10:8: εἰσίν ("are") = were, noted.
- 10:10: περισσόν = in all its fullness, noted.
- 10:12: καί after ὁ μισθωτός `s`; ὤν = is, noted.
- 10:14: τὰ ἐμά joined = My sheep (substantival article + possessive
  adjective), ×2.
- 10:27, 10:28: κἀγώ = I with a note (BSB uses no "and" there); in 10:15
  κἀγώ = and I.
- 10:29: ὅ = who, noted that N1904's relative is neuter ("what") and BSB
  renders it as who, referring to the Father. The evidence line shows no
  reading difference, so no reading is claimed.
- 10:32: ἔργον `s` (BSB: which of these).
- 10:33: ποιεῖς = declare (noted), `t to be`; καί before ὅτι `s`.
- 10:41: οὐδέν = never (noted).
- 10:42: αὐτόν = Jesus, noted.

## Doubts for the peer

- 10:19: "Jesus’ message" for τοὺς λόγους τούτους. I made τούτους and the
  article `s` and "Jesus’" `t`, because "Jesus’" does not mean "these". The
  publisher linked τούτους = Jesus; that could be argued as a shifted
  rendering of the demonstrative.
- 10:36: λέγετε … Βλασφημεῖς joined = "accuse Me of blasphemy". A finer split
  is λέγετε = accuse and Βλασφημεῖς = Me + of blasphemy.
- 10:18: ἦρεν = takes linked as the same verb in another form. If the tense
  difference counts as a different meaning, it should be `s`/`t` with the
  reading reason.
- 10:29: ὅ = who and τοῦ = My. BSB's English fits the reading ὅς … μείζων …
  τοῦ πατρός μου better, but the evidence line says the publisher's Greek
  equals N1904, so I linked and noted instead of claiming a reading.
- 10:14: τὰ ἐμά = "My sheep" as one unit. The alternative is ἐμά = My, τά
  `s`, `t sheep`.
- 10:12: `t and` before "the sheep are not his own" (οὗ = his). The
  alternative is οὗ = and + his.
- 10:26: οὐ πιστεύετε joined = refuse to believe; the publisher had οὐ =
  refuse.
