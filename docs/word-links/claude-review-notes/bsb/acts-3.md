## Publisher rows corrected

English words with no Greek counterpart that had been folded into a Greek
word (now `t`):

- Supplied names and objects: 3:7 "Peter" (ἤγειρεν), 3:3 "them" (ἠρώτα),
  3:12 "this" (ἰδών "saw this"), 3:13 the last "Him" (ἀπολύειν).
- Connectives BSB adds: 3:11 "and" before ran (ἔκθαμβοι is an adjective);
  3:15 "and" (was on ἡμεῖς) and "but" (was on Θεός); 3:25 "And" (was on
  ὑμεῖς); 3:22 "For" (was on μέν, which does not mean "for").
- Supplied verbs and frames: 3:16 "It is … that" cleft (was on αὐτοῦ and
  ἔδωκεν) and "comes" (was on the article ἡ); 3:21 "comes" (was on
  χρόνων); 3:14 "that" after asked and 3:18 "saying that" (the Greek uses
  an infinitive; "saying that" had been put on στόματος "mouth").
- 3:2 "where": the relative ὅν ("whom") is now "he"; ἐτίθουν = was put.
- 3:1 "One afternoon" had been put on δέ; it is a BSB time setting drawn
  from the ninth hour, so `t`, with δέ `s`.

Words on the wrong Greek word:

- Lone articles carrying a preposition or noun: 3:6 τοῦ "of" (now with
  Ναζωραίου, "of Nazareth"), 3:7 τῆς "by the" (now τῆς = the, χειρός = by
  + hand, as Mark 1:31), 3:10 τοῦ "of the", 3:16 τοῦ "in", 3:20 τοῦ "of
  the", 3:25 τῶν/τῆς/τῆς "of the" (each "of" now with its noun, as in
  reviewed Luke and John), 3:11 τῇ "the walkway" (walkway now on στοᾷ).
- When/While/even though moved to the participle or genitive absolute:
  3:3 "When" from ὅς to ἰδών (ὅς = he); 3:11 "While" from δέ to
  Κρατοῦντος; 3:12 "when" from Πέτρος to ἰδών; 3:13 "even though" from
  ἐκείνου to κρίναντος; 3:26 "When" from Θεός to ἀναστήσας.
- Subjects put on the wrong word: 3:18 "God" was on ἐπλήρωσεν and "He" on
  Θεός (now Θεός = God, προκατήγγειλεν = He foretold, ἐπλήρωσεν = has
  fulfilled); 3:4 εἶπεν "said Peter" (Peter now on Πέτρος, see below);
  3:5 ἐπεῖχεν "the man gave them his attention" (ὁ = the man, αὐτοῖς =
  them, ἐπεῖχεν = gave + his attention).
- Objects inside verb phrases: 3:13 "handed Him over" (Him now on ὅν,
  verb = handed + over); 3:21 "take Him in" (Him on ὅν, verb = take + in).
- 3:1 ἐνάτην "ninth hour": the repeated "hour" now links to ὥραν.
- 3:12 "by" moved from ἰδίᾳ ("own") to δυνάμει (by + power).
- 3:13 "of" moved from ἡμῶν to πατέρων (of + fathers).
- 3:16 ἀπέναντι "in your presence": your now on ὑμῶν (was TODO); πάντων
  `s` (BSB has no "all").
- 3:17 ὥσπερ "as did": "did" now links to ἐπράξατε, understood again
  with the leaders; καί ("also") `s`.
- 3:20 ἀποστείλῃ "that He may send": the second "that" now links to ὅπως
  ἄν, which governs both verbs.
- 3:24 Σαμουήλ "Samuel on": "on" now renders καὶ τῶν καθεξῆς.

Negatives split off verbs: 3:6 οὐχ ὑπάρχει μοι (not / do + have; μοι =
I), 3:23 ἐὰν μή (who / not / does + listen).

## Notable decisions

- Words understood again, linked to the stated word: 3:1 hour + hour
  (ὥραν; ἐνάτην = ninth), 3:4 Peter + Peter (Πέτρος is the subject of
  both ἀτενίσας and εἶπεν), 3:11 walkway + Colonnade (στοᾷ, repeated in
  the name Solomon's Colonnade), 3:17 you acted + did, 3:20 that + that
  (ὅπως ἄν), 3:13 Him + Him (ὅν is the object of both παρεδώκατε and
  ἠρνήσασθε).
- Supplied copula: none in this chapter; 3:16 "It is" is a cleft frame
  (`t`), as in Luke 11:15 and John 12:27.
- Vocatives: 3:12 Ἄνδρες = Men, Ἰσραηλεῖται = of Israel (as Acts 1:11
  Men / of Galilee); 3:17 ἀδελφοί = brothers. No "O"/"You" in BSB here.
- 3:4 σὺν τῷ Ἰωάνῃ: the publisher's "as did" on σύν was kept (it renders
  "with John"), unlike 3:17 where ὥσπερ only means "as".
- 3:9 the opening καί = When was kept (publisher row). The connective
  that actually joins 3:9 to 3:10 is the δέ of 3:10, which is `s`; a
  cross-verse link was not used.
- 3:12 ἤ `s` (BSB starts a new question); πρός absorbed into addressed.
- 3:16 ἐστερέωσεν is active with τὸ ὄνομα αὐτοῦ as subject; BSB makes it
  passive ("has been made strong") and moves the name into the cleft
  sentence. αὐτοῦ = "of Jesus" / "Jesus'" (pronoun rendered by the name,
  as Luke 8:54).
- 3:22-23 Old Testament quotation linked word by word: ὁ = The (before
  Lord), ὅτι `s` (introduces the quotation), ἔσται δέ `s`, 3:23 τοῦ = his
  (possessive article). 3:25 the quoted Καί `s`, ἧς `s` (English zero
  relative).
- 3:26 ἕκαστον = "each of you" kept (as 1 Peter 4:10, Philippians 2:4).

## Doubts for the peer

- 3:15: δέ `s` and BSB's "but" `t`. The δέ contrasts the killing with the
  release of the murderer (3:14); BSB's "but" introduces the relative
  clause ὃν ὁ Θεὸς ἤγειρεν. If the peer reads "but" as δέ shifted, link
  them.
- 3:13: ὅν linked to both "Him"s (handed Him over, rejected Him). The
  alternative is ὅν = the first Him, the second `t`.
- 3:1: "One afternoon" `t` with δέ `s` (alternative: δέ = One afternoon,
  as "Meanwhile"/"Just then" are elsewhere on δέ).
- 3:9: καί = When kept although it is the verse-opening καί; the stricter
  option is καί `s` and "When" `t`.
- 3:5 ὁ (article as pronoun) = "the man"; 3:11 αὐτοῦ = "the man"; 3:12
  αὐτόν = "this man" (all with notes).
