## Notable decisions

- 2:1 ὑπεροχὴν λόγου = "eloquence" as one unit; σοφίας = "wisdom" alone
  (BSB does not repeat the "superiority" idea).
- 2:2 οὐ … τι = "nothing" (the negative and its object together).
- 2:4 καὶ … δυνάμεως: BSB's "the Spirit’s power" joins the two genitives,
  so the second καί is `s`; the opening καί is `s` ("and"/"both"). "of the
  Spirit’s" stays on Πνεύματος (publisher row, not wrong).
- 2:7 ἀλλά = "No" (BSB's corrective opening); λαλοῦμεν = "we speak of"
  (the "of" is the English verb's complement, unlike 2:6's supplied "a
  message of").
- 2:9 Ἅ and ὅσα joined to the single "what" (BSB states the object once
  for both the negated verbs and "has prepared"); ἐπὶ … ἀνέβη = "has
  imagined" (the idiom "come up upon the heart"); ἀνθρώπου `s`; both καί
  `s` (BSB lists the clauses).
- 2:10 γάρ (first) = "But" (BSB renders it with a different connective;
  EVIDENCE shows the publisher's Greek equals N1904, so no reading is
  claimed); the second γάρ is `s`.
- 2:11 τά (twice) = "the thoughts" (article used as a pronoun); the
  attributive τό before ἐν αὐτῷ is `s`.
- 2:13 καί = "And" (BSB reads it as the connective rather than "also");
  πνευματικοῖς = "in spiritual words" and πνευματικά = "spiritual truths"
  (substantival adjectives with BSB's supplied nouns); συνκρίνοντες =
  "expressing".
- 2:14 ἐστίν = "they are" (singular verb with a neuter plural subject).
- 2:15 μέν `s`: EVIDENCE shows BSB's Greek differs by one word here
  (probably τά in place of μέν); the reason in the file says only that
  BSB's Greek text differs.
- 2:16 ὅς = "so as" (relative with future expressing result); συμβιβάσει
  = "to instruct".

## Doubts for the peer

- 2:1: linking κἀγώ to all three "I"s (Acts 22:3 style) leaves ἦλθον with
  "did + come" only. Alternatives: κἀγώ = first "I" with ἦλθον keeping
  "I did + come" and the participle "I" `t`.
- 2:9: joining Ἅ with ὅσα to one "what" versus Ἅ `s`.
- 2:15: ὑπ’ could instead take "subject to" (ὑπό "under"), leaving
  οὐδενός = "not + anyone’s" and ἀνακρίνεται = "is + judgment".
- 2:16: ὅς = "so as" could be read as a word of different meaning (then
  ὅς `s`, "so as" `t`); kept linked because the relative-with-future
  carries the result sense BSB expresses.
- 2:14: "The natural" kept on ψυχικός although the Greek has no article
  (publisher row; "The" is generic and not wrong).
