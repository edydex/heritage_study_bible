# BSB Mark 3: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: 35 of 35 (no verse was kept from an earlier reviewer).

Check: `fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 35, "problems": 0}`.

44 publisher rows in 24 verses were corrected because they were wrong or

neighbouring correction (for example οὐ + δύναται → cannot).

## Publisher rows corrected

- 3:1, 3:3, 3:9, 3:13, 3:20, 3:33: "Jesus entered / said / asked / went up /
  went / replied" → the verb only; `t Jesus` (the name comes from the verb
  ending). Same for "Jesus looked around at" (3:5) and "Jesus called them
  together" (3:23), where the subject comes from a participle.
- 3:1: τήν = "a" (definite article on an indefinite "a") → `s`; "a" goes
  with ἐξηραμμένην (a withered). 3:20: ὁ = "a" → `s`; ὄχλος = a crowd.
- 3:2: εἰ = "to see if" → if; `t to see` (English verb of seeing).
- 3:4: Ἔξεστιν = "Which is lawful" → is lawful, `t Which`.
  ἀποκτεῖναι = "to destroy it" → to destroy; "it" joins ψυχήν (life + it),
  the shared object. ἐσιώπων = "they were silent" → were silent; οἱ = they.
- 3:5: τῇ = "their" was the wrong token → αὐτῶν = their, τῇ `s`. καί(18) =
  "Then" was the wrong καί → καί(18) = So, the verse-opening καί = Then.
  ἐξέτεινεν = "So he stretched it out" → he stretched + out; `t it` (object
  supplied).
- 3:6: ἐδίδουν = "and began plotting" → συμβούλιον ἐδίδουν = began plotting;
  `t and` (participle rendered as a finite verb).
- 3:8: πολύ = "The large" → large; `t The` (as in Matthew 4:25).
- 3:9: πλοιάριον = "have a boat" → a boat; προσκαρτερῇ = have + ready.
  θλίβωσιν = "would not crush" → would + crush; μή = not.
- 3:10: πολλούς = "so many" → many; ὥστε = so + that. εἶχον = "who had" →
  had; ὅσοι = all who.
- 3:11: καί = "And when" → And; ὅταν = when.
- 3:16: τούς = "These are the" (a lone article carrying "are") → the;
  `t These are`. ἐπέθηκεν = "whom He named" → ἐπέθηκεν ὄνομα = He named;
  `t whom`.
- 3:20: δύνασθαι = "He and His disciples could" → could; αὐτούς = He and
  His disciples.
- 3:21: καί = "When" → `s`; ἀκούσαντες = When + heard; `t about this`.
  αὐτόν = "of Him" → Him; "of" goes with to take custody of.
- 3:23: ἔλεγεν = "and began to speak" → began to speak; `t and`.
- 3:24, 3:25, 3:26: δύναται / δυνήσεται = "cannot" folded the negative →
  οὐ + verb = cannot (one English word).
- 3:26: τέλος = "his end", ἔχει = "has come" → τέλος ἔχει = his end has come
  ("he has an end"; "his" is not in τέλος).
- 3:27: δήσῃ = "he first ties up" → he + ties up; πρῶτον = first.
- 3:29: οὐκ = "will never", ἔχει = "be", ἄφεσιν = "forgiven" →
  οὐκ … εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα = never; ἔχει ἄφεσιν = will + be forgiven.
- 3:30: ὅτι = "Jesus made this statement because" → because;
  `t Jesus made this statement`.
- 3:31: ἀπέστειλαν = "They sent someone in" → They sent; `t someone`.
  πρός = "to" was wrong (that "to" is the infinitive marker of "to summon")
  → πρὸς αὐτόν = in; καλοῦντες = to summon.
- 3:32: λέγουσιν = "He was told" → was told; αὐτῷ = He (dative made the
  passive subject). ἔξω = "are outside" → outside; ζητοῦσιν = are + asking for.

## Notable decisions

- Joint units: εἰσῆλθεν εἰς = entered (3:1); εἰσελθών … εἰς = enter (3:27);
  εἰς οἶκον = home; ἄρτον φαγεῖν = eat (3:20); εἰς τὸ μέσον = among us (3:3);
  ὦσιν μετ’ = accompany (3:14); ἐπέθηκεν ὄνομα = (He) named (3:16, 3:17);
  ὅ ἐστιν = meaning; τοῦ Ἰακώβου = his (3:17); οἱ παρ’ αὐτοῦ = His family
  (3:21); ἐὰν μή = unless; οὐ … οὐδείς = no one (3:27); ὅσα ἐάν = as many as
  (3:28); ὃς ἄν = whoever (3:29, 3:35); ἀποκριθεὶς … λέγει = replied (3:33);
  ἡ βασιλεία / οἰκία ἐκείνη = it (3:24, 3:25); ἡ χεὶρ αὐτοῦ = it (3:5).
- Article + participle: τῷ … ἔχοντι = with (3:3); οἱ … καταβάντες = who had
  come down (3:22); τοὺς … καθημένους = those seated (3:34).
- One Greek possessive rendered once for two nouns: αὐτοῦ … αὐτοῦ = Jesus’
  (3:31); σου … σου = Your (3:32).
- Pronouns rendered with the name are kept as links: αὐτοῦ / αὐτόν = Jesus
  (3:2, 3:6, 3:19, 3:31). αὐτοῖς = whom kept (3:17).
- Textual differences from N1904 (only where the publisher Greek differs):
  - 3:1 "the": BSB matches εἰς τὴν συναγωγήν → `t`.
  - 3:5 "your": BSB matches τὴν χεῖρά σου → `t`; τήν `s`.
  - 3:14 "whom He designated as apostles": N1904 lacks οὓς καὶ ἀποστόλους
    ὠνόμασεν → `t`. "of them" is a separate supplied partitive.
  - 3:32: N1904's καὶ αἱ ἀδελφαί σου ("and Your sisters") has no English → `s`.
  - 3:33 second "My": BSB matches οἱ ἀδελφοί μου → `t`.
  - 3:35 "For": BSB matches ὃς γὰρ ἄν → `t`.
  - Same word, different form, linked normally: ἀγαθὸν ποιῆσαι (BSB text
    ἀγαθοποιῆσαι) = to do good (3:4); συνλυπούμενος = sorrow (3:5); ποιεῖ =
    He was doing (3:8, present kept in reported speech; BSB text ἐποίει);
    Ἰωάνην = John and ὄνομα = named (3:17); στῆναι = stand (3:25).
- Kept from the publisher on purpose: πέραν = the region beyond and περί =
  the vicinity of (3:8, prepositions used as place names); ὅσα = what great
  things (3:8); ἠκολούθησεν = accompanied by (3:7); διαρπάσει = he can
  plunder (3:27); λέγει = asked (3:4); τοῖς = on the (3:2, 3:4).
- Left unexpressed (`s`): list-joining καί and ἀπό (3:7, 3:8, 3:16–18),
  recitative ὅτι and λέγοντα (3:11, 3:21, 3:22, 3:28), ἀλλά replaced by a
  semicolon (3:26, 3:29), κατ’ αὐτοῦ (3:6), διά (3:9), αὐτῷ (3:10), resumptive
  οὗτος (3:35), the two objects αὐτόν in 3:2.

## Doubts for the peer

- 3:6: καί + εὐθύς joined as "At this". The alternative is the publisher's
  καί = At this with εὐθύς `s` (BSB has no "immediately").
- 3:5: καί(0) = Then (moved to BSB's second sentence) rather than `s` with
  `t Then`; and "your" as a reading difference (`t`) rather than τήν = your
  (article as possessive). The second unmatched publisher word is very likely
  σου, but its row cannot be seen.
- 3:31: πρὸς αὐτόν = "in" (sent in to Him). Alternatively `t in` with
  πρὸς αὐτόν `s`.
- 3:4: ψυχήν = life + it; the alternative is `t it`.
- 3:4 "Which" and 3:8 "The" as `t`: the peer may judge them English idiom for
  the question / the resumed crowd and restore the publisher rows.
- 3:1 "the": BSB's "the synagogue" could be plain English idiom; it is `t`
  as a reading difference because the publisher Greek has one extra word here.
