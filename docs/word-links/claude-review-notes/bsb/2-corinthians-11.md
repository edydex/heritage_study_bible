## Notable decisions

- 11:1 BSB reads the second ἀνέχεσθε as a statement ("you are already
  doing that"): ἀνέχεσθε = "you are + doing that"; the second μου `s`;
  καί `s` and "already" `t` (καί "indeed" does not mean "already").
- 11:2 and 11:20: one ὑμᾶς linked to each English "you" it governs
  (11:2 ἡρμοσάμην/παραστῆσαι; 11:20 the first three verbs).
- 11:3 "devotion" `t`: BSB turns the nouns ἁπλότης and ἁγνότης into the
  adjectives "simple" and "pure" and supplies a head noun. αὐτοῦ `s`:
  "the serpent's" is ὁ ὄφις made the owner of the cunning.
- 11:6 ἰδιώτης = "not … polished + lacking" (understood again in the
  second clause); ἀλλ’ (6) = "certainly" (ἀλλά in an apodosis after a
  concessive clause); ἐν πᾶσιν `s`, "every way possible" left on παντί.
- 11:12 the first τὴν ἀφορμήν `s`: BSB says "opportunity" once.
- 11:23-29 catalogue: every noun linked; "in"/"from" sit on ἐν or on the
  dative/genitive noun; repeated κινδύνοις and ἐν that BSB does not repeat
  are `s`; list "and" without καί `t`; πολλάκις = "frequent"/"often".
- 11:24-25 numbers: πεντάκις "Five times", τρίς "Three times"/"three
  times", ἅπαξ "once", νυχθήμερον "a night and a day".
- 11:3 EVIDENCE shows one extra publisher word, probably τόν before
  Χριστόν; it has no English counterpart, so no reading note. 11:1, 11:4,
  11:19 and 11:23 show differences with 0 unmatched words (order or
  accents), so no reading is claimed.

## Doubts for the peer

- 11:1 καί `s` + "already" `t`: linking καί = "already" (as the
  "indeed, in fact" of the statement) is the alternative.
- 11:6 "lacking" on ἰδιώτης (understood again) rather than `t`; and
  ἀλλ’ = "certainly".
- 11:6 ἐν πᾶσιν `s` with "possible" kept on παντί; linking ἐν πᾶσιν =
  "possible" is the alternative.
- 11:7 "Was" on ἐποίησα (the question's verb) with "it" `t`; `t` for "Was
  it" is the alternative.
- 11:10 "As surely as" on ἔστιν (following 1:18, where it sits on the
  oath predicate πιστός).
- 11:12 καθώς = "equals" with ἡμεῖς = "our"; εὑρεθῶσιν keeps "be regarded
  as".
- 11:29 "burn with grief" kept whole on πυροῦμαι (figurative burning);
  11:23 "danger" `t` with "of death" on θανάτοις.
- 11:33 second διά `s` + "in" `t`; keeping the publisher's διά = "in" is
  the alternative.
