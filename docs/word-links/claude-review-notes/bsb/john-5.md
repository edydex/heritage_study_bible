## Readings (only where EVIDENCE shows a difference)

- 5:3b-4: BSB's Greek lacks ἐκδεχομένων τὴν τοῦ ὕδατος κίνησιν (5:3 tokens
  9-13). These are `s` with the reading reason. 5:4 (the angel stirring the
  water) has no BSB verse: `x targetAbsent`, and all 27 Greek tokens are `s`.
  No link is invented.
- 5:2 Βηθζαθά → Bethesda: same pool, named in a different form in BSB's Greek
  (probably Βηθεσδά).
- 5:33, 5:36 Ἰωάνην / Ἰωάνου → John: spelling only (probably Ἰωάννην /
  Ἰωάννου).
- 5:15 εἶπεν → told: BSB's Greek has a different verb with the same sense
  (probably ἀνήγγειλεν).
- 5:17 ὁ (in ὁ δέ) → Jesus: BSB's Greek has an extra word (probably Ἰησοῦς).
- 5:19 ἂν μή → unless: BSB's Greek differs slightly (probably ἐὰν μή).
- 5:10 "your" is `t`: an extra word (probably σου); N1904 has only the
  article.
- 5:29 the second "and" is `t`: an extra word (probably δέ before the second
  οἱ).
- 5:12: EVIDENCE shows one extra word in BSB's Greek, but no English word is
  left for it (perhaps an untranslated οὖν). No reading is claimed.

## Publisher rows corrected

Case words moved from a lone article to the noun (as in the later Luke
chapters): 5:1 τῶν → the, Ἰουδαίων → of + Jews; 5:22 τῷ → the, Υἱῷ → to +
Son; 5:25 τοῦ → the, Υἱοῦ → of + Son; 5:33 τῇ → the, ἀληθείᾳ → to + truth;
5:18 τῷ is `s` and Θεῷ → with God.

Lone articles carrying other words:

- 5:8: ὁ carried "Then". ὁ is now `s`; "Then" is `t` (the Greek has no
  connective).
- 5:15: ὁ carried "And the". "And" is now `t`.
- 5:28: οἱ carried "who are". Now οἱ → who, and "are" is `t`.
- 5:29: each τά carried "who". Now οἱ … ποιήσαντες / οἱ … πράξαντες → those
  who have done, τὰ ἀγαθά → good, τὰ φαῦλα → evil.
- 5:30: τό carried "own". Now ἐμόν → My own, and both τό are `s`.
- 5:35: ὁ carried "a". Now ὁ is `s` and λύχνος → a lamp.
- 5:36: τοῦ (with Ἰωάνου) carried "that". Now τοῦ is `s` and "that" is `t`
  (English supplies "that [testimony]").
- 5:43: τῷ carried "his". Now ἰδίῳ → his own, as ἴδιον → His own in 5:18.
- 5:44: τήν carried "that comes". Now τήν → that, and "comes" is `t`.
- 5:47: each τοῖς carried "what". Now γράμμασιν → what + wrote and ῥήμασιν →
  what + say; the articles are `s`.

Negatives split off verbs (οὐ/μή → not; the verb keeps do/did/will): 5:13,
5:23 (twice), 5:24 (will + come), 5:28, 5:30, 5:34, 5:38, 5:41, 5:42, 5:43,
5:44, 5:45, 5:47. Negative pairs joined: οὐ … οὐδέν → nothing (5:19, 5:30);
οὐδέ … οὐδένα → no one (5:22); οὔτε … πώποτε → never (5:37, where the verb had
carried "You have never heard"); καί … οὐκ → nor (5:38); οὐκ ἔξεστιν → It is
unlawful (5:10); οὐ θέλετε → you refuse (5:40).

"When"/"while" moved to the participle: 5:6 "When" from Ἰησοῦς to ἰδών (When +
saw); 5:13 "while" from ὄχλου to ὄντος (genitive absolute).

Article + participle joined (rule 3): 5:2 ἡ ἐπιλεγομένη (which + is called),
5:3 τῶν ἀσθενούντων, 5:7 ὁ ἀσθενῶν, 5:10 τῷ τεθεραπευμένῳ, 5:11 Ὁ ποιήσας,
5:12 ὁ εἰπών, 5:13 ὁ ἰαθείς, 5:15 ὁ ποιήσας, 5:23 ὁ μὴ τιμῶν and τὸν
πέμψαντα, 5:24 ὁ … ἀκούων and τῷ πέμψαντι, 5:25 οἱ ἀκούσαντες, 5:29 (twice),
5:30 τοῦ πέμψαντος, 5:32 ὁ μαρτυρῶν, 5:35 ὁ καιόμενος, 5:39 αἱ μαρτυροῦσαι,
5:45 ὁ κατηγορῶν.

English-only words that a Greek word had claimed (now `t`): "and" in the list
of 5:3; "there" (5:6, κατακείμενον → lying); "in this condition" (5:6, χρόνον
→ time); "it" (5:12, Ἆρον → to pick + up); "Jesus" (5:16, ἐποίει → was doing);
"even" (5:20, μείζονα → greater); "them" (5:21, ζωοποιεῖ → gives + life);
"only" (5:30, καθώς → as); "very" (5:39); "but" (5:43, ἐάν → if).

Other wrong anchors:

- 5:5: τριάκοντα carried "for". Now τριάκοντα καὶ ὀκτώ → thirty-eight and ἔτη
  → for + years. ἦν is joined with ἔχων → had been.
- 5:7: βάλῃ carried "to help". Now ἵνα → to and βάλῃ → help.
- 5:11: ἀπεκρίθη carried "he". The relative ὅς (in ὃς δέ) now → he.
- 5:14: ὑγιής carried "made". Now γέγονας → you have been made, ὑγιής → well.
- 5:18: ἔλεγεν carried "even". Now καί (in ἀλλὰ καί) → even.
- 5:25: νῦν carried "now come". Now νῦν → now and ἐστιν → has + come.
- 5:32: ἡ carried "His". Now ἣν μαρτυρεῖ (TODO) → His, and ἡ is `s`.
- 5:34: δέ carried "Even though". It moves to ἀλλά, the connective between the
  two clauses BSB turns into "Even though …, …"; δέ is `s`. παρὰ ἀνθρώπου →
  human.
- 5:37: the article ὁ carried "who" and Πατήρ "the Father". Now ὁ → the,
  πέμψας → who sent, Πατήρ → Father. ἐκεῖνος (TODO) → Himself, which the verb
  had carried ("has Himself testified").
- 5:38: ἔχετε carried "does". Now μένοντα → does + abide; ἔχετε is `s`
  (BSB recasts "you do not have His word abiding").
- 5:39: the article carried "the very words that". Now αἱ μαρτυροῦσαι → the +
  words that testify, and "very" is `t`.

Other joined units: Μετὰ ταῦτα → Some time later (5:1) and Afterward (5:14);
ἐν ᾧ → While (5:7) and when (5:28); ἐν τῷ τόπῳ → there (5:13); ἵνα μή → or

(5:19); ἃ … ἄν → whatever (5:19); ἐπιστεύετε ἄν → you would believe (5:46).

## Notable decisions

- 5:9: Ἦν keeps "this happened", like ἐστιν → "This is" in 5:10 (subject built
  into the verb). ἐκείνῃ ("that" day) is `s`.
- 5:16: τὸν Ἰησοῦν stays → Him (publisher row), because BSB moves the name
  into the because-clause; that "Jesus" is `t`.
- 5:19: the resumptive ταῦτα and ὁμοίως ("likewise") are `s`; καί → also.
  ἐκεῖνος → the Father is kept (publisher row).
- 5:20 ἅ and 5:36 the second ἅ (relative "that") and αὐτά are `s`; 5:38 ὅν is
  `s` (the One He sent).
- 5:22: οὐδέ joins οὐδένα (no one), and γάρ → Furthermore.
- 5:3: ταύταις → these walkways is kept (a pronoun rendered with its referent,
  like αὐτόν → the man in 5:14).
- 5:28: τοῖς → their (article as possessive, majority convention).
- 5:24, 5:25: ὅτι after "Truly, truly, I tell you" is `s`, as in John 3:11.
- 5:36: μείζω keeps "more substantial than" and 5:20 τούτων keeps "than these"
  (both publisher rows).

## Doubts for the peer

- 5:5: ἦν … ἔχων → had been. The other option is ἦν `s` (BSB drops "there
  was") and ἔχων alone → had been.
- 5:16: Ἰησοῦν → Him with "Jesus" `t`. The other option is the lexical link
  Ἰησοῦν → Jesus with "Him" `t`.
- 5:32: ἣν μαρτυρεῖ → His with ἡ `s`. The other option is ἡ → His (article as
  possessive) with ἣν μαρτυρεῖ `s`.
- 5:34: ἀλλά → Even though. The other option is "Even though" `t` with ἀλλά
  `s`.
- 5:6: "in this condition" is `t`. It could instead stay with ἔχει as part of
  BSB's "had spent … in this condition".
- 5:9: ἐκείνῃ is `s`. One could argue BSB's "this" reflects it.
- 5:39: "very" is `t`, but "words" stays in the αἱ μαρτυροῦσαι group (like
  "man" in "The man who made", 5:11).
