# BSB Luke 8: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: 56 (8:1-56). The draft had no KEEP blocks. In 8:53 every
publisher row was checked and kept unchanged. Final check: `fromtext` →
`{"versesOk": 56, "problems": 0}`.

Of the 879 publisher `g` rows, 654 are unchanged. 36 only gained unlinked
Greek tokens with the English unchanged (for example ἐν τῷ = as/When/As 8:5,
8:40, 8:42; ἐν μέσῳ = among 8:7; ἔχων δαιμόνια = demon-possessed 8:27; εἰ μή
= except 8:51). 33 were joined with a neighbouring row (article + participle
under rule 3, for example ὁ ἔχων = He who has 8:8, οἱ ἀκούσαντες = those who
hear 8:12, 8:14, τὸ γεγονός = what had happened 8:34-35, 8:56). The other 156
rows, in 51 verses, were corrected, split or moved. Mark 4:1-25, 4:35-41,
5:1-43 and 3:31-35, and Matthew 8:23-34, 9:18-26, 12:46-50 and 13:1-23 were
checked as parallels.

## Publisher rows corrected

Names, subjects and objects supplied by English (now `t`):

- "Jesus" on a verb or participle: 8:8 λέγων ("As Jesus said" → As + said),
  8:22 εἶπεν, 8:29 παρήγγελλεν. 8:24 "Jesus got up", 8:52 "Jesus said", 8:56
  "Jesus ordered" → ὁ (of ὁ δέ) = Jesus, the verb alone on the verb.
- "the demons" 8:31 παρεκάλουν, 8:32 παρεκάλεσαν; "The disciples" 8:24
  προσελθόντες; "the man" 8:28 ἰδών, 8:39 ἀπῆλθεν; "this man" 8:27
  ἐνεδύσατο; "the people" 8:35 ἐξῆλθον.
- 8:6 "the seedlings withered" → withered, `t the seedlings` (as Mark 4:6).
  8:23 "the boat was being swamped" → was being swamped, `t the boat`
  (συνεπληροῦντο has the sailors as subject).
- Objects: 8:4 εἶπεν "He told them" and διὰ παραβολῆς "this parable" (`t them`,
  `t this`); 8:16 τίθησιν "puts it"/"he sets it" (`t it` twice); 8:32
  ἐπέτρεψεν "He gave them permission" → He gave + permission, αὐτοῖς = them;
  8:34 βόσκοντες "tending the pigs", ἀπήγγειλαν "reported this"; 8:38
  ἀπέλυσεν "He sent him away" → αὐτόν = him; 8:42 ὑπάγειν "went with him";
  8:44 ὄπισθεν "behind Jesus"; 8:45 "denied it"; 8:49 λέγων "he told Jairus";
  8:50 ἀκούσας "overheard them"; 8:55 δοθῆναι "be given something".
- Supplied words: 8:1 σύν "were with" (`t were`); 8:13 οἵ "are those who"
  (`t are`); 8:25 Ποῦ "Where is" (`t is`); 8:10 ἐν "I speak in" (`t I
  speak`); 8:11 ἔστιν "is the meaning" (`t the meaning of`); 8:26 ἀντιπέρα
  "across the lake from" (`t the lake`); 8:23 "in great danger" (`t great`);
  8:29 "though he was bound" (`t though`); 8:49 τοῦ "the house of" (`t the
  house of`, as Mark 5:35); 8:43 ἀπ’ = "but" was wrong (`t but`).

Supplied "and" after a participle (now `t`): 8:8 ἐποίησεν, 8:12 σωθῶσιν,
8:16 καλύπτει, 8:24 διήγειραν and ἐπετίμησεν, 8:25 ἐθαύμασαν, 8:28
προσέπεσεν, 8:33 εἰσῆλθον, 8:37 ὑπέστρεψεν, 8:39 κηρύσσων, 8:44 ἥψατο, 8:50
ἀπεκρίθη, 8:54 ἐφώνησεν.

"When", "As" and "While" moved to the participle or genitive absolute: 8:4 δέ
"While" → Συνιόντος; 8:23 αὐτῶν "As they" → πλεόντων = As + sailed, αὐτῶν =
they; 8:27, 8:28, 8:34, 8:51 δέ "When" → the participle; 8:49 Ἔτι "was
still" / αὐτοῦ "While He" → still / He / λαλοῦντος = While + was + speaking
(as Mark 5:35).

Negatives split off verbs: 8:10 (twice), 8:12, 8:14, 8:17 (οὐ, οὐ μή), 8:18,
8:27, 8:47, 8:50 (Μή = not, φοβοῦ = Do + be afraid), 8:51, 8:52 (Μή = Stop,
οὐκ = not); 8:49 μηκέτι = not + anymore, σκύλλε = Do + bother; 8:56 μηδενί =
not + anyone (publisher: εἰπεῖν "not to tell"). 8:19 οὐκ ἠδύναντο = were
unable; 8:43 οὐκ … ἀπ’ οὐδενός = no one.

"of" off a lone article, "a" with its noun: 8:1 τήν, 8:10 τά and τῆς, 8:11
ἡ, 8:22 τῆς, 8:26 τῶν, 8:37 τῆς and τῶν ("of the" → the; of moved to the
genitive noun, as Luke 4:43); 8:5 τοῦ "of the"; 8:10 τοῖς "to" → τοῖς
λοιποῖς = to others; 8:54 τῆς "by the" → the, χειρός = by + hand; 8:28 τοῦ
"of" → `s`, Θεοῦ = of + God. 8:3 Ἡρῴδου "of Herod’s" → Herod’s, of on Χουζᾶ;
8:44 αὐτοῦ "of His" → His, of on ἱματίου. 8:15 καλῇ "a noble" → noble,
καρδίᾳ = a + heart; 8:32 ἱκανῶν "a large" → large, ἀγέλη = a + herd; 8:27
ἱκανῷ "For a long" → long, χρόνῳ = For a + time.

Other words on the wrong Greek word:

- 8:1: ἐν = "afterward", καθεξῆς = "Soon" → ἐν τῷ καθεξῆς = Soon afterward.
  κώμην "village to another" → village; κατά = from one + to another.
- 8:7: ἀπέπνιξαν "and choked" → choked; καί = and (see doubts);
  συνφυεῖσαι = grew up with, `t it`.
- 8:13, 8:15, 8:21: the shared object τὸν λόγον = word + it (as Matthew
  13:19, 13:23); δέχονται "and receive it" → receive (`t and`, ὅταν `s`, as
  Mark 4:16); κατέχουσιν "cling to it" → cling to; ποιοῦντες "carry it out" →
  carry + out. 8:15 οἵτινες "those" was wrong → οὗτοι = those, οἵτινες = who,
  ἀκούσαντες = hear.
- 8:18: πῶς "to how" → how; βλέπετε = Pay attention + to.
- 8:22: ἐνέβη "got into" → got, εἰς = into. The καί before ἀνήχθησαν now has
  "and" (before "set out"); the καί between boarding and speaking has "So"
  (publisher had them the other way round).
- 8:27: ὑπήντησεν "He was met by" → was met by; αὐτῷ = Jesus + He. ἀλλ’ "but
  he stayed" → but; ἔμενεν = lived + he stayed (the verb understood again).
  καί … οὐκ (20, 23) = or.
- 8:28: φωνῇ "shouting in a loud voice" → in a + voice; μεγάλῃ = loud; εἶπεν
  = shouting. Τί ἐμοὶ καὶ σοί = What + with me + You, `t do`, `t want`
  (identical to Luke 4:34 and Mark 5:7).
- 8:29: φυλασσόμενος = "with" was wrong → `s` (BSB leaves "being kept under
  guard" unexpressed); ἁλύσεσιν = with chains.
- 8:30, 8:10, 8:21: εἶπεν "he replied"/"He replied" → ὁ = he/He, the verb =
  replied. 8:20: ἀπηγγέλη "He was told" → was told, αὐτῷ = He (as Mark 3:32).
  8:48: εἶπεν "said Jesus" → said, ὁ = Jesus.
- 8:40: ἦσαν "they had all been" → ἦσαν … προσδοκῶντες = they had + been
  waiting for; πάντες = all.
- 8:42: ὡς "who was about" → about; ἦν = was, αὐτῷ = his, `t who`.
- 8:46: ἔγνων "know that" → know; ἐξεληλυθυῖαν = that + has gone out (as
  Mark 5:30).
- 8:47: ἔλαθεν and ἰάθη split from their negative and adverb.

## Notable decisions

- Readings. Only 8:3, 8:7, 8:14, 8:29, 8:41, 8:43, 8:51 and 8:52 show
  publisher Greek different from N1904.
  - Names linked under rule 8: Joanna 8:3, Jairus 8:41, John 8:51.
  - 8:7 συνφυεῖσαι and 8:14 συνπνίγονται: N1904's unassimilated spellings,
    same verbs (as Mark 4:19).
  - 8:29: διαρήσσων linked as the same verb, probably spelled διαρρήσσων in
    BSB's Greek. "by" is linked to N1904's ἀπό, which also marks the agent
    here. BSB's Greek probably reads ὑπό.
  - 8:43: "had spent all her money on physicians" is `t` because BSB follows
    the longer reading, probably ἰατροῖς προσαναλώσασα ὅλον τὸν βίον (five
    words, matching the EVIDENCE count). N1904 lacks it. ἥτις stays = She.
  - 8:52: BSB's Greek probably reads οὐ γάρ for οὐκ. The extra γάρ is not
    rendered in English, so nothing is claimed.
- Parallels kept consistent:
  - Sower (Mark 4, Matthew 13): A farmer = ὁ σπείρων; which = αἱ ἄκανθαι;
    the seedlings = αὐτό; The seeds = the article (Mark 4:15).
  - The parable's interpretation: 8:18 αὐτῷ `s` and `t more` (Mark 4:25);
    8:17 negatives as Mark 4:22.
  - Storm: windstorm = λαῖλαψ ἀνέμου (Mark 4:37); one another = πρὸς
    ἀλλήλους and ἄρα `s` (Mark 4:41).
  - Demoniac: enter = εἰσελθεῖν + εἰς (Mark 5:12); He gave + permission
    (Mark 5:13); shared in/the and `t this` (Mark 5:14); home (Mark 5:19);
    those tending + `t the pigs` (Matthew 8:33).
  - Jairus and the woman: bleeding (Mark 5:25, 5:29); not / Do + be afraid
    (Mark 5:36); Child as vocative (Mark 5:41); `t something` (Mark 5:43);
    the / by + hand (Matthew 9:25).
- Prepositions: ἀπό after ἐξελθεῖν/ἐξελθόντα gives "of" in "come/came out
  of", with "out" on the verb (8:29, 8:33). ἀφ’ … ἐξῆλθεν/ἐξεληλύθει = left
  (8:35, 8:38), as ἀπῆλθεν ἀπ’ = left in Luke 5:13.
- ὁ δέ: the article is "He/he" where English has a pronoun (8:10, 8:21, 8:30)
  and "Jesus" where BSB names Him (8:24, 8:48, 8:52, 8:56), as Luke 4:43.
- 8:5 and 8:8: the καί before the trampling and sprouting clauses is linked to
  BSB's "where" (with a note). The "and" between φυέν and ἐποίησεν in 8:8 is
  `t`.

## Doubts for the peer

- 8:7 and 8:29: καί is linked to the English "and" that BSB places after the
  participle: "grew up with it and choked", "had broken the chains and been
  driven". Otherwise the καί would be `s` and that "and" `t`, a split pair.
  The alternative is to follow the participle-"and" `t` convention strictly.
- 8:22: BSB reorders boarding and speaking. αὐτούς = His disciples and οἱ
  μαθηταὶ αὐτοῦ = them are kept from the publisher. The two καί were
  re-assigned (So / and), which is open to judgment.
- 8:42: ἦν = was (in "who was about twelve") with `t who`, and αὐτῷ = his.
  The alternative is ἦν αὐτῷ = his with `t who was`.
- 8:19: πρός = "to see" is kept from the publisher with a note. "see" has no
  Greek word. The alternative is `t to see` with πρός `s`.
- 8:41: ἀνήρ = "a" is kept (merged into "a synagogue leader"), and καὶ
  οὗτος … ὑπῆρχεν is `s`. 8:12 εἶτα = "but" is also kept, with a note.
- 8:27: one dative αὐτῷ = Jesus + He (the one stepping ashore and the one
  met).
