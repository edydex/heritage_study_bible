## Publisher rows corrected

English-only words folded into Greek words (now `t`):

- 13:1 δέ "It was now" → now, `t It was`; Ἰησοῦς "and Jesus" → Jesus, `t and` (BSB splits the participle clause into a second main clause); τούς "who were" → who, `t were` (no copula with τοὺς ἐν).
- 13:3 εἰδώς "Jesus knew", 13:12 Ὅτε "When Jesus", 13:28 εἶπεν "Jesus had said", 13:31 ἐξῆλθεν "Judas had gone out": `t Jesus` / `t Judas`.
- 13:4 ἐγείρεται "So He got up" → He got up, `t So` (BSB makes εἰδώς of 13:3 a main verb).
- 13:5 ἐκμάσσειν "dry them" and 13:26 δίδωσιν "gave it" → `t them` / `t it`.
- 13:9 Κύριε "Then, Lord" → Lord, `t Then`.
- 13:10 λελουμένος "has already bathed" → Ὁ λελουμένος = Whoever has + bathed, `t already` (as John 9:37).
- 13:18 ἵνα "this is to" → to, `t this is` (elided main clause after ἀλλ’ ἵνα, as John 9:3).
- 13:19 εἰμι "am He" → am, `t He`.
- 13:23 ὅν "the one whom" → whom, `t the one`.
- 13:26 ἐστιν "It is" → is, `t It` (cleft; the Greek subject is ἐκεῖνος).
- 13:27 καί "And when Judas had taken" → And; μετά = when; `t Judas had taken` (BSB expands "after the morsel" into a clause).
- 13:34 ἵνα "so" → `s`; `t so` ("so" answers "As"; it does not render ἵνα).

Negatives and auxiliaries split off verbs:

- 13:7 οὐκ "do not" → not, οἶδας = do + realize. 13:18 λέγω "I am not speaking" → οὐ = not, λέγω = I am + speaking.
- 13:6 νίπτεις "are You going to wash" → σύ = You, νίπτεις = are + going to wash.
- 13:2 ἤδη "had already" → already, βεβληκότος = had + put. 13:32 δοξάσει "will also glorify" → καί = also, δοξάσει = will + glorify.

Articles, prepositions and connectives on the wrong word:

- 13:2 τοῦ "and the" → the; καί (verse-opening) `s`, `t and` (the English "and" joins the two genitive absolutes, which have no connective). Ἰούδας "of Judas" → Judas, `t of` (in Greek Judas is the subject of παραδοῖ, not the owner of the heart).
- 13:5 τῷ "with the" → the, λεντίῳ = with + towel; τούς "the" → `s`, "the" moved to τῶν (the disciples’).
- 13:29 τοῖς "to the" → the, πτωχοῖς = to + poor. 13:33 τοῖς "to the" → the, Ἰουδαίοις = to + Jews.
- 13:11 καθαροί "are clean" / ἐστε "of you" → καθαροί = clean, ἐστε = are, πάντες = all of you (as 13:10).
- 13:16 οὐδέ "nor is" → nor; ἔστιν = is + is (understood again).
- 13:12 ἀνέπεσεν "He reclined with them" → He reclined; αὐτοῖς = with them (noted). εἶπεν "and asked" → asked; the καί before ἀνέπεσεν = and (moved by BSB).
- 13:25 οὕτως "back against" → `s`; ἀναπεσών = Leaning back, ἐπί = against; ἐκεῖνος = he (from "he asked"); τὸ στῆθος `s`.
- 13:26 αὐτῷ "He" → `s` (resumptive after ᾧ); βάψας = He dipped. βάψω "after I have dipped it" → I have dipped; the καί between βάψω and δώσω = after (BSB reverses the two verbs; same logic as the When-rule); `t it`.
- 13:30 λαβών "As soon as he had received" → he had received, εὐθύς = As soon as (convention). ἦν "into the" → `s` (with δέ); `t into the`.
- 13:34 καινήν "A new" → new; "A" moved to ἐντολήν (A + commandment).
- 13:4 ἑαυτόν "around His waist" → His waist; διέζωσεν = wrapped + around (the δια- of "gird around").

Joined units:

- Article + participle (rule 3): 13:11 τὸν παραδιδόντα, 13:16 τοῦ πέμψαντος, 13:18 Ὁ τρώγων, 13:20 ὁ λαμβάνων (×2) and τὸν πέμψαντα, 13:10 Ὁ λελουμένος.
- 13:1 εἰς τέλος = to the very end; μεταβῇ ἐκ … = leave + return (see decisions).
- 13:13 καλῶς λέγετε = rightly so (λέγετε alone had "so").
- 13:29 χρείαν ἔχομεν = was needed (ἔχομεν alone had "was"). 13:33 ἔτι μικρόν = only a little while longer (ἔτι alone had "only").
- 13:3 αὐτῷ + τάς = His (the dative "to him" is the real possessor of "His hands").
- 13:20 ἄν τινα = the one. 13:23 τοῦ Ἰησοῦ = His.

## Readings

- Only the EVIDENCE lines of 13:2, 13:10, 13:19, 13:21, 13:24, 13:25, 13:27, 13:29, 13:36 and 13:37 show publisher Greek different from N1904.
- 13:2: Iscariot linked to Ἰσκαριώτης with "Same name; BSB's Greek text has it in a different form here" (probably the genitive Ἰσκαριώτου, which makes Iscariot describe Simon, as BSB's English does).
- 13:19: you will believe linked to πιστεύητε with a same-meaning note (probably aorist πιστεύσητε).
- 13:24: the only real wording difference. N1904 καὶ λέγει αὐτῷ Εἰπέ … ἐστιν are `s` with the reading reason; "to ask" is `t` with the reading reason (probably πυθέσθαι … ἂν εἴη, "to inquire who it might be"); "Jesus" `t` as the supplied object.
- 13:10, 13:21, 13:27, 13:29, 13:37 (probably an extra ὁ before the name), 13:25 (probably an extra οὖν) and 13:36 (probably αὐτῷ after ἀπεκρίθη): the extra publisher word has no English counterpart, so no reading is claimed.

## Notable decisions

- 13:1: μεταβῇ ἐκ … πρός linked to both "leave" and "return" (one verb of passing from the world to the Father, split by BSB into two English verbs); "and" between them `t`.
- 13:8: οὐ μή … εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα = Never (John 8:51-52 convention).
- 13:10: οὐκ … εἰ μή = only and ἔχει χρείαν = needs; ἀλλά = and (noted); ἔστιν = he will be (present rendered future, noted).
- 13:12: αὐτοῖς ("to them", addressees of εἶπεν) linked to "with them", which BSB attaches to reclined; the same pronoun with its relation shifted.
- 13:14: ὁ before Κύριος = your (article as possessive; majority convention), the second ὁ `s`.
- 13:24: οὗ `s`: "which one" is both τίς and the object of "about".
- 13:27: αὐτῷ = to Judas (pronoun rendered by name); ποιεῖς = you are about to do (noted).
- 13:30: ἐκεῖνος = Judas (noted); ἦν δὲ νύξ recast as "into the night": νύξ = night, ἦν and δέ `s`, "into the" `t`.
- 13:32: αὐτόν = the Son (noted).
- 13:34: first ἵνα `s` (content given as the imperative "Love"); second ἵνα `s`, "must" stays with ἀγαπᾶτε (as "should" with ποιῆτε in 13:15).
- 13:38: οὐ μή … ἕως οὗ ("will not … until") joined = before, since BSB reverses the clauses and drops the negative.

## Doubts for the peer

- 13:1: "return" linked to μεταβῇ. Alternative: `t and return` with μεταβῇ ἐκ = leave only.
- 13:12: αὐτοῖς = "with them". Alternative: αὐτοῖς `s` (addressee unexpressed) and `t with them`.
- 13:38: οὐ μή joined into "before". Alternative: ἕως οὗ = before and οὐ μή `s` ("BSB reverses the clauses and drops the negative").
- 13:26: καί = "after". Alternative: καί `s` and `t after`.
- 13:5: "the" moved from τούς to τῶν (the disciples’ feet); either article could carry it.
- 13:2: "The evening meal" keeps "The" on the anarthrous δείπνου (publisher row kept); John 9 made such a "the" `t`.
- 13:30: ἦν δὲ νύξ might instead be one group = "into the night".
- 13:13: καλῶς λέγετε joined = rightly so; the publisher had λέγετε = so.
