## Readings

EVIDENCE says the publisher's Greek differs in 9:1, 9:4, 9:13, 9:15 and 9:19
only. No cross-verse links.

- 9:1: probably συμμαρτυρούσης (spelling); linked "as confirmed by" with a
  same-word note.
- 9:4: probably Ἰσραηλῖται (spelling); linked with a same-word note.
- 9:13: probably καθώς for καθάπερ; "So" linked with the 3:4-style note.
- 9:15: probably οἰκτιρήσω / οἰκτίρω (spelling, the two unmatched words);
  linked with same-word notes.
- 9:19: the publisher has one extra word, probably a second οὖν inside the
  question (Τί οὖν ἔτι), where BSB puts its single "Then". I linked the N1904
  οὖν to that "Then" with a note rather than leaving a split pair; see
  doubts.

## Notable decisions

- 9:5 is linked word by word with no interpretive note on the doxology: ὁ ὤν
  "who is", ἐπὶ πάντων "over all", Θεός "God", εὐλογητός "worthy of praise"
  (the 1:25 gloss note only), εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας "forever", ἀμήν "Amen".
- Theological terms linked word for word: υἱοθεσία "adoption as sons",
  διαθῆκαι "covenants", ἐπαγγελία "promise(s)", ἐκλογή "election", ἐλεέω /
  ἔλεος "have mercy" / "mercy", οἰκτείρω "have compassion", σκληρύνω
  "harden", καλέω "call / be called / be reckoned" (9:7 with a gloss note).
- Vocative: 9:20 ὦ = "O", ἄνθρωπε = "man"; "you" goes to the explicit σύ.
- Connectives BSB renders differently are linked with notes: 9:11 γάρ "Yet",
  9:20 μενοῦνγε "But", 9:24 καί "including", 9:27 ἐάν "Though"; unexpressed
  δέ (9:6, 9:10, 9:22, 9:27, 9:30), καί (9:10, 9:23, 9:25, 9:29 twice) and ἤ
  (9:21), μέν (9:21) are `s`.
- 9:26 ἐκεῖ `s` and "very" `t`: BSB moves the emphasis into "the very
  place", but "very" does not mean "there".
- 9:26 "It will happen that" kept on ἔσται and ζῶντος "of the living" kept
  (participle, not a lone article): rule 10.
- 9:31 εἰς `s` ("attained it" needs no preposition); νόμον = "it".

## Doubts for the peer

- 9:8 ταῦτα = "who" (resumptive "these" rendered by the cleft relative);
  the alternative is ταῦτα `s` + "who" `t`.
- 9:19 οὖν = "Then": BSB's "Then" stands where the publisher's extra word
  probably is; the alternative is οὖν `s` + "Then" `t` with a reading
  reason.
- 9:3 "and cut off" placed on ἀνάθεμα (with ἀπό = "from"); it could also be
  read as the pregnant sense of ἀπό.
- 9:16 "it does … depend" `t` with "on" in the participle groups; one could
  link "depend on" to the genitive.
- 9:32 "over" kept with the verb (Romans 4 convention) rather than on λίθῳ.
