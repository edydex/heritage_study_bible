# BSB Romans 16: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: all 26 drafted verses (16:1-23, 16:25-27). 16:24 is absent
from both N1904 and BSB's main text and is not in the draft; it was not
added. No KEEP blocks in this chapter. Final check: `fromtext` →
`{"versesOk": 26, "problems": 0}`.

64 of the 362 publisher `g` rows changed, in 20 verses. Every publisher row
was checked, including the six verses whose rows stay unchanged apart from
resolved TODOs (16:3, 16:6, 16:8, 16:9, 16:14, 16:15). No "of the"/"to
the"/"in the"/"for" sits on a lone article or a number. Articles with no
English "the" are `s` with the standard reason (README rule 5).

Conventions followed: Romans 1:5 and 1:7 (εἰς ὑπακοὴν πίστεως = "to the
obedience … from faith" with "that comes" `t`; τοῖς οὖσιν = "who are"),
Romans 4:14 (article as pronoun = "those who"), Acts 16:6 and 20:16
("province of" `t`), Acts 19:40 (καὶ γάρ = "For"), Philippians 4:21-23 and
Colossians 4:10-15 ("send + greetings" on ἀσπάζομαι with ὑμᾶς = "you"; "as"
on καί and "do/does" resuming the verb; "a" with the noun), 1 Thessalonians
5:28 and 2 Thessalonians 3:18 ("of + Lord" on Κυρίου, "our" on ἡμῶν, "be"
`t`), Galatians 1:5 and Philippians 4:20 (εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας (τῶν αἰώνων) as
one unit), and Romans 2:7 / 4:12 (a verb repeated from an earlier verse is
`t`, not a cross-verse link).

## Publisher rows corrected

Lone articles carrying a preposition, verb or other word:

- 16:1 "of the" on τῆς → "the"; "of + church" on ἐκκλησίας.
- 16:4 "of the" on τῶν → "the"; "of + Gentiles".
- 16:10 "who is" on τόν → "who"; "is" `t` (compiler also rejects it).
- 16:18 "of the" on τῶν → "the"; "of + naive". "own" on τῇ → τῇ `s`;
  ἑαυτῶν = "their own".
- 16:19 "what is" on τό (twice) → "what"; both "is" `t`.
- 16:25 "by the" on τό → "the"; the second "by" joins κατά ("by + by"),
  which governs both nouns.
- 16:26 "of the" on τοῦ → "the"; "of + God" on Θεοῦ.
- 16:27 "be" on ἡ → ἡ `s`; "be" `t`.

Supplied words folded into Greek words (now `t`):

- 16:4 "are grateful to them" on εὐχαριστῶ → "are grateful"; "to them" =
  οἷς (relative "to whom", a TODO).
- 16:5 "Greet also" on καί → "also"; "Greet" `t` (repeats verse 3's
  ἀσπάσασθε; the Greek sentence continues). "that meets at" on κατ’ → "at";
  "that meets" `t`. "in the province of Asia" on Ἀσίας → "in + Asia";
  "the" = τῆς; "province of" `t`.
- 16:13 "who has been a mother to me" on ἐμοῦ → "to me"; "a mother" joins
  μητέρα (stated once, repeated by BSB); "who has been" `t`.
- 16:17 "obstacles that are" → obstacles; "that are" `t`.
- 16:20 "be with" on μεθ’ → "with"; "be" `t`.
- 16:26 "that comes from faith" on πίστεως → "from faith", "that comes"
  `t`; "to the" on εἰς → "to", "the obedience" on ὑπακοήν. "in order to
  lead" on the second εἰς → `t` (supplied purpose verb, as Romans 1:5 "to
  call"); that εἰς is `s` (see doubts).

Person, modal, verb and connective words on the wrong owner:

- 16:7 "I was" on ἐμοῦ → "I"; "was" repeats γέγοναν ("were + was").
- 16:16, 16:21, 16:23 (twice) "send(s) you greetings" on the verb → "send(s)
  + greetings"; "you" = ὑμᾶς (TODOs).
- 16:21 "as do" on καί → "as"; "do" on Ἀσπάζεται. 16:23 "as does" likewise.
- 16:18 "are not" on οὐ → "not"; "are + serving" on δουλεύουσιν.
- 16:19 "about your" on ὑμῶν → "your"; "about" joins ἀφίκετο ("has heard +
  about"); εἰς πάντας = "Everyone" (εἰς was a TODO).
- 16:20 "will soon crush" → "will + crush"; ἐν τάχει = "soon" (both TODOs).
  "of our" on ἡμῶν → "our"; "of + Lord" on Κυρίου.
- 16:26 τε had "through" → διά = "through" (διά was a TODO); τε = "and",
  which links the two participles; γνωρισθέντος had "and made known" →
  "made known".

"a"/"the" with the noun: 16:2 "of the saints" on ἁγίων → "the" = τῶν,
"of + saints"; 16:16 "a holy" → "holy", "a + kiss".

Units joined: 16:11 τοὺς ὄντας = "who are"; 16:12 τὰς κοπιώσας = "women
who have worked hard"; 16:17 τοὺς … ποιοῦντας = "those who create"; 16:22
ὁ γράψας = "who wrote down"; 16:25 Τῷ δυναμένῳ = "to Him who is able";
16:2 ἐν ᾧ ἂν … πράγματι: ᾧ ἂν πράγματι = "anything"; 16:2 καὶ γάρ =
"For"; 16:4 ψυχῆς μου = "me" (ὑπὲρ τῆς ψυχῆς μου, "for my life" → "for
me"); 16:25 χρόνοις αἰωνίοις = "for ages past"; 16:27 εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας τῶν
αἰώνων = "forever".

## Notable decisions (publisher rows kept)

- Names: every name in the greeting list stays linked to its Greek name
  (Phoebe, Cenchrea, Prisca, Aquila, Epenetus, Mary, Andronicus, Junia,
  Ampliatus, Urbanus, Stachys, Apelles, Aristobulus, Herodion, Narcissus,
  Tryphena, Tryphosa, Persis, Rufus, Asyncritus, Phlegon, Hermes, Patrobas,
  Hermas, Philologus, Julia, Nereus, Olympas, Timothy, Lucius, Jason,
  Sosipater, Tertius, Gaius, Erastus, Quartus). ἀσπάσασθε = "Greet" each time.
- 16:10, 16:11 τοὺς ἐκ τῶν + genitive name: τούς = "those (who)", ἐκ =
  "belong to"/"from", τῶν = "the household" (article used as a pronoun for
  the household), "of" on the declinable genitive name.
- Articles used as possessives or demonstratives stay linked (majority
  convention): 16:12 τήν = "my", 16:23 ὁ = "our", 16:22 τήν = "this".
- 16:4 τράχηλον ("neck") = "lives" kept, with a note on the idiom.
- 16:7 καί before πρὸ ἐμοῦ = "and" kept (BSB's "and they" for οἳ καί).
- 16:23 ὁ ξένος μου: ὁ = "who", ξένος = "has hosted" (noun rendered as a
  verb, noted).
- 16:2 ἵνα before προσδέξησθε `s` (BSB makes it the command "Welcome");
  αὐτοῦ in ἐμοῦ αὐτοῦ `s`. 16:7 the second μου `s` (BSB says "my" once).
- Doxology 16:25-27 linked word by word; notes give only literal glosses.
  16:27 ᾧ ("to whom") `s`: BSB ascribes glory directly to "the only wise
  God".

## Readings

- 16:1: EVIDENCE shows one extra publisher word, probably καί before
  διάκονον. BSB's English has no "also", so nothing is claimed.
- 16:20: one extra publisher word, probably Χριστοῦ. "Christ" is `t` with
  the reading reason.

## Doubts for the peer

- 16:26 εἰς πάντα τὰ ἔθνη: the publisher linked "in order to lead" to this
  εἰς. I made "in order to lead" `t` (supplied purpose verb, Romans 1:5
  precedent) and the εἰς `s`. Linking "in order to" to it would also be
  defensible.
- 16:19 ἡ … ὑπακοὴ εἰς πάντας ἀφίκετο ("your obedience reached all") is
  recast "Everyone has heard about": εἰς πάντας = "Everyone", ἀφίκετο =
  "has heard + about". "about" could be `t` instead.
- 16:4 ψυχῆς joined with μου to "me" rather than left `s`; "lives" stays
  on τράχηλον per the publisher.
- 16:5 "Greet" `t` rather than a cross-verse link to 16:3's ἀσπάσασθε
  (Colossians 4:11 used a link; newer Romans/Acts reviews use `t`).
