## Publisher rows corrected

- Supplied "Jesus" folded into a verb, now `t`: 7:14 (called), 7:17 (had
  left), 7:24 (left), 7:29 (told), 7:31 (left), 7:33 (took him). 7:6
  εἶπεν = "Jesus answered" → answered, with ὁ (of ὁ δέ) = Jesus. 7:37
  ἐξεπλήσσοντο = "The people were utterly astonished" → were + astonished,
  ὑπερπερισσῶς = utterly, `t The people`.
- Participle "and", now `t` with the verb keeping its own word: "and said"
  (7:14, 7:37), "and went" (7:24, 7:31), "and she came" / "and fell" (7:25),
  "and found" (7:30), "and put" / "and touched" (7:33). 7:31 ἀνά = "and
  into" → ἀνὰ μέσον = into, `t and` (no καί there).
- Negatives folded into verbs: 7:3 οὐκ = "do not" and 7:4 ἐσθίουσιν = "they
  do not eat" → not + (they) do + eat; 7:18 νοεῖτε = "Do you not understand";
  7:19 εἰσπορεύεται = "it does not enter". 7:24 ἤθελεν = "Not wanting
  anyone" → wanting, οὐδένα = Not + anyone; οὐκ ἠδυνάσθη joined = was unable.
- English-only words claimed by Greek, now `t`: 7:4 "on returning" (ἀπ’ =
  from) and "including" (βαπτισμούς = the washing); 7:10 "his" (πατέρα);
  7:11 "that" (λέγετε), "is" (Κορβᾶν) and "devoted to God" (Δῶρον = a gift);
  7:15 "these" (ἐστιν = are); 7:20 "is what" (ἐκεῖνο = that); 7:23 "are
  what" (κοινοῖ = these + defile); 7:24 "He was there" (γνῶναι = to know);
  7:27 "it" (βαλεῖν = toss); 7:22 "and" (ἀφροσύνη = foolishness).
- Words on the wrong Greek word: 7:5 σου = "do Your" → Your, περιπατοῦσιν =
  do + walk; κοιναῖς = "with defiled" → defiled, χερσίν = with + hands.
  7:9 ἀθετεῖτε = "You neatly set aside" → You + set aside, Καλῶς = neatly;
  the article τήν = "own" → `s`, ὑμῶν = your own. 7:3 τήν = "to the" → the,
  κρατοῦντες = in holding to. 7:12 αὐτόν = "he is" → he, ἀφίετε = is +
  permitted. 7:13 ὑμῶν = "the" was wrong → `s` (BSB leaves "your"
  unexpressed), τῇ = by the. 7:14 ὄχλον = "crowd to Him" → crowd, the middle
  προσκαλεσάμενος = called + to Him. 7:1 συνάγονται = "gathered around" →
  gathered, πρός = around.
- 7:17: εἰσῆλθεν = "Jesus had left" and εἰς = "and gone into" were wrong →
  εἰσῆλθεν = had + gone, εἰς = into, ἀπό = left (BSB's verb for "away from
  the crowd"), `t Jesus`, `t and`.
- 7:19: εἰς = "it goes into" → into, with "it goes" added to εἰσπορεύεται
  (one verb governs both clauses); πάντα = "Thus all" → all, καθαρίζων =
  Thus + are clean.
- 7:26: ἦν = "she was", γυνή = "woman", Ἑλληνίς = "a Greek" → ἡ γυνή = she,
  ἦν = was, Ἑλληνίς = a Greek woman (Ἑλληνίς is the noun "Greek woman").
- 7:28: ἀπεκρίθη = "she replied" → ἡ = she, ἀπεκρίθη καὶ λέγει = replied.
- 7:33: κατ’ = "aside" → κατ’ ἰδίαν = privately, ἀπολαβόμενος = took +
  aside, αὐτόν = him.

## Notable decisions

- Textual differences, only where EVIDENCE allows:
  - 7:4: N1904 ῥαντίσωνται (sprinkle themselves) is `s` and "they wash" is
    `t` (BSB follows βαπτίσωνται); "and couches for dining" is `t` (καὶ
    κλινῶν). This follows the Matthew 19:29 treatment of a different word in
    the same slot.
  - 7:21/7:22: verse division only. μοιχεῖαι opens N1904 7:22 but BSB has
    "adultery" at the end of 7:21: linked with `g @7:22 0` in 7:21 and
    `m 0 > 7:21` in 7:22.
  - 7:24: οὐκ ἠδυνάσθη linked to "was unable"; the note says only that
    ἠδυνάσθη is an alternative aorist form of δύναμαι.
  - 7:37: `t the` before "mute" (BSB follows τοὺς before ἀλάλους).
- Joint units: ἐὰν μή = until (7:3) / unless (7:4); ἃ παρέλαβον =
  traditions for them; Διὰ τί = Why; θανάτῳ τελευτάτω = must be put to
  death; ὃ ἐάν = Whatever; πᾶν … οὐ = Nothing (7:18); εἰς τὸν ἀφεδρῶνα
  ἐκπορεύεται = is eliminated; ὀφθαλμὸς πονηρός = envy; ἧς … αὐτῆς = whose
  (resumptive pronoun); ἵνα + subjunctive = to drive / to place; ἀπεκρίθη
  καὶ λέγει = replied; εἰς τὸν οἶκον αὐτῆς = home (article `s`, as in Mark
  2:11); κατ’ ἰδίαν = privately; verbs of motion + εἰς/ἐκ (enters, He
  entered, has left, left).
- Pronoun ↔ name, linked with a note: αὐτόν/αὐτοῦ = Jesus (7:1, 7:5, 7:25,
  7:26, 7:32); αὐτοῦ = the man’s (7:33, 7:35); τὸν ἄνθρωπον = him (7:15,
  7:20).
- Articles kept as possessives (later-chapter convention): τάς = their
  (7:3), τοῖς = with their (7:6), τῷ = to his / for his (7:11, 7:12), τό =
  her (7:30), τήν = His (7:32).
- `s` with reasons: ὅτι before a quotation (7:6, 7:20) or recast (7:2,
  7:18); unrendered objects τοὺς ἄρτους / τὸν ἄρτον (7:2, 7:5), αὐτόν
  (7:15, 7:17); addressees αὐτοῖς/αὐτῇ/αὐτῷ (7:9, 7:14, 7:18, 7:27, 7:28);
  πάλιν (7:31); partitive ἀπό (7:28); ὁ δεσμός (7:35, BSB says "his tongue
  was released"); the relatives ᾗ (7:13) and ὅ (7:15).
- 7:13 παρόμοια = such matters, τοιαῦτα = so, πολλά = in many kept from the
  publisher, with one note.

## Doubts for the peer

- 7:4: `s` ῥαντίσωνται + `t they wash` looks like a split pair; linking
  "wash" to ῥαντίσωνται with a note is the alternative, but it would show a
  church member "sprinkle" under "wash".
- 7:17: ἀπό = "left" (a preposition rendered by a verb), with "had" given to
  εἰσῆλθεν (had + gone) and `t and`.
- 7:15: τοῦ ἀνθρώπου = "a man" (with ἔξωθεν = from the outside) and εἰς
  αὐτόν split (εἰς joined to enters, αὐτόν `s`); "a man" as the rendering of
  εἰς αὐτόν is the alternative.
- 7:18: καί = "still" kept from the publisher (καὶ ὑμεῖς, "you too").
- 7:19: "it goes" added to εἰσπορεύεται rather than `t`.
- 7:23: "these" left on κοινοῖ (subject in the verb ending), `t are what`.
- 7:3: γάρ = "Now" and πυγμῇ = "ceremonially" kept, each with a note.
- 7:13: whether "in" of "in many" should be `t`.
