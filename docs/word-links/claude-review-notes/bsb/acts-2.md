# BSB Acts 2: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: 46 (2:1-41 and 2:43-47). 2:42 is a KEEP block (reviewed
earlier by Codex) and is copied unchanged. Final check: `fromtext` →
`{"versesOk": 47, "problems": 0}`.

124 of the 645 publisher `g` rows changed, in 38 verses. 47 of them keep
their English: 23 only absorbed a TODO token and 24 were joined into one
larger group (for example 2:1 ἐν τῷ
= When, ἐπὶ τὸ αὐτό = in one place; 2:3 ἕνα ἕκαστον = each; 2:4 ἐδίδου …
ἀποφθέγγεσθαι = enabled; 2:12 ἄλλος πρὸς ἄλλον = one another; 2:25 διὰ
παντός = always; 2:26 διὰ τοῦτο = Therefore; 2:30 καρποῦ τῆς ὀσφύος =
descendants; 2:33 τοῦτο ὅ = what; 2:35 ἕως ἄν = until; 2:43 πάσῃ ψυχῇ =
everyone; 2:44 and 2:47 ἐπὶ τὸ αὐτό; 2:46 and 2:47 καθ’ ἡμέραν = daily; every
article + participle unit: 2:7, 2:9, 2:10, 2:14, 2:16, 2:41, 2:44, 2:47).
The other 77 were corrected, split or moved, as listed below.

Precedent followed: Luke 20:42-43 for the Psalm 110 quotation (2:34 τῷ Κυρίῳ
= to + Lord with the article `s`; 2:35 is word for word the Luke 20:43
block); Luke 9:51 (ἐν τῷ + infinitive = When); Luke 17 / Matthew 22 (ἐπὶ τὸ
αὐτό joined); Matthew 23:21 (the article + participle of κατοικέω carries
"dwell in"); Luke 24:36 (ἐν μέσῳ = among); Luke 24:53 (διὰ παντός); the
Gospels' τοῦ Ναζωραίου = of Nazareth (article + adjective joined); Luke 20:43
"for + feet" (case word on the noun, not the article); the pleonastic
ἀποκριθεὶς … εἶπεν = replied joins, applied to the address formula Ἄνδρες
ἀδελφοί.

## Publisher rows corrected

Negatives split off verbs (the verb keeps do/will/did):

- 2:15 "are not drunk" → οὐ = not, μεθύουσιν = are + drunk.
- 2:25 σαλευθῶ carried "I will not be shaken" while μή was TODO → μή = not,
  σαλευθῶ = I will + be shaken; ἵνα `s` (BSB states the result plainly).
- 2:27 "You will not abandon" (οὐκ TODO) → οὐκ = not, verb = You will +
  abandon.
- 2:31 "He was not abandoned" (οὔτε TODO) → οὔτε = not, verb = He was +
  abandoned; "did His" on αὐτοῦ → αὐτοῦ = His, εἶδεν = did + see.
- 2:34 "did not" on οὐ → οὐ = not, ἀνέβη = did + ascend.
- 2:24 οὐκ … δυνατόν = impossible (one English word for both; ἦν = it was).

When/As on the participle, not on δέ:

- 2:6 δέ carried "And when" → γενομένης = when + rang out (genitive
  absolute), δέ = And.
- 2:37 δέ carried "When" and Ἀκούσαντες carried "the people heard this" →
  Ἀκούσαντες = When + heard, δέ `s`, "the people" and "this" `t`.

Articles that carried prepositions (now: article = "the" or `s`; the case word
moves to the noun or verb):

- 2:15 τῆς "of the" → the + of … day; 2:21 τό "on the" → calls on + the;
  2:23 τῇ "by" → by + plan; 2:24 τάς "from the" → releasing … from + the,
  τοῦ "of" → of death; 2:31 τοῦ "of the" → the + of … Christ; 2:33 τῇ "to
  the" → the + to … right hand; 2:34 τῷ "to" → to + Lord (Luke 20:42); 2:35
  τῶν "for" → for + feet (Luke 20:43); 2:37 τήν "to the" → the + to …
  heart; 2:38 τοῦ "of the" → the + of … Spirit, and εἰς "for the" → for,
  with "the" on ἄφεσιν; 2:9 τήν "of" → residents of (participle, Matthew
  23:21); 2:22 τόν "of" → τὸν Ναζωραῖον = of Nazareth (joined).
- 2:20 τήν carried the "the" of "the coming" and μεγάλην carried "of the
  great" → ἐλθεῖν = the coming of, τήν = the (before great), μεγάλην =
  great.
- 2:39 τοῖς carried "who are" → τοῖς = who, "are" `t`; εἰς μακράν = far off.

English-only words that had been folded into a Greek word (now `t`):

- Objects: "them" (2:8 hears, 2:13 mocked), "Him" twice (2:23), "Him"
  (2:24 releasing), "this" (2:31 Foreseeing, 2:37 heard).
- Names/subjects: "David" (2:31 ἐλάλησεν), "the people" (2:37).
- Copulas: "was" (2:22 was a man), "are" (2:39 who are far off).
- Others: "only" (2:15), "and" joining the participle to the main verb
  (2:13), "and" + "you" were on διά in 2:23 (now διά = by; "you" goes to
  ἀνείλατε; "and" `t`), "But" on the article ὁ (2:24; the Greek continues
  with ὅν), "from the dead" (2:24, the Greek has only ἀνέστησεν), "to the
  believers" (2:41), "their" (2:46 meals), "now" (2:33, was on καί "both",
  which is now `s`).

Wrong word or over-claim:

- 2:4 πάντες carried "they were all" → ἐπλήσθησαν = they were + filled with,
  πάντες = all.
- 2:8 ἐν carried the "in" of "in his own native language"; ἐν belongs to ἐν ᾗ
  ἐγεννήθημεν → ἐν ᾗ ἐγεννήθημεν = native, "in" goes with διαλέκτῳ (in +
  language); καί = then (was TODO), πῶς = How is it + that.
- 2:12 θέλει = "does", εἶναι = "mean" → τί θέλει … εἶναι idiom: θέλει εἶναι
  = does + mean.
- 2:14 ἔστω carried "let this be" while τοῦτο was TODO → τοῦτο = this, ἔστω =
  let + be; κατοικοῦντες "dwell" and Ἱερουσαλήμ "in Jerusalem" → οἱ
  κατοικοῦντες = who dwell in, Ἱερουσαλήμ = Jerusalem.
- 2:22 μέσῳ carried "among you" → ἐν μέσῳ = among, ὑμῶν = you.
- 2:29 παρρησίας carried "with confidence" while μετά was TODO → μετά = with.
- 2:30 ὀσφύος carried "his descendants" while αὐτοῦ was TODO → αὐτοῦ = his.
- 2:32 Ἰησοῦν carried "Jesus to life" → Ἰησοῦν = Jesus, ἀνέστησεν = has

- 2:36 πᾶς carried "let all" → γινωσκέτω = let + know, πᾶς = all; οἶκος
  Ἰσραήλ = Israel.
- 2:38 ὑμῶν carried "of your" → ἁμαρτιῶν = of + sins, ὑμῶν = your.
- 2:40 τε carried "With" → λόγοις = With + words, τε `s`.

- 2:47 ἐπί = "to", τό = "their", αὐτό = "number" → ἐπὶ τὸ αὐτό = to their

## Notable decisions

- Vocatives: BSB has no "O"/"You" before any of them. Ἄνδρες Ἰουδαῖοι = Men +
  of Judea (2:14) and Ἄνδρες Ἰσραηλεῖται = Men + of Israel (2:22) stay word
  by word; Ἄνδρες ἀδελφοί = Brothers (2:29, 2:37) is one group, as a fixed
  form of address (like ἀποκριθεὶς … εἶπεν = replied).
- List of nations (2:9-11): every name is linked. Every English "and" there
  renders a Greek καί or τε καί (joined); καί rendered by a comma or
  semicolon is `s`. "of Libya" is the genitive; "residents of" is the
  participle with its object (Matthew 23:21); "from Rome" renders Ῥωμαῖοι.
- 2:33 τε: it joins the participles ὑψωθείς and λαβών; BSB's only "and"
  stands before has poured out. Same meaning, shifted, so τε = and (no split
  pair) rather than τε `s` + "and" `t`.
- 2:23 τοῦτον = He (BSB makes the object of ἀνείλατε the subject of a passive
  clause, ἔκδοτον = was delivered up); both later "Him" are `t`.
- 2:17-18 partitive ἀπό ("some of My Spirit") `s`; repeated ὑμῶν / ἐπί …
  μου that BSB does not repeat are `s`.
- 2:5 ἄνδρες (appositional "men") `s`; 2:41 ψυχαί ("souls") `s`; 2:45 πᾶσιν
  `s` (BSB's "anyone" is τις).
- 2:14 αὐτοῖς = the crowd and 2:16 ἀλλά = No are kept with notes.

## 2:42 (KEEP, unchanged)

No meaning error. One convention point only: τῇ (tokens 3 and 10) alone
carries "to the" twice, a lone article carrying a preposition, which later
chapters split (the article = "the", "to" with the noun). Left as is.

## Doubts for the peer

- 2:24 "from the dead" `t` vs 2:32 "to life" linked with ἀνέστησεν. I
  followed the precedent for each phrase (the reviewed Gospels link "raised to
  life" to the verb and have "from the dead" `t` where the Greek lacks ἐκ
  νεκρῶν); the peer may prefer to treat both alike.
- 2:9 / 2:14 "of"/"in" with the participle of κατοικέω rather than with the
  place name (publisher had "of" on the article and "in Jerusalem" on the
  name).
- 2:29 / 2:37 Ἄνδρες ἀδελφοί joined to "Brothers" rather than Ἄνδρες `s`.
- 2:36 οἶκος Ἰσραήλ joined to "Israel" rather than οἶκος `s`.
- 2:3 πυρός kept as "flames of fire" (publisher row): "flames" could be `t`.
- 2:45 πᾶσιν `s` and καθότι ἄν `s`, with "who was in" kept on εἶχεν.
