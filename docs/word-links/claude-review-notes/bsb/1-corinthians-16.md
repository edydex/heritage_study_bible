- 16:24: the extra word is probably ἀμήν. "Amen" is `t` with the

## Publisher rows corrected

Lone articles, numbers or prepositions carrying "of the"/"to the"/"for"/"be":

- 16:15 εἰς "to the" → "to". "the#1" moves to διακονίαν ("the#1 service",
  which has no Greek article). τοῖς "of the" → "the#2", and ἁγίοις = "of +
  saints".
- 16:23 τοῦ "of the" → "the", and Κυρίου = "of + Lord" (2 Corinthians
  13:14). μεθ’ "be with" → "with". "be" is `t` (a wishing copula,
  Romans 16:20).
- 16:24 μετά "be with" → "with", and "be" is `t`.
- 16:4 τοῦ "for" → joined to its infinitive ("to go", Acts 10:25). "for"
  moves to κἀμέ ("for me + also"): it marks the infinitive's subject. "also"
  moves off πορεύεσθαι onto κἀμέ, because the crasis καί + ἐμέ is the
  "also".
- 16:2 κατά "of#0 every" → "On + every" (distributive κατά). μίαν "On the
  first day" → "the first", and "day" is `t` (Acts 20:7 / John 20:1).
  σαββάτου "week" → "of#0 + week".

Over-claiming links:

- 16:2 θησαυρίζων "saving it up" → "saving + up". "it" is `t` (a supplied
  object). ἐάν "a portion" → joined ὅ τι ἐάν = "a portion". παρ’ "aside" →
  joined παρ’ ἑαυτῷ = "aside".
- 16:3 παραγένωμαι "on my arrival" → "my arrival". ὅταν = "on".
- 16:6 ὑμᾶς "you#0 awhile" → "you#0", and "awhile" is `t` (an added
  adverb). με "me on my journey" → "me". The journey idiom goes on the verb:
  προπέμψητε = "can help + on my journey" (3 John 6).
- 16:7 θέλω "I do not want" → "I#0 do + want", with οὐ = "not" (negatives
  are split from verbs). ἐν "only in" → "in", and "only" is `t`.
- 16:9 "a" moves from μεγάλη to θύρα ("a + door"). καί "for" → `s`, because
  "for" does not mean "and". ἐνεργής = "for effective work". ἀντικείμενοι
  "oppose me" → "oppose", and "me#1" is `t`.
- 16:10 πρός "while he is with" → "with". γένηται = "he#0 has + he#1 is",
  and "while" is `t`. κἀγώ "I am" → "I". "am" resumes ἐργάζεται, like
  "do" for ἀσπάζεται in Romans 16:21.
- 16:11 ἐξουθενήσῃ "should treat him with contempt" → "should treat +
  with#0 contempt", with "him#0" on the first αὐτόν (a TODO). "on his way"
  moves from αὐτόν to προπέμψατε.
- 16:12 παρεκάλεσα "I strongly urged" → "I + urged", with πολλά =
  "strongly". ἦν "He was" → "was", and "He" is `t`. The Greek subject is
  θέλημα ("will"); BSB makes Apollos the subject.
- 16:15 τήν "and#0 his" → "his". "and#0" is `t`: BSB recasts "the household
  of Stephanas" as "Stephanas and his household".
- 16:17 χαίρω "I am glad that" → "I am glad", and ἐπί = "that". τό "what"
  and ὑστέρημα "was lacking" → joined as "what was lacking".
- 16:18 ὑμῶν "yours as well" → joined τὸ ὑμῶν = "yours", and "as well" is
  `t`.
- 16:19 Ἀσπάζονται "send you greetings" → "send + greetings", with
  "you#0" on ὑμᾶς (Romans 16:16). Ἀσίας "in#0 the province of Asia" →
  "in#0 + Asia". τῆς = "the#0", and "province of" is `t` (Romans 16:5).
  σύν "and#1 so does" → "and#1". "so does" resumes ἀσπάζεται ("greet + so
  does"). κατ’ "that meets at" → "at", and "that meets" is `t` (Romans
  16:5).
- 16:20 "send you greetings" → "send + greetings", with "you" on ὑμᾶς.
  ἀδελφοί "brothers here" → "brothers", and "here" is `t` (an added place
  word). "a" moves from ἁγίῳ to φιλήματι ("a + kiss", Romans 16:16).
- 16:21 τῇ "is in my" / ἐμῇ "own" / χειρί "hand" → τῇ χειρί = "in + hand",
  ἐμῇ = "my own", and "is" is `t` (Colossians 4:18, 2 Thessalonians 3:17).
- 16:22 "does not" → οὐ = "not", and φιλεῖ = "does + love".

## Notable decisions

- 16:1 οὕτως and καί ("so … also") are `s`. BSB's "you are to do as I
  directed" renders neither. The two articles τῆς (4, 12) are `s`, and "of
  Galatia" stays on the declinable name.
- 16:2 γίνωνται ("take place") stays linked to "will be needed", with a
  note. τότε is `s`.
- 16:3 δέ = "Then" (a connective rendered with a different connective).
  οὓς ἐάν is `s`, because BSB leaves the relative unexpressed ("those you
  recommend") and τούτους carries "those".
- 16:9 καί = "even though" (a connective rendered with a concessive
  connective).
- 16:14 ὑμῶν is `s`. BSB recasts "let all your things be done" as "Do
  everything".
- 16:16 ὑμεῖς and καί ("you also") are `s`. BSB's "I urge you … to submit"
  carries the person from 16:15. τῷ συνεργοῦντι is joined (article +
  participle).
- 16:17 The first καί is `s` (a list comma, Romans 16:21).

## Doubts for the peer

- 16:22: the link for μαρὰν ἀθά follows the coordinator's instruction. By
  rule 9 alone, N1904's division ("our Lord has come") differs in meaning
  from BSB's "Come, O Lord", which would argue for `s`/`t`.
- 16:2: "a portion" for ὅ τι ἐάν ("whatever"), and "of his income" for
  εὐοδῶται ("he may prosper"). An alternative is one joined group for the
  whole clause.
- 16:10: "has" is put on γένηται and "nothing to fear" on ἀφόβως. The
  reverse (ἀφόβως = "has nothing to fear") is also arguable, but it would
  put a verb on an adverb.
- 16:2: "On" is on κατά (with "every"). John 20:1 puts "on" on μιᾷ, but
  there no preposition was present.
