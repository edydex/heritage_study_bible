49 of the 216 publisher `g` rows changed, in 12 of the 14 verses (13:12 and

Conventions followed: Romans 7:7 and Matthew 19:18 for the commandments

## Publisher rows corrected

Lone articles carrying a case word, copula or noun:

- 13:3 τήν "of the" → "the"; "of" joins the verb ("to be unafraid of", as
  "were afraid of" in Mark 11:32, Luke 20:19). τό "what is" → "what"; "is"
  `t`.
- 13:4 τῷ "to the" → joined with κακόν and the participle πράσσοντι: τῷ …
  κακὸν πράσσοντι = "to the wrongdoer" (one English word renders the object
  and the participle); the inner τό is `s`.
- 13:9 τό "The commandments" → "The"; "commandments" `t` (supplied noun).
  τῷ (16) "one" → `s`; "one" `t` (added emphasis; the article does not mean
  "one").
- 13:10 τῷ "to its" → joined with the indeclinable πλησίον: τῷ πλησίον =
  "to its neighbor" (the article both makes the adverb a noun and carries
  the dative, so it is the case word's only owner).
- 13:14 τῆς "of the" → "the"; "of" on σαρκός ("of + flesh").
- 13:1 αἱ "The authorities that" (lone article) → joined with its participle
  οὖσαι: "The authorities that exist".

Supplied words moved off Greek words (`t`):

- 13:1 "that which is" off ὑπό, which keeps "from".
- 13:3 "Then" off ποίει, which keeps "do" (no Greek connective; δέ sits in
  the question and is `s`).
- 13:5 "it is" off ἀνάγκη ("necessary"); "to authority" off ὑποτάσσεσθαι
  ("to submit"), a supplied object.
- 13:6 "This is also why" split: διά = "why", τοῦτο = "This", καί = "also",
  "is" `t`. "the authorities" off εἰσιν ("are"), a subject the Greek gives
  only by the verb ending.
- 13:7 "you" and "him" off ὀφειλάς ("owe"): a noun has no person, and "him"
  is a supplied object. "are due"/"is due" (4×) off the first φόρον, τέλος,
  φόβον, τιμήν: the Greek clauses have no verb.
- 13:10 "is" off the second ἀγάπη ("love").
- 13:11 "do" off καί ("And"); "your" off ὕπνου ("slumber"); "is" off
  ἐγγύτερον ("nearer"); "first" off ἐπιστεύσαμεν ("we + believed"), an added
  adverb.

Negatives split from verbs:

- 13:4 οὐ = "not"; φορεῖ = "he does + carry".
- 13:9 the four commandments: Οὐ/Οὐκ = "not"; μοιχεύσεις = "Do + commit
  adultery", φονεύσεις = "Do + murder", κλέψεις = "Do + steal",
  ἐπιθυμήσεις = "Do + covet" (publisher had each whole command on the verb).
- 13:10 "does no wrong" on κακόν → κακόν = "wrong", οὐκ = "no", ἐργάζεται =
  "does".

Content word split:

- 13:1 "to the governing authorities" on ἐξουσίαις → "to the + authorities";
  ὑπερεχούσαις (no link) = "governing".

Joined units (English unchanged): 13:1 Πᾶσα ψυχή = "Everyone", εἰ μή =
"except", τεταγμέναι εἰσίν = "have been appointed"; 13:2 ὁ ἀντιτασσόμενος =
"whoever resists", οἱ ἀνθεστηκότες = "those who do so"; 13:3 ἐξ αὐτῆς =
"his"; 13:6 αὐτὸ τοῦτο = "their work"; 13:8 εἰ μή = "except", τὸ ἀγαπᾶν =
"in love", ὁ ἀγαπῶν = "he who loves".

## Notable decisions

- 13:1, 13:3, 13:5, 13:9, 13:10: the civic and theological terms are linked
  word by word to what BSB prints: ἐξουσία = "authority/authorities" (13:3
  "one in authority", by metonymy), διάκονος = "servant" (twice), ὀργή =
  "retribution" (13:4) and "punishment" (13:5), συνείδησις = "conscience",
  φόρος = "taxes", τέλος = "revenue", ἀγάπη = "Love/love", πλήρωμα = "the
  fulfillment". No interpretive notes.
- 13:4 ἔκδικος εἰς ὀργήν kept as publisher: ἔκδικος = "an agent", εἰς =
  "of", ὀργήν = "retribution", so ὀργή stays visible on its own.
- 13:7 τῷ used as a pronoun keeps "to whom" (4×); in each pair the φόρον etc.
  inside the τῷ clause = "taxes" after "to whom", the second = the "taxes"
  paid. The other eight articles are `s`.
- 13:8 μηδέν ("anything") is `s`: BSB has only "to no one". ἀλλήλους = "to
  one another", parallel to "to no one".
- 13:9 the second ἐν τῷ is `s` (a repeated frame BSB replaces with a colon);
  εἰ in εἴ τις is `s`, τις = "any".
- 13:5 both διά kept linked: "to avoid" and "as a matter of" each give the
  reason for submitting.
- 13:11 ἤδη = "has come" kept (it is already the hour = the hour has come).

## Doubts for the peer

- 13:9 "commandments#0" is `t`; it could instead link to ἐντολή as a word
  understood again (both are subjects of "are summed up").
- 13:9 Galatians 5:14 joins both ἐν to one "in" (`g 4 8`); here the second
  ἐν τῷ is `s` under the rule for an untranslated repeated frame.
- 13:1 "authorities#1" sits in the article + participle unit αἱ οὖσαι; it
  could instead be `t` (supplied head noun) with αἱ = "The", οὖσαι = "that
  exist".
- 13:5 διά = "to avoid" and 13:11 ἤδη = "has come": kept as shifted
  renderings of the same meaning; `s` + `t` is the stricter alternative.
- 13:10 τῷ πλησίον joined; 13:9 keeps τόν (before πλησίον σου) as `s`
  because "your" belongs to σου.
