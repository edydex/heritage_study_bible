# BSB Romans 10: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: all 21 (10:1-21). No KEEP blocks in this chapter. Final
check: `fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 21, "problems": 0}`.

74 of the 287 publisher `g` rows changed, in 19 verses. Every publisher row
was checked, including the two verses whose rows stay unchanged apart from
resolved TODOs and notes (10:2, 10:16). No "of the"/"to the"/"in the"/"for"
sits on a lone article. Articles with no English "the" are `s` with the
standard reason (README rule 5).

Conventions followed: Romans 1 and 3 (concise notes; article + participle
joined; γάρ = "because"; μή in a question `s`; supplied "is", "comes" `t`),
Galatians 3:12 ("who does" on ποιήσας, not on the noun), Acts 13:37 and
John 10:36 (a relative with no antecedent = "the One whom"), Acts 9:14 and
2 Timothy 2:22 ("call on" on ἐπικαλέω), Acts 21:20 ("all of them" = πάντες),
Matthew 20:6 ("day long" = ἡμέραν), and the brief's rules on participles,
negatives, supplied copulas and τε … καί.

## Publisher rows corrected

Lone articles or nouns carrying a preposition or verb word:

- 10:3 "to" on τῇ → τῇ `s`, "to + righteousness" on δικαιοσύνῃ; "of" had
  been on Θεοῦ ("of God's") → "were ignorant of" on ἀγνοοῦντες (the "of" is
  the English verb's, the genitive is "God's").
- 10:5 "concerning the" on τήν → τήν = "the", "concerning + righteousness".
- 10:18 "of the" on τῆς → "the", "of + world" on οἰκουμένης.
- 10:21 "a disobedient" → "a" goes with the noun ("a + people").

Supplied words folded into Greek words (now `t`):

  that is by (that is), 10:8 it is in (it is), 10:9 is Lord, 10:12 is Lord of
  all, 10:15 beautiful are.
- 10:1 "their salvation" → salvation; "their" `t` (αὐτῶν is already "the
  Israelites"; different role).
- 10:4 "to bring" on εἰς → to; "bring" `t`.
- 10:10 "with your heart/mouth" → with + heart/mouth, "your" `t`.
- 10:11 "It is just as" on γάρ → "just as" = γάρ, "It is" `t`.
- 10:12 "and gives richly" → gives richly; "and" `t` (participle, no καί).
- 10:17 "faith comes" → faith; "comes" `t`.
- 10:18 "Indeed they did" → Indeed = μενοῦνγε; "they did" `t`.
- 10:19 "by those who are" → by = ἐπ’; "those who are" `t`.

Person, modal and verb words on the wrong owner:

- 10:3 "they were ignorant" → participle ἀγνοοῦντες = "were ignorant of";
  "they" `t` (Romans 1:21-22).
- 10:7 "up from" on ἐκ → from; "up" joins ἀναγαγεῖν ("to bring … up").
- 10:10 "you believe" / "you confess" on impersonal passives → "you" `t`;
  "and are" had been on εἰς → εἰς `s`, "and" `t`, "are justified" /
  "are saved" on δικαιοσύνην / σωτηρίαν.
- 10:12 "on Him" → Him; "on" joins the verb ("who call on"), as 10:13.
- 10:14 εἰς had "on" → εἰς = "in" (believe in whom); "on" joins the verb
  ("can they call on"); ὅν = "the One … whom"; οὗ = "the One of whom" (was
  inside "can they believe in the One"); "in whom they have not believed"
  had been all on ἐπίστευσαν.
- 10:19 "did Israel" → Israel; "did + understand" on ἔγνω.
- 10:5 "man who" on ἄνθρωπος → man; "who does" on ποιήσας.

Negatives split: 10:3 οὐχ, 10:6 Μή ("Do + say"), 10:11 οὐ = never ("will +
be put to shame"), 10:14 οὐκ twice, 10:18 οὐκ, 10:20 μή twice.

Units joined: 10:4, 10:11 τῷ/ὁ + participle = "who believes"; 10:12 τοὺς
ἐπικαλουμένους = "who call on"; 10:13 ὃς ἄν = "who"; 10:15 ἐὰν μή =
"unless"; 10:20 τοῖς + participle = "by/to those who did … seek/ask for",
ἐμφανὴς ἐγενόμην = "I revealed Myself" (ἐγενόμην had no link).

Other rows: 10:12 τε had "between" → τε `s` (no "both"), "between Jew" on
Ἰουδαίου (genitive); Lord#1 linked to Κύριος with Lord#0 (see doubts).

## Readings

- 10:5: EVIDENCE shows three extra publisher words. BSB's "these things" is
  `t` with the reading reason (probably αὐτά). "them" stays on αὐτῇ, which
  the publisher row itself links, with a note.
- 10:15: one N1904 word unmatched; As = καθάπερ with the same-meaning note
  (probably καθώς, as Romans 3:4). The other extra word is probably τά
  before ἀγαθά; nothing is claimed for it.
- 10:3 and 10:20: one extra publisher word each (probably a second
  δικαιοσύνην after ἰδίαν, and ἐν before τοῖς). BSB prints no word for the
  first; in 10:20 "by" is carried by the dative article + participle, so no
  reading is claimed.

No cross-verse links.

## Notable decisions

- Theological terms are linked word for word, no interpretive notes:
  δικαιοσύνη = righteousness (10:3-6), are justified (10:10, noun rendered as
  verb), νόμος = law, ὁμολογέω = confess, πιστεύω = believe, σῴζω = be
  saved, σωτηρία = salvation / are saved, κηρύσσω = proclaim / preach,
  ἀκοή = message (10:16), hearing (10:17), ῥῆμα = word.
- 10:15 τῶν εὐαγγελιζομένων ἀγαθά joined = "of those who bring good news"
  (verb and object together; "good news" cannot be split between them).
- 10:2 ζῆλον = zealous, ἔχουσιν = "they are" kept with a note (the finite
  verb of "have zeal" → "are zealous").
- 10:16 ὑπήκουσαν = welcomed, 10:18 and 10:19 λέγω = I ask, ἀλλά = instead,
  10:21 πρός = as for: same role, shifted English, linked with a note.
- 10:18 and 10:19 μή in μὴ οὐκ / μὴ … οὐκ is `s` (question marker), οὐκ =
  not.
- 10:8 relative ὅ `s` (BSB "the word of faith we are proclaiming").

## Doubts for the peer

- 10:5: in N1904 (no αὐτά) τὴν δικαιοσύνην is most naturally the object of
  ὁ ποιήσας; BSB's "concerning" follows its own reading's syntax. I linked
  "concerning + righteousness" to the noun rather than marking "concerning"
  `t`.
- 10:12: Κύριος linked to both "Lord" words (BSB repeats it as subject and
  predicate of the verbless clause). Lord#0 `t` would also be defensible.
- 10:10: εἰς `s` + "and" `t` rather than εἰς = "and"; the impersonal
  passives keep only "believe"/"confess", "you" `t`.
- 10:14: "in#1" of "believe in" put on πιστεύσωσιν (no Greek preposition in
  that clause), while "in#0" is the Greek εἰς of the first clause.
- 10:18: "they did" `t`; it could be argued as ἤκουσαν understood again.
