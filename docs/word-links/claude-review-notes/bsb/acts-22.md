# BSB Acts 22: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: all 30 (22:1-30). No KEEP blocks in this chapter. Final
check: `fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 30, "problems": 0}`.

95 of the 460 publisher `g` rows changed their English, in 27 verses (22:7,
22:15 and 22:21 kept every publisher row and only resolved TODO tokens). 15
rows only gained a token, with the English unchanged: 22:1 Ἄνδρες ἀδελφοί =
Brothers (as 1:16, 7:2, 15:7, 15:13); 22:2 Ἑβραΐδι διαλέκτῳ = in
Hebrew; 22:3 ἀνὴρ Ἰουδαῖος = a Jew; 22:5 τοὺς … ὄντας = these
people; 22:10 πρός με = me; 22:11 οὐκ ἐνέβλεπον = had blinded me,
χειραγωγούμενος … ἦλθον = led me by the hand; 22:13 πρὸς ἐμέ = me, εἰς
αὐτόν = him; 22:15 πάντας ἀνθρώπους = everyone; 22:18 ἐν τάχει = quickly,
ἐξ Ἱερουσαλήμ = Jerusalem; 22:19 ἤμην φυλακίζων = imprisoned; 22:20 ἤμην
ἐφεστώς = stood there; 22:22 τὸν τοιοῦτον = him.

Verse division: N1904 and BSB divide 22:1-2 the same way (22:2 ends with
καὶ φησίν = "Then Paul declared"), and every other boundary matches too, so
there are no `m` or `g @` cross-verse links. The EVIDENCE lines of 22:2,
22:3 and 22:29 say "differs" but with 0 unmatched words either way, so no
reading is claimed anywhere in the chapter.

## Publisher rows corrected

English words with no Greek counterpart that had been folded into a Greek
word (now `t`):

- Participle "and" (6 rows): 22:5 and I was on my way, 22:10 and go, 22:22
  and shouted, 22:26 and reported, 22:27 and asked, 22:30 and had him
  stand. Also 22:24 "and interrogated" (BSB turns μάστιξιν "with whips"
  into flogged and joins it to interrogated).
- Supplied subjects: 22:2 "Paul" (φησίν), 22:18 "the people here"
  (παραδέξονται), 22:22 "The crowd" (Ἤκουον), 22:30 "the commander" and
  "Paul" (verb endings only).
- Supplied objects and complements: 22:4 "them" (throwing), 22:5 "them"
  (bring), 22:25 "him" (strap), 22:26 "this" (hearing) and "it"
  (reported), 22:28 "a citizen" (γεγέννημαι means only "I have been
  born").
- Other BSB additions: 22:12 "There" (it had been on δέ) and "named" (no
  ὄνομα, as 10:5); 22:22 "he made" (the Greek says only "until this word").

Lone articles carrying a preposition or noun (the article keeps "the" or
goes `s`; the preposition goes on the noun): 22:3 τοῦ "to the" and τοῦ
"for", 22:8 ὁ "of", 22:9 τοῦ "of the One" (now joined with λαλοῦντος), 22:11
τῶν "my companions" (now τῶν συνόντων = companions, μοι = my), 22:12 τόν
"of the", 22:13 τῇ "at", 22:19 τάς "after another", 22:20 τῶν "of those
who" (now joined with ἀναιρούντων), 22:26 τῷ "to the", 22:28 τήν "for my"
(now τήν = my, ἐκτησάμην = paid + for), 22:5 τούς "these people" (now
joined with ὄντας). 22:11 ἐκείνου had carried "of the" (now `s`; τοῦ = the,
φωτός = of + light).

"When/As/On" moved to the participle or genitive absolute: 22:2 "When"
(from δέ to ἀκούσαντες), 22:6 "as" (from ἐγένετο to ἐγγίζοντι), 22:17
"when" (from μοι to ὑποστρέψαντι), 22:23 "As" (from τε to κραυγαζόντων),
22:26 "On" (from δέ to ἀκούσας).

First person never comes from a participle (Acts 16 peer rule): 22:3
"I#1"/"I#2" moved from πεπαιδευμένος and ὑπάρχων to Ἐγώ (I + I + I); 22:5
"I#0" moved from δεξάμενος to ἐπορευόμην (I + I was on my way). The third-
person "he brought" (22:30) and "He directed" (22:24) stay on their
participles by convention (16:30).

Other words on the wrong Greek word:

- 22:1 "to" moved from μου to ἀκούσατε (listen + to), and 22:22 "to" from
  αὐτοῦ to Ἤκουον (listened to), as 7:2 and 15:13.
- 22:3 "just as" moved from ζηλωτής to καθώς (just as + as); "in … to"
  moved from ἀκρίβειαν and τοῦ to κατά (in + conformity to), ἀκρίβειαν =
  strict.
- 22:4 "the" moved from ἄχρι to θανάτου (the death).
- 22:5 "even" moved from δεξάμενος to καί (12); "to" moved from δεδεμένους
  to the purpose participle ἄξων (to + bring); the adverbial καί (22) had
  carried "and" (now `s`, with "and" `t`).
- 22:9 "not" split off ἤκουσαν (they could + understand); οἱ σὺν ἐμοὶ
  ὄντες = companions, ἐμοί = My.
- 22:13 κἀγώ = And + I ("I" had been on ἀνέβλεψα); αὐτῇ τῇ ὥρᾳ = that / at +
  moment.
- 22:14 ὁ = he (article as pronoun; "he said" had been on εἶπεν); "of"
  moved from ἡμῶν to πατέρων; "His" moved from φωνήν to αὐτοῦ (24), whose
  ἐκ τοῦ στόματος BSB leaves out.
- 22:16 "away" moved from ἁμαρτίας to ἀπόλουσαι (wash + away).
- 22:18 "not" split off παραδέξονται (will + accept).
- 22:19 αὐτοί = they (had been folded into ἐπίστανται); distributive κατά
  = in one + after another; τοὺς πιστεύοντας joined.
- 22:20 "of" moved from σου to Στεφάνου.
- 22:22 λόγου = statement ("this" had been on it; τούτου = this); αὐτόν =
  He, οὐ = not, καθῆκεν = is + fit (all had been on καθῆκεν).
- 22:25 αὐτόν = him ("him" had been folded into προέτειναν, now they
  stretched + out); ἄνθρωπον Ῥωμαῖον joined = a Roman citizen (as 16:37).
- 22:27 ὁ = he (article as pronoun; had been on ἔφη).
- 22:29 οἱ μέλλοντες joined; in καὶ ὁ χιλίαρχος δέ, δέ = and and καί =
  himself (they had been the other way round); "in chains" moved from
  Ῥωμαῖος to the periphrastic ἦν δεδεκώς (he had put + in chains).
- 22:30 "down" moved from Παῦλον to καταγαγών (he brought + down); τὸν
  Παῦλον = Paul + him (one object shared by καταγαγών and ἔστησεν, as
  16:3 and 16:37); τὸ ἀσφαλές … τὸ τί split: τό = the, ἀσφαλές = real, τί =
  reason.

## Notable decisions

- Parallels with reviewed Acts 9: 22:7 = 9:4 (Saul + Saul, why, do you
  persecute, Me; τε `s`), 22:8 = 9:5 (are You, are persecuting), 22:13
  Σαοὺλ ἀδελφέ = Saul / Brother as 9:17; 22:10 σοι = you with the passive,
  as 9:6. 22:4 τε = both, because BSB writes "both" (8:12, 8:38, 10:39);
  9:2 makes τε `s` only because BSB has no "both" there.
- Names linked: Tarsus, Cilicia, Gamaliel, Damascus (×4), Ananias,
  Jerusalem, Stephen, Nazareth (Ναζωραῖος = of Nazareth, article `s`, as
  3:6). Pronouns BSB renames are linked: 22:18 αὐτόν = the Lord, 22:22,
  22:24 (twice), 22:27 and 22:29 = Paul.
- 22:2 τῇ Ἑβραΐδι διαλέκτῳ: adjective and noun jointly "in Hebrew" (the
  English noun names the language); ὅτι `s`.
- 22:6 and 22:17 Ἐγένετο `s` (narrative formula, as 9:3); 22:17 δέ =
  Later (as 9:1 "Meanwhile"); the dative μοι = I, and 22:17 μου (subject of
  the second participle) `s`.
- 22:11 χειραγωγούμενος and ἦλθον jointly = led me by the hand: BSB makes
  the companions the subject (ὑπό `s`) and the first person of ἦλθον
  becomes "me".
- 22:12 kept the publisher's split: τις = a, ἀνήρ = man, εὐλαβής = a
  devout, κατά = observer of.
- 22:24 δι’ ἣν αἰτίαν: ἥν `s`; "the reason" stays on αἰτίαν, "for" on διά.
- 22:28 ταύτην `s` (BSB "my citizenship" for "this citizenship").
- 22:29 BSB merges "that he is a Roman and that he had bound him" into one
  clause: Ῥωμαῖος = a Roman citizen, the second ὅτι = that; the first ὅτι,
  ἐστιν, καί and αὐτόν `s`. εὐθέως `s`; οὖν = Then.

## Doubts for the peer

- 22:3 Ἐγώ = I + I + I and 22:5 ἐπορευόμην = I + I was on my way: the
  alternative is `t` for the repeated "I". 22:20 "giving my approval" keeps
  "my" inside the participle's paraphrase.
- 22:11 the joint χειραγωγούμενος … ἦλθον group; the alternative is ἦλθον
  `s` with "me" left on the participle, which the first-person rule
  forbids.
- 22:29 καί = himself, δέ = and: the alternative is the publisher's καί =
  and with δέ `s` and "himself" `t`.
- 22:5 καί (22) `s` + "and#2" `t`: the καί means "also", while BSB's "and"
  joins two verbs it made up (apprehend, bring), so I did not link them.
- 22:30 τὸ τί = reason (τί) with the second τό `s`; τὸ ἀσφαλές = the real.
- 22:13, 22:10, 22:18 join a preposition with its object when BSB makes
  that object the direct object of a verb (as 9:11 πρὸς αὐτόν = him).
