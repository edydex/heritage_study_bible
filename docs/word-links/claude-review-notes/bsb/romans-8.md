## Readings

- 8:16 συνμαρτυρεῖ, 8:17 συνπάσχομεν, 8:22 συνστενάζει: same word, spelled
  differently in BSB's Greek text (probably συμμαρτυρεῖ, συμπάσχομεν,
  συστενάζει); linked with a note.
- 8:21: BSB's Greek probably has ὅτι where N1904 has διότι. "that" introduces
  the content of the hope, so διότι is `s` and "that" `t`, both with the
  reading reason.
- 8:28: BSB's Greek has two extra words, probably ὁ θεός as subject. "God"
  is `t` with the reading reason; τὸν Θεόν = "Him" (Θεόν linked, τόν `s`).
- 8:15 (form/order only, 0 words) and 8:34 (one extra publisher word,
  probably καί before ἐστιν, which BSB does not render): no reading claimed.

  "Spirit" (8:10 and 8:16 human "spirit"); σάρξ = "flesh" (8:3 σαρκὸς
  ἁμαρτίας = "of + man" + "sinful"; 8:12 σάρκα = "it"); κατάκριμα =
  "condemnation"; κατακρίνω = "condemned"; υἱοθεσία = "of sonship" (8:15),
  "adoption as sons" (8:23); δόξα = "glory", 8:21 δόξης = "glorious";
  ἐλπίς = "hope"; προγινώσκω = "foreknew"; προορίζω = "predestined"; δικαιόω

  φρόνημα = "mind"; φρονέω = "set their minds on"; δικαίωμα = "righteous

- 8:15 Ἀββᾶ = "Abba", Πατήρ = "Father", ὁ `s` (as Galatians 4:6).
- 8:35 and 8:38-39: every list item and every ἤ/οὔτε is linked; 8:39 τις =
  "anything", ἑτέρα = "else", κτίσις = "creation".

- Connectives rendered with another connective stay linked: 8:10 δέ = "yet",

## Doubts for the peer

- 8:24 τις = "he" and τί ("why") = "Who" as a shifted question word; the
  alternatives are τί `s` with "Who" `t`, or the publisher's τις = "Who".
- 8:21 διότι `s` / "that" `t`; διότι can sometimes mean "that", so linking it
  with a note is the alternative.
- 8:26 τί καθό joined = "how"; the alternative is τί = "how" and καθὸ δεῖ
  joined = "ought".
- 8:8-9 "controlled" on ἐν; the alternative puts "controlled" on the εἰμί
  form (ὄντες/ἐστέ) or makes it `t`.
- 8:3 περί = "as an offering for"; the alternative is "as an offering" `t`.
- 8:5 "live" in the second clause and 8:35 "Shall" linked as words understood
  again; `t` is the alternative for each.
- 8:15 πάλιν = "returns" with "that" and "you" `t`.
- 8:18 τοῦ νῦν καιροῦ joined = "present" and τά = "our".
- 8:33 and 8:38 "any" `t` (split from "will bring + charge" and "powers").
