## Publisher rows corrected

English-only words folded into Greek words (now `t`):

- 15:2 πᾶν "every branch" (second clause) → every, `t branch` (the Greek has only πᾶν, "every one").
- 15:4 κἀγώ "and I will remain" → and I, `t will remain` (the Greek is only κἀγὼ ἐν ὑμῖν).
- 15:5 ὑμεῖς "and you are" → you, `t and`, `t are` (asyndeton, no verb).
- 15:6 ὡς "he is like" → like, `t he is` (BSB's comparison frame).
- 15:17 ἐντέλλομαι "is My command" → My command, `t is`.
- 15:19 ἐφίλει "would love you" → love; ἄν = would; τό "as its" → `s`, ἴδιον = its own; `t you as` (the Greek object is τὸ ἴδιον).
- 15:25 ἀλλ’ "But this is" → But, `t this is`.

Negatives and auxiliaries split off verbs:

- 15:2 φέρον "that bears no" → μή = no, φέρον = that bears.
- 15:6 μή "does not" → not, μένῃ = does + remain.
- 15:15 οὐκ "does not" → not, οἶδεν = does + understand.
- 15:16 οὐχ "did not" → not, ἐξελέξασθε = did + choose.
- 15:21 οἴδασιν "they do not know" → οὐκ = not, οἴδασιν = they do + know.
- 15:22, 15:24 "I had not come" / "I had not done" → μή = not, verb = I had + come / done.
- 15:22, 15:24 εἴχοσαν "they would not be guilty of sin" → ἁμαρτίαν = sin, οὐκ = not, εἴχοσαν = they would + be guilty of.

Articles, prepositions and pronouns on the wrong word:

- 15:4 ἑαυτοῦ "by itself" → ἀφ’ = by, ἑαυτοῦ = itself. οὐδέ "neither can" → neither; ὑμεῖς "you bear fruit" → you (can / bear / fruit are the understood δύναται, φέρειν, καρπόν).
- 15:6 τό "a" → `s`, "a branch" on κλῆμα (English "a" goes with the noun).
- 15:8 τούτῳ "This is to" → ἐν τούτῳ = This; ἐδοξάσθη = is to + glory.
- 15:9 κἀγώ "so" → so + I; ἠγάπησα "have I loved" → have + loved.
- 15:2 ἵνα "to make it", φέρῃ "even more fruitful" → ἵνα = to, φέρῃ = make it, καρπόν = fruitful, πλείονα = even more.

Joined units (rule 3, article + participle):

- 15:2 τὸ … φέρον = that does bear; 15:5 ὁ μένων = The one who remains; 15:21 τὸν πέμψαντα = the One who sent; 15:23 ὁ … μισῶν = Whoever hates; 15:25 ὁ … γεγραμμένος = is written.

## Readings

- Only the EVIDENCE lines of 15:8, 15:14 and 15:16 show publisher Greek different from N1904. In each, the differing publisher word is the one BSB's English renders, so all three are linked with a same-sense note.
- 15:8: γενήσεσθε = proving yourselves to be (probably the subjunctive γένησθε).
- 15:14: ὅ = what (probably plural ἅ).
- 15:16: ὅ τι ἄν = whatever (as 14:13; probably ὅτι written as one word).

## Notable decisions

- 15:4: the second clause κἀγὼ ἐν ὑμῖν has no verb. BSB's "will remain" is `t` rather than linked to μείνατε, because the imperative "you remain" is a different person and mood. In the last clause, οὐδὲ ὑμεῖς does leave δύναται καρπὸν φέρειν understood, so BSB's repeated can / bear / fruit are linked to those same words.
- 15:2 and 15:5: the resumptive αὐτό (twice) and οὗτος repeat a fronted phrase that English states once: `s`.
- 15:2: "to make it even more fruitful" is linked word by word (fruitful ← καρπόν, even more ← πλείονα, make it ← φέρῃ) with a short note, not as one block.
- 15:6: ἐβλήθη keeps BSB's "that is thrown" (BSB applies it to the branch); "he is" is `t`. αὐτά = Such branches (the object becomes BSB's passive subject).
- 15:18: πρῶτον ὑμῶν ("before you") → first; ὑμῶν `s`.
- 15:19: δέ = Instead; διὰ τοῦτο `s` (BSB reorders the sentence and uses "because" for ὅτι).
- 15:21: πάντα `s` ("like this" does not mean "all"); εἰς `s` (ὑμᾶς is the object of treat).
- 15:24: of the four καί, the first ("both … and" with the verbs) is `s`; the others are and / both / and.

## Doubts for the peer

- 15:4 "will remain": `t` or linked to μείνατε as an understood verb? I chose `t`; linking would be consistent with can / bear / fruit later in the verse.
- 15:2 φέρῃ = make it: an alternative is joining καρπὸν … φέρῃ = make it + fruitful.
- 15:6 "that is thrown" vs `t he is`: the alternative is ἐβλήθη = he + is thrown with `t is`/`t that`; I kept BSB's English structure.
- 15:8 ἐν τούτῳ joined as This; ἐν could be `s` instead.
- 15:16 "that will remain" (μένῃ) and 15:6 "that is thrown" keep the English relative "that" with the verb, as the publisher did.
