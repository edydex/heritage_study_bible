## Publisher rows corrected

English-only words folded into Greek words (now `t`):

- 12:3 "and she anointed" → she anointed, `t and` (participle λαβοῦσα + main verb). 12:24 "and dies" → dies, `t and`. 12:36 "and was hidden" → was hidden, `t and`.
- 12:5 καί "and the money" → and, `t the money` (as Matthew 26:9). 12:6 "Judas did not say" → οὐχ = not, εἶπεν = did + say, `t Judas`; "was put into it" → what was put, `t into it`. 12:9 ἐστιν "Jesus was" → was, `t Jesus`. 12:11 "were deserting them" → were deserting, `t them`.
- 12:16 ὅτι "and they realized that" → that, `t and they realized`; "these very things" → these + things, `t very`. 12:17 the unlinked "continued to testify" now on Ἐμαρτύρει; `t that`.
- 12:18 "That is also why" → διὰ τοῦτο = That + why, καί = also, `t is`; "they heard that" → they heard, `t that` (accusative + infinitive).
- 12:19 "this is doing you no good" → ὠφελεῖτε = is doing you + good, οὐκ … οὐδέν = no, `t this`; "Look how" → Look, `t how`; "whole world" → world, `t whole`.
- 12:21 τῷ "who was" → who, `t was` (a lone article never carries was). 12:22 "relayed this appeal" → ἔρχεται … καὶ λέγει = relayed, `t this appeal`; "and both of them" → Ἀνδρέας καὶ Φίλιππος = both of them, `t and` (asyndeton).
- 12:24 "only a seed" → μόνος = only, `t a seed`. 12:27 "it is for this purpose" / "that I have come" → διά = for + purpose, τοῦτο = this, ἦλθον = I have come, `t it is`, `t that` (cleft). 12:28 "glorified it" / "glorify it" → `t it` twice.
- 12:29 "heard it" → heard, `t it`; "and said that" → καί = and, ἔλεγεν = said, `t that`; "said that" (12:29b) → said, `t that` (direct speech made indirect).
- 12:37 "they still did not believe" → οὐκ = not, ἐπίστευον = they + did + believe, `t still`. 12:38 ἵνα "This was" → `t This was`, ἵνα = to, πληρωθῇ = fulfill. 12:42 "did not confess Him" → οὐχ = not, ὡμολόγουν = they did + confess, `t Him`. 12:44 "Me alone" → Me, `t alone`. 12:47 "keep them" → does + keep, `t them`. 12:49 "to say it" → to say, `t it`.

## Readings

- Only the EVIDENCE lines of 12:4, 12:12 and 12:17 show publisher Greek different from N1904.
- 12:4: the unlinked "of" is linked to μαθητῶν with a note that BSB's Greek adds a preposition of the same meaning (probably ἐκ before τῶν μαθητῶν); N1904's partitive genitive gives "of" anyway.
- 12:12: one extra publisher word, but every English word already has an N1904 owner (probably the article before Ἰησοῦς). No reading claimed.
- 12:17: the evidence shows 0 unmatched and 0 extra words, so no reading is claimed; the unlinked "continued to testify" is linked to Ἐμαρτύρει.

## Notable decisions

- 12:3: τοὺς πόδας αὐτοῦ = them on πόδας; αὐτοῦ `s`. λίτραν = about a pint, noted as a Roman pound.
- 12:13: the second "Blessed is" is not καί. καί ("even") `s`; εὐλογημένος links to both "Blessed", as a word understood again.
- 12:16: BSB reverses the two facts. ταῦτα (second) = what, ἐποίησαν = had been done (noted passive), καί = also (publisher's choice kept).
- 12:17: ὁ ὤν = they had been, noted that BSB turns "the crowd that was with him" into what they testified.
- 12:29: καί between the participles = and (BSB makes heard and said the two verbs). Linking avoids a split pair.
- 12:30: ἀπεκρίθη = In response, εἶπεν = said, καί `s`; δι’ ἐμέ = for + benefit, My.
- 12:47: ἐάν τις joined = As for anyone who (the publisher split "As for" / "anyone who"; "As for" alone does not render ἐάν).
- 12:50: the two λαλῶ kept as the publisher had them (speak / to say). Both are the same word and BSB merges the clauses.

## Doubts for the peer

- 12:16: καί could equally be the "and" of "and they realized", leaving "also" as `t`. I kept the publisher's καί = also.
- 12:5 / 12:40: "wasn’t" is joined to οὐκ ἐπράθη, but "cannot" is linked to μή alone. "can" is not a Greek auxiliary; ἐπράθη really contains the "was".
- 12:19: ὠφελεῖτε = "is doing you … good", with "this" `t`. One group for the whole clause ("this is doing you no good") is an alternative.
- 12:24: "a seed" `t`; one could argue it is BSB's rendering of μόνος ("alone, by itself").
- 12:48: ἔχει = There is + for and αὐτόν `s`. The publisher had αὐτόν = for.
- 12:27: ἀλλά = No kept (publisher). It is a strong rendering of "but" that rejects the prayer.
- 12:29: "standing there" kept whole for ὁ ἑστώς; "there" could be `t`.
