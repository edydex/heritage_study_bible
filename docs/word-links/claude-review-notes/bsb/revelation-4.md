Conventions followed:
- Revelation 1:4 and 1:8 for the title ὁ ἦν καὶ ὁ ὢν καὶ ὁ ἐρχόμενος. Each
  article is joined to its verb (4:8: ὁ ἦν = "who was", ὁ ὤν = "is", ὁ
  ἐρχόμενος = "is to come"). Revelation 1:8 for Κύριος ὁ Θεός, where ὁ =
  "the" and Κύριος = "Lord".
- Revelation 1:6 and 1:18 for εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας = "forever" and τῶν αἰώνων =
  "and ever" (4:9, 4:10).
- README rule 3 for article + participle units: ὁ καθήμενος (4:3, 4:9,
  4:10) and τῷ ζῶντι (4:9, 4:10).
- Revelation 1:5 for the repeated article of an attributive adjective `s`
  (4:1 ἡ πρώτη, 4:7 τὸ πρῶτον).
- Revelation 1:14 for a comparison word = "as … as" (4:6 ὁμοία). In 1:14
  the quality "white" has a Greek word. In 4:6 "clear" has none, so it is
  `t`.
- Luke 7:12 and Acts 8:27 for ἰδού rendered by a seeing verb (4:1 "saw",
  4:2 "I saw").
- Romans 12:5 for a distributive unit joined to "each" (4:8 ἓν καθ’ ἕν), and
  Luke 9:3 for an unrendered ἀνά `s`.
- John 20:28 for the articles of titles used in address `s` (4:11).
- 1 Timothy 6:13, Ephesians 1:10 and Hebrews 1:3 for τὰ πάντα = "all
  things" joined (4:11).

## Publisher rows corrected

Supplied words moved off Greek words (`t`):
- 4:4 "were" (off εἴκοσι, "were twenty-four"). The Greek has no verb; the
  accusatives hang on the vision.
- 4:4 "other" (off θρόνους, "other thrones"). It is added to set these
  thrones apart from the central throne.
- 4:6 "was something" (off ὡς, "was something like"). ὡς = "like".
- 4:6 "clear" (off ὁμοία, "as clear as"). ὁμοία = "as#0 + as#1".
- 4:6 "were" before "four living creatures" was a TODO. It is a supplied
  copula, so `t`.
- 4:7 "was#0" and "was#1" (off ὅμοιον 5 and 26, "was like"). ὅμοιον =
  "like".
- 4:8 "is#0" (off Κύριος, "is the Lord"). "the#1" now sits on ὁ (29),
  following Revelation 1:8.
- 4:8 "and#0" (off γέμουσιν, "and was covered with"). There is no καί
  between ἔχων and γέμουσιν.
- 4:3 "there" (off καθήμενος, "seated there") is an added adverb. "that"
  (off ὁράσει, "that gleamed") is an added relative.

Over-claims and wrong owners:
- 4:1 ἤκουσα "I had previously heard" → "I#1 had + heard". "previously"
  renders πρώτη ("the first voice", 1:10), which was TODO. Its repeated
  article ἡ (13) is `s`, and the relative ἥν (15) is `s`.
- 4:8 ἔχουσιν "they never" → "they". οὐκ = "never" (negative split off the
  verb). ἀνάπαυσιν = "stop" is kept.
- 4:9 and 4:10 τῶν "and#1" (a lone article carrying "and") → joined with
  αἰώνων = "and#1 ever".

Units joined (TODO token plus a publisher row; English unchanged except as

- Article + participle: 4:3 ὁ καθήμενος = "The One seated"; 4:9 τῷ

## Notable decisions

- 4:3 ὁράσει is linked twice to the English that stands for it: "looked"
  (ὅμοιος ὁράσει, "looked like") and "gleamed" (the rainbow "that gleamed
  like an emerald"). The comparison word "like" goes on ὅμοιος each time.
- 4:3 κυκλόθεν = "encircled" (kept from the publisher, with a note: the
  adverb is rendered as a verb, and the Greek has no verb). 4:4 κυκλόθεν =
  "Surrounding" (kept).
- 4:3 λίθῳ (5) is `s`, because "jasper" already names the stone. 4:4
  ἱματίοις (17) is `s`, because "dressed in white" leaves "garments"
  implied. I did not fold either noun into "jasper"/"white".
- 4:6 "In the center, around the throne": BSB gives "the throne" only
  once. The first τοῦ θρόνου (12 13) is `s`, and the καί (14) that BSB
  turns into a comma is `s`.
- 4:7 BSB does not repeat "living creature" after "second", "third" and
  "fourth", so ζῷον (10, 16, 25) is `s`. καί (7, 13) is `s`, because BSB
  uses commas. The article of πρόσωπον (18) is `s` ("a face").
- 4:8 αὐτῶν = "of" is kept from the publisher ("each of the four living
  creatures"), with a note. 4:5 ἅ = "These" is kept, with a note.
- 4:11 Ἄξιος εἶ = "Worthy" + "are You". The copula here is the Greek εἶ, so
  it stays linked. ἦσαν = "they exist" and ἐκτίσθησαν = "came to be" are
  linked with tense/voice notes.
- Opening καί left `s` where BSB starts a new sentence: 4:3, 4:4, 4:5 (×2),
  4:6 (9), 4:7, 4:8 (17), 4:10 (20). In 4:11 the καί (22) after the
  semicolon is `s`.

## Doubts for the peer

- 4:8 ἀνάπαυσιν οὐκ ἔχουσιν: I made the smallest fix (οὐκ = "never",
  ἔχουσιν = "they", ἀνάπαυσιν = "stop"). The other choice is to join
  ἀνάπαυσιν + ἔχουσιν = "they + stop" ("have rest" → "stop").
- 4:8 ἀνά (9) is `s`. You could argue it belongs in the "each" group with ἓν
  καθ’ ἕν, since it is also distributive.
- 4:6 the first τοῦ θρόνου is `s`. The other choice is to read "the throne"
  as rendering both, and link θρόνου (13) and (17) to "throne#1".
- 4:6 "something" is `t`. It could instead be read as part of BSB's
  rendering of ὡς ("as it were") and joined to "like".
- 4:4 "other" is `t`. If you read it as BSB's rendering of the contrast
  between θρόνου and θρόνους, the publisher's "other thrones" could stand.
- 4:2 τὸν θρόνον = "it": I put "it" on θρόνον and left τόν `s`.
