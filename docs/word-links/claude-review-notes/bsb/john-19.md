# BSB John 19: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: all 42 (19:1-42). The draft had no KEEP blocks, so no verse
was kept from an earlier review. Final check: `fromtext` →
`{"versesOk": 42, "problems": 0}` (and `check --target BSB` gives the same).

661 publisher `g` rows. 517 are unchanged. The other 144 rows, in 40 verses,
were corrected, split, joined or moved; about 15 of them were only joined to
absorb a TODO token with the English unchanged (τότε οὖν = Then 19:1, 19:16;
διὰ τοῦτο = Therefore 19:11; ἐκ τούτου = From then on 19:12; εἰ μή = but
19:15; ἐντεῦθεν καὶ ἐντεῦθεν = one on each side 19:18; Μετὰ ταῦτα =
Afterward 19:38; λίτρας ἑκατόν = seventy-five pounds 19:39; τῶν Ἰουδαίων =
Jewish 19:42; article + participle units in 19:11, 19:12, 19:32, 19:35,
19:39). In 19:22 and 19:30 every publisher row is kept.

Parallels followed where the Greek matches: Matthew 27:28-31 and Mark 15:17-20
(mockery: πλέξαντες στέφανον ἐξ ἀκανθῶν ἐπέθηκαν = twisted together, a crown,
of, thorns, set + on, `t it`, as Matthew 27:29); Matthew 27:26 / Mark 15:15
(one object serving two verbs = Jesus + Him, had + flogged); Luke 23:21
(Crucify … Him with one αὐτόν = Him + Him); Luke 23:38 (title: ΤΩΝ = THE,
ΙΟΥΔΑΙΩΝ = OF + JEWS); Matthew 26:71 / John 18:5 (ὁ Ναζωραῖος = of Nazareth);
Matthew 27:48 / Mark 15:36 (sponge: `t it`, `t and` for the participle);
Matthew 27:50 (yielded up His spirit); Mark 15:43 (ἐλθών … εἰσῆλθεν = went,
used for ἦλθεν … φέρων = brought); Luke 23:53 (ἦν … κείμενος = had + been
laid). John conventions from John 1-13 and the author review of John 18:
ὁ before names `s`; unrendered αὐτῷ / αὐτοῖς after verbs of speaking `s`;
οὖν `s` when BSB leaves it; ἵνα = to, πληρωθῇ = fulfill, `t This was`
(John 12:38); Ἴδε / Ἰδού = Here is (John 1:47); ὑμεῖς = You, Λάβετε = take
(John 18:31); ἔδωκεν ῥάπισμα = slapped + in the face (John 18:22); τῶν =
the, Ἰουδαίων = of + Jews (John 3:1, 5:1, 18:33).

## Publisher rows corrected

Wrong Greek word carrying the English:

- 19:3 αὐτόν "Him again and again" → Him; "again and again" moved to the
  imperfect ἤρχοντο (they went up + again and again).
- 19:4 Καί "Once" → `s`; πάλιν = Once again.
- 19:11 οὐδεμίαν "over" → κατ’ = over; Οὐκ … οὐδεμίαν joined = no.
- 19:15 ἐκεῖνοι "with Him" → they; Ἆρον ἆρον = Away with / Away with, and
  the one αὐτόν = Him + Him + Him (Luke 23:21).
- 19:18 δέ "with" → `s`, `t with` (δέ does not mean with).
- 19:19 τίτλον "had a notice" → a notice; had moved to ἔθηκεν (had + posted).
- 19:23 ἔλαβον "they divided" → `s`; ἐποίησαν τέσσερα μέρη = they divided +
  into, four, parts.
- 19:29 αὐτοῦ "it" → His; τῷ "to His" → `s`, στόματι = to + mouth.
- 19:31 γάρ "and" → `s`, `t and` (γάρ is "for"); ἐκείνου "the next" →
  `s`, ἡ = the, `t next`; αὐτῶν "the bodies" → `s`, `t the bodies` (the
  subject of ἀρθῶσιν, not the possessor of the legs).
- 19:38 δέ "Afterward" → `s`; Μετὰ ταῦτα = Afterward.
- 19:42 ὅτι "and" → joined with διά = because; `t and`.

"When" on the right word:

- 19:5 οὖν "When" → `s`; When on the καί between the two clauses.
- 19:13 and 19:26 οὖν "When" → `s`; When + heard / When + saw on the
  participles ἀκούσας and ἰδών.

English-only words folded into Greek words (now `t`):

- 19:5, 19:14, 19:16 "Pilate said" / "Pilate handed" → `t Pilate`; 19:16
  "the soldiers took" → took + away, `t the soldiers`; 19:41 "Jesus was
  crucified" → `t Jesus`.
- 19:1 "had Him flogged" → had + flogged, Jesus + Him. 19:2 "set it on" →
  set + on, `t it`. 19:6 "Crucify Him" twice → Crucify, `t Him` (no αὐτόν
  there); "crucify Him" → Him + Him on the one αὐτόν.
- 19:13 δέ "which", Γαββαθα "is Gabbatha" → `s`, `t which`, `t is`,
  Gabbatha.
- 19:20 "and Greek" → Greek, `t and`. 19:21 "but only" → but, `t only`.
- 19:23 "tunic remaining" → tunic, `t remaining`.
- 19:24 "This was to" → to, `t This was`; "to see" `t`; "that is what" →
  that, `t is what`.
- 19:25 "as well as Mary" → Mary, `t as well as`.
- 19:28 "and to" → to, `t and`.
- 19:29 "was sitting there" → `t there`; "they soaked" → soaked + in (μεστόν),
  `t they`; "put it on" / "lifted it" → `t it` twice, `t and`.
- 19:33 "and saw that" → saw, `t and`, `t that`.
- 19:35 "saw it" / "has testified to this" → `t it`, `t to this`.
- 19:37 "as another" → another, `t as`. 19:42 "because it was" → because,
  `t it was`.

Negatives and auxiliaries split off verbs:

- 19:8 "he was even more afraid" → μᾶλλον = even more, ἐφοβήθη = he was +
  afraid. 19:9 "gave no" → οὐκ = no, gave. 19:10 "Do You refuse to speak" →
  οὐ = refuse, λαλεῖς = Do You + to speak; "Do You not know" → not, Do You +
  know. 19:21 "Do not" + write → not, Do + write. 19:24 "Let us not tear it"
  → not, Let us + tear, it. 19:31 "would not remain" → not, would + remain.
  19:33 "they did not break" → not, they did + break. 19:36 "Not one" → Not;
  one + bones on the singular Ὀστοῦν.
- 19:28 "had now been accomplished" → ἤδη = now, had + been accomplished.
  19:33 "was already" / "dead" → already, was + dead. 19:41 "had yet been" →
  οὐδέπω = yet, had + been laid.

Articles and prepositions:

- "of the" on a lone article → the, of + noun: 19:3, 19:19 (title), 19:20,
  19:21 (three times), 19:32 (twice), 19:34, 19:38. 19:26 τῇ "to" and 19:27
  τῷ "to the" → His / the, with to on the noun.
- 19:2 "in a purple" → purple; ἱμάτιον = a + robe, περιέβαλον = dressed + in.
  19:41 "a new" → new, a + tomb. 19:31 a + Sabbath, High.
- 19:9 "You from" → You; Πόθεν = Where + from. 19:7 "Himself to be" →

- 19:17 "The Place" → τόν = The, τόπον = Place. 19:40 "the Jewish" →

- Every verse that names Pilate shows one unmatched pair: Πειλᾶτος = Pilate,
  "Same name, spelled differently in BSB's Greek text" (19:1, 4, 6, 8, 10,
  12, 13, 15, 19, 21, 22, 31, 38 twice), as in Luke 23 and John 18.
- 19:11 (one extra publisher word), 19:24 (two extra), 19:38 (one extra
  besides the two Pilate pairs): no English word lacks an N1904 owner, so no
  reading is claimed (probably αὐτῷ after ἀπεκρίθη; ἡ λέγουσα after
  πληρωθῇ; the article before ἀπὸ Ἁριμαθαίας).
- 19:23 and 19:32 show one differing pair each; the English is the same
  either way, so links are kept without a reading note (probably τέσσαρα and
  συσταυρωθέντος, spelling only).

## Notable decisions

- 19:1 / 19:16: τότε οὖν joined = Then (the English Then renders τότε; John
  11:14 left τότε `s` only because BSB wrote So there).
- 19:3: ὁ Βασιλεύς (nominative of address) article `s`; ἐδίδοσαν …
  ῥαπίσματα joined = slapping + in the face, αὐτῷ = Him.
- 19:10: the negative οὐ = refuse (BSB's "Do You refuse to speak"), with the
  verb keeping Do You + to speak; the repeated ἔχω `s`.
- 19:19: ἔγραψεν and its καί `s` (BSB compresses "wrote … and put" into "had
  … posted"); ἦν … γεγραμμένον = It read (Mark 15:26).
- 19:23: ἔλαβον and its καί `s` (BSB compresses "took … and made four
  parts"); ἐκ τῶν ἄνωθεν … δι’ ὅλου = from, top, in one piece + to bottom.
- 19:24: περὶ αὐτοῦ joined = it; τίνος = who, ἔσται = will get; μέν `s`
  (answered by δέ in 19:25).
- 19:25: τῆς μητρὸς αὐτοῦ joined = her; ἡ τοῦ Κλωπᾶ = the wife, of Clopas.
- 19:27: τὰ ἴδια = his, home (John 1:11 convention, article as possessive).
- 19:29: μεστόν (first) = of, μεστόν (second) = soaked + in, ὑσσώπῳ = a stalk
  of hyssop.
- 19:32: τοῦ συνσταυρωθέντος αὐτῷ = who had been crucified with, Jesus; BSB
  moves it to the first man. `t those`.
- 19:39: ἦλθεν … φέρων joined = brought; τὸ πρῶτον = previously.
- 19:42: διά and ὅτι joined = because (both give reasons); τῶν Ἰουδαίων =
  Jewish (John 11:55).

## Doubts for the peer

- 19:19 / 19:23: ἔγραψεν and ἔλαβον are `s`. Joining each with the next verb
  (had + posted; divided + into) is the alternative, as Mark 15:43 joins two
  verbs of motion; I did not, because "posted" and "divided" do not mean
  "wrote" or "took".
- 19:31: ἐκείνου `s` and `t next`; "next" could be read as BSB's rendering of
  "that (coming) Sabbath". αὐτῶν `s` with `t the bodies` is the other close
  call.
- 19:10: οὐ = refuse could instead be joined with λαλεῖς as one group (Do
  You refuse to speak).
- 19:29: "there" `t` from the publisher's "was sitting there"; it is close to
  a verb rendering of ἔκειτο and could stay with it.
- 19:15: one αὐτόν linked to all three "Him" (Luke 23:21); `t` for the two
  after Ἆρον is the alternative.
- 19:36: "one" with Ὀστοῦν (bones) rather than with οὐ; the publisher had
  "Not one" on οὐ.
