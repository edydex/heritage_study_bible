## Notable decisions

- Key terms linked word by word with no interpretive notes: καθαρίσωμεν =
  "let us cleanse", μολυσμοῦ = "that defiles", ἁγιωσύνην = "holiness",
  λύπη = "sorrow" (7:10, twice), λυπέω forms = "caused … sorrow" / "were
  made sorrowful" / "your sorrow" / "felt the sorrow", μετάνοια =
  "repentance", παράκλησις = "comfort"/"encouragement", ἐκδίκησιν =
  "vindication", ζῆλος = "zeal".
- Names linked: Μακεδονίαν = "Macedonia"; Τίτου = "of Titus" (7:6, 7:13)
  and "Titus" (7:14).
- 7:11 list: each ἀλλά is linked to its own "what" and each noun to its
  own English; ἀπολογίαν = "eagerness to clear yourselves" kept as one
  rendering of the noun.
- 7:3: the σύν of both infinitives is shown once, under συναποθανεῖν
  ("die together with"), where BSB places "together with"; συνζῆν =
  "live".
- Articles as possessives: 7:8 τῇ and ἡ = "my" (both "my letter").
- 7:14 ὅτι = "Indeed" (connective by another connective).

## Doubts for the peer

- 7:11 (hardest): ἀλλά = "what" for each item (the brief's pairing) and
  πόσην = "what" + "what". The alternative is πόσην understood again for
  every "what" with each ἀλλά `s`, or the first "what" `t`.
- 7:4: "my" placed on ὑπερπερισσεύομαι ("I overflow"), with τῇ `s`; the
  alternative is τῇ = "my" as a possessive article.
- 7:9: εἰς = "led + to" with "you" `t`, and κατά = "that + had intended"
  with Θεόν = "God"; the alternative is "that" `t` in both 7:9 and 7:10 /
  7:1 (kept with the recast word here for consistency).
- 7:8: ἐκείνη `s` with ἡ = "my"; the alternative is ἐκείνη `s` and ἡ `s`
  with "my" `t`. εἰ καί = "but only" joined; εἰ `s` with καί = "but only"
  is the alternative.
- 7:15: ὡς = "as" (publisher had "that"), with "that" `t`; this reads ὡς
  as introducing the welcome, not the content of the remembering.
- 7:5: ἐν παντί joined = "from every direction" instead of ἐν = "from".
- 7:3: συνζῆν = "live" with "we" and the last "you" `t`.
