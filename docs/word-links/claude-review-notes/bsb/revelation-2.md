# BSB Revelation 2: author review (Claude)

## Scope

All 29 verses (2:1–29) reviewed from the publisher rows. No verse was kept
from an earlier review; nothing was marked KEEP. Every publisher row was
checked, including the unchanged ones. 158 of 499 publisher rows changed.
Most changes are systematic: the lone-article rule, joining article +
participle units, and moving supplied words to `t`. Final check:
`{"versesOk": 29, "problems": 0}`.

The shared formulas follow the finished Revelation 3 review, so the seven
letters link the same way:

- **Address line** (2:1, 8, 12, 18): τῷ = "the", ἀγγέλῳ = "To + angel",
  τῆς = "the", ἐκκλησίας = "of + church", ἐν = "in", name, γράψον = "write".
  The opening Καί of 2:8, 12 and 18 is `s`.
- **Τάδε λέγει**: Τάδε = "These", λέγει = "the words of", and "are" is `t`
  (a supplied copula). The title after it is an article + participle unit
  ("Him who holds", "the One who holds"), or a bare article + noun ("the First",
  "the Son"), so "of" never sits on a lone ὁ.
- **Οἶδα … τὰ ἔργα σου**: Οἶδα = "I know", σου = "your", τά `s`.
- **Ὁ ἔχων οὖς ἀκουσάτω …** (2:7, 11, 17, 29): Ὁ ἔχων = "He who has",
  οὖς = "an ear", ἀκουσάτω = "let him hear", ταῖς = "the",
  ἐκκλησίαις = "to + churches".
- **The one who overcomes**: always joined. The dative Τῷ νικῶντι (2:7, 2:17)
  carries "To the one who overcomes", and its resumptive αὐτῷ is `s`. The
  nominative ὁ νικῶν in 2:26 is "the one who overcomes", and the resumptive
  αὐτῷ = "to" (as Rev 3:21).
- **Names** are linked despite spelling differences: Ephesus, Nicolaitans
  (2:6, 15), Smyrna, Satan (2:9, 13 ×2, 24), Pergamum, Antipas, Balaam,
  Balak, Israel (υἱῶν Ἰσραήλ = "Israelites"), Thyatira (2:18, 24), Jezebel.

## Corrected publisher rows (verse: what, why)

- **Lone articles** (2:1, 6, 7, 8, 11, 12, 14, 15, 17, 18, 19, 24, 29):
  "To the", "of the", "to the", "of the One who" and "than your" were moved
  off lone articles. The preposition now goes on its noun or on the article +
  participle unit. In 2:19, "than" goes on πρώτων (genitive of comparison).
- **Article + participle joined**: 2:1 ὁ κρατῶν and ὁ περιπατῶν; 2:2 τοὺς
  λέγοντας; 2:7, 11, 17, 29 Ὁ ἔχων; 2:7, 11, 17, 26 the "overcomes" unit;
  2:9 τῶν λεγόντων; 2:12 ὁ ἔχων; 2:17 ὁ λαμβάνων; 2:20 ἡ λέγουσα; 2:22 τοὺς
  μοιχεύοντας; 2:23 ὁ ἐραυνῶν; 2:26 ὁ τηρῶν. Where a second article +
  participle shares the first one's "who" (2:1 "and walks", 2:26 "and
  continues"), the English "and" is `t` in 2:1, because the Greek has no
  καί there.
- **2:2**: καί was linked to "I know". Now Οἶδα links to both "I know"s (the
  verb is understood again with the ὅτι clause), and the καί is `s`. The
  possessive σου after ὑπομονήν now carries the "your" of "perseverance"; the
  article before κόπον carries the "your" of "labor". καὶ οὐκ εἰσίν =
  "falsely" (οὐκ alone had it). ἑαυτούς no longer carries "to be": it is `s`,
  and "to be" is `t`.
- **2:3**: καί was "Without". Now οὐ = "Without" and καί is `s`. ὑπομονὴν
  ἔχεις is joined as "you have persevered", because "have" is the English
  perfect auxiliary, not ἔχω's "have". The supplied object "many things" is `t`.
- **2:4, 2:20**: ὅτι was "this" in 2:4, and ἔχω had "I have this" in 2:20. In
  both, "this" is now a supplied object (`t`) and ὅτι is `s` (BSB's colon).
  The same applies to 2:6, where ὅτι was "to your credit"; that phrase is now `t`.
- **2:5**: "you did" moved off ἔργα to `t`. The negative was split:
  μή = "not" and μετανοήσῃς = "you do + repent". ἐὰν μή is `s`, because BSB
  states the condition once.
- **2:9**: καί was "And I am aware of". Now Οἶδα also links to "I am aware of"
  and καί = "And". ἀλλά was "but are in fact". Now ἀλλά = "but", εἰσίν = "are",
  καὶ οὐκ = "falsely", and "in fact" is `t`.
- **2:10**: "for" moved off the number δέκα onto ἡμερῶν. φοβοῦ = "Do + fear",
  μή = "not". The added adverb "even" is `t`.
- **2:11**: ἀδικηθῇ was "will not be harmed". Now οὐ μή = "not" and
  ἀδικηθῇ = "will + be harmed".
- **2:13**: Σατανᾶ was "of Satan sits"; "sits" is now `t`. "to" moved off μου
  onto κρατεῖς ("have held fast to"). "when" is `t`.
- **2:14, 2:15**: ἐκεῖ was "some", ἔχεις was "of you", and σύ was "some of you".
  Now ἔχεις = "some of (you)", ἐκεῖ is `s`, and σύ = "you" in 2:15. "to" moved
  onto κρατοῦντας ("hold to"). In 2:14, φαγεῖν = "so + would eat", and "they"
  is `t` (an infinitive has no person, as in 2 Corinthians 4:4).
- **2:16**: δέ alone was "Otherwise". Now εἰ δὲ μή is joined as "Otherwise".
  "of" moved off μου onto στόματος.
- **2:17**: "a" now goes with its noun (ψῆφον = "a + stone", ὄνομα = "a + name").
  καί was "with". It is `s`, and "with" now goes on γεγραμμένον ("inscribed with").
  The other καί = "also" (it had been folded into δώσω). οὐδεὶς … εἰ μή =
  "only" (εἰ alone had it). οἶδεν = "known + to". "it" is `t`.
- **2:18**: ὀφθαλμούς carried "eyes are", and ὅμοιοι carried "are like". Now
  ὁ ἔχων … αὐτοῦ = "whose + are", and the second "are" is a supplied copula
  (`t`). φλόγα πυρός is joined as "a blazing fire".
- **2:19**: σου after ὑπομονήν was "your" for "love"; it is now the "your" of
  "perseverance". The article before ἀγάπην is "your" for love. "are" (in
  "are greater") is `t`.
- **2:20**: "By" moved off διδάσκει onto the καί between the two verbs. This is
  the "When" convention: BSB subordinates the first clause. διδάσκει =
  "her teaching".
- **2:21**: "Even though" moved from the opening καί to the καί that joins the
  two clauses (same convention). οὐ θέλει is joined as "she is unwilling".
- **2:22**: "of sickness" moved off κλίνην to `t`. εἰς was "will suffer"; now
  εἰς is `s` and "will suffer" is `t`, because the English verb has a
  different meaning from the understood βάλλω. ἐὰν μή is joined as "unless".
- **2:23**: ἐν θανάτῳ is joined as "dead".
- **2:24**: οὐκ was "do not"/"have not"; it is now "not", and "do"/"have" go
  on the verbs. ταύτην was "her"; it is now `s`, with "her" `t` (different
  meaning). οἵτινες was "and"; it is now `s`, with "and" `t`. ὡς λέγουσιν is
  joined as "so-called".
- **2:25**: "to" moved off ὃ onto κρατήσατε ("hold fast to"). ἄχρι οὗ ἄν is
  joined as "until".
- **2:26**: "in" moved off μου onto τηρῶν ("continues in").
- **2:27–28**: συντρίβεται was "and shatter them". It is now "shatter", with
  "and" and "them" `t`. ῥάβδῳ = "an + scepter". σκεύη … κεραμικά is joined as
  "pottery".

## Readings and verse division

- 2:5 πέπτωκες, 2:10 μή and 2:22 μετανοήσουσιν are where the EVIDENCE line
  shows the publisher's Greek differs. The publisher's words are probably
  πέπτωκας, μηδέν and μετανοήσωσιν. Each has the same meaning, so each is
  linked with a neutral note.
- 2:13, 2:19 and 2:25 report "differs" with 0/0 unmatched words. No reading
  is claimed there.
- 2:27/2:28 is a real verse-division difference. N1904 puts ὡς κἀγὼ εἴληφα
  παρὰ τοῦ Πατρός μου in 2:28, but BSB prints "just as I have received authority
  from My Father" at the end of 2:27. Those words are `m … > 2:27` in 2:28 and
  linked with `g @2:28` in 2:27. Their article stays `s` in 2:28, and
  "authority" is a supplied object (`t`).

## Doubts for the peer

1. **2:2 and 2:19 possessives.** I followed the article-as-possessive
   convention, and each σου goes on the "your" next to its own noun. The other
   option is the 1 Thessalonians 1:3 precedent, where one σου links to every
   "your" in the list.
2. **2:9 εἰσίν.** I linked it to "are" in "but are in fact a synagogue",
   because it is the verb of "not … but". In 2:2 the same three words are
   joined as "falsely", because no other English word is left for εἰσίν.
3. **2:20 "By" and 2:21 "Even though".** Both now sit on the inner καί, by the
   "When" convention; the publisher had them on the verb and the opening καί.
4. **2:10 "even unto".** I made "even" a `t` added adverb. Keeping
   "even unto" on ἄχρι would also be defensible.
5. **2:2 κακούς.** I kept "those who are evil" whole: the "are" is inside the
   rendering of a substantival adjective, not a clause copula.
6. **2:23 ἀποκτενῶ.** I kept it as "I will strike", with ἐν θανάτῳ = "dead"
   (close to the publisher), rather than joining all three words to
   "strike … dead".
