Verses reviewed: 39 of 40 (14:1-36, 14:38-40). 14:37 is a KEEP block
(already reviewed by Codex) and is copied unchanged. Final check:
`fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 40, "problems": 0}`.

138 of the 504 publisher `g` rows (excluding 14:37) changed, in 36 of the
39 verses. Many of these changes only join a TODO token into a unit or
split a negative off its verb. 14:14, 14:32 and 14:35 keep every publisher

Unchanged rows were checked too. Every TODO token is resolved, and so is
the one English TODO ("in", 14:16).

Readings: EVIDENCE shows the publisher's Greek differs from N1904 only in
14:6 and 14:16 (one extra publisher word in each). 14:6: no reading is
claimed. The extra word is probably ἐν before διδαχῇ; BSB does not
translate any ἐν in that list, so all three N1904 ἐν are `s`. 14:16: the
extra word is probably ἐν before πνεύματι. The bare dative already means
"in spirit", so "in + spirit" is linked to πνεύματι with a same-meaning
reading note (rule 9).

KEEP block 14:37: checked and no error found ("considers himself a prophet"
as one unit, "let him acknowledge" on the imperative, "is" on ἐστίν).

## Publisher rows corrected

Lone articles carrying a case word, copula or connective:

- 14:11 τῷ "to#0 the" → joined with λαλοῦντι as "to#0 the speaker"
  (article + participle). τῆς "of someone’s" → "someone’s" (the article
  rendered as a possessive), and "of" moves to φωνῆς ("of + language").
- 14:15 τῷ (4×) "with my" → "my" (the article used as a possessive, as in
  Romans 7:25). "with" goes on πνεύματι/νοΐ ("with#0 + spirit#0" etc.).
- 14:20 τῇ "In regard to" → `s`, and κακίᾳ = "In regard to evil". ταῖς
  "in your" → "your", and φρεσίν = "in + thinking#1".
- 14:21 τούτῳ "to#0 this" → "this"; λαῷ = "to#0 + people"; τῷ `s`.
- 14:22 τοῖς "for#0", "is for" (before πιστεύουσιν) → joined into the
  article + participle units "for#0 believers" and "for#2 believers". "is" is
  `t` (a supplied copula, not linked to the εἰσιν of the first clause).
  τοῖς "for#1", "for#3" before ἀπίστοις → `s`, with "for" on the adjective.
- 14:25 τά "and#0 the" → "the"; "and#0" `t` (no καί; it only joins the verse
  to 14:24). τῆς "of" → `s`, and καρδίας = "of + heart".
- 14:27 τό "at" → joined τὸ πλεῖστον = "at most".
- 14:33 τῶν "of#2 the" → "the#1", and ἁγίων = "of#2 + saints".

Wrong or over-claiming links:

- 14:1 ἵνα "the gift" → `s`. προφητεύητε "of prophecy" → "prophecy", and
  "the gift of" is `t` (a noun frame BSB adds for the ἵνα clause).
- 14:5 μᾶλλον "I#1 would rather" → "rather". θέλω is understood again in
  "I#1 would + have". ἵνα "have" → `s`. θέλω "I#0 wish that" → "I#0 wish",
  with "that" `t`. μείζων "is greater" → "greater", with "is" `t`.
- 14:7 δῷ "they are playing" was wrong. διαστολὴν … δῷ ("give a
  distinction") = "are#1 distinct". τὸ αὐλούμενον ἢ τὸ κιθαριζόμενον = "the#2
  tune + are#0 playing", with "they" `t` (a participle has no person). φωνὴν
  διδόντα = "instruments". ὅμως "Even in the case of" → "Even", with "in the
  case of" `t`. γνωσθήσεται "will anyone recognize" → "will + recognize",
  with "anyone" `t` (a passive rendered as an active).
- 14:9 καί "it is" → `s` (BSB has no "also"). ὑμεῖς "with#0 you" → "you#0",
  and "it is with" is `t`. ἔσεσθε "You will just be" → "You will + be", with
  "just" `t`. γνωσθήσεται: "anyone" `t`, as in 14:7. τὸ λαλούμενον = "what +
  are saying", with "you#2" `t`.
- 14:10 οὐδέν "none of them is" → "none"; "of them" and "is" are `t`.
- 14:11 ὁ λαλῶν "he is" → "he" (unit), with "is" `t`.
- 14:12 οὕτως "It is the same" → "the#0 same", with "It is" `t`. ὑμεῖς
  "with you" → "you#0", with "with" `t`. ζηλωταί "eager to have" → "eager",
  with "to#0 have" `t`. τήν "in gifts that" was wrong: πρὸς … οἰκοδομήν = "that
  build up", with "in gifts" `t` and τήν `s`.
- 14:15 and 14:26 ἐστιν "shall I do" / "shall we say" → "shall + do" /
  "shall + say", with "I#0" / "we" `t` (impersonal ἐστιν, as in Acts 21:22).
  14:15 "also" moves from the verbs to the two καί.
- 14:16 ὁ "can someone" was wrong: ὁ ἀναπληρῶν τὸν τόπον = "someone who is",
  ἰδιώτου = "uninstructed", and "can" goes on ἐρεῖ ("can + say").
- 14:17 σύ "You may" → "You"; "may" goes on the concessive μέν (see the
  doubts below). καλῶς "well enough" → "well", with "enough" `t`.
- 14:18 λαλῶ "that I speak" → "I#1 speak", with "that" `t`.
- 14:21 οὐδ’ "even" → "even + not" (the "not" was on the verb).
- 14:22 ἀλλά (second) "however" → `s` (BSB reverses the two phrases), and
  δέ = "however" (it was TODO).
- 14:23 ἰδιῶται "some#0 who are uninstructed" → "some#0 + uninstructed",
  with "who are" `t`.
- 14:24 ἀνακρίνεται "and called to account" → "called to account", with
  "and" `t` (asyndeton). The first ὑπὸ πάντων is `s` because BSB's one "by
  all" covers both verbs.
- 14:25 πεσών "he will fall" → "fall". The person and "will" go on the

- 14:26 ἔχει "or#0"/"or#1" was wrong: the repeated ἔχει are `s` (BSB's one

- 14:28 λαλείτω "speak only" → "speak", with "only" `t`.
- 14:29 διακρινέτωσαν "should#1 weigh carefully what is said" → without

- 14:30 "who" moves from ἄλλῳ to the participle ("who is seated").

- 14:33 ἐστιν "God#0 is" → "is". Θεός = "God#0 + a God" (understood again),

- 14:38 ἀγνοεῖ "ignores this" → "ignores", with "this" `t`. ἀγνοεῖται "he

## Notable decisions

- Tongues: every bare dative γλώσσῃ/γλώσσαις carries "in" ("in a tongue",
  "in tongues"). 14:19 has a real ἐν, which keeps "in#1". 14:26 γλῶσσαν = "a
  tongue".
- προφητεύω, οἰκοδομέω/οἰκοδομή, ἑρμηνεία/διερμηνεύω, ἰδιώτης, ἄπιστος and
  ἀποκάλυψις are linked word by word, with notes only for real shifts
  (14:3 "for + edification", 14:26 "build up", 14:30 "a revelation comes").
- 14:6 λαλήσω = "I#2 bring" and 14:2 ἀκούει = "understands" stay linked,
  with notes (dynamic renderings of the same act, like 1 Corinthians 1:16
  "remember").
- 14:16 "someone who is" renders ὁ ἀναπληρῶν τὸν τόπον ("the one who fills
  the place"). "who is" is on the participle phrase, not a supplied copula.

## Doubts for the peer

- 14:17 μέν = "may". "You may be giving thanks well enough, but" is BSB's
  concession, which μέν … ἀλλά marks. The verb is indicative. Putting "may" on
  εὐχαριστεῖς (the modal-on-finite-verb rule) is the alternative. I chose
  μέν because "may" adds no mood to the verb itself.
- 14:5 θέλω understood again = "I#1 would + have" and ἵνα `s`. The
  alternative is "would rather" on μᾶλλον, with "I#1" and "have" `t`.
- 14:12 πρὸς … οἰκοδομήν = "that build up" with "in gifts" `t`. BSB recasts
  "abound for the building up of the church" heavily, so the peer may prefer
  πρός `s`.
- 14:10 εἰ τύχοι ("it may be, perhaps") = "Assuredly". This is linked as the
  idiom's contextual sense (compare ESV "doubtless"). It could instead be
  `s`/`t` if the peer reads "Assuredly" as a different meaning.
- 14:7 τὸ αὐλούμενον ἢ τὸ κιθαριζόμενον is one group ("the tune … are
  playing") because BSB collapses "what is piped or harped" into one phrase.
- 14:29 "weigh carefully" is kept whole on διακρινέτωσαν. "carefully"
  renders the δια- sense of discernment, not an added adverb.
- 14:20 μὴ … γίνεσθε joined as "stop", as opposed to μή = "stop" with
  γίνεσθε `s`.
