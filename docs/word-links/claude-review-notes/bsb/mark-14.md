## Publisher rows corrected

"When" or "While" had been put on καί or on εὐθύς. The genitive absolute now
carries it, and the connective is `s`:
14:3 ὄντος → While + was; 14:17 γενομένης → When + came; 14:18 ἀνακειμένων →
while + were reclining (καί keeps And); 14:22 ἐσθιόντων → While + were eating,
αὐτῶν → they; 14:43 λαλοῦντος → While + was + speaking, ἔτι → still,
αὐτοῦ → Jesus, and εὐθύς (which BSB leaves out) is `s`; 14:66 ὄντος → While + was.
In 14:41 "When" moves from the opening καί to the καί between "comes" and "says".

Supplied "and" joining a participle to the main verb (now `t`): 14:1 (and kill),
14:3 (and poured), 14:22 (and broke), 14:23 (and gave), 14:39, 14:40, 14:47,
14:50, 14:52, 14:57, 14:60, 14:63, 14:67, 14:69, 14:72. In 14:15 "and ready"
→ ready, with "and" `t` (the Greek is asyndetic).

## Doubts for the peer

- 14:2: γάρ → But. BSB's only connective is "But"; the alternative is `s` γάρ
  with `t But`, which would be a split pair.
- 14:4: ἦσαν → "those present" reads ἦσαν as existential ("there were some").
  The alternative is the periphrastic reading: ἦσαν ἀγανακτοῦντες → expressed
  their indignation, with "of those present" `t`.
- 14:11: πῶς and εὐκαίρως are joined to "an opportunity". The alternative is
  εὐκαίρως → an opportunity with πῶς `s`.
- 14:30: σήμερον ("today") → very. The alternative is `s` σήμερον with
  `t very` (Matthew 26:34 has `t very`, but its Greek has no σήμερον).
- 14:67: the publisher's swap is kept: τὸν Πέτρον → him (object of saw) and
  αὐτῷ → Peter (object of looked at), following BSB's sentence structure.
- 14:45: ἐλθών is `s`; it could instead be joined with προσελθών → Going.
- 14:66 "came down" and 14:31 "Even if": I made "down" and "Even" `t` because the
  Greek has only ἔρχεται and ἐάν. Keeping them on the verb and on ἐάν is also
  defensible.
- 14:21: ἐκείνῳ keeps the publisher's "to that" and τῷ is `s`; Matthew 26:24 puts
  "to" on τῷ instead.
- 14:8: εἰς → "of" in "in advance of My burial" and τόν → My are kept from the
  publisher; both are loose.
