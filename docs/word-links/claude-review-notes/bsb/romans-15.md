# BSB Romans 15: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: 32 (15:1-26, 15:28-33). Kept unchanged: 15:27 (KEEP block,
already reviewed by Codex). Final check: `fromtext` →
`{"versesOk": 33, "problems": 0}`.

91 of the 437 publisher `g` rows changed, in 30 verses (many are TODO tokens
joined into an existing row). Every publisher row was checked, including the
two verses whose rows stay as they were apart from resolved TODOs (15:10,
15:11). No "of the"/"to the"/"in the"/"among the"/"for" sits on a lone
article. Articles with no English "the" are `s` with the standard reason.

Five verses have EVIDENCE lines showing different publisher Greek:

- 15:7: BSB's "you" (Christ accepted you) follows a different pronoun,
  probably ὑμᾶς; N1904 ἡμᾶς is `s` and "you" `t`, both with the reading reason.
- 15:15: probably τολμηρότερον for τολμηροτέρως (and perhaps ὑπό for the
  second ἀπό); "bold" is linked with a hedged same-meaning note, and the
  second ἀπό is `s` because BSB recasts the passive with God as subject.
- 15:19: BSB's "Spirit of God" follows a reading with Θεοῦ where N1904 has
  Ἁγίου; Ἁγίου is `s` and "of God" `t`.
- 15:26: ηὐδόκησαν linked to "were pleased" with a same-word spelling note
  (probably εὐδόκησαν).
- 15:4 also shows one differing word, probably the second διά missing in
  BSB's Greek; its `s` reason ("one through covers both phrases") stands
  either way, so no reading is claimed in the review file.

Conventions followed: Romans 1:5 and 4 (supplied verb or relative frame for
a genitive is `t`), Romans 1:11 and 1:20 (εἰς τό + infinitive joined),
Romans 9:22 (supplied possessive `t`), Romans 5:5 and 9:1 ("the Holy" on
Ἁγίου), Galatians 6:1 (article rendered "who", "are" `t`), Matthew 11:28
(article in an address rendered "you"), 1 Thessalonians 5:23 and
2 Thessalonians 3:16 (optative "may" on the verb; wishing "be" `t`),
Colossians 4:8 and Ephesians 6:3 (one ἵνα linked to a repeated "that"),
Luke 11:1 (ἐν τῷ + infinitive joined), the in-progress Romans 12:16
(τὸ αὐτὸ φρονεῖν joined), and the brief's rules on participles (no person),
negatives, supplied copulas, objects and adverbs.

## Publisher rows corrected

Lone articles carrying a preposition, copula or modal:

- 15:1 οἱ "who are" → "who", "are" `t`; τῶν "of the" → "the", ἀδυνάτων =
  "of + weak".
- 15:4 τῶν "of the" → "the", γραφῶν = "of + Scriptures".
- 15:5 and 15:13 ὁ "may the" → "the"; the optatives δῴη and πληρώσαι carry
  "may + grant" / "may + fill".
- 15:8 τῶν "to the" → "the", πατέρων = "to + patriarchs"; "made" `t`.
- 15:9 τῷ "to" → `s`, ὀνόματι = "to + name".
- 15:16 τό "of the" → "the", εὐαγγέλιον = "of + gospel".
- 15:26 τῶν "among the" → "the", ἁγίων = "among + saints".
- 15:30 τοῦ "of the" → "the", Πνεύματος = "of + Spirit".
- 15:31 τοῖς "to the" → "the", ἁγίοις = "to + saints".
- 15:3 τῶν "of those who" joined with ὀνειδιζόντων; 15:12 ὁ "One who"
  joined with ἀνιστάμενος; 15:31 τῶν joined with ἀπειθούντων (article +
  participle units).
- 15:12 Ἰεσσαί "of Jesse": the case word moves to τοῦ (indeclinable name).

Negatives split from verbs: 15:3 οὐχ "not" / "did + please"; 15:18 οὐ
"not" / "I will + presume"; 15:20 οὐχ and μή; 15:21 both οὐκ.

Person and modal words: participles lose supplied "I" (15:20
φιλοτιμούμενον, 15:23 ἔχων twice, 15:28 ἐπιτελέσας, 15:29 ἐρχόμενος, all
`t`); 15:32 "I may" moves from the participle ἐλθών to the finite
συναναπαύσωμαι, and "and" there is `t`; 15:14 "you" moves from αὐτοί to
ἐστε ("you + are"), αὐτοί = "yourselves"; 15:24 ὑμῶν (agent) = "you", and
"can", "me" and "my" are `t`; 15:13 "you" and "in Him" with the infinitive
are `t`.

Supplied English taken off Greek words: 15:2 "him" (build him up); 15:6 ἐν
"and" → `s`, "and" `t`; 15:7 "bring"; 15:8 "you" and "that" after λέγω;
15:9 "His" (for His mercy); 15:5 "who gives" (relative frame for the
genitive); 15:16 "to God" after εὐπρόσδεκτος; 15:18 "in leading" (εἰς now
"to", ὑπακοήν "obedience"); 15:19 "and" before the second "by"; 15:23 "that"
in "now that"; 15:25 and 15:31 "there"; 15:28 "service" after τοῦτο;
15:30 "my" (my struggle); 15:31 "Pray"; 15:33 "be".

Other corrections:

- 15:5 τὸ αὐτὸ φρονεῖν joined as "harmony".
- 15:13 "of" moved from Ἁγίου to Πνεύματος ("of + Spirit", "the Holy").
- 15:14 "with" moved from πάσης to γνώσεως; πάσης `s` (BSB's "brimming"
  carries the fullness).
- 15:17 ἔχω … τὴν καύχησιν joined as "I exult".
- 15:19 καί "all the way" → `s`; "all the way … to" now on μέχρι ("as far as").
- 15:22 τὰ πολλά = "often" (was folded into the verb); τοῦ ἐλθεῖν joined.
- 15:23 μηκέτι "there are no further" → "no further"; ἔχων = "there are";
  ἐπιποθίαν … ἔχων = "since + have longed"; τοῦ ἐλθεῖν πρός = "to visit".
- 15:24 ὡς ἂν πορεύωμαι + διαπορευόμενος = "on my way"; προπεμφθῆναι =
  "equip + for + journey"; ἐκεῖ (was "for my journey") → `s`; ἐὰν … πρῶτον =
  "after"; ὑμῶν = "your company" (was folded into ἐμπλησθῶ).

## Notable decisions

- 15:9 δέ keeps the publisher's "so that": BSB renders the connective that
  continues the εἰς τό purpose of 15:8 with a different connective.
- 15:11 τά and οἱ in the address keep "you" (Matthew 11:28).
- 15:17 τά (used as a pronoun, "the things") keeps "in my service".
- 15:18 the second οὐ keeps "except" (the double negative "not … anything
  which Christ did not accomplish" recast).
- 15:6 ὁμοθυμαδόν keeps "with one mind"; ἐν, whose "with" BSB shares, is `s`.

## Doubts for the peer

- 15:9: whether "so that" should instead go on δοξάσαι ("so that + may
  glorify") with δέ `s`.
- 15:23: whether "for me" should join ἔχων with "there are" rather than be
  `t` (I followed the no-person rule for participles).
- 15:24: the four-token "on my way" group and "for … journey" on
  προπεμφθῆναι (rather than on ἐκεῖ).
- 15:30: whether "my" (my struggle) belongs with μοι rather than `t`.
- 15:27 (KEEP, unchanged): "They were pleased to do it" folds the supplied
  "to do it", and "they owe it" folds a supplied "it", into Greek verbs; by
  this chapter's conventions both would be `t`. Not changed.
