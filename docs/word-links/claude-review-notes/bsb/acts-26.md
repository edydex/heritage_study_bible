# BSB Acts 26: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: all 32 (26:1-32). No KEEP blocks in this chapter. Final
check: `fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 32, "problems": 0}`.

110 of the 494 publisher `g` rows changed, in 29 verses (26:15, 26:27 and
26:31 keep every publisher row). About 17 of them only gained a token or
were joined with the English unchanged or nearly so: ὧν ἐγκαλοῦμαι = the
accusations (26:2); κατὰ Ἰουδαίους = Jewish (26:3); ἐναντία πρός = to oppose
(26:9); ὑπὲρ … λαμπρότητα = brighter than (26:13); εἰς τοῦτο = to (26:16);
τοῦ + infinitive = so that they may turn / that … may receive (26:18, Acts
10:25); article + participle (26:18 those sanctified, 26:29 who hear, 26:30
those seated with); ἕνεκα τούτων = For this reason (26:21); ἀπὸ τοῦ Θεοῦ =
God's (26:22); εὐξαίμην ἄν = I wish (26:29, as Acts 8:31); ἐν ὀλίγῳ / ἐν
μεγάλῳ = Short time / long, τοιούτους ὁποῖος = what (26:29). The rest were
corrected, split or moved.

The Damascus-road verses follow reviewed Acts 9:3-6 and Acts 22:6-10 (peer
text): 26:13 τοὺς σὺν ἐμοὶ πορευομένους = my companions (22:9 pattern, ἐμοί =
my); 26:14 τῇ Ἑβραΐδι διαλέκτῳ = in Hebrew (22:2), "to the ground", "a voice",
"say", "Saul, Saul", "why do you persecute Me" as 9:4 / 22:7; 26:15 = 22:8
(ἐγώ = I, εἶπα = asked, εἶ = are You, Κύριε = Lord, δέ `s`); 26:16 ἀνάστηθι =
get up (9:6).

## Publisher rows corrected

Lone articles carrying a preposition or other word (article now "the", a
possessive or `s`; the preposition goes on the noun, all nouns declinable):
26:6 τῆς "in the" (in → ἐπαγγελίας); 26:10 τῶν "of the"; 26:12 τῆς "of"
(now "the" before authority, "of" on ἀρχιερέων); 26:14 τῇ "in" (now `s`,
"in Hebrew" on Ἑβραΐδι διαλέκτῳ); 26:18 τῆς "from the" (the ἀπό of darkness
is understood again: ἀπό = from + from, as 9:15 ἐνώπιον); 26:19 τῇ "to the";
26:20 τήν "in the", τοῖς "to the", τῆς "of their" (now "their"); 26:23 τῷ
"to" (now the possessive "our", "to" on λαῷ) and τοῖς "to the"; 26:32 τῷ
"to" (now on Φήστῳ).

English-only words folded into a Greek word (now `t`):

- "and" joining a participle or genitive absolute to the main verb with no
  καί there: 26:1 (and began), 26:5 (and can testify), 26:11 (and I tried),
  26:14 (and I heard; genitive absolute, as Acts 12:13), 26:21 (and tried),
  26:22 (and I stand).
- Supplied subjects of participles, the person coming from a finite verb
  elsewhere (Acts 23:27-30 rule): 26:5 "They" (from ἴσασι, 26:4); 26:11 "I"
  (τιμωρῶν) and "my" (ἐμμαινόμενος); 26:12 "I" (πορευόμενος; εἶδον is in
  26:13); 26:13 "as I was" (κατὰ τὴν ὁδόν); 26:17 "I" (ἐξαιρούμενος; ὤφθην is
  in 26:16); 26:22 "I" before have had (τυχών) and before am saying (λέγων).
  Each finite verb keeps its own "I".
- 26:9 "I" before ought: δεῖν is impersonal (Acts 5:29, 20:35); "I could" of
  "all I could" (πολλά = all).
- Supplied "that" before an infinitive (Acts 4:32, 10:28, 23 note): 26:9,
  26:20, 26:26, 26:29 (the publisher had 26:29 "that" on ἄν).
- Supplied objects and possessives: 26:3 "you" (I beg you); 26:6 "my" hope
  (as Acts 23:6); 26:7 "God" (serve God); 26:10 "my" vote, "them" (against
  them); 26:11 "them" (persecute them).
- Supplied frames: 26:7 "It is … that" (cleft, as Acts 23:6); 26:10 "is what"
  (cleft; ὅ = that, as John 19:24); 26:14 "It is" hard; 26:22 "here"; 26:28
  "said" and 26:29 "replied" (no verb of speaking in the Greek); 26:20
  "then" in "and then" (καί = and); 26:13 "About" (no περί, unlike 22:6).

Other corrections:

- 26:3 τε of τε καί was on "and"; now `s`, καί = and (no "both"). "since" moved
  from σε to the participle ὄντα.
- 26:4 βίωσίν μου: μου = I (the pronoun takes a new role), βίωσιν = how … have
  lived; "the" with Ἰουδαῖοι.
- 26:5 "as" moved from ἔζησα to Φαρισαῖος (as a Pharisee); "of" from
  ἡμετέρας to θρησκείας.
- 26:7 εἰς was "the promise"; now the relative ἥν = the promise, εἰς `s`
  (goes with καταντῆσαι = to realize); ἐν ἐκτενείᾳ = earnestly (was inside
  "as they earnestly serve God").
- 26:8 "would" moved from τί to κρίνεται; παρ’ ὑμῖν joined = any of you.
- 26:10 "when" moved from αὐτῶν to the genitive absolute ἀναιρουμένων;
  ψῆφον = vote split from κατήνεγκα (= I cast + against).
- 26:11 πολλάκις = frequently split from τιμωρῶν; "I" moved from καί to the
  finite ἐδίωκον (καί = even); αὐτούς = them + them (object of both τιμωρῶν
  and ἠνάγκαζον); περισσῶς = raging.
- 26:21 με = me + me (object of both συλλαβόμενοι and διαχειρίσασθαι).
- 26:23 second εἰ was "and as the"; now `s`, "and" `t` (εἰ "that" is not
  "and"), "as the" on πρῶτος.
- 26:24 "in" moved from μεγάλῃ to φωνῇ (in a + voice).
- 26:25 οὐ = not split from μαίνομαι.
- 26:26 "notice" moved from αὐτόν to λανθάνειν (λανθάνω = escape notice);
  οὐ … οὐθέν joined = none.
- 26:32 μή = not split from ἐπεκέκλητο.

## Notable decisions

- 26:4 μὲν οὖν: οὖν = Surely, μέν `s` (Acts 14:3, 19:38 pattern); 26:9 οὖν =
  So then. ἐκ νεότητος and ἀπ’ ἀρχῆς are merged by BSB: publisher ἀπ’ = from
  kept, ἀρχῆς = the earliest days, νεότητος = of + youth, ἐκ `s`, the
  attributive τὴν … γενομένην `s`, "my" (youth) `t`.
- 26:10 and 26:16 τε … τε: first τε `s`, second τε = and. 26:20 τε … καί
  `s` + and; πᾶσάν τε … τε = then (connective). 26:22 μικρῷ τε καὶ μεγάλῳ:
  τε = alike (kept). 26:23 τῷ τε λαῷ καί: τε `s`.
- 26:22 οὖν = But and 26:10 adverbial καί = And kept as connectives rendered
  by another connective.
- 26:20 τοῖς ἐν Δαμασκῷ: the substantival article keeps "to those" (it is the
  only word carrying the dative; not a lone article of a noun).
- 26:12 τῆς (attributive article of ἐπιτροπῆς) = "the" before authority, not
  `s`.
- Infinitive subjects stay with the infinitive (Acts 17:26-27): 26:18 "they
  may turn", 26:20 "they should repent".
- Readings: EVIDENCE shows publisher differences only in 26:4, 26:7, 26:21,
  26:26 and 26:31; none changes the English, and no reading is claimed.

## Doubts for the peer

- 26:9 ἐμαυτῷ: the publisher linked "too" to it. I joined ἔδοξα ἐμαυτῷ ("I
  thought to myself") = was convinced and made "too" `t`; "I too" may be BSB's
  way of rendering the emphatic ἐγὼ … ἐμαυτῷ ("I myself"), in which case the
  publisher link is the alternative.
- 26:2 μέλλων `s` and "to stand" `t` (publisher had μέλλων = to stand). Joining
  μέλλων with ἀπολογεῖσθαι = to defend myself is the other option.
- 26:11 ἕως = went (publisher) kept: BSB recasts "persecuted them as far as
  foreign cities" as "went to foreign cities to persecute them". `t` for
  "went" with ἕως `s` (or ἕως … εἰς = to) is arguable.
- 26:30 ἥ τε Βερνίκη: τε = along with kept as the connective joining Bernice
  and the others to the king and governor; read as τε … καί with the next
  καί, it would be `s` and "along with" `t`.
- 26:13 ἡμέρας μέσης = noon with "About" `t`; linking "About" as the
  genitive-of-time word is possible.
- 26:24 ταῦτα = At this stage, αὐτοῦ = of Paul's, ἀπολογουμένου = defense kept
  from the publisher (a recast genitive absolute).
- 26:16 με = from Me kept (publisher); the Greek is "seen me".
