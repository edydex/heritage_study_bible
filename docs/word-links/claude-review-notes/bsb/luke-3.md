# BSB Luke 3: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: 38 (3:1-38). No verse was kept from an earlier review (the
draft had no KEEP blocks). Final check: `fromtext` →
`{"versesOk": 38, "problems": 0}`.

47 of the 484 publisher `g` rows changed, in 18 verses. 6 of them only gained
unlinked Greek tokens, with the English unchanged: ἔσται εἰς = shall be made
(3:5), ἤδη … κεῖται = lies ready (3:9, as Matthew 3:10), τὸ διατεταγμένον =
are authorized (3:13), μή ποτε = if (3:15), the resumptive οὗ … αὐτοῦ = whose
(3:16, as Mark 1:7), and both περί = regarding (3:19). The other 41 rows were
corrected, split or moved. Matthew 3 and Mark 1 were followed for 3:1-22.

## Publisher rows corrected

Names and words supplied by English that had been folded into a Greek word:

- 3:7, 3:11, 3:18: "John" sat on Ἔλεγεν, ἀποκριθείς and εὐηγγελίζετο; now `t John`.
- 3:20: "Herod added" → added; `t Herod`.
- 3:17: πτύον "winnowing fork is" → winnowing fork; `t is`.
- 3:23: ἀρχόμενος "when He began His ministry" → when He began; `t His ministry`.

"While", "When" and "as" on the wrong word:

- 3:1: Ποντίου carried "while". Now the genitive absolute ἡγεμονεύοντος = while + was governor.
- 3:21: δέ carried "When". Now ἐν τῷ (with the infinitive) = When, and Ἐγένετο δέ is `s`.
- 3:19: ἐλεγχόμενος "when he rebuked" → when + rebuked; ὑπ’ αὐτοῦ ("by him") = he, since BSB makes the agent of the passive the subject.

Negatives and auxiliaries:

- 3:8: μή "do not" → not; ἄρξησθε = do + begin.
- 3:9: ποιοῦν "that does not produce" → that does + produce; μή = not.
- 3:14: διασείσητε "Do not take money by force" → Do + take money by force; Μηδένα ("no one") = not.
- 3:14: ποιήσωμεν "should we do" → should + do; the explicit ἡμεῖς = we (rule 4).
- 3:15: πάντων "were all" → all; διαλογιζομένων = were + wondering.

Prepositions and "the" on a lone article:

- 3:1: τῆς "of the" → the; ἡγεμονίας = of + reign.
- 3:7: τοῖς "to the" → the; ὄχλοις = to + crowds.
- 3:9: τῶν "of the" → the; δένδρων = of + trees.
- 3:16: τῶν "of" → `s`; ὑποδημάτων = of + sandals.
- 3:4: προφήτου "the prophet" → prophet; τοῦ = the.

Other words on the wrong Greek word:

- 3:11: article + participle joined (rule 3): Ὁ ἔχων = Whoever has, τῷ … ἔχοντι = him who has (μή = none), ὁ ἔχων = whoever has. ἀποκριθεὶς … ἔλεγεν = replied.
- 3:13: εἶπεν "he answered" → answered; ὁ (with δέ) = he. πλέον "more than" → more; παρά = than.
- 3:17: ἀσβέστῳ "with unquenchable" → unquenchable; πυρί = with + fire.
- 3:18: μέν carried "With these and". Now παρακαλῶν = With + exhortations, καί = and, `t these`; μὲν οὖν is `s`.
- 3:20: κατέκλεισεν "He locked" → He locked + up.
- 3:22: σωματικῷ "in a bodily" → bodily; εἴδει = in a + form ("a" goes with the noun).

## Notable decisions

- Genealogy (3:23-38): the publisher's pattern was checked once and applied to
  every pair: τοῦ = "the son", the following name = "of Name". In τοῦ Ἡλί the
  article is the substantive "the (son)" in apposition to the previous name,
  and the genitive name gives "of", as in τὸν τοῦ Ἀλφαίου = son (Mark 2:14,
  3:18; Matthew 10:2-3). A note on the first τοῦ of each verse says so.
- Spelling differences linked as the same name with a note (rule 8; they
  account for every EVIDENCE difference except 3:5 and the extra καί in 3:20):
  Πειλάτου (3:1), Ἰωάνην/Ἰωάνου/Ἰωάνης (3:2, 3:15, 3:16, 3:20), Ἡλεί (3:23),
  Ματθάτ, Λευεί, Μελχεί (3:24), Ἐσλεί (3:25), Σεμεείν (3:26), Νηρεί (3:27),
  Μελχεί, Ἀδδεί (3:28), Ἰωρείμ, Λευεί (3:29), Ἐλιακείμ (3:30), Ἀδμείν, Ἀρνεί
  (3:33). Ἰησοῦ in 3:29 = Joshua, with a note.
- 3:5: the publisher's Greek has singular εὐθεῖαν; "straight" is linked to
  N1904's εὐθείας with a note (same meaning). The second εἰς (… εἰς ὁδοὺς
  λείας) is `s` because English leaves "shall be made" understood.
- 3:20: the publisher's extra καί before κατέκλεισεν has no English word, so
  nothing is claimed for it.
- 3:15: one "John" is linked to both Ἰωάνου (in περὶ τοῦ Ἰωάνου) and the
  pronoun αὐτός of the question; περί is `s`.
- 3:14: the καί of καὶ ἡμεῖς ("we too") is linked to the opening "And" of the
  soldiers' question, as the publisher did; the earlier καί ("also") is `s`.
- Kept publisher rows that are free renderings but not wrong: οὖν = and (3:9,
  as Matthew 3:10), Πατέρα = as our father and ἔχομεν = We have (3:8), ὁ = One
  (3:16), ἀρχιερέως = the high priesthood (3:2), πᾶσιν = them all (3:20),
  "with#2 fire" on πυρί (3:16).

## Doubts for the peer

- Genealogy: the brief says a lone article never carries a noun, but here τοῦ
  is the only Greek word for each "the son". I kept the publisher's
  τοῦ = the son. The alternative is τοῦ = the son of + name alone, which
  would break the publisher's pattern in all 76 pairs.
- 3:23: ὢν = "He was", ὡς = "as", ἐνομίζετο = "regarded" were kept from the
  publisher. In English "was" is the passive auxiliary of "regarded", so
  ἐνομίζετο = was + regarded with ὢν = He is a defensible alternative.
- 3:18: καί = "and" assumes BSB's "these and many other" renders the
  adverbial καί ("also"). Otherwise the καί of πολλὰ καὶ ἕτερα is idiomatic
  ("many other"), so `s` for καί and `t these and` would be the alternative.
- 3:15: joining Ἰωάνου and αὐτός to one "John" could instead be
  Ἰωάνου = John with αὐτός `s`.
- 3:5: the second εἰς is `s`. Joining it with λείας ("become smooth") is the
  alternative.
