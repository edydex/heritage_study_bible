## Notable decisions

- Key terms are linked word by word: σκῆνος = "tent" (5:1, 5:4),
  οἰκοδομή = "a building", οἰκία = "house" (and "live in" for ἡμῶν οἰκία
  in 5:1, kept from the publisher), ἀρραβών = "as a pledge", ἐνδημέω = "are
  at home" (5:6), "at home" (5:8), "are here" (5:9), ἐκδημέω = "are away"
  (5:6), "to be away" (5:8), "away from" (5:9), βῆμα = "judgment seat",
  καταλλάσσω = "reconciled", "reconciling", "Be reconciled", καταλλαγή =
  "of reconciliation" (5:18, 5:19), πρεσβεύομεν = "we are ambassadors",
  κτίσις = "creation".
- 5:21 is linked word by word with only factual notes: τὸν … γνόντα =
  "Him who + knew", μή = "no", ἁμαρτίαν = "sin" (twice), ὑπέρ = "on +
  behalf", ἡμῶν = "our", ἐποίησεν = "made", with "God" and "to be" `t`.
- Possessives inside BSB's periphrasis for one verb stay with the verb, as
  in 2 Corinthians 1:22: 5:4 βαρούμενοι = "under our burdens", 5:10
  κομίσηται = "may receive his due", 5:13 ἐξέστημεν = "we are out of our
  mind", 5:20 παρακαλοῦντος = "were making His appeal".
- Connectives rendered by other connectives are linked: 5:1 γάρ = "Now",
  5:4 καὶ γάρ = "So", 5:6 καί = "although", 5:8 δέ = "then". γάρ in 5:13,
  δέ in 5:11 (first) and 5:18, ἀλλά and νῦν in 5:16, and καί in 5:3 and 5:9
  are `s` (no English word).
- 5:1: the publisher's "the" stays on τοῦ σκήνους ("of the tent", BSB's
  "tent"); ἡ is `s`.
- 5:4 τῷ σκήνει = "this tent" (τῷ = "this") and 5:4 τό = "our" (article
  as possessive, "our mortality") are kept.
- 5:10: τά is `s`; BSB gives "the things" once, on the relative ἅ.
- 5:11: πεφανερώμεθα = "What we are is clear" is kept as BSB's recast of
  one finite verb; φόβον = "to fear" (noun as verb).
- 5:19: αὐτῶν = "men's" (pronoun rendered by the noun it stands for).

## Doubts for the peer

- 5:2 (hardest): τούτῳ = "this" with "tent" `t`. The publisher had "this
  tent" on τούτῳ, like the "this man" rows for οὗτος elsewhere. I split it
  because "tent" is a specific noun brought in from verse 1 and σκῆνος is
  a key term. In 5:5 "this very purpose" stays one unit because εἰς αὐτὸ
  τοῦτο is a fixed phrase with reviewed precedent.
- 5:10: κομίσηται = "may receive his due" with τά `s`. Alternatives:
  "his" `t` (supplied possessive), or τά = "the things" with ἅ `s`.
- 5:9: ἐνδημοῦντες = "are + here" with "we" and "in this body" `t`;
  ἐκδημοῦντες = "away from" with "it" `t`. The alternative is "here" `t`
  as an added place word, with ἐνδημοῦντες = "are" only.
- 5:4: καὶ γάρ = "So" (a connective rendered by another connective, even
  though "So" reads as result); `s` + `t` is the alternative.
- 5:16: νῦν (the second one) `s`; joining νῦν οὐκέτι = "no longer" is the
  alternative. "once" `t` follows the brief's rule for added adverbs.
- 5:5: ὁ δούς = "has given" with "and" `t`, by analogy with an "and" that
  only joins a participle clause; the publisher had "and has given".
