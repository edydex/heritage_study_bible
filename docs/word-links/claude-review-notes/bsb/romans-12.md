## Imperatival participles and adjectives (12:9-21)

- A participle or infinitive used as a command carries its English command,
  including do-support: "Detest", "cling to", "Be joyful" (χαίροντες),
  "patient", "persistent", "Share with", "Practice", "Rejoice", "Do + repay"
  (12:17), "Do + avenge" (12:19), "Do + be proud" (12:16, τὰ ὑψηλὰ
  φρονοῦντες), "Do + think" (12:3, infinitive).
- Where the Greek is an adjective with no verb, the English frame is `t`:
  "must be" (12:9 ἀνυπόκριτος), "Be" (12:10 φιλόστοργοι), "Do" and "let"
  (12:11 μὴ ὀκνηροί, rendered "subside").
- Finite imperatives keep their do-support: 12:16 γίνεσθε = "Do + be",
  12:21 νικῶ = "Do + be overcome", 12:14 καταρᾶσθε = "do + curse".

## Publisher rows corrected

Lone articles carrying a preposition, copula or other word:

- 12:1 τήν "which is" → "which", "is" `t`.
- 12:2 τῇ "by the" → "the", ἀνακαινώσει = "by + renewing"; τοῦ "of your" →
  "your", νοός = "of + mind".
- 12:5 ἕν "are one" → "one"; ἐσμεν "we who are" → "we + are" (the copula is
  the finite verb's), οἱ = "who", the first "are" `t`.
- 12:6 τῆς "to his" → "his", πίστεως = "to + faith".
- 12:8 three articles ὁ carried "if it is" (no εἴτε there) → joined with
  their participles; "if it is" `t`.
- 12:9 τό and τῷ "what is" → "what", "is" `t` twice.
- 12:13 ταῖς "who are" → article `s`, "who are" `t`.

Negatives split from verbs:

- 12:3 ὑπερφρονεῖν carried "Do not think of yourself" (μή unlinked): μή =
  "not", ὑπερφρονεῖν = "Do + think + more highly" ("more highly" moved off
  παρ’, which with ὅ is now "than").
- 12:11 μή "Do not let" → "not", "Do" and "let" `t`.
- 12:14 μή "do not" → "not", καταρᾶσθε = "do + curse".
- 12:16 φρονοῦντες "Do not be proud" → μή = "not", τὰ ὑψηλὰ φρονοῦντες
  joined = "Do + be proud"; second μή "Do not" → "not", γίνεσθε = "Do + be".
- 12:17 ἀποδιδόντες "Do not repay anyone" → μηδενί = "not + anyone",
  verb = "Do + repay".
- 12:19 μή "Do not" → "not", ἐκδικοῦντες = "Do + avenge".
- 12:21 μή "Do not" → "not", νικῶ = "Do + be overcome".

- 12:3 "of yourself" twice (supplied object of "think").
- 12:1 "and" (no καί between the adjectives); 12:2 "is" (what is … will);

- 12:19 "God's" (was folded into "for God's wrath"; the Greek has the

Wrong owners and over-claims:

- 12:3 δεῖ "than you ought" → "ought"; φρονεῖν (20) "think of yourself" →

- 12:5 εἷς carried "belongs to one" and ἀλλήλων only "another": now τὸ καθ’

- 12:11 ζέοντες carried "keep your spiritual fervor": τῷ = "your",

  ποιῶν "in so doing" → τοῦτο = "so", ποιῶν = "in + doing".

## Notable decisions

- Theological terms linked word by word, no interpretive notes: θυσία =
  "as + sacrifices", λογικήν = "spiritual", λατρείαν = "service of worship"
  (12:1); μεταμορφοῦσθε = "be transformed", ἀνακαινώσει = "by + renewing",
  νοός = "of + mind", θέλημα = "will" (12:2); χάρις = "grace" (12:3, 12:6),
  χαρίσματα = "gifts" (12:6), πίστεως = "of faith"/"to + faith" (12:3,
  12:6), ἀγάπη = "Love" (12:9), φιλαδελφίᾳ = "in brotherly love" (12:10).
- 12:7-8: the ἐν-noun becomes BSB's verb where it names the same activity
  ("serve", "teach", "encourage"; ἐν and the article `s`). For giving,
  leading and showing mercy the ἐν-phrase is the manner ("generously", "with
  diligence", "cheerfully") and BSB's verb repeats the participle, so
  "give", "lead" and "do it" link to that participle as a verb understood
  again.
- 12:6 "one's gift is" is `t`, not a repeat of χαρίσματα: the Greek has no
  noun there and the English noun has a different role.
- 12:2 εἰς τό = "Then" (result); δοκιμάζειν keeps the publisher's "will be
  able to test and approve" (Philippians 1:10 has "may be able to test and
  prove" on the same infinitive). τί = "what", "is" `t`. The publisher's
  "the" on the second τό (τὸ ἀγαθόν) is kept; τὸ θέλημα's article is `s`.
- 12:3 τῷ ὄντι `s` (BSB's "every one of you" has no word for "who is";
  ἐν = "of"); the second φρονεῖν (after δεῖ) `s`.
- 12:4 ἔχομεν = "each of us has" kept (publisher): it is BSB's wording of
  "we have" in the body analogy; ἐν = "with" as BSB recasts "in one body"
  as "one body with".
- 12:11 ὀκνηροί = "subside": BSB turns "not slack" into "do not let … subside";
  kept as the rendering of that adjective.

## Doubts for the peer

- 12:3 ἑκάστῳ ("to each") = "you" (publisher row kept with a note). It could
  be `s` + `t` if "you" is read as a different word rather than the same
  recipient.
- 12:8 "do it" linked to ὁ ἐλεῶν as the repeated verb; it could be `t` as a
  pro-verb, with "give" and "lead" then also `t` for consistency.
- 12:11 "keep + fervor" on ζέοντες: "keep" could be `t` as a command frame,
  as "Be" is in 12:10; I linked it because the participle is the verb BSB
  renders, as "Be joyful" in 12:12.
- 12:2 "will be able" left inside the δοκιμάζειν group (Philippians 1:10);
  a stricter reading would make "will be able" `t`.
- 12:19 "God's" `t` with τῇ `s`; the article could instead be linked to
  "God's" as an article used as a possessive.
- 12:2 "to this" stays on τούτῳ (publisher); "to" could go on αἰῶνι.
