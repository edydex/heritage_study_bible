## Publisher rows corrected

English words with no Greek counterpart that had been folded into a Greek
word (now `t`):

- Supplied frames and copulas: 10:1 "there was" (had been on τις; the
  Greek sentence has no verb until εἶδεν in 10:3), 10:2 "were" (were
  devout), 10:22 "He is" (on δίκαιος), 10:39 "are" (are witnesses), 10:15
  "spoke" (the clause has no verb; ἐγένετο of 10:13 is in another verse).
- Supplied names and subjects: 10:2 "He" (had been on σύν), 10:4 "The
  angel" (answered), 10:7 "Cornelius" (called), 10:23 "Peter" (invited),
  10:27 "Peter" (talked with).
- Participle "and": 10:8 "and sent", 10:17 "and approached", 10:21 "and
  said", 10:23 "and went", 10:24 "and had called together", 10:25 "and fell".
- BSB additions: 10:11 "its" (by its four corners), 10:22 "has sent us",
  10:33 "to tell us", 10:34 "now" (I now truly understand), 10:38 "and how"
  (second how-clause; had been on ὅς), 10:39 "although" (And although), 10:28
  and 10:43 "that" (before an infinitive clause; had been on κἀμοί and
  πάντα).

Words on the wrong Greek word:

- Lone articles carrying a preposition: 10:2 τῷ "to the" and τοῦ "to";
  10:7 τῶν "of his"; 10:12 τῆς "of the" and τοῦ "of"; 10:17 τό "of the";
  10:23 τῶν "of the"; 10:36 τοῖς "to the"; 10:39 τῶν "of the"; 10:42 τῷ "to
  the"; 10:45 τοῦ "of the". The articles keep "the" (or "his" as a
  possessive in 10:7); the prepositions go on the nouns.
- "When/As/While" on the wrong word: 10:9 "as" moved from ἐκείνων to the
  genitive absolute ἐγγιζόντων; 10:10 "while" moved from αὐτῶν to
  παρασκευαζόντων (αὐτῶν `s`); 10:19 "As" moved from δέ to διενθυμουμένου;
  10:27 "As" moved from καί to συνομιλῶν; 10:44 "While" moved from Πέτρου to
  λαλοῦντος, and "still" from λαλοῦντος to Ἔτι.
- 10:3 εἶδεν had "he had a clear vision": now εἶδεν = he had, ἐν ὁράματι =
  a + vision, φανερῶς = clear. τῆς had "One": τῆς ἡμέρας = One day.
- 10:4 ἀτενίσας had "Cornelius stared": ὁ (with δέ) = Cornelius.
- 10:10 ἤθελεν had "wanted something": "something to eat" is now on
  γεύσασθαι.
- 10:15 κοίνου had "Do not call anything impure": now Do + call + impure,
  μή = not, Ἅ = anything + that (it had only "that"), σύ `s`.
- 10:22 εἶπαν had "they said" (οἱ = they); τε had "with" (now `s`;
  μαρτυρούμενος = with a good reputation); καί had "so he could" (now `s`;
  ἀκοῦσαι = so he could hear); σε = your (to request + presence).
- 10:26 καί had "only": καὶ ἐγὼ αὐτός is "I myself also"; "only" does not
  mean "also", so καί is `s` and "only" `t`.
- 10:28 ὁ had "But": κἀμοί (καὶ ἐμοί) = But + me. μηδένα = not + any (the
  negative had been in "I should not call" and "any" in "any man").
- 10:30 "the" (the ninth hour) moved from τῆς (with ταύτης) to τήν (with
  ἐνάτην).
- 10:33 πάντες had "are all": πάρεσμεν = are + here, πάντες = all.
- 10:34 καταλαμβάνομαι had "I now truly understand": Ἐπ’ ἀληθείας = truly,
  "now" `t`. Ἀνοίξας τὸ στόμα = began, εἶπεν = to speak.
- 10:47 καί had "have": the repeat "have" now links to ἔλαβον (have
  received + have), and καί ("also") is `s`.
- 10:48 "for" moved from τινάς to ἡμέρας (for + days, as Acts 9:43), and
  "they" from βαπτισθῆναι to αὐτούς.

Negatives split off verbs: 10:14 οὐδέποτε = never, with I have + eaten;
10:15 μή = not; 10:28 μηδένα = not + any, with I should + call.

Words understood again (Acts 6:12): 10:25 αὐτῷ = him + him (met him … to
worship him); 10:28 ἀλλοφύλῳ = a foreigner + him (associate with … visit);
10:39 ὅν = Him + Him (put Him to death by hanging Him); 10:47 ἔλαβον = have
received + have.

## Readings

- 10:1 Καισαρίᾳ and 10:24 Καισάριαν: spelled differently in the publisher's
  Greek (probably Καισαρείᾳ / Καισάρειαν); linked to "Caesarea" with a note.
- 10:37 Ἰωάνης: spelled differently (probably Ἰωάννης); linked to "John".
- 10:19: N1904 has δύο ("two"); BSB has "three" (the publisher's Greek
  probably has τρεῖς). δύο is `s` and "three" `t`, each with the reading
  reason, since the meaning differs. "to him" is `t` for the publisher's
  extra word (probably αὐτῷ).
- 10:39 "in#1" (in Jerusalem) is `t` for the publisher's extra word
  (probably ἐν before Ἱερουσαλήμ).

## Notable decisions

- Names linked despite spelling: Cornelius (Κορνήλιος, Κορνηλίου,
  vocative Κορνήλιε), Caesarea, Joppa, Simon (the tanner, βυρσεύς = the
  tanner), Peter, Nazareth, Galilee, Judea, John. BSB has no "O"/"You"
  before any vocative here (Κορνήλιε, Κύριε, Πέτρε; Μηδαμῶς = No).
- Scene openers: 10:1 δέ opens the episode with no English word (`s`, as
  9:10 and 9:36). 10:3 "One day" renders τῆς ἡμέρας (of the day); there is
  no connective in 10:3, and a cross-verse link to the δέ of 10:1 would not
  be a verse-division difference.
- 10:2 and 10:22 "God-fearing": φοβούμενος = fearing, Θεόν = God (the
  hyphenated English is two words for the compiler).
- Acts 11 parallel: 10:11 καταβαῖνον `s` (the descent is rendered once, by
  being let down = καθιέμενον), σκεῦός τι = something, τέσσαρσιν = four,
  ἀρχαῖς = by + corners with "its" `t`; 10:13 = 11:7 word for word; 10:15 Ἃ
  ὁ Θεὸς ἐκαθάρισεν σὺ μὴ κοίνου linked as 11:9; 10:16 τοῦτο δὲ ἐγένετο ἐπὶ
  τρίς as 11:10. The Acts 11 author note reports the same links.
- Holy Spirit without article: 10:38 Πνεύματι = with the + Spirit, Ἁγίῳ =
  Holy (as Acts 6:5, 9:17).
- 10:24 δέ = where kept as the publisher row with a note, as Acts 6:13 (τε
  = where).
- 10:36 τόν = this kept (the article pointing to the relative clause; ὅν
  `s` because BSB makes the relative clause the main clause).
- 10:4 καί = and (stared at him in fear and asked) kept: the καί joins the
  two participles, which BSB merges, and its "and" is shifted to the main
  verb rather than added.
- Kept "a devout"/"a large"/"a righteous"/"A holy" on the adjective, as
  reviewed Acts 6 and Luke 8.
- 10:10 "the meal" stays with παρασκευαζόντων (while + the meal was being
  prepared): BSB makes the implied object the subject of a passive.

## Doubts for the peer

- 10:1 "there was" `t` with δέ `s`: an alternative reads "there was" as a
  scene opener on δέ; I kept it `t` because it fills the missing verb, not a
  connective.
- 10:26 καί `s` + "only" `t`: the alternative keeps the publisher's καί =
  only as a pragmatic rendering of "I too".
- 10:39 "in#1": `t` for the extra word; the alternative is ἔν = in#0 + in#1
  (one ἐν governs both nouns in N1904, as Acts 6:11, 6:13).
- 10:3 τῆς ἡμέρας = One day: "One" could be `t` with τῆς `s` and ἡμέρας =
  day.
- 10:4 the second σου is `s` (one "Your" serves both nouns); it could join
  the first σου as one group.
- 10:30 μέχρι = at and Ἀπό = ago kept as publisher rows with notes; BSB
  recasts "from the fourth day until this hour" as "four days ago … at this
  hour".
