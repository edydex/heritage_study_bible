# BSB 1 Corinthians 4: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: all 21 (4:1-21). No KEEP blocks in this chapter. Final
check: `fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 21, "problems": 0}`.

69 of the 287 publisher `g` rows changed, in 20 verses. About a dozen of
these only take in a TODO token as a joined unit (ἕως ἄν, μὴ … τι, article
+ participle, a periphrastic ἐστέ, δι’ ὑμᾶς). Every publisher row was
checked, including 4:1, whose rows all stand. After the changes no "of
the"/"to the"/"in the"/"for" sits on a lone article or a number. Articles
with no English "the" are `s` with the standard reason (README rule 5).
Terms (ὑπηρέτης, οἰκονόμος, πιστός, μωρός, φρόνιμος, μιμητής) are linked
word by word with no interpretive notes. Names: Ἀπολλῶν = "Apollos",
Τιμόθεον = "Timothy".

Conventions followed: Hebrews 6:12 (μιμηταί verbalized "imitate", the
γίνεσθαι form takes the verbal frame), John 12:18, 16:15 and Matthew 14:2
(διὰ τοῦτο = "That + why", "is" `t`), 1 Peter 1:20 and 1 Thessalonians
1:5 (δι’ ὑμᾶς = "for your sake/benefit" as one unit), 2 Timothy 4:3 and
1 Thessalonians 2:14 (ἴδιος = "their/your own", article `s`),
1 Thessalonians 4:11 (dative "hands" = "with + hands"), Mark 14:30
(emphatic "this very"), John 12:19 ("whole" world `t`), Galatians 1:12
("any man" kept with the noun), and Acts 10:29 and 26:11 (no person
on a participle).

## Publisher rows corrected

Lone articles carrying a preposition, copula or other word:

- 4:5 τά "what is" → "what"; "is" `t` (supplied copula). τῶν "of men’s" →
  "men’s" (article rendered as a possessive); "of + hearts" on καρδιῶν.
- 4:9 ὁ "that" → `s` (article); "that" `t` (added complementizer). τῷ "to
  the" → "the"; "to + world" on κόσμῳ; "whole" `t`.
- 4:12 ταῖς "with our" → `s`; ἰδίαις "our own"; χερσίν "with + hands".
- 4:13 τοῦ "of the" → "the"; κόσμου "of + earth".
- 4:11 τῆς "this" and ἄρτι "very" → τῆς ἄρτι = "this very" ("the
  present"); "very" alone does not render ἄρτι.

Negatives split off verbs:

- 4:3 ἀνακρίνω "I do not even judge" → "I do + judge"; οὐδέ = "not even".
- 4:4 δεδικαίωμαι "does not vindicate me" → "does + vindicate me"; οὐκ =
  "not".
- 4:6 φυσιοῦσθε "you will not take pride" → "you will + take pride"; μή =
  "not".
- 4:7 ἔλαβες "you did not receive" → "you did + receive"; οὐκ = "not".
  λαβών "you did not" → "did"; μή = "not"; "you" `t` (participle).
- 4:14 γράφω "I am not writing" → "I am + writing"; Οὐκ = "not".
- 4:15 οὐ "you do not" → "not"; πολλούς "have many" → "many"; the
  understood ἔχητε takes "you have + you do + have" (verb understood
  again across the change of mood).
- 4:18 ἐρχομένου "were not coming" → "were + coming"; μή = "not".

Supplied words folded into Greek words (now `t`):

- 4:5 ἕως "wait until" → ἕως ἄν = "until"; "wait" `t`.
- 4:6 ὑπέρ "to go beyond" → "beyond"; "to go" `t` (verb supplied for the
  elliptical saying).
- 4:7 ἔλαβες "you did receive it" → "you did + receive"; "it" `t`.
- 4:10 six "are" on the predicate adjectives (fools, wise, weak, strong,
  honored, dishonored) → `t`; verbless clauses.
- 4:12 ἀνεχόμεθα "we endure it" → "we endure"; "it" `t`.
- 4:14 νουθετῶν "to warn you" → "to warn"; "you" `t` (supplied object).
- 4:17 Διά "That is why" → διὰ τοῦτο = "That + why"; "is" `t`; αὐτό `s`.
  καθώς "which is exactly what" → "exactly what"; "which is" `t`.
- 4:18 τινες "Some of you" → "Some"; "of you" `t`.
- 4:19 οὐ "not only" → "not"; "only" `t`. δύναμιν "power they have" →
  "power"; "they have" `t`.
- 4:20 ἐν "a matter of" → "of"; "a matter" `t`. Θεοῦ "of God is" → "of
  God"; "is" `t`.

Person words on a participle or the wrong word:

- 4:12 λοιδορούμενοι "When we are vilified" → "When + are vilified";
  διωκόμενοι likewise; both "we" `t`.
- 4:13 δυσφημούμενοι "when we are slandered" → "when + are slandered";
  "we" `t`.
- 4:2 εὑρεθῇ "they be found" → "be found"; τις (TODO) = "they".
- 4:7 διακρίνει "makes you so superior" → "makes + so superior"; σε (TODO)
  = "you".
- 4:15 ἐγέννησα "became your father" → "became + father"; ὑμᾶς (TODO) =
  "your".

Connectives and other mis-assigned words:

- 4:2 ὧδε "Now" → `s` (ὧδε "in this case"); λοιπόν (TODO) = "Now". "of
  stewards" on οἰκονόμοις → ἐν = "of", οἰκονόμοις = "stewards".
- 4:15 γάρ "Even" → `s`; ἐάν "if" → "Even if" (concessive).
- 4:17 μου "of my" → "my"; "of" goes with ἀναμνήσει ("will remind +
  of", the English verb's complement).
- 4:21 τε "and with" → "and" (lone τε, not τε … καί); πνεύματι "with a
  + spirit" (dative under ἐν; English "a" with its noun); πραΰτητος "a
  gentle" → "gentle".

Units joined (rule 3 or idiom): 4:3 εἰς ἐλάχιστόν ἐστιν = "care very
little" (ἐστιν alone had it); 4:4 οὐδὲν ἐμαυτῷ σύνοιδα = "My conscience is
clear" (σύνοιδα alone had it), ἐν τούτῳ = "that", ὁ … ἀνακρίνων = "who
judges"; 4:5 μὴ … τι = "nothing"; 4:8 κεκορεσμένοι ἐστέ = "you have all you
want"; 4:12 κοπιῶμεν ἐργαζόμενοι = "We work hard"; 4:19 τῶν πεφυσιωμένων =
"these arrogant people".

## Notable decisions

- 4:3 ἐμοί = "I" (the dative becomes the English subject); "any human"
  kept on ἀνθρωπίνης (Galatians 1:12 "any man"); ἀλλά = "In fact" and ἵνα
  = "if" kept (connectives rendered with different connectives).
- 4:5 καί ("also") after ὅς and the καί before τότε are `s`; ὁ = "his"
  (article as possessive, publisher row).
- 4:6 τό before the quoted saying is `s`; εἷς (the distributive subject)
  is `s`, as BSB expresses it only through "you"; ἵνα = "Then" kept.
- 4:7 εἰ δὲ καί: καί ("indeed") is `s`; BSB's emphatic "did" stays with
  ἔλαβες.
- 4:8 γε = "really"; ἐβασιλεύσατε = "you + were kings". The last verb is
  linked with a note that BSB's Greek text spells it differently
  (EVIDENCE shows one word differs; probably συμβασιλεύσωμεν for
  συνβασιλεύσωμεν). Both καί `s`.
- 4:9 ὅτι `s` (BSB starts a new sentence); the first καί ("both") `s`, the
  second = "as well as"; ἐσχάτους = "at the end of the procession" kept.
- 4:11 the first καί ("both") and the three καί BSB replaces with commas
  are `s`; the second = "and".
- 4:13 ὡς `s`; κόσμου = "earth" and πάντων = "of the world" (BSB's
  synonyms).
- 4:16 μιμηταί = "imitate", γίνεσθε = "to" (Hebrews 6:12 pattern).
- 4:17 ὅς ἐστίν `s` (BSB makes the relative clause an apposition); both
  τάς `s`.
- 4:19 καί = "and then" kept (majority of reviewed rows, e.g. Luke 19:12,
  Mark 7:19); τόν = "what" and τήν = "what" kept (article rendered by the
  English interrogative frame).

## Doubts for the peer

- 4:5 τῶν = "men’s" follows the article-as-possessive convention; the
  alternative is τῶν `s` with "men’s" `t`, since there is no ἀνθρώπων.
- 4:12 κοπιῶμεν ἐργαζόμενοι joined to "We work hard"; κοπιάω alone means
  "work hard", so κοπιῶμεν = "We work hard" with ἐργαζόμενοι `s` is also
  defensible.
- 4:14 "you" after "warn" is `t` as a supplied object; ὑμᾶς could instead
  link to both "you"s (shared object of both participles).
- 4:15 "Even" put on ἐάν (concessive "Even if"); it could instead be `t`.
- 4:3 εἰς ἐλάχιστόν ἐστιν = "care very little" as one idiom; a finer split
  (ἐλάχιστον = "very little", ἐστιν = "care") would link ἐστιν to a word of
  different meaning.
- 4:21 "with" before "a gentle spirit" put on πνεύματι (dative case word);
  it could be ἐν understood again ("in + with").
