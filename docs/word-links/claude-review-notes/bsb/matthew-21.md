## Publisher rows corrected

Supplied English that had been folded into a Greek word's link (now `t`):

- 21:2: δεδεμένην "tied there" → tied; πῶλον "her colt" → colt; λύσαντες "Untie them" → Untie; ἀγάγετε "and bring them" → and bring. N1904 has no "there", no possessive and no objects.
- 21:3: ἐρεῖτε "tell him" → tell (no addressee in Greek).
- 21:7: ἐπεκάθισεν "Jesus sat" → sat (the name comes only from the verb ending).
- 21:8: ἐστρώννυον "spread them" → spread (no object).
- 21:9: Εὐλογημένος "Blessed is" → Blessed (the copula is supplied).
- 21:16: οὗτοι "these children" → these (the noun is supplied).
- 21:20, 21:32: ἰδόντες "saw this" → saw (the object is supplied).
- 21:28: ἄνθρωπος "There was a man" → a man; πρώτῳ "first one" → first.
- 21:30: προσελθών "the man went" → went; δευτέρῳ "second son" → second; εἶπεν "and told him" → and told.
- 21:34, 21:41: καρπούς "share of the fruit" → fruit. In 21:41, τούς is now linked to "the", and "his share of" is supplied.
- 21:36: πρώτων "first group" → first; ἐποίησαν "the tenants did" → did.
- 21:45: λέγει "Jesus was speaking" → was speaking.

Negatives and auxiliaries that had been put on the wrong word:

- 21:16, 21:42: ἀνέγνωτε "Have you never read" → never goes to οὐδέποτε; the verb keeps "Have you … read".
- 21:21: μή "do not" → not; "do" moves to διακριθῆτε.
- 21:25: ἐπιστεύσατε "did you not believe" → not goes to οὐκ.
- 21:27: οἴδαμεν "We do not know" → not goes to Οὐκ; ἐγώ "will I" → I, with "will" moved to λέγω.
- 21:28: ὑμῖν "do you" → you; "do" moves to δοκεῖ.
- 21:20: Πῶς "How did" → How; "did" moves to ἐξηράνθη.
- 21:32: "you did not believe" is split: not goes to οὐκ. "did not repent" is split: not goes with even to οὐδέ.
- 21:24: Ἐρωτήσω "I will also ask" → will … ask; "I … also" goes to κἀγώ.

Wrong anchors:

- 21:10, 21:20, 21:23, 21:38, 21:45, 21:46: "When" (or "Although", "But when") sat on καί, δέ or a pronoun. It now goes to the temporal or concessive participle, and καί becomes `s`.
- 21:12: τούς carried "who were", which a lone article may not anchor. It is now joined with πωλοῦντας.
- 21:21: τό carried "what was done", which a lone article may not anchor. τό → what, and "was done" is supplied.
- 21:25: τό (the second, attributive article) carried "of". It is now `s`, and "of" goes to πόθεν ("What … the source of"). ἦν → was. ἐξ "Was it from" → from, and "Was it" is supplied.
- 21:17: ηὐλίσθη "where He spent the night" → He spent the night; "where" goes to καὶ … ἐκεῖ.
- 21:19: γένηται "May you never bear" → May … bear; Οὐ μηκέτι → never + again; ἐκ σοῦ → you.
- 21:31: ὑμᾶς "before you" → you; "before" goes to προάγουσιν (+ εἰς), giving "are entering … before".
- 21:33: αὐτῷ "around it" → it, and "around" goes to περιέθηκεν. ἐξέδετο "he rented it out" → he rented … out, and αὐτόν → it.
- 21:39: "him" in "threw him" and "killed him" now links to the one object αὐτόν, together with the first "him".
- 21:41: κακῶς "to a wretched end" → wretched; "to a … end" goes to ἀπολέσει (will destroy).
- 21:42: εἰς carried "the". It is now `s`, and κεφαλὴν γωνίας → "the cornerstone".
- 21:44: ὅν carried "he" and ἐφ’ carried "on whom". Now ἐφ’ → on, ὃν … ἄν → whom, and αὐτόν → he (the active "it will crush him" becomes a passive in BSB).
- 21:9, 21:26, 21:46: the "of" in "went ahead of" and "afraid of" moves from the object or article to the verb.

## Notable decisions

- **Joint tokens:** ἤγγισαν εἰς / εἰσῆλθεν εἰς / εἰσελθόντος … εἰς → approached / entered. Also: εἴπῃ τι → questions; χρείαν ἔχει → needs; εἰ μή → except; πάντα ὅσα ἄν → whatever; Διὰ τί → Why; διὰ τοῦτο → Therefore; ὃν ἐάν → and if; ἀποκριθεὶς … εἶπεν → replied/answered; ἄνθρωπος οἰκοδεσπότης → a landowner; ὁ καιρὸς τῶν καρπῶν and καιροῖς αὐτῶν → harvest time; ἐν τῷ ἱερῷ → there; τοῦ πιστεῦσαι → and believe; ἐπίστευσαν αὐτῷ → did (21:32).
- **Article with participle joined (rule 3):** τὸ ῥηθέν, οἱ προάγοντες, οἱ ἀκολουθοῦντες, ὁ ἐρχόμενος, τοὺς κράζοντας, τῶν πωλούντων, ὁ πεσών.
- **ὁ/οἱ δέ** before a verb is linked as the subject pronoun: They (21:25) and he (21:29, 21:30).
- **Pronoun rendered by a name** is linked: αὐτοῦ → Jesus (21:10, 21:23), αὐτός → Jesus (21:27). A name that comes only from a verb ending is `t` (21:7, 21:18, 21:45).
- **Textual difference, 21:29-31.** N1904 (the Vaticanus order) has the first son say "I go, sir" and not go, and the second son refuse and later go; the answer is ὁ ὕστερος. BSB follows the other reading, in which the answers are reversed and the answer is "The first". In each verse only the shared frame (ὁ δὲ ἀποκριθεὶς εἶπεν) and the verb ἀπῆλθεν are linked, each with a note. The other N1904 words are `s` and the BSB-only words are `t`, both giving the reading reason. I did not invent cross-verse links.
- Other N1904 readings checked against BSB that needed no special handling: ἐπεκάθισεν (singular, 21:7), ποιεῖτε (21:13), no τοῦ θεοῦ (21:12), δευτέρῳ (21:30), οὐδέ (21:32), εἰς προφήτην (21:46).

## Doubts for the peer

- 21:29-31: is s/t right for the reversed answers, or would `g @21:30` cross-verse links (for example, BSB 21:29 "changed his mind" → N1904 21:30 μεταμεληθείς) serve readers better?
- 21:19: εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα is `s` ("never … again" carries it). An alternative is to join it to the "never" group.
- 21:12: one article governs both participles. I put "who were" with τοὺς πωλοῦντας (selling), although in English it stands next to "buying".
- 21:23: "began to" stays with the participle διδάσκοντι ("and began to teach"). It is BSB's interpretive rendering, and could instead be `t`.
- 21:24: "and" in "and if" is shared by ὃν ἐάν. The relative's linking force is plausible but not certain.
