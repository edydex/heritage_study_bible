# BSB Matthew 26: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: 75 of 75 (no verse was kept from an earlier reviewer).

Check: `fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 75, "problems": 0}`.

118 publisher rows in 62 verses were changed because they were wrong or
over-claimed. Another 25 rows only took a word freed by a neighbouring
correction, 29 rows only gained an unlinked token (for example ἐὰν μή →
unless), and 34 rows were joined into one unit with their English unchanged
(article + participle, idioms).

## Publisher rows corrected

- Participle "and": the English "and" only joins a participle rendered as a
  finite verb, so it is now `t` and the verb keeps its own word. "and asked"
  (26:8, 26:15, 26:17, 26:62), "and began" (26:22, 26:37), "and pray(ed)"
  (26:36, 26:42, 26:44), "and went away" (26:44), "and found" (26:43),
  "and fled" (26:56), "and sat down" (26:58), "and declared" (26:61, 26:65),
  "and said" (26:68), "and wept" (26:75). In 26:26 "and eat" → eat
  (`t and`: "Take, eat" has no καί).
- Negatives folded into verbs: the negative is now linked on its own.
  οὐ = "you will not" (26:11); "had not been born" (26:24); οὐ μή → not /
  never with the verb as I will + drink / I will + deny (26:29, 26:35);
  "Were you not able" (26:40); "you will not enter" (26:41); "did not
  arrest" (26:55); "did not find any" (26:60, with `t any`); Οὐδέν = no in
  "Have You no answer" (26:62); "I do not know" (26:70, 26:72, 26:74).
  In 26:42 οὐ δύναται = cannot jointly.
- δέ/καί carrying "When"/"While"/"As" of a participle: 26:6, 26:8, 26:20,
  26:21 ("And while"), 26:26, 26:30 ("And when"), 26:47 (καί = While),
  26:71. The When/While now goes with the participle or genitive absolute.
  26:60: "though" moved from πολλῶν to the genitive absolute προσελθόντων,
  and δέ = "Finally" → ὕστερον = Finally.
- Wrong tokens:
  - 26:2 εἰς = "over": "over" belongs to παραδίδοται (will be handed over);
    εἰς τὸ σταυρωθῆναι = to be crucified (purpose).
  - 26:21 αὐτῶν = "to them": αὐτῶν is the subject (they) of the genitive
    absolute; "to them" is `t`.
  - 26:32 με = "I" of "I will go": με is the subject of ἐγερθῆναι (I have
    risen); προάξω = I will go ahead of. Same in 26:35: με = I (have to die).
  - 26:52 ὁ = "to him" → αὐτῷ = to him, ὁ explained.
  - 26:22, 26:25 εἰμι = "Surely not" / "Surely", Μήτι = "not" → Μήτι =
    Surely not, εἰμι `s`.
  - 26:41 μέν = "For" → μέν `s`, `t For` (no γάρ in the Greek).
  - 26:58 τῆς = "to the" → ἕως = right up to, τῆς = the.
  - 26:24 ἐκείνῳ = "to that" → τῷ = to (dative), ἐκείνῳ = that.
  - 26:54 αἱ = "would the" → the; would goes with πληρωθῶσιν. ὅτι = "that
    say" → that, `t say`; πῶς = "But how" → how, `t But`.
  - 26:56 ὅλον = "has all" → all; has goes with γέγονεν.
  - 26:39 τοῦτο = "let this" → this; παρελθάτω = let + pass. 26:42 σου =
    "may Your" → Your; γενηθήτω = may + be done.
  - 26:53 δοκεῖς = "Are you not aware" → Are you + aware, οὐ = not (BSB moves
    the negative of "I cannot" into the question). παραστήσει no longer
    claims "at once" (ἄρτι) or "My" (μοι).
  - 26:75 ῥήματος = "word that" → word, `t that`.
- English-only words claimed by a Greek word, now `t`: Jesus (26:20, 26:25,
  26:40, 26:43), Judas (26:16), Peter (26:71), the men (26:50), Him (26:4,
  26:57, 26:67), it (26:25, 26:26 twice, 26:27, 26:61, 26:64, 26:70, 26:72),
  this / of this (26:8, 26:10), perfume and the money (26:9), cup (26:42),
  very (26:31, 26:34), other (26:35), to them (26:74), that (26:18), armed
  and sent (26:47), the house of (26:57), you would (26:55), all of (26:64),
  the second will (26:39), is (26:41 twice), do (26:50), one (26:73).
- Other over-claims:
  - 26:1 ἐτέλεσεν = "had finished saying" → had finished; λόγους = saying +
    things (as in 19:1).
  - 26:11 ἔχετε = "you will always have" → πάντοτε = always.
  - 26:13 "will also be told" → καί = also. μνημόσυνον = "memory of" →
    αὐτῆς = of her.
  - 26:22 "deeply" → σφόδρα. 26:31 "will all fall away" → Πάντες = all.
    26:45 "Are you still sleeping" → λοιπόν = still. 26:47 "was still
    speaking" → ἔτι = still.
  - 26:12 ἐποίησεν = "she has prepared" → αὕτη = she.
  - 26:7 βαρυτίμου = "of expensive" → expensive, μύρου = of + perfume;
    κατέχεεν = "which she poured" → καί = which, κατέχεεν = she poured.
  - 26:15 αὐτόν = "Him over" → Him, παραδώσω = hand + over. "they set out" →
    οἱ = they.
  - 26:18 "He answered" → ὁ = He, εἶπεν = answered; αὐτῷ = "him that" →
    him, `t that`. 26:23 ἀποκριθείς = "Jesus answered" → ὁ = Jesus,
    ἀποκριθεὶς εἶπεν = answered.
  - 26:25, 26:64 εἶπας = "have said it yourself" → Σύ = You + yourself,
    εἶπας = have said, `t it`.
  - 26:52 εἰς = "back in" → Ἀπόστρεψον = Put + back, εἰς = in.
  - 26:59 θανατώσωσιν = "put Him to death" → αὐτόν = Him.
  - 26:62 οὗτοι = "are these men" → these men; are goes with
    καταμαρτυροῦσιν.
  - 26:63 Ἐξορκίζω = "I charge You under oath" → σε = You.
  - 26:73 ἐξ = "one of" → of, `t one`.

## Notable decisions

- Joint units: ἀποκριθεὶς … εἶπεν (26:23, 26:25, 26:33, 26:66); article +
  participle (26:3 whose name was, 26:14, 26:23, 26:25, 26:28, 26:46, 26:48,
  26:52, 26:57, 26:68 ὁ παίσας = hit, 26:73); ἵνα μή = or; ὅπου ἐάν;
  ἐὰν μή; ἕως οὗ; Εἰς τί = Why; κόπους παρέχετε; εἷς ἕκαστος = one after
  another; πάλιν ἐκ δευτέρου; ἐκ τρίτου; ἐπὶ πρόσωπον αὐτοῦ = facedown;
  ἐπέβαλον τὰς χεῖρας ἐπί = seized; χρείαν ἔχομεν = do we need; δῆλόν …
  ποιεῖ = gives … away; καὶ γάρ = for; μετ’ αὐτοῦ = accompanied by;
  πρὸς σέ = at your house; τὸν δεῖνα = a certain man; ἵνα … εἴπῃς = Tell;
  Ἔνοχος … ἐστίν = He deserves; Καὶ ἰδού = At this; τοῦ Ναζωραίου = of
  Nazareth.
- Whole phrases rendered by a pronoun: ὁ Υἱὸς τοῦ ἀνθρώπου = He and
  ὁ ἄνθρωπος ἐκεῖνος = he (26:24).
- English series "and": the καί joining two participles is linked to the
  "and" of BSB's list (26:26 and#0, 26:27).
- Kept from the publisher on purpose: the crossed renderings in 26:12
  (σώματος = body in "My body", με = Me in "on Me") and 26:36 (αὐτῶν =
  His disciples, τοῖς μαθηταῖς = them), with notes; πρώτῃ = first day;
  ἄλλη = another servant girl; τοῖς = to the people (26:71); μετά = away
  (26:2); κἀγώ = if I (26:15); ὑμῖν = do you (26:66); μίαν = for one (26:40).
- Spelling or form differences in the publisher's Greek, linked anyway:
  Ῥαββεί = Rabbi (26:25, 26:49), Γεθσημανεί = Gethsemane (26:36), εὐθύς =
  immediately (26:74). 26:36 Τότε = At that time (the publisher's table
  English has "Then"). 26:45: the publisher's Greek has one word more than
  N1904; no English word depends on it, so nothing is linked for it.
- Left unexpressed (`s`): ἐκτείνας τὴν χεῖρα (26:51), resumptive οὗτος
  (26:23), Οὕτως (26:40), ἤ (26:53), τότε (26:52), the second πάλιν (26:44),
  ἐν in "this very night" (26:31, 26:34), addressee datives, λέγων before
  quotations.

## Doubts for the peer

- 26:12 and 26:36: I kept the publisher's word-for-word links across BSB's
  reordering. The alternative is meaning links (26:12: ἐπὶ τοῦ σώματός μου =
  on Me, με = My body; 26:36: αὐτῶν = them, μαθηταῖς = disciples).
- 26:39: προσευχόμενος καὶ λέγων = "and prayed" as one group, or prayed with
  `s` καί and `t and`?
- 26:41: `t For` with μέν `s`. Is this a split pair, or is "For" plain
  English?
- 26:47 "armed", 26:57 "the house of", 26:64 "all of": made `t`. The peer
  may judge them fair renderings of μετά, πρός and plural ὑμῖν.
- 26:4: `t Him`, or Ἰησοῦν = Jesus + Him (one object for both verbs)?
- 26:45: λοιπόν = still. I am fairly but not fully sure this is the word
  BSB renders as "still".
