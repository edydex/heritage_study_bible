# BSB 1 Corinthians 9: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: 26 of 27 (9:1-16, 9:18-27). Kept unchanged: 9:17 (KEEP
block, reviewed earlier by Codex). Final check: `fromtext` →
`{"versesOk": 27, "problems": 0}`.

116 of the 360 publisher `g` rows changed (9:17 not counted), in all 26
reviewed verses. 6 of them only joined a token with the English unchanged,
and 12 more merged two rows into one article + participle unit (9:3, 9:13
twice, 9:14, 9:24, 9:25); see "Joined units". Unchanged rows were checked
too.

Readings: EVIDENCE shows the publisher's Greek differs from N1904 only in
9:13, where BSB's Greek spells the verb "share" differently (probably
συμμερίζονται for N1904 συνμερίζονται). Same word, so it is linked to
"partake of" with a note. No other reading is claimed.

Conventions followed: 1 Corinthians 3:16 and Romans 6:16 for οὐκ οἴδατε
(οὐκ = "not", οἴδατε = "Do you + know"); Romans 10:18 for μὴ οὐκ (μή `s`,
οὐκ = the negative, the verb keeps "Have we"); 1 Corinthians 1:13 for ἤ
before a new question (`s`); John 7:42 for "Doesn’t" on οὐ alone; Luke
24:21 for ἀλλά γε as one unit; James 3:5 for οὕτως = "In the same way" with
καί `s`; 1 Timothy 5:18 for the same quotation in 9:9 ("Do + muzzle",
"while it is treading out the grain" on ἀλοῶντα); Acts 10:25 for an
articular infinitive's article joining its infinitive; README rule 3 for
article + participle units.

## Notable decisions

- Names: Κηφᾶς = "Cephas" (9:5), Βαρνάβας = "Barnabas" (9:6), Μωϋσέως =
  "of Moses" (9:9), Ἰησοῦν = "Jesus" (9:1).
- 9:9 quotation (Deuteronomy 25:4) linked word by word: Οὐ = "not",
  κημώσεις = "Do + muzzle", βοῦν = "an ox", ἀλοῶντα = "while it is
  treading out the grain" (kept whole, as 1 Timothy 5:18).
- Rhetorical questions: every μή question marker is `s`; every οὐ/οὐκ/οὐχί
  is linked on its own; "Am I", "Have we", "Do you + know" stay on the
  verbs. 9:8 οὐ = "Doesn’t" and 9:12 οὐ = "shouldn’t" keep the contraction
  on the negative (John 7:42).
- 9:6 the double negative "have no right not to work" is rendered "must
  work": the four tokens οὐκ ἔχομεν ἐξουσίαν μή are joined to "must"
  rather than spread over English that has no negative.
- 9:7 ἐσθίει (25) = "drink" with a note (the food is milk); same verb and
  role, so not a different-meaning `s`/`t` pair.
- 9:12 μετέχουσιν = "have#0 + have#1" (verb understood again in "shouldn’t
  we have it"); "it" `t`.
- 9:20 ἐγενόμην = "I#0 became + I#1 became" (understood again in the
  second clause); αὐτός = "I#2 myself" (explicit intensive pronoun; the
  rule for a person supplied to a participle does not apply).
- 9:24 τὸ βραβεῖον = "the#1 + the#2" / "prize#0 + prize#1": BSB repeats
  the object that καταλάβητε leaves understood (see doubts).
- 9:25 the two elliptical clauses: "for#1" and "a#1 crown" link to ἵνα …
  λάβωσιν and στέφανον as understood again; "do it" (both) and "that is"
  (both) are `t`.
- Connectives rendered with a different connective stay linked: 9:10, 9:15
  γάρ = "Indeed"; 9:16 γάρ = "Yet"; 9:12 ἀλλά = "Instead"; 9:27 ἀλλά =
  "No"; 9:2 εἰ = "Even if".

## KEEP block (9:17)

Left exactly as it was. One observation only: the supplied copulas are
folded into Greek groups ("my preaching is voluntary" on ἑκὼν τοῦτο
πράσσω, "it is not voluntary" on ἄκων). Current convention would make
"is#0" and "it is#1" `t`. Not an error in meaning, so not changed.

## Doubts for the peer

- 9:10 both ἐπ’ ἐλπίδι (11-12 and 19-20) are joined to the one "expect",
  since BSB merges the plowman's and thresher's clauses ("they should also
  expect"). The stricter alternative links only the second (with "to share
  in") and makes the first `s`. "threshes" `t` (the Greek leaves that verb
  understood). Linking it to ἀλοῶν is the alternative.
- 9:21 ὤν = "though + am#0 + am#1": one participle governs both predicates
  (μὴ ὢν ἄνομος Θεοῦ ἀλλ’ ἔννομος Χριστοῦ), so the second "am" is read as
  the same verb, not a supplied copula. `t` for "am#1" is the alternative.
- 9:24 "the#2 prize#1" linked to τὸ βραβεῖον as understood again (same
  object, λαμβάνει / καταλάβητε). `t` as a supplied object is the
  alternative.
- 9:15 οὕτως `s` and "something" `t` (manner adverb vs subject pronoun);
  the publisher's οὕτως = "something" is the looser alternative. ἵνα = "to
  + that" with "suggest" `t`; keeping "to suggest that" on ἵνα with a note
  is the alternative.
- 9:11 εἰ = "for" (the εἰ clause recast as "for us to reap"); the
  publisher's ἡμεῖς = "for us" with εἰ `s` is the alternative.
- 9:25 πάντα ("in all things") `s`; it could be read as BSB's "strict".
- 9:9 "it" and "the grain" kept inside ἀλοῶντα's rendering (1 Timothy
  5:18); `t` for them as supplied subject/object is the stricter option.
- 9:13 τὰ ἱερά: τά = "the#0", ἱερά = "in + temple" (sacred things → the
  temple); "offerings" `t` with θυσιαστηρίῳ (17) = "its#1".
