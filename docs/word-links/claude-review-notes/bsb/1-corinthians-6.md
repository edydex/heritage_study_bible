check: `fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 20, "problems": 0}`.

73 of the 280 publisher `g` rows changed, in 17 of the 20 verses. 6:6,

Conventions followed: Romans 6:16 and Romans 11:2 for οὐκ
οἴδατε (οὐκ = "not", οἴδατε = "Do you + know"; ἤ `s` when BSB has no

James 1:16 for μὴ πλανᾶσθε (μή = "not", πλανᾶσθε = "Do + be deceived");

Matthew 19:5 and Mark 10:8 for ἔσονται … εἰς = "will become"; Acts 4:12 and

frame ("that" on the Greek word, "is what" `t`); 1 Corinthians 2:13 for a head noun BSB supplies for a
substantival adjective staying inside the adjective (βιωτικά = "the things
of this life", 6:3); README rule 3 for article + participle units.

## Publisher rows corrected

Lone articles carrying a case word (article = "the" or `s`, the case word
goes to the noun):

- 6:11 τοῦ "of the" → "the", "of + Lord" on Κυρίου; τοῦ "of" → `s`,
  "of + God" on Θεοῦ.
- 6:13 τῇ "for the" → "the", "for + stomach"; τῷ "for the" twice → "the",
  "for + Lord" and "for + body".
- 6:17 τῷ "with the" → "the", "with + Lord".
- 6:19 τοῦ "of the" → "the", "of + Spirit".

Negatives, modals and person moved:

- 6:2, 6:3, 6:9, 6:15, 6:19 οἴδατε "Do you not know" → "Do you + know";
  οὐκ (TODO) = "not". 6:16 οἴδατε "don't you know" → "you know"; οὐκ =
  "don't".
- 6:9 κληρονομήσουσιν "will not inherit" → "will + inherit", οὐ = "not";
  μή "Do not" → "not", πλανᾶσθε = "Do + be deceived".
- 6:12 ἐξουσιασθήσομαι "will not be mastered" → "will + be mastered", οὐκ
  (TODO) = "not".
- 6:1 τις "If any" → "any"; "If" goes to the participle ἔχων ("If + has"),
  which BSB recasts as a condition. κρίνεσθαι "he go to law" → "go to law";
  "he" joins the finite Τολμᾷ ("how dare he").
- 6:15 ἄρας "Shall I then take" → "take"; οὖν (TODO) = "then"; ποιήσω "and
  unite them" → ποιήσω … μέλη = "Shall I + unite"; "and" (joining the
  participle to the main verb) and "them" `t`.
- 6:5 οὐκ "no" + οὐδείς "one" → οὐκ … οὐδείς = "no one" (double negative).

Supplied words moved off Greek words (`t`):

- 6:5 "this" off λέγω (supplied object).
- 6:7 "that#1" off ἐστιν, which keeps "means"; "are" off ὅλως, which keeps
  "thoroughly" ("are" joins ἥττημα: "are + defeated").
- 6:8 "against your own" off τοῦτο: καὶ τοῦτο = "even", ἀδελφούς =
  "against + brothers", "your own" `t`.
- 6:13 "is" off σῶμα and "intended" off οὐ (the clause has no verb).
- 6:14 "from the dead" off Κύριον; "us also" off ἐξεγερεῖ (ἡμᾶς = "us",
  καί = "and + also").
- 6:16 "with her" off ἕν; 6:17 "with Him" off ἕν.
- 6:18 "other" off πᾶν, which keeps "Every".
- 6:19 "who is" off ἐστιν: ἐστιν is the main verb, so it takes "is#0" from
  ναός (now "a temple"); "who is#1" `t` (English turns the attributive ἐν
  ὑμῖν into a relative clause).
- 6:11 "is what" off ταῦτα, which keeps "that" (cleft frame).

Other content fixes:

- 6:2 ἀνάξιοι "are you not competent" → "not competent"; ἐστε (TODO) =
  "are you". κριτηρίων "cases" → "to judge + cases"; ἐλαχίστων "to judge
  trivial" → "trivial" (the judging belongs to the noun "courts/cases", not
  to "least").
- 6:4 βιωτικά "everyday matters" → "everyday"; κριτήρια "to settle" → "to
  settle + matters".
- 6:5 ὑμῖν "to your" → "your", πρός (TODO) = "to"; σοφός "wise enough" →
  "wise", ὃς δυνήσεται (TODO) = "enough".
- 6:13 καί "both" and ταύτην "them" → καὶ … καί = "both", ταύτην … ταῦτα =
  "them" (the second καί and ταῦτα were TODO; English unchanged).

Joined units (English unchanged): 6:1 καὶ οὐχί = "instead of"; 6:2 ἐν
ὑμῖν = "you"; 6:4 τοὺς ἐξουθενημένους = "of no standing"; 6:5 ἀνὰ μέσον =
"between"; 6:7 διὰ τί = "Why" twice; 6:15 μὴ γένοιτο = "Never"; 6:16 Ἔσονται
… εἰς = "will become"; 6:16, 6:17 ὁ κολλώμενος = "he who unites himself";
6:18 ὁ πορνεύων = "he who sins sexually".

## Notable decisions

- The vice list (6:9-10) is linked noun by noun with no interpretive
  notes: πόρνοι = "the sexually immoral", εἰδωλολάτραι = "idolaters",
  μοιχοί = "adulterers", μαλακοί = "men who submit to", οὔτε = "or",
  ἀρσενοκοῖται = "perform homosexual acts" (BSB's paired rendering, kept
  word by word as the publisher has it), κλέπται = "thieves", πλεονέκται =
  "the greedy", μέθυσοι = "drunkards", λοίδοροι = "verbal abusers", ἅρπαγες
  = "swindlers"; every οὔτε/οὐ = "Neither"/"nor".
- The Genesis quotation (6:16) is linked word by word: οἱ = "The", δύο =
  "two", Ἔσονται … εἰς = "will become", μίαν = "one", σάρκα = "flesh".
  φησίν = "it is said".
- 6:11: the second and third ἀλλά are `s` (BSB says "But" once); ἀπελούσασθε,
  ἡγιάσθητε, ἐδικαιώθητε keep "you were washed/sanctified/justified".
- 6:7: ὅτι keeps "The very fact that" (it introduces the subject clause,
  as τοῦτο = "the fact" in 2 Peter 3:5); μὲν οὖν `s`; ἐστιν = "means"; ἥττημα = "are +
  defeated" (noun rendered as a verb).
- 6:14: the first καί of καὶ … καί is `s` (BSB has no "both", as the τε …
  καί convention); the second καί = "and + also".
- 6:15: ἄρας = "take" keeps no person; πόρνης = "with a prostitute".
- 6:18: ὃ ἐάν `s` (contact clause); ποιήσῃ = "can commit" (modal on the
  finite verb); τοῦ and τό = "his" (article as possessive).
- 6:20: γάρ `s` (BSB joins the clause to 6:19 with a semicolon); δή =
  "Therefore".

## Doubts for the peer

- 6:1 Τολμᾷ = "how dare he": "how" is kept as part of BSB's exclamatory
  rendering of the question "does he dare"; `t` for "how" is the stricter
  alternative.
- 6:4 καθίζετε = "do you appoint as judges": "as judges" kept on the verb
  (καθίζω used of seating judges); `t` for "as judges" is
  the alternative. ἔχητε = "you need" and κριτήρια = "to settle + matters"
  could also be split as ἔχητε = "you need to settle", κριτήρια =
  "matters".
- 6:7 ἐστιν = "means" with "are" on ἥττημα; the alternative is ἐστιν =
  "are" with "means that" `t`. "very" stays inside ὅτι's "The very fact
  that".
- 6:5 οὕτως = "really" (publisher row, kept: "is it so that" rendered
  "really"); ὃς δυνήσεται = "enough" (the relative clause "who will be
  able" rendered "wise enough to").
- 6:19 ἔχετε = "you have received" (publisher row, kept): "received" could
  be `t`.
- 6:6 ἀδελφός = "one brother", ἀδελφοῦ = "another" (publisher rows, kept);
  "one" could be `t`.
- 6:14 καί = "and + also" on the second καί; joining both καί to "and +
  also" is the alternative.
