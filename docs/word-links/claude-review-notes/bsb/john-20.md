# BSB John 20: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: 31 (20:1-31). The draft had no KEEP blocks, so no verse was
kept from an earlier review. Final check: `fromtext` →
`{"versesOk": 31, "problems": 0}`.

83 publisher `g` rows changed in 29 verses. 14 of them were only extended or
joined to absorb a TODO token (listed last); the rest were wrong or
over-claimed and are corrected below. Every publisher `g` row in 20:12 and
20:31 is kept (those verses gained only `s` lines).

Parallels and precedent followed: Luke 24:1 and Mark 16:2 / Matthew 28:1
(μιᾷ = first, "day" `t`; the article does not carry "on"/"of"); Luke 24:36
(Εἰρήνη = Peace, ὑμῖν = with you, "be" `t`); Luke 24:40 (the participle
εἰπών carries the When/After clause; τάς = His); Matthew 28:10 (Μή = not,
the verb = Do + verb); John 1:38 (Ῥαββεί, spelled differently, with ὃ λέγεται
… Διδάσκαλε linked word by word); John 13:23 (ὃν ἠγάπα ὁ Ἰησοῦς: "the one" `t`);
John 6:43 (ἀπεκρίθη … καὶ εἶπεν = replied, one group); John 7:24 and 6:43
(present prohibition μή = Stop); John 2:4 and 7:6 (οὔπω = not yet); Mark 16:6
and John 1:38 (ὁ/οἱ δέ = he/they); Luke 6:8, Mark 3:3 (εἰς τὸ μέσον =
among us/them, one group); Matthew 5:3 (Blessed, "are" `t`).

## Vocatives and 20:28

- Γύναι = Woman (20:13, 20:15), Κύριε = Sir (20:15), Μαριάμ = Mary (20:16).
  BSB has no "You"/"O" before any of them, so nothing else joins them.
- 20:16 Ῥαββουνεί = Rabboni, noted as the same word spelled differently in
  BSB's Greek text (the EVIDENCE line shows the spelling difference). The
  explanatory clause is word by word: ὃ = which, λέγεται = means (noted),
  Διδάσκαλε = Teacher.
- 20:28 Ὁ Κύριός μου καὶ ὁ Θεός μου: Κύριος = Lord, μου = My, καί = and,
  Θεός = God, μου = my; both articles `s` (the possessive is μου). No
  interpretive note. ἀπεκρίθη … καὶ εἶπεν = replied (one group, as John 6:43);
  αὐτῷ `s`.

## Publisher rows corrected

Negatives split off verbs (the verb keeps do/will/did):

- 20:2 "we do not know", 20:13 "I do not know" → οὐκ = not, verb = we/I do +
  know.
- 20:5 "he did not go in" → οὐ = not, εἰσῆλθεν = he did + go in.
- 20:9 ᾔδεισαν carried "they still did not understand" while οὐδέπω was TODO
  → οὐδέπω = still + not, ᾔδεισαν = they + did + understand.
- 20:14 "she did not recognize" → οὐκ = not, ᾔδει = she did + recognize.
- 20:17 μή carried "Do not" → μή = not, ἅπτου = Do + cling to; ἀναβέβηκα
  carried "I have not yet ascended" while οὔπω was TODO → οὔπω = not yet,
  ἀναβέβηκα = I have + ascended.
- 20:25 πιστεύσω carried "I will never believe" while οὐ μή were TODO →
  οὐ μή = never, πιστεύσω = I will + believe.
- 20:27 ἄπιστος carried "Stop doubting" while μή and γίνου were TODO →
  μή = Stop (present prohibition), γίνου ἄπιστος = doubting.
- 20:29 "have not seen" → οἱ … ἰδόντες = those who have + seen (article and
  participle joined), μή = not.

Objects and pronouns folded into a verb:

- 20:13 Κύριον carried "Lord away" → "away" moves to ἦραν (have taken … away).
- 20:15 ἐβάστασας carried "Him" and ἀρῶ carried "Him" while both αὐτόν were
  TODO → each αὐτόν = Him; ἐβάστασας = have carried + off; ἀρῶ = will get.
- 20:21 ἀπέσταλκεν carried "Me" while με was TODO → με = Me.
- 20:22 ἐνεφύσησεν carried "them" while αὐτοῖς was TODO → αὐτοῖς = them
  (noted: the "to them" of λέγει is BSB's object of breathed on).
- 20:23 τινων carried "anyone his" while τάς was TODO → τάς = his (article as
  possessive), τινων = anyone.

Connectives and participle clauses:

- 20:5, 20:16, 20:18 "and" joining a participle to the main verb was on the
  verb ("and looked", "and said", "and announced") → `t`.
- 20:8 οὖν carried "Then" while τότε was TODO → τότε = Then, οὖν `s`.
- 20:20 and 20:22 καί carried "After"/"When" → καί `s`; the participle εἰπών
  carries After/When He had said (Luke 24:40).
- 20:25 εἶπεν carried "he replied" while ὁ was TODO → ὁ = he, εἶπεν = replied.

Wrong words:

- 20:6 καί ("also") carried "just" and ἀκολουθῶν only "after" → ἀκολουθῶν =
  just after, καί `s`.
- 20:7 μετά ("with") carried "from" and τόπον ("place") carried "separate" →
  οὐ μετὰ … ἀλλὰ χωρίς ("not with … but apart") = separate from (one group);
  εἰς ἕνα τόπον ("in one place") `s`.
- 20:19 τῇ carried "and that", ἐκείνῃ only "very", καί carried "with", ὅπου
  ("where") carried "while", ἦσαν carried "were together" → ἐκείνῃ = that very
  (noted), τῇ `s`, the genitive absolute κεκλεισμένων = with + locked, καί `s`
  with "and" `t`, ὅπου `s` with "while" `t` (different meaning), ἦσαν = were,
  "together" `t`.
- 20:26 the article τῶν carried "with the" → τῶν = the, κεκλεισμένων = with +

  (were + was), μετ’ = with.
- 20:27 λέγει carried "Jesus said" → "Jesus" `t`; βάλε carried "put it" → "it"
  `t`.
- 20:18 καί carried "And she told them" → καί = And, "she told them" `t`
  (the Greek says only that He had said these things to her).

## Readings (only where EVIDENCE shows a difference)

- 20:16 Ῥαββουνεί: same word, spelled differently in BSB's Greek text.
- 20:25: one N1904 word is unmatched. τόπον (31) was the only content word the
  publisher left unlinked, so BSB's Greek probably reads τύπον ("mark") here,
  as in the first half of the verse. BSB's "where the nails have been" fits
  either word, so εἰς … τόπον = where is linked, with a note that BSB's Greek
  has a different word of similar sense. The review file does not name τύπον.
- 20:30: BSB's Greek has one extra word, probably αὐτοῦ after μαθητῶν; "His"
  is `t` with that reason.

## Notable decisions

- 20:7: BSB turns "not lying with the linen cloths but rolled up apart in one
  place" into "was rolled up, lying separate from the linen cloths". The
  four tokens οὐ μετὰ … ἀλλὰ χωρίς are one group, "separate from", because
  neither "separate" nor "from" renders any one of them alone. εἰς ἕνα τόπον
  is `s`.
- 20:19: "while the disciples were together" renders ὅπου ἦσαν οἱ μαθηταί
  ("where the disciples were"). "while" does not mean ὅπου, and nothing in
  N1904 says "together" (the EVIDENCE line forbids claiming the
  συνηγμένοι reading), so both are `t` and ὅπου is `s`.
- 20:22: αὐτοῖς (the indirect object of λέγει) is linked to "them" after
  breathed on: same referent, shifted, so per the split-pair rule it is
  linked, not left as `s` + `t`.
- 20:26: the understood ἦσαν is linked to both "were" and "was" (Thomas was
  with them).
- 20:17: πρός is linked to both "to"s ("to My Father … to My God"), as the
  understood verb rule allows.

## Doubts for the peer

- 20:7 "separate from" as one four-token group: a peer may prefer χωρίς =
  separate and οὐ μετά `s`, but "from" then has no honest owner.
- 20:3: ἤρχοντο joined to "set out" with ἐξῆλθεν and the καί between them; the
  alternative is ἤρχοντο `s` (folded into set out for).
- 20:19 ἐκείνῃ = "that very" (the day's demonstrative moved to the evening)
  and Οὔσης = "It was" (the participle of the evening clause moved to the day
  statement). Both are BSB's reordering; I kept the links with notes rather
  than leaving them `s`/`t`.
- 20:1 ἐκ τοῦ μνημείου = from the entrance: μνημείου linked to "entrance"
  with a note, not `s` + `t`.
- 20:17 linking one πρός to two English "to"s, and 20:26 one ἦσαν to "were"
  and "was".
