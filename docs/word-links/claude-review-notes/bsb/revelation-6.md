## The repeated seal formula

Each repetition is linked the same way:
- καί = "And"/"Then"/"So"; ὅτε = "when" ("as" in 6:1); ἤνοιξεν =
  "opened"/"open". "the Lamb" is `t` in 6:3, 6:5, 6:7, 6:9 and 6:12, because
  only 6:1 names τὸ Ἀρνίον.
- τὴν σφραγῖδα τὴν δευτέραν/τρίτην/τετάρτην/ἕκτην: the first article =
  "the", and the repeated attributive article is `s` (6:3, 6:5, 6:7, 6:12).
- ἤκουσα = "I heard"; λέγοντος = "say" (no "and" and no person in these
  verses); Ἔρχου = "Come".
- καὶ εἶδον, καὶ ἰδού: εἶδον = "I looked", καί = "and", ἰδού = "saw" (6:2,
  6:5, 6:8). The Revelation 4 work file does the same in 4:1.
- ἵππος = "a + horse" with the colour adjective on its own (6:2, 6:5, 6:8).
- ὁ καθήμενος ἐπ’ αὐτόν = "its rider" as one unit (6:2, 6:5; dative τῷ
  καθημένῳ in 6:4). In 6:8, ὁ καθήμενος ἐπάνω αὐτοῦ and the resumptive
  possessive αὐτῷ = "Its rider’s".
- ἐδόθη + αὐτῷ/αὐτοῖς = "was/were given" + "he/they" (6:2, 6:4, 6:8, 6:11).

## Notable decisions

- 6:6 prices: δηναρίου = "for a denarius" is kept on the genitive of price.
  This follows Matthew 10:29 (ἀσσαρίου = "for a penny") and Luke 12:6
  (ἀσσαρίων = "for + pennies"). σίτου/κριθῶν keep "of wheat"/"of barley".
  BSB gives the oil and the wine one "the", so the second article τόν is `s`.
- 6:15 seven classes: each "the" goes on its own article. καί before
  μεγιστᾶνες, χιλίαρχοι, πλούσιοι and ἰσχυροί is `s` (BSB uses commas), and
  the last two καί carry "and#0"/"and#1". "free man" stays on ἐλεύθερος, as
  in Revelation 1:17 (νεκρός = "a dead man").
- 6:11 ὡς καί: ὡς = "just as" and καί is `s`, the majority pattern (Acts
  10:47, Hebrews 3:2, 2 Peter 2:1).
- 6:12 ἐγένετο (11) keeps "there was": the existential renders a real
  ἐγένετο. καί (8) is `s` because ὅτε itself carries "when".
- 6:10: BSB reverses the two verbs ("avenge … and judge"), so καί (15) =
  "and#1" sits between them. "You" goes on ἐκδικεῖς, the verb next to it.
  οὐ and ἐκ are `s`, since BSB recasts the negative question and makes those
  who dwell the object of "judge". λέγοντες has no English word (`s`).
- 6:4 αὐτῷ (11) is `s` because it only repeats the dative τῷ καθημένῳ. In
  6:8, αὐτῷ joins the rider unit because only it supplies the possessive ’s.
- 6:8 θανάτῳ = "plague", with a note that θάνατος here means deadly disease.

## Doubts for the peer

- 6:1 ὡς … βροντῆς = "thunderous" joins two tokens that are not next to each
  other. The other choice is ὡς `s` ("as it were") with βροντῆς =
  "thunderous". I joined them so 6:1 matches 6:12 ὡς αἷμα = "blood red".
- 6:2 ἵνα = "to" and νικῶν = "overcome". The other choice is νικῶν = "to
  overcome" (publisher), with ἵνα joined to "conquer".
- 6:10 I kept the publisher's split Ἕως = "until", πότε = "How long". The
  idiom could instead be joined as Ἕως πότε = "How long + until".
- 6:4 "permission" `t` and 6:12 "goat" `t`: both are English additions under
  the supplied-noun rule. A looser reading would keep them in the publisher
  phrases "was granted permission" and "of goat hair".
- 6:8 αὐτῷ inside "Its rider’s" (the other choice is `s`).
- 6:13 βάλλει keeps the publisher's "dropping from". "from" only marks the
  tree, which is the subject in the Greek, so it could instead be `t` or go
  on συκῆ.
