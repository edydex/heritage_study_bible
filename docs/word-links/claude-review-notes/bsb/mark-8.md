# BSB Mark 8: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: 38 of 38 (no verse was kept from an earlier reviewer).

Check: `fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 38, "problems": 0}`.

75 publisher rows in 33 verses were corrected because they were wrong or
over-claimed. Another 32 rows only gained a TODO token or a word freed by a
neighbouring correction (for example μὴ … τί = nothing, κοφίνους … πλήρεις =
basketfuls, ὃς ἐάν = whoever).

## Publisher rows corrected

- English-only "Jesus" claimed by a verb or participle, now `t Jesus`:
  called (8:1, 8:34), asked (8:5, 8:17), blessed (8:7), had dismissed (8:9),
  sighed deeply (8:12), placed (8:25), sent (8:26), warned (8:30). Same for
  other supplied subjects: "The people" ate and "the disciples" picked up
  (8:8), "the disciples" had forgotten (8:14), "The man" looked up (8:24),
  "some people" brought (8:22, impersonal plural).
- Participle "and": English "and" only joins a participle to the main verb,
  so it is now `t` and the verb keeps its own word: and said (8:1, 8:12,
  8:24, 8:26), and broke (8:6), and ordered (8:7), and went (8:10), and
  crossed (8:13), and led (8:23), and placed (8:23, two participles),
  and began (8:32), and He told (8:34).
- Supplied objects or recipients claimed by a verb, now `t`: them (8:6 three
  times, 8:7 "set before them", 8:25 "opened them"), the people (8:6),
  "to Him" folded into μαθητάς / ὄχλον (8:1, 8:34; now called + to Him for
  the middle προσκαλεσάμενος), "Him" inside "took Him aside" (8:32, now
  αὐτόν = Him), "of their conversation" (8:17), "find enough" and "all"
  (8:4), "that" (8:7), "back" (8:26), "the boat" (8:13).
- Negatives folded into verbs, now linked on their own: Do you still not see
  (8:17) and Do you still not understand (8:21) → οὔπω = still not; do you
  not see / hear / remember (8:18); not to tell anyone (8:30) → μηδενί = not
  + anyone, ἵνα … λέγωσιν = to tell; you do not have in mind (8:33);
  Do not go back (8:26) → Μηδέ = not, εἰσέλθῃς = Do + go.
- Words belonging to another Greek word: "already" (8:2, now ἤδη); "back"
  (8:13, now πάλιν); "Once again" was on εἶτα (8:25, now πάλιν; εἶτα `s`);
  "this" was on ἐρημίας (8:4, now ὧδε = this); "does" on αὕτη (8:12, now
  ζητεῖ = does + demand); "do" on ἄνθρωποι (8:27, now λέγουσιν = do + say);
  "can" on ἄνθρωπος (8:37, now δοῖ = can + give); "also" in "will also be
  ashamed" (8:38, now καί = also); "My" in "for My sake" (8:35, now ἐμοῦ);
  "what about" on δέ (8:29, now `t`); "Jesus" on δέ in "But Jesus" (8:33,
  now ὁ = Jesus, δέ = But); "others say" (8:28, `t say`).
- Lone articles carrying a preposition or a noun: τῷ = "in" and ταύτῃ = "to
  this" (8:12), τοῖς = "to" and τῷ = "to the" (8:6), τῆς = "by the" and "of
  the" (8:23), τῶν = "of the" (8:15, 8:28), τοῦ = "for the" (8:35), τῆς =
  "for" (8:37). The preposition now goes with the dative/genitive noun (or
  with ἔξω, ἕνεκεν, ἀντάλλαγμα). 8:33: τοῦ and τῶν carried "things"; the
  neuter plural τά now carries "the things".
- 8:20: ὅτε = "when I broke" and ἑπτά = "seven loaves" → when, seven; `t I
  broke` and `t loaves` (elliptical Greek repeating 8:19).
- 8:22: καί(0) = "When" → the second καί (joining "they come … and they
  bring") = When, as in Mark 5:15 and 5:38.
- 8:25: "and when" on καί(9) → καί(9) = and, καί(11) = when (same pattern).
- 8:31: ἀποκτανθῆναι = "that He must be killed" → be killed; ὅτι = that +
  that, δεῖ = must + must (repeated in English), `t He#1`.

## Notable decisions

- 8:9–10: BSB "As soon as Jesus had dismissed the crowd, He got into the
  boat" renders εὐθύς of 8:10 at the end of its 8:9. It is linked from 8:9
  (`g @8:10 1`) and marked moved (`m 1 > 8:9`) in 8:10; καί(4) in 8:9 and
  καί(0) in 8:10 are `s`.
- Publisher Greek differences: 8:3 "have come" follows ἥκασιν (N1904
  εἰσίν) → `t have come`, εἰσίν `s`. 8:20: the publisher's Greek reads ὅτε
  καί and λέγουσιν αὐτῷ; BSB's "And" renders that καί → `t And`; the extra
  αὐτῷ has no English. Same word, other spelling or form, linked normally:
  εἶχαν/εἶχον (8:7), συνζητεῖν/συζητεῖν (8:11), Καισαρίας/Καισαρείας (8:27,
  "around Caesarea"), Ἰωάνην, Ἡλείαν (8:28). I checked the 8:3, 8:7 and 8:20
  publisher words on the public BibleHub interlinear (BSB Greek).
- 8:7: the opening καί = also ("They also had"), to avoid leaving the καί
  `s` beside a `t also`.
- Joint units: εἰς οἶκον αὐτῶν / αὐτοῦ = home (8:3, 8:26); ἀπὸ μακρόθεν =
  a great distance (8:3); οὐκ … εἰ μή = except for (8:14); ἀποκριθεὶς …
  λέγει = answered (8:29); ὃς ἐάν = If anyone (8:38); σπυρίδων πληρώματα
  and κοφίνους … πλήρεις = basketfuls (8:19, 8:20).
- Kept from the publisher on purpose: εἰ = no (8:12, oath formula);
  καί = For (8:3); γάρ = Or (8:37); ὅτι = but and ὁρῶ = they look (8:24);
  ἀπεκατέστη = his sight was restored (8:25); τάς = His (8:23, 8:25,
  possessive article, as Matthew 19:13); pronoun rendered as a noun (αὐτῷ /
  αὐτόν = Jesus, αὐτούς = the crowd, αὐτοῦ = the man's, αὐτός = Jesus).
- Left unexpressed (`s`): recipients αὐτῷ / αὐτούς / αὐτοῖς (8:1, 8:4, 8:5,
  8:19, 8:22, 8:23, 8:29), recitative ὅτι and λέγων / λέγοντες (8:4, 8:15,
  8:27, 8:28), γάρ (8:36, 8:38), ὑμῶν (8:17), the second τῆς ζύμης (8:15),
  question εἰ (8:23).

## Doubts for the peer

- 8:2 τόν = "this crowd" and 8:32 τόν = "this message": kept as the
  publisher's article rendered "this" (as Mark 6:2). Matthew 15:32 uses
  `t this` for the same Greek and English.
- 8:22 `t some people` (impersonal plural) versus Mark 6:14, where "people
  were saying" stayed with the verb.
- 8:13: ἐμβάς = got + into with `t the boat`. Alternative: ἐμβάς = got into
  the boat (the verb means "embark"). 8:25 διέβλεψεν = he opened with
  `t them` is the same kind of choice.
- 8:9 τετρακισχίλιοι = "four thousand men" and 8:4 τούτους = "these people"
  are kept as substantives with a supplied noun; "men" could be `t`.
- 8:17 πεπωρωμένην = "such hard": "such" is an English intensifier; kept.
- 8:7 καί = also: the publisher row for the opening καί could not be seen.
  The alternative is `s` for καί and `t also`.
