## Notable decisions

- Key terms linked word by word, without interpretive notes: διακονία =
  "service" (9:1), "ministry" (9:12, 9:13); λειτουργία = "service" (9:12);
  εὐλογία = "bountiful gift" (9:5), with ὡς/ἐπί "generously" (9:5, 9:6);
  πλεονεξία with ὡς = "begrudgingly" (9:5); ἱλαρός = "cheerful", δότης =
  "a + giver" (9:7); αὐτάρκεια = "need" (9:8); δικαιοσύνη = "righteousness"
  (9:9, 9:10); ἁπλότης = "generous"/"generosity" (9:11, 9:13); κοινωνία =
  "contribution" (9:13); ἀνεκδιήγητος = "indescribable", δωρεά = "gift"
  (9:15).
- 9:1 γάρ = "Now" (connective rendered with a different connective); μέν
  `s`; περισσόν = "no need"; ἐστιν = "there is" (the verb itself).
- 9:2 ὑπὲρ ὑμῶν: ὑμῶν = "you" (BSB merges "about you" into "you in
  Achaia"), ὑπέρ `s`; Ἀχαΐα = "in Achaia".
- 9:3 ἔπεμψα (aorist) = "I#0 am sending"; ἦτε = "you#1 will be" (person and
  modal on the finite verb).
- 9:4 ἵνα μὴ λέγωμεν ὑμεῖς keeps the publisher's split ("to", "nothing",
  "say", "of#0 you").
- 9:7 ἐξ `s`: BSB's one "out of" stays with ἐκ.
- 9:9 ἔδωκεν keeps the publisher's "His#0 gifts" ("he gave"; "His" is the
  verb's own person).
- 9:10 σπόρον keeps "store of seed" (as one unit for "seed for sowing").
- 9:12 ταύτης = "this" with a note (Greek: "this service"; BSB: "this
  ministry"); εὐχαριστιῶν = "expressions of thanksgiving".
- 9:13 διά = "Because of"; εἰς (15) = "of#1"; ἁπλότητι keeps "for#1 the
  generosity" (ἐπί understood again).

## Doubts for the peer

- 9:14 αὐτῶν linked to both "their#0" and "their#1": it is the subject of
  ἐπιποθούντων and BSB reads it with δεήσει too. `t` for "their#1" is the
  stricter option. ἐπιποθούντων = "express + affection" with "will" `t`;
  `t` for "express" as well is the alternative.
- 9:14 ἐφ’ ὑμῖν ("upon you") → "God has given you": ἐφ’ `s` + "has given"
  `t` (a verb, different meaning). The publisher linked ἐφ’ = "has given".
- 9:5 ὑμῶν = "you#1" (the promiser) and ταύτην = "your gift#1". Keeping the
  publisher's ὑμῶν = "your" would leave "you#1" `t`; ταύτην = "gift#1" with
  "your" `t` is the stricter option.
- 9:11 ἥτις = "your giving" with "so that" `t`; ἥτις `s` with "your giving"
  `t` is the alternative. πᾶσαν keeps "on every#1 occasion" as one unit;
  "on … occasion" `t` with πᾶσαν = "every#1" is the stricter option.
- 9:8 αὐτάρκειαν = "need" with "that#1 you#1" `t`; one unit "that you need"
  is the alternative.
- 9:12 ἐστίν = "is#0 + is#1" (one periphrastic ἐστίν with two participles,
  same clause); `t` for "is#1" is the alternative.
- 9:7 καθώς ("just as") = "what" with a note; `s` + "what" `t` is the
  alternative.
- 9:9 ἔδωκεν = "His#0 gifts"; "His#0" `t` is the stricter option.
