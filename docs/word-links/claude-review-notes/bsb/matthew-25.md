## Publisher rows corrected

- Participle clauses joined to the main verb by a supplied "and" (the "and"
  is now `t`, the verb keeps only itself): 25:1 "and went out", 25:16 "and put
  them to work", 25:20 "and presented", 25:22 "and said", 25:25 "and went out
  and". The same applies to 25:11, where καί ("also") had been linked to "and"
  (καί now `s`), and 25:24, where καί had been linked to the "and" of "came
  and said".
- Negatives folded into verbs, now οὐ/οὐκ = not and the verb = do/did/have +
  verb: 25:12 "I do not know", 25:13 "you do not know", 25:24 and 25:26 "have
  not sown", "have not scattered", 25:43 (three verbs), 25:44 "did not
  minister", 25:45 (both "did not do"). In 25:42 the second οὐκ now carries
  "nothing" and με "Me" (both had been folded into the verb).
- 25:3: οὐκ = "any extra" was wrong and ἔλαβον carried "but did not take
  along" → οὐκ = not, ἔλαβον = did + take, μεθ’ ἑαυτῶν = along; "but" and "any
  extra" are `t`; τάς = their (article as possessive).
- 25:2: "five were" → five; the one ἦσαν = were + were.
- 25:5: δέ = "When" → χρονίζοντος = When + was delayed (genitive absolute);
  "they all became drowsy" over-claimed "all" → πᾶσαι = all.
- 25:9: οὐ = "No" was wrong → Μή ποτε = or, οὐ μή = not, ἀρκέσῃ = there may +
  be enough, "No" is `t`. "both", "oil" (sell oil) and "some" (buy some) are
  English-only → `t`; τοὺς πωλοῦντας joined = those who sell.
- 25:10: αὐτῶν = "while they" → while goes with the genitive absolute
  participle; "to buy it" → `t it`.
- 25:11: "open the door" → open, `t the door`.
- 25:12: ἀποκριθείς = "he replied" → ὁ = he, ἀποκριθεὶς εἶπεν = replied.
- 25:14: Ὥσπερ = "it is just like" → just like, `t it is`; "who called" →
  called, `t who`; αὐτοῦ = "with his" → his; "with" goes with entrusted.
- 25:15, 25:17, 25:18: numerals carried an understood noun ("two talents",
  "one talent", "two talents", "one talent") → the numeral alone. In 25:15 the
  τάλαντα in the verse carries all three nouns; in 25:17 and 25:18 the noun
  is `t`. In 25:17 ὁ = "the one with" → the one, `t with`.
- 25:18: "dug a hole" → dug, `t a hole`.
- 25:20, 25:22: "with five", "with two" → the numeral; "with" goes with
  entrusted.
- 25:21, 25:23: σε = "you in charge" → you; καταστήσω = I will put + in charge.
- 25:22: τά = "who had received the" was wrong (N1904 has no participle) →
  τά = the, `t who had received` (reading difference).
- 25:24: εἶ = "you are" → σε = you, εἶ = are (prolepsis "I knew you, that …").
  "scattered seed" → scattered, `t seed` (same in 25:26).
- 25:25: the article τό carried "what belongs" → τὸ σόν = what belongs to you.
- 25:27: ἐμόν = "it back" → τὸ ἐμόν = it; ἐκομισάμην ἄν = would have received
  + back.
- 25:28: "give it" → give, `t it`; τῷ ἔχοντι joined = to the one who has.
- 25:29: "will be given more" → `t more`; καί = "and he" → and, the verb takes
  he; μή = "does not" → not, "does" goes with τοῦ … ἔχοντος.
- 25:32: ἀλλήλων = "one from another" → one + another; ἀπ’ = from.
- 25:33: "His left" → left; the one αὐτοῦ = His + His.
- 25:34: οἱ = "you who are" (lone article carrying "are") → οἱ εὐλογημένοι =
  you who are blessed.
- 25:35, 25:37: "something" (to eat / to drink) is `t`; με = Me where the verb
  had carried it ("gave Me something to drink", "took Me in").
- 25:36, 25:43: "I was naked", "I was sick" on the adjective → adjective alone,
  `t I was` (verb understood).
- 25:37, 25:38: supplied objects "feed You", "give You", "take You in",
  "clothe You" → the verbs alone, `t You`.
- 25:41: "His left" → left, `t His` (N1904 has no αὐτοῦ).
- 25:45: "the King will answer" on the verb → will answer, `t the King`.

## Notable decisions

- Joint units: μέσης νυκτός = At midnight; ἀπεκρίθησαν … λέγουσαι = said;
  ἀποκριθεὶς εἶπεν = replied (25:12, 25:26); ἀποκριθεὶς … ἐρεῖ = will reply;
  Μή ποτε = or; οὐ μή = not; μεθ’ ἑαυτῶν = along; αἱ ἕτοιμοι = Those who were
  ready; δὲ καί = Finally (25:24); ἐφ’ ὅσον = whatever (25:40, 25:45);
  ἤλθατε/ἤλθομεν πρός = visited/visit; ἐκομισάμην ἄν = would have received +
  back; article + participle = The servant who had received (25:16, 18, 20,
  24), to the one who has, who has (25:29), the one who does … have,
  you who are blessed.
- Article + participle "The servant who had received": "servant" stays in the
  joint group as BSB's word for "the one", as rule 3 keeps "one".
- One Greek word, several English copies: ἦσαν = were + were (25:2); τάλαντα =
  talents + talents + talent (25:15); αὐτοῦ = His + His (25:33).
- Cross-verse: N1904 prints Εὐθέως at the end of 25:15; it is `m` there and
  linked from 25:16 as `g @25:15 19 = at once`.
- Textual differences from N1904:
  - 25:22 "who had received": N1904 has no λαβών (the Byzantine text has it)
    → `t`.
  - 25:6 "him": N1904 has no αὐτοῦ after ἀπάντησιν (the Byzantine text has it)
    → `t`.
  - 25:41 "His": no αὐτοῦ after εὐωνύμων → `t`.
- Kept from the publisher on purpose: γέγονεν = rang out; Ἰδού = Here is;
  τόν = that (25:30); ἐκεῖ = where there (25:30); αὐτούς = the people (25:32);
  οὐκ = nothing (25:42); τότε = And (25:44, with a note); You wicked (25:26,
  vocative, as in Matthew 23).
- Left unexpressed (`s`): addressee datives (αὐτῷ, αὐτοῖς), λέγοντες/λέγων
  before quotations, ἐκεῖναι (25:7), ὅτι (25:8), καί "also" (25:11, 25:41),
  τότε (25:31), καί before συναίρει (25:19, rendered as "to settle"), and
  τάλαντα after "five/two more" (25:20, 25:22).

## Doubts for the peer

- 25:9: "No" as `t` with Μή ποτε = or. Some read μή here as an elliptical
  "No!"; linking Μή to "No" and ποτε to "or" is the alternative.
- 25:24: δὲ καί = Finally as one group, or δέ = Finally with καί ("also") `s`?
- 25:3: τάς = their. The Byzantine text adds αὐτῶν after λαμπάδας, which BSB's
  "their" may follow; I linked the article.
- 25:44: τότε = "And" kept with a note; `s` for τότε plus `t And` is the
  alternative, though it splits a pair.
- 25:30: ἐκεῖ = "where there" kept; ἐκεῖ = where with "there" joining ἔσται
  ("there will be") is the alternative.
- 25:17: ὁ = the one with `t with`, while 25:22 has ὁ = The servant. Check that
  the elliptical article groups read consistently.
