# BSB Luke 5: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: 39 (5:1-39). No verse was kept from an earlier review (the
draft had no KEEP blocks). Final check: `fromtext` →
`{"versesOk": 39, "problems": 0}`.

110 of the 597 publisher `g` rows, in 33 verses, were corrected, split or
merged. Another 28 rows only gained unlinked Greek tokens with the English
unchanged (for example ἀποκριθεὶς … εἶπεν = replied 5:5, 5:22; εἰ μή = but
5:21; Διὰ τί = Why 5:30; ἀπῆλθεν ἀπ’ = left 5:13; εἰς τὸν οἶκον = home 5:24,
5:25). Mark 1:40-45 and 2:1-22 and Matthew 8:2-4 and 9:1-17 were checked as
parallels.

## Publisher rows corrected

Names, objects and frames supplied by English (now `t`):

- "Jesus" sat on a verb or participle: 5:3 ἐμβάς, 5:4 ἐπαύσατο, 5:13
  ἐκτείνας, 5:20 ἰδών, 5:27 ἐξῆλθεν. "the man" on ἀναστάς (5:25); "Levi" on
  ἀναστάς (5:28).
- 5:12: πλήρης "who was covered" → covered; `t who was`.
- 5:14: προσένεγκε "present the offering" → present; `t the offering` (Luke
  has no object; καθώς is `s`, see doubts).
- 5:15: ἀκούειν "to hear Him" → to hear; `t Him`. 5:17: ἰᾶσθαι "to heal the
  sick" → τὸ ἰᾶσθαι = to heal; `t the sick` (αὐτόν, "Him", is the subject).
  καθήμενοι "sitting there" → sitting; `t there`.
- 5:19: ἀναβάντες "So they went up" → they went up; `t So`. μέσον "middle of
  the crowd" → middle; `t of the crowd` (the Greek says only "into the
  middle").
- 5:22: διαλογίζεσθε "are you thinking these things" → are you thinking;
  `t these things` (Luke has no ταῦτα, unlike Mark 2:8).
- 5:7: ὥστε "so full that" → so + that; `t full`.

Connectives carrying a participle's "When", and "and" joining a participle
to its main verb:

- 5:6 καί, 5:8 δέ, 5:20 καί "When" → `s`; ποιήσαντες / ἰδών = When + verb.
  5:11 καί "And when" → And; καταγαγόντες = when they had brought. 5:12 καί
  "While" was on the frame καὶ ἐγένετο → `s`; ἐν τῷ = While.
- Supplied "and" folded into the main verb, now `t`: 5:2 ἔπλυνον, 5:3
  ἠρώτησεν, 5:11 ἠκολούθησαν, 5:12 ἐδεήθη, 5:13 ἥψατο, 5:19 καθῆκαν, 5:25
  ἀπῆλθεν, 5:26 λέγοντες, 5:28 ἠκολούθει, 5:7 ἐλθόντας (and help), 5:36
  ἐπιβάλλει ("and sews it"; "it" is also `t`).
- 5:24: πορεύου "and go" → go; the real καί (between ἔγειρε and πορεύου) =
  and, as Mark 2:12.
- 5:16: καί "to" was wrong → `s`; προσευχόμενος = to pray.

Negatives and auxiliaries split:

- 5:10: Μὴ φοβοῦ "Do not" + "be afraid" → μή = not, φοβοῦ = Do + be afraid.
- 5:14: μηδενὶ εἰπεῖν "anyone" + "Do not tell" → not + anyone, Do + tell.
- 5:19: "they could not find" → μή = not, εὑρόντες = they could + find.
- 5:31: Οὐ "It is not" → not, `t It is`. 5:32: "I have not come" → οὐκ =
  not, ἐλήλυθα = I have + come (as Mark 2:17).
- 5:5: οὐδέν "anything" and ἐλάβομεν "without catching" → οὐδέν = without +
  anything, ἐλάβομεν = catching.
- 5:36: συμφωνήσει "will not match" → will + match, οὐ = not.

Prepositions on a lone article; "a" with its noun:

- 5:1 τοῦ "of" → `s`, Θεοῦ = of God. 5:9 τῶν "of" → `s`, ἰχθύων = of fish.
  5:12 τῶν and 5:33 τῶν "of the" → the; πόλεων = of + towns, Φαρισαίων = of +
  Pharisees. 5:2 τήν "of the" → the; παρά = at the edge of.
- 5:7 τοῖς "to their" → their; μετόχοις = to + partners. 5:14 τῷ "to the" →
  the; ἱερεῖ = to + priest. 5:24 τῷ "to the" → joined with the participle
  (τῷ παραλελυμένῳ = to the paralytic, rule 3).
- 5:1 τόν "with the" → the; ἐν τῷ = with (see doubts).
- "a" moved to its noun: 5:6 a + number, 5:8 a + man, 5:18 a + man, 5:29
  a + banquet and A + crowd, 5:36 a + garment and an + one.

Other words on the wrong Greek word:

- 5:21: ὃς "man who" → who; οὗτος = this man (as Mark 2:7).
- 5:22: αὐτῶν "what they" → they; τοὺς διαλογισμούς = what + were thinking
  (as Matthew 9:4).
- 5:34: νυμφῶνος (bridal chamber) = "bridegroom" was lexically wrong →
  υἱοὺς τοῦ νυμφῶνος joined = guests of the bridegroom (as Mark 2:19).
- 5:10: ὁμοίως "so were" → ὁμοίως … καί = so; `t were` (English repeats
  "were astonished" from 5:9).
- 5:6: πολύ "such a large" → large; δέ = such + that (BSB's result clause).
- 5:33: εἶπαν "they said" → said; the article Οἱ (with δέ) = they.
- 5:36: Ἔλεγεν "He also told" → He + told; καί = also.
- 5:18: αὐτόν "to bring him inside" → εἰσενεγκεῖν = to bring + inside,
  αὐτόν = him; ὃς ἦν παραλελυμένος joined = paralyzed.
- Joined units: ἦν ὑποχωρῶν = frequently withdrew (5:16, the periphrastic
  carries the habit; ἦν alone had "frequently"); ἦσαν ἐληλυθότες = had come
  and ἐν μιᾷ τῶν ἡμερῶν = One day (5:17); οἱ ὑγιαίνοντες = the healthy and
  οἱ κακῶς ἔχοντες = the sick, χρείαν ἔχουσιν = who need (5:31); δι’ ὅλης
  νυκτός = all night (5:5).

## Notable decisions

- Readings: the EVIDENCE lines show differences in 5:2, 5:6, 5:7, 5:9, 5:10,
  5:18, 5:27, 5:29 and 5:33. In 5:2, 5:6, 5:7, 5:10, 5:27, 5:29 and 5:33 the
  only plausible candidate is a spelling or form of the same word (fishermen,
  began to tear, came, John, Levi, John’s); they are linked with "spelled
  differently in BSB's Greek text". 5:18 is the one real reading: the
  publisher has an extra word (19 against 18) and only "him" after "set" is
  unowned, so `t him#1` gives the reading reason. 5:9: no reading is claimed;
  every unowned token there is a translation choice (πάντας `s`, the relative
  `s`).
- Lukan frames: Ἐγένετο δέ = On one occasion (5:1); καὶ ἐγένετο `s` (5:12,
  5:17); ἐν τῷ + infinitive = While (5:12). The καί that opens the main
  clause after ἐγένετο is `s` in 5:17 and joins ἰδού in 5:12 (καὶ ἰδού =
  came along).
- Pronouns rendered as names are linked with a note: αὐτός = Jesus (5:1,
  5:14, 5:17), αὐτόν = Jesus (5:12), αὐτοῦ = Jesus (5:15, 5:18, 5:30), αὐτῷ =
  for Jesus (5:29), αὐτοῦ = the man (5:13), τῷ Σίμωνι = his (5:10), ὁ νυμφίος
  = He (5:34).
- Articles as possessives kept (publisher rows): τά = their (5:2, 5:11), τήν
  = His (5:13), τῷ = his (5:19), avoiding a split `s` + `t`.
- 5:17: the one ἐκ links to both English "from" (from Jerusalem, from every
  village).
- Parallels: the paralytic, Levi and fasting sections follow Mark 2 (εἰ μή =
  but, Διὰ τί = Why, ἐν ᾧ = while, εἰ δὲ μήγε = If he does, ἐν ἐκείναις ταῖς
  ἡμέραις `s`, οἶκον … = home). Where the Greek differs the links differ:
  5:22 `t these things` (no ταῦτα), 5:38 βλητέον = is poured (Mark 2:22 has no
  verb), 5:14 καθώς instead of Mark's ἅ.

## Doubts for the peer

- 5:1: BSB swaps the clauses. ἐν τῷ (with the crowd pressing) = "with" and
  the main-clause καί = "while" (Jesus was standing). The alternative is ἐν
  τῷ = "while" with "with" `t` and καί `s`; I avoided it because "while"
  then names the wrong clause and leaves a split pair.
- 5:14: καθώς `s` with `t the offering`. Mark 1:44 links "the offering" to
  the relative ἅ; Luke's καθώς ("just as") does not mean "offering", but a
  peer may prefer καθώς = "the offering" with a note.
- 5:14 τῷ ἱερεῖ and 5:31 οἱ ὑγιαίνοντες / οἱ κακῶς ἔχοντες differ from the
  reviewed Mark 1:44 and 2:17 (which kept "to the" on τῷ and split the
  article from the participle). I followed the lone-article rule and rule 3,
  as Luke 4 did.
- 5:13: Matthew 8:3 has identical Greek and English but links its opening
  καί to "and" and makes "His" `t`. Here the opening καί is `s`, "and" `t`
  (participle joined to the main verb) and τήν = His.
- 5:3: ἓν τῶν ("one of the") joined = "the". The alternative is ἕν `s`
  with τῶν = "the".
- 5:10: οἳ ἦσαν κοινωνοί joined = "partners" and "were" `t`. "were" stands
  for "were astonished" from 5:9; it is not the ἦσαν of the relative clause.
- 5:12: καὶ ἰδού = "came along" (presentational, kept from the publisher's
  ἰδού row).
