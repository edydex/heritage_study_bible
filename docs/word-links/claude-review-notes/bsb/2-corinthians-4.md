## Doubts for the peer

- 4:1 "since": I put it on ἔχοντες (causal participle, as 3:12) and joined
  καθὼς ἠλεήθημεν = "in + mercy". The publisher put "since" on καθώς;
  καθώς = "since" (Romans 1:28) with ἠλεήθημεν = "in + mercy" alone is the
  alternative.
- 4:6 ἔλαμψεν = "made + shine#1" with "His light#1" `t`. If ἔλαμψεν is read
  as intransitive "shone", "made" `t` is the stricter option.
- 4:2 "ways" is joined into τὰ κρυπτά ("hidden things" → "secret … ways");
  "ways" `t` is the alternative.
- 4:17 four tokens joined as "far beyond comparison". Splitting καθ’
  ὑπερβολήν = "beyond comparison" and εἰς ὑπερβολήν = "far" is the
  alternative.
- 4:7 "to show" `t` with ἵνα = "that"; the publisher's ἵνα = "to show that"
  is the looser option.
- 4:18 "So" `t`; putting it on the genitive absolute σκοπούντων is the
  alternative. "our eyes" stays inside "fix our eyes" (idiom for σκοπέω).
- 4:15 διά "through" = "to#0" linked; `s` + "to#0" `t` is the stricter
  option if the change of meaning is judged too large.
