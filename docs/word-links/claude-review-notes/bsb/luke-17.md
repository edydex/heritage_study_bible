Verses reviewed: 36 (17:1-35 and 17:37). No verse was kept from an earlier
review (the draft had no KEEP blocks). 17:36 has no block: N1904 lacks the
verse and BSB prints it empty, so the draft skipped it and there is nothing
to link or flag. Final check: `fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 36, "problems": 0}`.

## Publisher rows corrected

Names and words supplied by English that had been folded into a Greek word (now `t`):

- "Jesus" sat on Εἶπεν (17:1), πορεύεσθαι (17:11), ἰδών (17:14), εἶπεν (17:19), ἀπεκρίθη (17:20) and εἶπεν "Jesus answered" (17:37, where ὁ of ὁ δέ now = Jesus).
- "People" sat on ἤσθιον (17:27, 17:28) and ἐροῦσιν (17:21 "will people say", 17:23 "People will tell").
- Supplied "and" between asyndetic verbs: ἔπινον "and drinking", ἐγαμίζοντο "and being given in marriage" (17:27); ἔπινον, ἐπώλουν, ᾠκοδόμουν (17:28). Supplied "and" after a participle: ἀνάπεσε "and sit down to eat" (17:7), δοῦναι "and give" (17:18), πορεύου "and go" (17:19), λάμπει "and lights up" (17:24).
- Supplied subject and verb in the sayings: ὧδε "here it is" and Ἐκεῖ "There it is" (17:21), ἐκεῖ "there He is" and ὧδε "here He is" (17:23), Ὅπου "Wherever there is" (17:37) (as Matthew 24:23, 24:26, 24:28).
- 17:1: οὐαί "woe to the one" → woe; `t to the one` (the Greek has only δι’ οὗ, no dative as in Matthew 18:7).
- 17:10: πεποιήκαμεν "we have only done" → we have + done; `t only`.
- 17:17: οἱ "the other" → the, `t other`; ποῦ "Where then are" → Where, δέ = then, `t are`.
- 17:22: ὄψεσθε "you will not see it" → you will + see; οὐκ = not; `t it`. 17:23: διώξητε "chase after them" → chase after; `t them`.
- 17:31: ἀγρῷ "the field" → field; `t the` (no Greek article). 17:12: κώμην "the villages" → villages; `t the`.

Negatives and auxiliaries split off verbs:

- 17:20: Οὐκ "will not" → not; ἔρχεται = will + come. 17:23: μή "Do not" → not; ἀπέλθητε = Do + go out.
- 17:31: μή "let no one" (×2) → no; καταβάτω = let + come down, ἐπιστρεψάτω = let + return; ὅς = one (first clause), ὁ = one (second), as Matthew 24:17-18.
- 17:18: εὑρέθησαν "Was no one found" → Was + one found; οὐχ = no.

"When/As/While" on the wrong word:

- 17:15: δέ "When" → `s`; ἰδών = When + saw. 17:12: καί "As" → `s`; εἰσερχομένου = As + entered.
- 17:11: Καί "While" → Καὶ ἐγένετο `s`; ἐν τῷ = While. 17:14: ἐγένετο "as" → `s`; ἐν τῷ = as. 17:14: ἰδών "When Jesus saw them" → When + saw, αὐτοῖς = them (see decisions).

Prepositions on articles:

- 17:5 τῷ "to the" → the, Κυρίῳ = to + Lord. 17:14 τοῖς "to the" → the, ἱερεῦσιν = to + priests.
- 17:22 τῶν, τοῦ "of the" → the; ἡμερῶν = of + days, Υἱοῦ = of + Son. 17:26 τοῦ "of the" → the, Υἱοῦ = of + Son; second τοῦ "of" → `s`, ἀνθρώπου = of Man.
- 17:4 τῆς "in a" → `s`; ἡμέρας = in a day. 17:7 τοῦ "in the" → the; `t in` (the Greek says ἐκ τοῦ ἀγροῦ, "from the field").
- 17:29, 17:30: ᾗ "on the" → the; ἡμέρᾳ = on + day. 17:34: ταύτῃ "on that" → that; νυκτί = on + night.
- 17:37: τό "a" → `s`; σῶμα = a carcass (as Matthew 24:28).

Other words on the wrong Greek word:

- 17:2: εἰ "to have" → to; περίκειται = have + hung. ἵνα "to cause" → to; σκανδαλίσῃ = cause + to stumble (as Matthew 18:6).
- 17:6: ὡς "the size" → the size of; σινάπεως "of a mustard seed" → mustard; κόκκον = a + seed. ταύτῃ "to this" → this; συκαμίνῳ = to + mulberry tree (as Matthew 17:20).
- 17:9: τὰ διαταχθέντα joined = what he was told (rule 3).
- 17:11: πορεύεσθαι keeps "was on His way" without "Jesus".
- 17:12: ἀπήντησαν "was met" → was met + by; δέκα "by ten" → ten.
- 17:15: μεγάλης "a loud" → loud; φωνῆς = a + voice (as Mark 15:37).
- 17:19: σέσωκεν "has made you well" → has made + well; σε = you (as Luke 8:48, Mark 5:34).
- 17:21: ἐντός "midst" → in + midst; ὑμῶν "in your" → your.
- 17:26: καί "Just" → `s`; καθώς = Just as.
- 17:31: τὰ ὀπίσω joined = anything he has left behind (the publisher had "he has left behind" on ὀπίσω alone).

## Readings

- Only the EVIDENCE lines of 17:1, 17:11, 17:12, 17:16, 17:17, 17:23 and 17:33 show publisher Greek different from N1904.
- Name spelling (rule 8): Samaria 17:11; Samaritan 17:16.
- Same meaning, linked with a note (rule 9): 17:1 δέ = but (probably πλήν in BSB's Greek); 17:17 Οὐχ = not (probably οὐχί); 17:33 καί = but and ἀπολέσει = loses (probably δ’ and the subjunctive ἀπολέσῃ).
- Longer readings, `t` with the reading reason: 17:12 `t He#1` (probably αὐτῷ, "met him", which BSB turns into the passive subject); 17:23 `t or#0` (probably ἤ between the two sayings).
- 17:33: "his life" after "loses" is `t`; the counts show no extra object word in BSB's Greek, so no reading is claimed for it.

## Notable decisions

- 17:1: Ἀνένδεκτον … μή ("impossible that … not") joined = inevitable; τοῦ = that.
- 17:7: ὅς `s` (it resumes Τίς); ἔχων = whose; ἀνάπεσε = sit down to eat.
- 17:10: ὃ ὠφείλομεν ποιῆσαι joined = our duty.
- 17:13: φωνήν = their voices kept (as Luke 11:27 "her voice"); αὐτοί `s`.
- 17:14: αὐτοῖς (dative of εἶπεν) linked to "them" after saw rather than `s` + `t` (as Luke 13:12).
- 17:16: αὐτοῦ = Jesus’ with a note.
- 17:17: οἱ = all kept from the publisher ("the ten" → "all ten").
- 17:24: τῆς ὑπό = one end and τὴν ὑπ’ = the other (each article with its preposition forms "the region under"); first τὸν οὐρανόν = the sky; second οὐρανόν `s`.
- 17:28: ὁμοίως καθώς joined = the same.
- 17:31: σκεύη and αὐτά (the object of ἆραι) joined = possessions (as Matthew 18:21 ἀδελφός … αὐτῷ = brother); καί, ἔσται and ἐν τῇ οἰκίᾳ `s`.
- 17:34: δύο = two people and 17:35 δύο = Two women kept (as Matthew 24:40-41).

## Doubts for the peer

- 17:6: κόκκον = a + seed and σινάπεως = mustard (the "a goes with the noun" convention, as the Luke 13:19 author file). Matthew 17:20 puts "a mustard" on σινάπεως; one of the two should probably be aligned.
- 17:24: the article + preposition joins (τῆς ὑπό = one end, τὴν ὑπ’ = the other) and the second οὐρανόν `s`. The alternative is article = one end / the other with both ὑπό and the second οὐρανόν `s`.
- 17:7: `t in#1` before "the field". BSB's "from plowing or shepherding in the field" reorders ἐκ τοῦ ἀγροῦ; ἐκ stays on "from".
- 17:12: τινα = one of with `t the`. The alternative is τινα = one of the.
- 17:31: τὰ ὀπίσω = anything he has left behind as one unit; the publisher's split (τά = anything, ὀπίσω = he has left behind) is also defensible.
- 17:31: ὅς = one with ἔσται `s`. The alternative is ὃς ἔσται = one.
- 17:9: ἔχει = Does he and χάριν = thank kept from the publisher; the alternative is joining the idiom ἔχει χάριν = Does he thank.
- 17:22: ἡμέραι = The time kept from the publisher, although the Greek has no article.
- 17:17: Οὐχ = not linked as the same negative in another form. If BSB's Greek differs in some other word here, "Were not" should instead be checked against the reading.
