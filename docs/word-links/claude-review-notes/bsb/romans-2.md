# BSB Romans 2: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: 27 (2:2-24, 2:26-29). Kept unchanged: 2:1 and 2:25 (KEEP,
already reviewed by Codex). Final check: `fromtext` →
`{"versesOk": 29, "problems": 0}` (also `check --target BSB`).

Of the 342 publisher `g` rows in the reviewed verses, 94 no longer stand as
they were. About 15 of those only gained a token with the English unchanged
(article + participle and other joined units, listed below). 2:5, 2:6 and
2:24 kept every publisher row (only TODOs resolved).

Conventions followed: Romans 1 (τὰ τοιαῦτα = τά `s` + "such things";
connectives rendered with another connective stay linked; untranslated γάρ/
μέν/οὖν `s`); Acts 17:11 and the "for those who …" Acts rows for an article
joined with its phrase when the English case-word needs a home; Acts 6:9 for
an article that stands as a noun ("men"/"a man"); Acts 23-24 for "that"
before an infinitive or predicate (`t`); the brief's rules on participles (no
person), τε … καί, negatives, vocatives and supplied copulas.

## Publisher rows corrected

Lone articles carrying a preposition (article keeps "the" or `s`, the
preposition goes on the noun or the joined unit):

- 2:4 τῆς "of" → `s`, "of" on χρηστότητος ("of + kindness").
- 2:15 τοῦ "of the" → "the", "of" on νόμου; 2:26 the same.
- 2:18 τά "of what" → joined τὰ διαφέροντα = "what is superior", "of" moved
  to δοκιμάζεις ("approve of").
- 2:19 τῶν "for those" → τῶν ἐν σκότει joined = "for those in darkness".
- 2:8 τοῖς "for those who" → τοῖς ἐξ ἐριθείας joined = "for those who" +
  "self-seeking" (ἐριθείας had "are self-seeking"; "are" `t`).

Articles or pronouns carrying a copula or a word that is not theirs:

- 2:27 ἡ "The one who is" → ἡ = "The", ἀκροβυστία = "one who is" +
  "uncircumcised" (metonymy, as 2:26 "a man who is not circumcised").
- 2:29 ὁ "a man is" → "a man", "is" `t`.
- 2:21 οὖν "you, then" → οὖν = "then", "you" joins ὁ διδάσκων ("you … who
  teach", as the KEEP 2:1 ὁ = "you who").
- 2:23 ὅς "You who" → ὅς = "who", "You" on the finite καυχᾶσαι.
- 2:17 καί "if you", 2:18 καί "if you", 2:19 τε "if you" → the connective
  keeps "if"; "you" moved to the finite verb (ἐπαναπαύῃ, γινώσκεις,
  πέποιθας).
- 2:14 οὗτοι "even though they" → οὗτοι = "they" (subject of εἰσιν, which
  loses "they"); "even though" on the concessive participle ἔχοντες; the
  second "they" `t`.
- 2:3 ὁ "you" → ὁ κρίνων joined = "when" + "pass judgment on"; "you" goes
  with the vocative ἄνθρωπε ("you" + "man"); δέ "So when" → "So".

Supplied words folded into Greek words (now `t`):

- Copulas and "there": 2:8 and 2:9 "there will be"/"There will be" (were on
  ὀργή, θλῖψις), 2:8 "are", 2:15 "is" (γραπτόν = "written"), 2:27 "are"
  (παραβάτην νόμου joined = "a lawbreaker"), 2:28 "is" before circumcision
  (was on περιτομή), 2:29 "is a matter" (was on περιτομή).
- Cleft frame in 2:13: "it is" twice (were on γάρ and ἀλλά) and "who" twice
  (were on δίκαιοι and δικαιωθήσονται).
- 2:7 "He will give" (was "He will give eternal" on αἰώνιον): the verb is
  understood from ἀποδώσει in 2:6, and no cross-verse link is made.
- Participle subjects: 2:18 "you" (κατηχούμενος), 2:20 "you" (ἔχοντα), 2:14
  "they" (ἔχοντες); 2:27 "you" after διά (no verb).
- 2:14 "requires" (was on νόμου in "what the law requires").
- 2:15 "that" (was on ἐνδείκνυνται), "them" (was on ἀπολογουμένων).
- 2:16 "proclaimed by" (κατά keeps "as").
- 2:27 "yet" (was on τελοῦσα; no Greek connective).
- 2:28 and 2:29 "because he is one" (was on ἐν); 2:28 "only" (was with
  φανερῷ) and "and" (was on ἐν before σαρκί); 2:29 "does … come" (was on
  οὐκ, no Greek verb).

Words on the wrong Greek word:

- 2:10 Ἰουδαίῳ and πρῶτον were swapped ("first" / "for the Jew").
- 2:12 "also" moved from ἀπολοῦνται to καί ("will + perish").
- 2:15 καί had "also", μεταξύ "either", ἀλλήλων "and their": καί = "and";
  "also" goes with the συν- of συνμαρτυρούσης ("also bearing witness"); τῶν
  = "their" (article as possessive); ἤ = "either … or"; μεταξὺ ἀλλήλων `s`;
  καί before ἀπολογουμένων `s`.
- 2:16 ᾗ "the" → "when" (see Readings); ἡμέρᾳ = "the day".
- 2:3 τοιαῦτα "the same things" → αὐτά (the object of ποιῶν); τοὺς …
  πράσσοντας joined = "others"; τὰ τοιαῦτα `s`.

Negatives split from verbs: 2:11 οὐ = "not", ἐστιν = "does + show"; 2:14 μή
= "not" twice, the participles keep "do … have"; 2:26 οὐχ = "not",
λογισθήσεται = "will + be regarded"; 2:29 οὐκ = "not".

Joined units with the English unchanged: 2:2 τοὺς … πράσσοντας = "those who
+ do"; 2:7 τοῖς … ζητοῦσιν = "To those who + seek"; 2:9 τοῦ
κατεργαζομένου and 2:10 τῷ ἐργαζομένῳ = "who + does"; 2:14 τὰ … ἔχοντα =
"who + do + have"; 2:21 ὁ κηρύσσων = "You who preach"; 2:22 ὁ λέγων μή =
"You who forbid" (μή inside "forbid") and ὁ βδελυσσόμενος = "You who
abhor"; 2:27 ἐκ φύσεως = "physically"; 2:28 ἐν τῷ φανερῷ = "outwardly" /
"outward", ἐν σαρκί = "physical"; 2:29 ἐν τῷ κρυπτῷ = "inwardly".

## Readings

- 2:15 συνμαρτυρούσης: same word, spelled differently in BSB's Greek text
  (probably συμμαρτυρούσης); linked to "also bearing witness" with a note.
- 2:16: BSB's Greek has one word N1904 lacks (probably ὅτε), which the
  publisher pairs with "when". "when" is linked to N1904's relative ᾗ ("on
  which day" = "on the day when") with a note.

## Notable decisions

- Theological terms linked word by word with no interpretive notes: νόμος =
  "law"/"the law" throughout (2:12 ἀνόμως = "apart from the law", ἐν νόμῳ =
  "under + the law", διὰ νόμου = "by + the law"), κρίμα = "judgment",
  δικαιοκρισία = "righteous judgment", ἔργα = "deeds", ἔργον = "work"/"in
  doing", δίκαιοι = "righteous", δικαιωθήσονται = "will be declared
  righteous", περιτομή = "circumcision", ἀκροβυστία = "uncircumcision" (2:26
  second) or the person ("a man who is not circumcised", "one who is …
  uncircumcised"), γράμμα = "written code", παραβάτην νόμου = "lawbreaker".
- Vocative 2:3 ὦ = "O", ἄνθρωπε = "you" + "man" (the brief's vocative rule;
  KEEP 2:1 gives ὦ ἄνθρωπε πᾶς = "you").
- Connectives rendered with another connective stay linked: 2:3 δέ = "So",
  καί = "yet"; 2:5 καί = "when"; 2:8 δέ = "and"; 2:9 and 2:10 καί = "then";
  2:14 γάρ = "Indeed"; 2:17 δέ = "Now", καί = "if"; 2:18 καί = "if"; 2:19 τε
  = "if"; 2:21 οὖν = "then"; 2:29 ἀλλά = "No". Untranslated: γάρ 2:12, 2:24,
  2:28; μέν 2:7; οὖν 2:26; καί 2:27; list καί 2:4, 2:7, 2:10.
- τε … καί in 2:9 and 2:10 ("first …, then"): τε `s`, καί = "then".
- Pronouns BSB moves into a new role stay linked: 2:6 ὅς = "God", 2:15
  οἵτινες = "So they", 2:29 οὗ = "Such a man's", 2:11 Θεῷ = "God" (subject;
  παρά `s`), 2:19 σεαυτόν = "you".
- Articles that stand as nouns keep their English: 2:28 ὁ = "A man", 2:29 ὁ =
  "a man", 2:14 τά = "what", 2:27 τόν = "who".
- 2:4 ἀγνοῶν = "not realizing" kept as one word (the negation is lexical).
- 2:21 μή = "against" ("preach against stealing").

KEEP verses: no real error found. In 2:1 the two ὁ κρίνων units are linked
article and participle separately ("who"/"you who" + "pass judgment"); the
meaning is right, so this is only a difference from rule 3.

## Doubts for the peer

- 2:3 "the same things" on αὐτά with τὰ τοιαῦτα `s`, against the
  publisher's τοιαῦτα; and τοὺς … πράσσοντας joined = "others" (the
  participle's "practice" is not in "others"; τοὺς alone = "others" with
  πράσσοντας `s` is the alternative).
- 2:3 "you" on ἄνθρωπε; the alternative is "you" on the joined ὁ κρίνων as in
  KEEP 2:1.
- 2:16 ᾗ = "when" under a reading difference; the alternative is the
  publisher's ᾗ = "the" with "when" `t` for the extra word.
- 2:19 τῶν ἐν σκότει joined to keep "for" off the lone article; the cost is
  that ἐν and σκότει lose their own links.
- 2:5 καί = "when" (publisher) rather than "when" on ἀποκαλύψεως.
- 2:15 οἵτινες = "So they" kept; "So" `t` is the alternative. ἤ = "either …
  or" rather than "either" `t`.
- 2:16 κατά = "as" with "proclaimed by" `t`, against the publisher's whole
  "as proclaimed by".
- 2:27 "yet" `t`; it could be read as the participle's own contrast.
- 2:28 and 2:29 ὁ = "A man"/"a man" on a lone article (as Acts 6:9 "men").
- 2:11 ἐστιν = "does + show" for the recast existential; `s` with "does show"
  `t` is the alternative.
