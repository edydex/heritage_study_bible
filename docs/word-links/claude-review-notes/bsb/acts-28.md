# BSB Acts 28: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: all 30 drafted verses (28:1-28, 28:30-31). No KEEP blocks
in this chapter. 28:29 is absent from both N1904 and BSB's main text, so it
has no block and none was added. Final check: `fromtext` →
`{"versesOk": 30, "problems": 0}`.

The draft had 485 publisher `g` rows. 352 are kept as they were. 16 only
gained a token, with the English unchanged: οὐ τὴν τυχοῦσαν = extraordinary
(28:2, as 19:11); Ἐν τοῖς περὶ τὸν τόπον ἐκεῖνον = Nearby (28:7); ἡμέρας
ἑπτά = the week (28:14); τὰ περί = about (28:15, 28:31); ἔλαβε θάρσος = he
was encouraged (28:15); τῷ φυλάσσοντι = to guard (28:16); τοὺς ὄντας =
the (28:17, as 22:5); Ἄνδρες ἀδελφοί = Brothers (28:17, as 22:1); ἃ
φρονεῖς = your views and γνωστὸν … ἐστιν = know (28:22); ἀσύμφωνοι ὄντες
= They disagreed (28:25); οὐ μή = never (28:26, twice); μή ποτε =
Otherwise (28:27); μετὰ πάσης παρρησίας = Boldly (28:31). The other 117
rows, in 29 verses, were corrected, split or moved. Only 28:16 kept every
publisher row and just resolved TODO tokens.

Verse division: N1904 and BSB divide every verse the same way, so there are
no `m` or `g @` cross-verse links.

## Readings

- 28:2 (EVIDENCE 1/1): "showed" stays on παρεῖχαν with the note "Same verb,
  in a different form in BSB's Greek text" (probably παρεῖχον).
- 28:13 (1/1): N1904 περιελθόντες means "sailing around"; BSB's "weighed
  anchor" renders a different word (probably περιελόντες, as NA28). The
  meanings differ, so περιελθόντες is `s` and "weighed anchor" is `t`, each
  with a reading reason (rule 9).
- 28:20 (1/1): εἵνεκεν = "because of" with a spelling note (probably
  ἕνεκεν).
- No other verse claims a reading.

## Publisher rows corrected

Lone articles and numbers carrying a preposition or other words (the
article keeps "the", a possessive, or `s`; the preposition goes on the
noun): 28:3 τῆς "by the" (ἀπό = by, τῆς = the) and τῆς "to" ("to" now
with καθῆψεν: fastened itself to, as 22:1 listen + to); 28:7 τῷ "belonging
to" (now on Ποπλίῳ: belonging to + Publius) and τῆς "of the" (νήσου = of +
island); 28:7 τρεῖς "for three" (ἡμέρας = for + days); 28:9 οἱ "of the"
(now οἱ … ἔχοντες ἀσθενείας = the sick, λοιποί = rest of); 28:15 ὁ "When"
(now on ἰδών); 28:17 τῶν "of the" (Ἰουδαίων = of + Jews); 28:19 τῶν "when
the" (When now on ἀντιλεγόντων); 28:21 τῶν "of the" (ἀδελφῶν = of +
brothers); 28:24 τοῖς "by what" (now joined with λεγομένοις); 28:28 τοῖς
"to the" (ἔθνεσιν = to + Gentiles). 28:20 τοῦ Ἰσραήλ: "of" moved from the
indeclinable name to the article.

Person: "we/I" moved off participles onto a finite verb ending or a
pronoun:

- 28:1 "we#0 were safely ashore" (διασωθέντες): ἐπέγνωμεν = we + we learned
  (as 22:5 I + I was on my way).
- 28:14 "we#0 found" (εὑρόντες): παρεκλήθημεν = we + invited us.
- 28:17 "although I have done" (ποιήσας): Ἐγώ = I + I (as 22:3).
- 28:19 "I#1 have" (ἔχων): ἠναγκάσθην = I was compelled + I.
- 28:10 "when we were ready to sail" (dative participle, no pronoun): "we"
  is now `t`.
- 28:24 "he said" (passive λεγομένοις): "he" is now `t`.

Participle "and" with no καί there (now `t`): 28:2 and welcomed; 28:5 and
suffered; 28:6 and said; 28:7 and entertained; 28:8 and placing (the καί
before after praying keeps and#1); 28:13 and came, and on the second day;
28:15 and traveled, and gave thanks; 28:17 and handed over; 28:18 and
wanted; 28:23 and many people; 28:25 and began to leave; 28:31 and freely.

Other English-only words that had been folded into a Greek word (now `t`):
28:3 "them" (laid); 28:8 "see" and 28:30 "visit" (πρός = to, as 21:18);
28:11 "It"; 28:12 and 28:30 "there"; 28:14 "who"; 28:17 "taken"; 28:18
"there" (there was); 28:20 "It is … that" (cleft; γάρ now `s`); 28:21
"even"; 28:22 "people" (subject of the passive ἀντιλέγεται); 28:23 "to
meet"; 28:25 "this" (ῥῆμα ἕν = final statement) and "was" (Καλῶς = right,
as John 4:17 and 8:48); 28:30 "Paul" (verb ending only).

"When/As/After/Although" moved onto the participle or genitive absolute:
28:1 "Once" (from Καί, now `s`, to διασωθέντες); 28:4 "Although" (from ὅν
to διασωθέντα); 28:9 "After" (from δέ to γενομένου); 28:15 "When" (from ὁ
to ἰδών); 28:17 "When" (from δέ to συνελθόντων); 28:19 "when" (from τῶν to
ἀντιλεγόντων); 28:25 "after" (from Παύλου to εἰπόντος).

Article used as a pronoun and named by BSB (as 10:4, 23:18): 28:5 ὁ = Paul
("Paul" had been on ἀποτινάξας); 28:6 οἱ = The islanders; 28:21 οἱ = The
leaders (both had been inside the verb's row). In 28:5 μὲν οὖν, οὖν = But
and μέν `s` (it had been the other way round).

Negatives split off verbs: 28:4 οὐκ = not, εἴασεν = has + allowed; 28:21
οὔτε = not, ἐδεξάμεθα = have + received.

Other words on the wrong Greek word:

- 28:3 "out" moved from ἀπό to ἐξελθοῦσα (driven out).
- 28:5 "off" moved from εἰς to ἀποτινάξας (shook + off).
- 28:6 ἐπὶ πολύ joined (a long time; "time" had been on ἐπί); αὐτῶν = they
  (the genitive-absolute subject), with "they" taken off μεταβαλόμενοι.
- 28:7 "us#1" moved from ἐξένισεν to ἡμᾶς (us + us, one object of
  participle and main verb, as 16:3); τῷ πρώτῳ split (the / chief
  official).
- 28:8 ἐγένετο `s` (narrative formula, as 22:6); "was" joined κατακεῖσθαι
  (was sick in bed).
- 28:10 πολλαῖς τιμαῖς joined (in many ways; τιμαῖς had carried "ways"
  alone).
- 28:11 "had#1" joined παρασήμῳ (had … as a figurehead); Διοσκούροις = the
  Twin Brothers.
- 28:18 "me#1" moved from ἀπολῦσαι to με (me + me, as 16:3); τό joins
  ὑπάρχειν (articular infinitive, as 10:25).
- 28:21 "have#1" moved from τις to ἀπήγγειλεν (have + reported).
- 28:22 "worth" moved from ἀκοῦσαι to ἀξιοῦμεν (we consider + worth); ἅ
  joined φρονεῖς; παρὰ σοῦ and περί `s`.
- 28:23 "he" (the place he was staying) is now αὐτόν; εἰς = to; πρός `s`;
  ξενίαν = place + was staying.
- 28:26 the cognate pairs split word by word: Ἀκοῇ = ever#0, ἀκούσετε =
  You will be + hearing; βλέποντες = ever#1, βλέψετε = you will be + seeing
  (the same split as the study heads in Matthew 13:14).
- 28:27 τοῖς/τῇ = their (article as possessive, majority convention); the
  noun takes "with" (with + ears, with + eyes, with + hearts); βαρέως =
  hardly (had been inside "they hardly hear").
- 28:30 διετίαν = two + years, ὅλην = full; τοὺς εἰσπορευομένους joined (who
  came).

## Notable decisions

- Names linked despite spelling: Malta (with a note), Publius, Alexandrian,
  the Twin Brothers, Syracuse, Rhegium, Puteoli, the Forum of Appius, the
  Three Taverns, Rome, Jerusalem, Caesar, Isaiah, Moses.
- 28:2 τὸν ἐφεστῶτα ("that had set in") = "it was" (it was raining): the
  perfect of ἐφίστημι means the rain was upon them. The second διά `s`
  (one "because" for both causes).
- 28:3 and 28:31 keep the third-person "as he laid" / "he proclaimed" on the
  participles (convention, 16:30, 22:24, 22:30).
- 28:4 ὅν = him (object of εἴασεν); "Although he was saved" stays together
  on διασωθέντα, since "he" has a different role from ὅν.
- 28:7 the whole phrase Ἐν τοῖς περὶ τὸν τόπον ἐκεῖνον = Nearby; ὀνόματι `s`.
- 28:9 οἱ … ἔχοντες ἀσθενείας = the sick (article + participle + object,
  with ἐν τῇ νήσῳ between them linked on its own).
- 28:17 δέσμιος = prisoner and παρεδόθην = was + handed over; εἰς τὰς
  χεῖρας: εἰς = to, τὰς χεῖρας `s`.
- 28:20 ὑμᾶς = you#0 only; the second "you" (speak with you) is `t`, as the
  Acts 16:39 peer rule links a pronoun twice only for one object shared by a
  participle and its main verb in one clause.
- 28:23 τε (19) = and (a single τε joining the participles); τε (25) of τε …
  καί `s` because BSB has no "both".
- 28:28 γνωστὸν ἔστω = Be advised (ἔστω = Be, the jussive on the verb);
  ὑμῖν `s`; τοῦτο `s`; αὐτοὶ καί: καί = and (BSB's only rendering of it).

## Doubts for the peer

- 28:2 τὸν ἐφεστῶτα = "it was": the alternative is `s` for both tokens and
  "it was" `t` (English turning the nouns into a clause).
- 28:14 παρεκλήθημεν = we + invited us: the alternative is "we#0" `t`. I
  linked it because the ending of the only finite first-person verb owns the
  subject of εὑρόντες (22:5).
- 28:18 με = me + me: the main verb is ἐβούλοντο and the second "me" goes
  with its infinitive ἀπολῦσαι. I treated this as one clause with a shared
  object; the 16:39 alternative is `t`.
- 28:5 οὖν = But: BSB's "But" contrasts with 28:4, while οὖν is "so". I
  linked it as a connective rendered by another connective.
- 28:6 αὐτῶν = they rather than ἔλεγον = they + said.
- 28:24 οἱ μέν = "Some of them" kept on the article (as 19:9 τινες = some of
  them).
- 28:25 ἐλάλησεν = "when He spoke" keeps the recast's "when" with the verb
  (as John 4:17 keeps "to say" with εἶπες).
