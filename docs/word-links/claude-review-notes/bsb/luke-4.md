# BSB Luke 4: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: 44 (4:1-44). No verse was kept from an earlier review (the
draft had no KEEP blocks). Final check: `fromtext` →
`{"versesOk": 44, "problems": 0}`.

125 of the 621 publisher `g` rows changed. 32 of them only gained unlinked
Greek tokens, with the English unchanged (for example οὐκ … οὐδέν = nothing
4:2, ἀποκριθεὶς … εἶπεν = answered 4:8 and 4:12, εἰ μή = but/only 4:26-27,
the genitive absolutes Δύνοντος τοῦ ἡλίου = At sunset 4:40 and Γενομένης
ἡμέρας = At daybreak 4:42). The other 93 rows, in 35 verses, were corrected,
split or moved. Matthew 4:1-11 was followed for the temptation (4:1-13) and
Mark 1:21-39 for Capernaum (4:31-44).

## Publisher rows corrected

Names and words supplied by English that had been folded into a Greek word (now `t`):

- 4:5: ἀναγαγών "the devil led Him up to a high place" → led + up; `t the devil`, `t to a high place` (the verse's EVIDENCE line shows no different reading, so the place is BSB's addition, not a reading).
- 4:9: Ἤγαγεν "the devil led" → led; ἔστησεν "set Him" → set; `t the devil`, `t Him` (N1904 has no αὐτόν after ἔστησεν).
- 4:16, 4:23, 4:38, 4:42: "Jesus" sat on ἦλθεν, εἶπεν, Ἀναστάς, ἐξελθών; now `t Jesus`.
- 4:6: ταύτην "these kingdoms" → these; ἅπασαν "over all" → all; καί "and all" → and; `t over`, `t kingdoms`, `t all#1` (the Greek says "this authority, all of it").
- 4:10: διαφυλάξαι "to guard You carefully" → τοῦ διαφυλάξαι = to guard + carefully; σε = You.
- 4:11: χειρῶν "their hands" → hands, `t their` (as Matthew 4:6).
- 4:12: Εἴρηται "It also says" → It + says, `t also` (Luke has no πάλιν).
- 4:18: Κυρίου "of the Lord is" → of the Lord, `t is`. 4:36: οὗτος "is this" → this, `t is`.
- 4:20: ἀποδούς "returned it" → returned, `t it`.
- 4:23: ἠκούσαμεν "we have heard that" → we have heard; γενόμενα "You did" → that + did, `t You`.
- 4:25: ἀληθείας "truthfully that" → ἐπ’ ἀληθείας = truthfully, `t that`.
- 4:41: ἐπιτιμῶν "He rebuked the demons" → He rebuked, `t the demons`.

Negatives and auxiliaries:

- 4:4: ζήσεται "shall not live" → shall + live; Οὐκ = not (as Matthew 4:4).
- 4:11: μή "so that You will not" → μή ποτε = so that + not; προσκόψῃς = You will + strike (as Matthew 4:6).
- 4:12: ἐκπειράσεις "Do not put" and Θεόν "God to the test" → Do + put + to the test; Οὐκ = not; Θεόν = God (as Matthew 4:7).
- 4:26: ἐπέμφθη "was not sent" → was + sent; οὐδεμίαν = not + any (it is the only negative).
- 4:41: εἴα "and would not allow" → would + allow; οὐκ = not; `t and`.
- 4:7: ἔσται "it will all be" → it will + be; πᾶσα = all.

"When", "After" and "where" on the wrong word:

- 4:2: τεσσεράκοντα "where for forty" → forty; ἡμέρας = for + days (accusative of duration); `t where`. αὐτῶν "when they" → they; the genitive absolute participle συντελεσθεισῶν = when + had ended.
- 4:13: Καί "When" → `s`; συντελέσας = When + had finished.
- 4:38: δέ "After" → `s`; Ἀναστὰς ἀπό = After + had left.
- 4:16: καί "And when" → And. "when" now links to the καί that opens 4:17 (`g @4:17 0` in 4:16, `m 0 > 4:16` in 4:17), the καί between "stood up" and "was handed".

Supplied "and" after a participle (now `t`): 4:5 ἔδειξεν, 4:15 δοξαζόμενος, 4:20 ἐκάθισεν, 4:30 ἐπορεύετο, 4:35 ἐξῆλθεν, 4:39 ἐπετίμησεν and διηκόνει, 4:41 εἴα.

Prepositions and "the" on a lone article, and "a" with its noun:

- 4:5 τῆς, 4:9 τοῦ, 4:14 τοῦ, 4:29 τοῦ, 4:43 τήν: "of the" → the; "of" moved to the genitive noun. 4:20 τῷ and 4:43 ταῖς: "to the" → the; "to" moved to the dative noun. 4:29 τῆς "of" → the; ἔξω = out of.
- 4:1: Ἁγίου "of the Holy" → Holy; Πνεύματος = of the + Spirit.
- 4:17: βιβλίον "the scroll of" → the scroll; προφήτου = of + prophet.
- 4:33: ἀκαθάρτου "of an unclean" → unclean; δαιμονίου = of an + demon. μεγάλῃ "in a loud" → loud; φωνῇ = in a + voice (as Mark 1:26).
- 4:38: μεγάλῳ "a high" → high; πυρετῷ = a + fever. αὐτῆς "her behalf" → her; περί = on + behalf.

Other words on the wrong Greek word:

- 4:11: ἀροῦσιν "they will lift" + σε "You up" → they will lift + up; σε = You.
- 4:29: κατακρημνίσαι "to throw Him over the cliff" → to throw + over the cliff; αὐτόν = Him.
- 4:35: ῥίψαν "threw the man down" → threw + down; αὐτόν = the man (as Mark 1:26). εἰς τὸ μέσον = before them all.
- 4:34: σοί "do You want" → the idiom τί ἡμῖν καὶ σοί = What + You + with us, with study heads; `t do`, `t want` (identical to Mark 1:24).
- 4:40: ἐθεράπευεν "He healed" → healed; ὁ (with δέ) = He. εἶχον "who were" → `s` (BSB recasts "all who had sick people brought them" as "all who were ill were brought"); ὅσοι = who; ἀσθενοῦντας = were ill; αὐτούς `s`.
- 4:43: εἶπεν "Jesus told" → told; ὁ (with δέ) = Jesus; πρὸς αὐτούς = them.
- 4:22: τοῖς ἐκπορευομένοις joined (rule 3) = that came; Οὐχί … ἐστιν = Isn’t.
- 4:16: κατά "As was" → As, `t was`; τὸ εἰωθός = custom; ἡμέρᾳ τῶν σαββάτων = Sabbath; εἰσῆλθεν … εἰς = He entered.
- Periphrastic ἦν + participle joined: ἦν διδάσκων = began to teach (4:31), ἦν κηρύσσων = continued to preach (4:44); ἐν ἐξουσίᾳ ἦν = had (4:32).

## Notable decisions

- Readings: only 4:17, 4:25 and 4:26 show publisher Greek different from N1904. 4:25-26 are the spelling Ἡλείας/Ἠλίας (Elijah), linked under rule 8. In 4:17 "Unrolling" is linked to N1904's ἀνοίξας ("opening") with a note: BSB's Greek reads ἀναπτύξας, the same action for a scroll (rule 9, same meaning).
- 4:5 "to a high place" and 4:9 "Him" stay `t`. The EVIDENCE lines show the publisher's Greek equals N1904 there, so no longer reading was claimed.
- Temptation parallels kept consistent with Matthew 4: "the Son" on Υἱός (Matthew 4:6), shall + live / not, they will lift + up, so that + not, Do + put + to the test.
- Capernaum parallels kept consistent with Mark 1: 4:34 = Mark 1:24, 4:35 `s` for the καί between the commands and for ἀπ’ αὐτοῦ, "with various" on ποικίλαις in 4:40 (as Mark 1:34), "a solitary" + "place" in 4:42, ἐπὶ τοῦτο = that is why (Mark 1:38).
- Pronouns rendered by nouns are linked with a note: αὐτός = Jesus (4:30), αὐτοῦ = Jesus (4:37), αὐτόν = Jesus (4:38, 4:40), αὐτῷ = the demon (4:35), αὐτούς = the people (4:31). ὁ διάβολος = he (4:6).
- Joined units: στιγμῇ χρόνου = an instant (4:5), οὗ εἵνεκεν = because (4:18), ἀποστεῖλαι … ἐν ἀφέσει = to release (4:18), μῆνας ἕξ = a half (4:25), γυναῖκα χήραν = the widow (4:26), ἐπλήσθησαν θυμοῦ = were enraged (4:28), μέσου αὐτῶν = the crowd (4:30), εἰς πάντα τόπον = throughout (4:37), κραυγάζοντα καὶ λέγοντα = shouting (4:41), τοῦ μή = from (4:42).

## Doubts for the peer

- 4:16-17: "when" is linked across the verse break to the καί opening 4:17, following the "When goes on the καί between the clauses" convention. Keeping "And when" on the καί of 4:16 and giving the καί of 4:17 an `s` is the alternative.
- 4:6: ταύτην = these and ἅπασαν = all#0, with `t over` and `t kingdoms`. BSB reworks "this authority, all of it" into "authority over all these kingdoms"; leaving ταύτην `s` with `t these` is the alternative.
- 4:26: εἰς Σάρεπτα = "of Zarephath" (the εἰς-phrase recast as the widow's town), with πρός = "to". The alternative is εἰς = `s`.
- 4:19 and 4:35 kept publisher rows: Κυρίου = "of the Lord’s" (rather than "of" on δεκτόν = favor), and ἀπ’ = "out of" in "Come out of him" (the "out" could be read as ἔξελθε's prefix; kept for consistency with Mark 1:25).
- 4:40: "with various" on ποικίλαις follows Mark 1:34. Elsewhere in this chapter the dative "in a" goes with its noun (4:33).
