# BSB Acts 5: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: all 42 (5:1-42). No KEEP blocks in this chapter. Final
check: `fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 42, "problems": 0}`.

134 of the 623 publisher `g` rows changed, across all 42 verses. About 20
of them only gained unlinked Greek tokens or were merged into one group,
with the English unchanged: article + participle (5:11 τοὺς ἀκούοντας,
5:7 τὸ γεγονός, 5:9 τῶν θαψάντων, 5:32 τοῖς πειθαρχοῦσιν);

No parallel Gospel chapter. Conventions followed from the reviewed Gospels:
article as pronoun (ὁ δέ / οἱ δέ) linked to the English pronoun or noun
with the standard note; "The preposition repeats the verb's prefix and is
absorbed" for 5:21 εἰς and 5:38 ἀπό; Luke 22:4 / 22:52 for the temple
officers; Luke 21:34-35 and 7:18-19 for the verse-division links.

## Readings

- 5:1 Σαπφείρῃ and 5:35 Ἰσραηλεῖται: linked with a spelling note (probably
  Σαπφίρῃ and Ἰσραηλῖται in BSB's Greek).
- 5:33: "resolved" linked to ἐβούλοντο with a note that BSB's Greek differs
  at this verb (probably ἐβουλεύοντο); "resolved" fits both.
- 5:12: flagged 1/1; probably ἅπαντες for πάντες (same meaning). πάντες is
  linked without claiming a reading; "Solomon's" is linked plainly.
- 5:28: one extra publisher word; BSB's English needs no extra word, so
  nothing is claimed.
- 5:39/5:40: a real verse-division difference. N1904 ends 5:39 with
  ἐπείσθησαν δὲ αὐτῷ; BSB opens 5:40 with it ("At this, they yielded to
  Gamaliel"). 5:39 has `m 14 15 16 > 5:40`; 5:40 links them with `g @5:39`
  (δέ = "At this", αὐτῷ = "to Gamaliel" with a note).

## Notable decisions

- 5:4: the one οὐχί governs both halves of the question, so both "not"s
  link to it (understood again). μένον = "before it was sold".
- 5:8: τοσούτου ("for so much") = "this … the price" and "that … the
  price"; the interrogative εἰ is `s`.
- 5:12: διὰ τῶν χειρῶν ("through the hands") is `s`; "performed" stays on
  ἐγίνετο alone, so "hands" is not attached to a word that does not mean it.
- 5:17: Ἀναστάς is linked to "They went out" at the end of the verse
  (publisher row kept, note added).
- 5:24: "temple guard" stays on ἱεροῦ, with a note on the title (compare
  Luke 22:4 "temple officers").
- 5:41: Οἱ μὲν οὖν, article as pronoun, = "The apostles"; μὲν οὖν `s`.
- 5:42: τὸν Χριστὸν Ἰησοῦν becomes "that Jesus is the Christ": "that" and
  "is" are `t`, τόν = "the", Χριστόν = "Christ", Ἰησοῦν = "Jesus".

## Doubts for the peer

- 5:12 πάντες = "the believers" (with note). The alternative is πάντες `s`
  + "the believers" `t`.
- 5:10 καί (16) = "and" after "came in": BSB recasts the participles, and
  this is the only English "and" that coordinates the clauses the way the
  καί does. The other "and" (carried … and buried) is `t`.
- 5:27 one αὐτούς linked to both "them"s (object of ἀγαγόντες and
  ἔστησαν). In 5:6 and 5:10 the repeated object pronouns are `t` because the
  Greek has no pronoun for those verbs.
- 5:7 καί = "also" and 5:27 καί = "where" were kept as publisher links with
  notes; either could be `s` + `t`.
- 5:38 "their" for ἡ … αὕτη ("this"), with τὸ … τοῦτο `s`.
