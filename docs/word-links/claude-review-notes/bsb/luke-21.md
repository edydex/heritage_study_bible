# BSB Luke 21: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: 37 (21:1-36, 21:38). Kept unchanged: 21:37, reviewed
earlier by Codex (KEEP block, identical to the base). Final check:
`fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 38, "problems": 0}`.

88 of the 470 publisher `g` rows changed, in 30 verses. 17 of them only
gained unlinked Greek tokens, with the English unchanged (21:4 τοῦ
περισσεύοντος = surplus, τοῦ ὑστερήματος = poverty, τὸν βίον = to live on,

ἐπιβαλοῦσιν ἐφ’ … τὰς χεῖρας αὐτῶν = they will seize; 21:15 οὐ … ἅπαντες =

γεγραμμένα = that is written; 21:23 ταῖς ἐν γαστρὶ ἐχούσαις = for pregnant,

21:30 ὅταν … ἤδη = When; 21:34 μή ποτε = or). 8 more were pairs merged into

21:35 τοὺς καθημένους, 21:36 τὰ μέλλοντα). The other 63 rows were corrected,

Parallels followed: Mark 12:41-44 (widow's offering; 21:4 has the same
English as Mark 12:44, but Luke's Greek differs), Mark 13 and Matthew 24
(21:6 "one stone … every one", 21:7 "when will … happen", "will be" `t`,
ὅταν = that, 21:8 "I am" + `t He`, 21:9 μή = not / do + be alarmed, 21:11
"There will be" for both verbs, 21:21 οἱ ἐν τῇ Ἰουδαίᾳ = those + `t who
are`, 21:23 "How miserable … for pregnant", 21:27 "At that time", 21:31
emphatic ὑμεῖς = you, 21:32-33 οὐ μή = not/never with the verb keeping will).

## Publisher rows corrected

English words with no Greek counterpart that had been folded into a Greek
word (now `t`):

- "Jesus" sat on Ἀναβλέψας (21:1), εἶπεν (21:5, 21:29); now `t`.
- 21:3: πάντων "than all the others" → than all; `t the others` (Mark 12:44
  links "the others" to τῶν βαλλόντων, which Luke lacks).
- 21:5: τινων "some of the disciples" → some; `t of the disciples`.
- 21:6: θεωρεῖτε "you see here" → you see; `t here` (EVIDENCE: publisher
  Greek = N1904; Mark 13:2's "here" was a reading, this one is not).
- 21:7: τί "what will be" → what; `t will be` (as Mark 13:4).
- 21:8: εἰμι "am He" → am; `t He` (as Mark 13:6).
- 21:9: οὐκ "is not" → not; `t is` (nominal clause).
- 21:12: διώξουσιν "persecute you" → persecute; παραδιδόντες "they will
  deliver you" → they will deliver; `t you` twice (objects English
  supplies). "and" before "they will bring you" → `t` (participle made
  finite).
- 21:14: ἀπολογηθῆναι "how to defend yourselves" → to defend yourselves;
  `t how`.
- 21:23: ἡμέραις "days will be" → days; `t will be` (as Mark 13:17).
- 21:30: γινώσκετε "and know" → know; `t and` (participle βλέποντες joined
  to the main verb).
- 21:32: πάντα "all these things" → all + things; `t these` (Luke has no
  ταῦτα, unlike Mark 13:30 / Matthew 24:34).
- 21:36: δεόμενοι "and pray" → pray; `t and`.

Words on the wrong Greek word, or missing links:

- 21:1: εἶδεν "and saw" → saw; τοὺς βάλλοντας joined (rule 3) = the … putting.
- 21:4: πάντες "they all" → all; οὗτοι ("these", was TODO) = they.
  ἔβαλον εἰς τὰ δῶρα = contributed (εἰς τὰ δῶρα was TODO).
- 21:5: Καί "As" → `s`; As now belongs to the genitive absolute λεγόντων
  (As + were remarking). περί `s` (the temple becomes the subject of the
  how-clause).
- 21:6: οὐκ "not one" → not; λίθος = one stone (as Mark 13:2). ὃς οὐ = every
  one (οὐ was TODO; as Mark 13:2 / Matthew 24:2).
- 21:7: πότε "when will" → when; ἔσται = will + happen (as Mark 13:4).
- 21:8: εἶπεν "Jesus answered" → answered; ὁ = Jesus (ὁ δέ convention).
  πλανηθῆτε "that you are not deceived" → you are + deceived; μή = that +
  not (as Matthew 24:6 / Mark 13:5). μή "Do not" → not; πορευθῆτε ὀπίσω =
  Do + follow (ὀπίσω was TODO).
- 21:9: μή "do not" → not; πτοηθῆτε = do + be alarmed (as Mark 13:7).
- 21:11: λοιμοί / λιμοί / καί (TODO) linked by meaning; ἔσονται and ἔσται
  share "There will be" (as Mark 13:8); τε (1) and καί (3) `s`.
- 21:13: ἀποβήσεται "This will be your opportunity" → This will be +
  opportunity; ὑμῖν (TODO) = your.
- 21:14: θέτε ἐν ταῖς καρδίαις = make up + mind (one idiom; ἐν, ταῖς were
  TODO).
- 21:15: ἀντικείμενοι "your adversaries" → οἱ ἀντικείμενοι = adversaries,
  ὑμῖν = your.
- 21:21: "let#0 those who are" sat on οἱ and "let#1" on καί; now each
  imperative carries its own let (let + flee, let + get out, let + stay out
  of), οἱ = those, `t who are` (as Matthew 24:16), καί (9) `s`. ἐν μέσῳ = in;
  αὐτῆς = the city; μὴ εἰσερχέσθωσαν εἰς = let + stay out of.
- 21:23: the article τῷ carried "against"; now λαῷ = against + people,
  τῷ `s`.
- 21:25: ἐν "by" → ἐν ἀπορίᾳ = bewildered; ἤχους = by the roaring.
- 21:26: τῷ carried "upon the" and τῶν "of the". Now τῶν ἐπερχομένων = over
  what is coming upon (ἐπ- "upon"), τῇ = the, τῶν = the, οὐρανῶν = of +
  heavens (lone article never carries a preposition; Luke 16:17 precedent).
- 21:28: δέ "When" → `s`; ἀρχομένων = When + begin (genitive absolute).
- 21:31: ἴδητε "you see" → see; ὑμεῖς = you (as Mark 13:29).
- 21:32: παρέλθῃ "will not pass away" → will + pass away; οὐ μή = not.
- 21:33: παρελεύσονται "will never pass away" → will + pass away; οὐ μή =
  never (as Matthew 24:35).
- 21:38: λαός "people would come" → people; ὤρθριζεν = early in the morning
  + would come; πρὸς αὐτόν `s` (Him is the object of hear).

Readings (only where EVIDENCE shows a difference):

- 21:11: English order "famines … pestilences" follows a reading that
  differs from N1904's λοιμοὶ καὶ λιμοί; probably the same two nouns in the
  other order. Linked by meaning with a note.
- 21:19: κτήσεσθε = you will gain, with a note. BSB's Greek probably has the
  aorist imperative κτήσασθε here, but the English future renders N1904's
  word exactly (rule 9, same-meaning case).
- 21:34-35: real verse-division difference. N1904 prints ὡς παγίς at the
  start of 21:35; BSB ends 21:34 with "like a snare". Linked cross-verse
  (`g @21:35 0` = like, `g @21:35 1` = a snare; `m 0 1 > 21:34` in 21:35).

## Notable decisions

- 21:1: τοὺς βάλλοντας … πλουσίους: the article goes with the participle, so
  "the" + "putting" form one group and πλουσίους = rich, even though English
  makes "the rich" the head.
- 21:2: ἐκεῖ ("there") = in, as the publisher had, with a note: "put in" =
  put there, into the treasury.
- 21:12: "you" after persecute and deliver is `t` (English-only objects),
  but "you" in "they will bring you" stays with ἀπαγομένους. That participle
  is accusative, agreeing with "you", and BSB turns the passive "(you)
  being led away" into an active verb.
- 21:20: γνῶτε (imperative) = you will know, with a note; τότε `s`.
- 21:22: ὅτι = For; "the days" kept on ἡμέραι (no Greek article; English
  "the" goes with the noun).
- 21:24: στόματι = by the edge (idiom "mouth of the sword").
- 21:30: προβάλωσιν = they sprout leaves (the verb is used of trees putting
  out leaves; "leaves" kept, with a note); second ἤδη `s`.
- 21:36: ταῦτα `s` ("all that is about to happen" renders πάντα τὰ μέλλοντα;
  compare Mark 13:4, where πάντα was `s`).

## Doubts for the peer

- 21:4: ἔβαλον εἰς τὰ δῶρα = contributed. I took "contributed" to include
  "into the offerings". The alternative is to leave εἰς τὰ δῶρα `s`
  ("not separately expressed").
- 21:3: πάντων = than all, with `t the others`. The publisher had "than all
  the others" on πάντων, and one could argue "the others" just spells out
  the genitive of comparison.
- 21:13: "opportunity" sits with ἀποβήσεται ("it will turn out"); it could
  instead be `t`.
- 21:11: τε (11) = along with. It is the clause-joining particle, but "along
  with" could also be read as English glue for the second ἔσται clause.
- 21:5: "with" stays on καλοῖς (with beautiful), as the publisher had. The
  dative covers λίθοις καλοῖς together, so it could move to the noun.
