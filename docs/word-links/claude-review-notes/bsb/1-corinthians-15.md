Verses reviewed: 57 of 58 (15:1-18, 15:20-58). Kept unchanged: 15:19 (KEEP
block, reviewed earlier by Codex). Final check: `fromtext` →

164 of the 724 publisher `g` rows changed (15:19 not counted), in 54 of the
57 reviewed verses; 15:7, 15:43 and 15:52 keep every publisher row (15:52
only gains an `s` for καί). 8 of the changed rows only joined a token with

Readings: EVIDENCE shows the publisher's Greek differs from N1904 in 15:10,
15:14, 15:17, 15:24, 15:28, 15:49 and 15:54.

- 15:10: one extra word, which the publisher linked to "that was" (probably
  the article ἡ before σὺν ἐμοί). "that was" is `t`, with a reading note.
- 15:14: one extra word (probably καί after ἄρα). No English depends on it;
  nothing claimed.
- 15:17: one extra word, which the publisher linked to "is". N1904 has no
  verb there, so "is" is a supplied copula (`t`) with a reading note.
- 15:24: παραδιδοῖ is spelled differently in BSB's Greek (probably
  παραδιδῷ). Same verb, linked to "He hands over" with a note.
- 15:28: one extra word (probably τά before the last πάντα). No English
  depends on it; nothing claimed.
- 15:49: N1904 φορέσωμεν ("let us bear"); BSB's Greek probably has the
  future φορέσομεν. Linked to "shall we bear" with a note, following Romans
  5:1 (ἔχωμεν / "we have").
- 15:54: one extra word, which the publisher linked to "the" before
  "imperishable". That "the" now goes with ἀφθαρσίαν, as in 15:53 where
  N1904 also has no article. No reading is claimed in the review file.

Conventions followed: Acts 2:24 and 1 Corinthians 6:14 for "raised … from
the dead" on the raising verb (15:15); 1 Corinthians 4:17 for "remind … of"
on the verb (15:1); 1 Thessalonians 5:21 for "hold … to" on κατέχω (15:2);
1 Corinthians 9:1 and 9:9 for negatives split off verbs; Romans 5:1 for the
mood variant; README rule 3 for article + participle units; John 15:2 for

## Doubts for the peer

- 15:5 δώδεκα carries "to#1 + Twelve". The dative shows only on the
  article, but "to the" may not sit on a lone article; check whether "to"
  should stay on the number.
- 15:26 "is" is `t` (identifying copula); the publisher had it in
  καταργεῖται's group ("to be destroyed is").
- 15:2 τίνι linked to "the" ("with what word" rendered "the word"); `s` is
  possible.
- 15:14 κενή (second) = "so" and καί = "and" in "and so is"; one could
  argue "so" renders καί.
- 15:20 Νυνί = "indeed"; 15:42 ἐν `s` with φθορᾷ/ἀφθαρσίᾳ as adjectives
  instead of joining ἐν with the noun.
- 15:54 keeps the publisher's "has been clothed with" on the verb, while
  15:53 has "with" on the noun (both publisher rows, not wrong).
