# BSB Matthew 28: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: 20 of 20 (no verse was kept from an earlier reviewer).

Check: `fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 20, "problems": 0}`.

29 publisher rows in 16 verses were changed because they were wrong or
over-claimed. Another 3 rows were joined under rule 3 (article + participle),
9 rows were reshaped only because a neighbouring correction moved a word to
them (for example "Do" moving to the imperative), and 7 rows only gained a
TODO token or word.

## Publisher rows corrected

- 28:1: μίαν = "the first day" → the first, `t day` (the noun is implied by
  the feminine numeral; N1904 has no word for it).
- 28:3: ἔνδυμα = "clothes were" → clothes. The one ἦν now carries was + were,
  since it also serves the verbless second clause.
- 28:5: Μή = "Do not" → not; φοβεῖσθε = Do + be afraid. ὑμεῖς = "you" was
  wrong: that "you" is the subject of ζητεῖτε (you are looking for), and the
  emphatic ὑμεῖς of the first clause is now `s`.
- 28:7: εἴπατε = "and tell" → tell, `t and` (after the participle
  πορευθεῖσαι).
- 28:8: ἔδραμον = "and ran" → ran, `t and` (after ἀπελθοῦσαι).
- 28:9: καί = "Suddenly" was the wrong anchor → ἰδού = Suddenly, καί `s`.
  λέγων = "and said" → said, `t and`. προσελθοῦσαι = "They came to Him" →
  came to; αἱ = They (article as pronoun); `t Him` (the participle has no
  object).
- 28:10: Μή = "Do not" → not; φοβεῖσθε = Do + be afraid. ἀπαγγείλατε = "and
  tell" → tell, `t and` (ὑπάγετε ἀπαγγείλατε has no conjunction).
- 28:11: δέ = "While" was wrong → δέ `s`; While + were on their way
  (genitive absolute). ἀπήγγειλαν = "and reported" → reported, `t and`.
- 28:12: καί = "And after" → And; συναχθέντες = "the chief priests had met" →
  after + had met, `t the chief priests` (the participle has no expressed
  subject).
- 28:13: λέγοντες = "and instructed them" → instructed, `t and`, `t them`.
  ἔκλεψαν = "and stole" → stole + away, `t and`; αὐτόν = "Him away" → Him.
  ἡμῶν = "while we" → we; κοιμωμένων = while + were asleep (genitive
  absolute).
- 28:14: τοῦτο = "this report" → this, `t report`.
- 28:15: ἐποίησαν = "and did" → did, `t and`. τῆς = "this" and σήμερον =
  "very" were wrong: σήμερον ("today") = this very, and the article is `s`.
- 28:17: καί = "When" was wrong → καί `s`; ἰδόντες = When + they saw.
  προσεκύνησαν = "they worshiped Him" → they worshiped; the one αὐτόν now
  carries Him + Him.
- 28:18: ἐλάλησεν = "and said" → ἐλάλησεν … λέγων = said, `t and` (after
  προσελθών).
- 28:19: πορευθέντες = "go and" → go, `t and`. πάντα = "of all" → all; "of"
  joins the verb (make disciples of).
- 28:20: διδάσκοντες = "and teaching" → teaching, `t and` (no conjunction
  between βαπτίζοντες and διδάσκοντες).

## Notable decisions

- Joint units from the TODO list: τῇ ἐπιφωσκούσῃ = at dawn (28:1);
  ἀποκριθεὶς … εἶπεν = said (28:5); ἀπελθοῦσαι ταχύ = they hurried away
  (28:8); συμβούλιον … λαβόντες = formed a plan (28:12); ἀκουσθῇ … ἐπί =
  reaches (28:14); πάσας τὰς ἡμέρας = always (28:20).
- Article + participle joined (rule 3): οἱ τηροῦντες = The guards (28:4),
  τὸν ἐσταυρωμένον = who was crucified (28:5), τὰ γενόμενα = that had
  happened (28:11). In 28:5 and 28:11 the publisher had put "who" / "that"
  on the lone article.
- καὶ ἰδού = Suddenly (28:2, 28:9): ἰδού carries Suddenly and καί is `s`.
  The other ἰδού: 28:7 (first) and 28:11 are `s`; 28:7 (second) = See;
  28:20 = surely.
- Pronoun rendered by its referent kept from the publisher: αὐτῶν = the women
  (28:11); οἱ (δέ) = the guards (28:15).
- 28:2: καὶ προσελθών is not rendered in BSB's condensed series (`s`, no
  textual difference claimed).
- 28:14: "him" is `t`; the reason notes that N1904 has no object after
  πείσομεν while many manuscripts read πείσομεν αὐτόν. No link was invented.
- Kept from the publisher on purpose: Ὀψέ = After; εἰς = on (28:1); ἀπό = in
  (28:4); καί = Then / So (28:7, 28:8, 28:18); δέ = Meanwhile (28:16);
  Εἴπατε = You are to say (28:13); ἀργύρια = sum of money; ἕως = even to
  (28:20).

## Doubts for the peer

- 28:17: the one αὐτόν is linked to both "Him"s (shared object of ἰδόντες and
  προσεκύνησαν), following 21:39. The alternative is `t Him#1`; the Byzantine
  text reads προσεκύνησαν αὐτῷ, which N1904 lacks, so a reading reason could
  also be argued.
- 28:1: "day" is `t` (as with "son" in 21:30). In the idiom μία σαββάτων one
  could instead keep "first day" on μίαν.
- 28:3: ἦν = was + were (gapped copula, as with εἰσιν in 22:14). The
  alternative is `t were`.
- 28:15: τῆς σήμερον: the article is `s` and σήμερον = this very. Joining τῆς
  σήμερον as one attributive unit is also defensible.
- 28:18: αὐτοῖς = them stays linked although Greek construes it with ἐλάλησεν
  and BSB places it after "came to".
- 28:20: "even" stays inside ἕως = even to; it may be BSB emphasis that
  deserves `t`.
