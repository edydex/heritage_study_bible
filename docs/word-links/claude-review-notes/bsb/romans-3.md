## Publisher rows corrected

Lone articles carrying a copula, preposition or noun:

- 3:1 "is the" on τό → the; "of" on τοῦ → moved to the noun (of being a
  Jew), τοῦ `s`.
- 3:5 "That" on ὁ → ὁ `s`, "That" `t` (English frame for the question).
- 3:23 "of the" on τῆς → the; "of" joins the verb (fall short of).
- 3:24 "by" on τῇ → by + grace on χάριτι, τῇ `s`.
- 3:27 "On that" on τῶν → τῶν `s` (see decisions below).
- 3:29 "Is God the" on ὁ → ὁ `s`; God = Θεός.

Supplied copulas, "there", verbs and objects folded into Greek words (now
`t`): 3:1 what is (is), 3:5 μή = is (is `t`, μή `s`), 3:13 throats are, is
on, 3:15 feet are, 3:16 lie in, 3:19 to those who are, 3:22 comes through,
3:24 is in, 3:27 then, is (on οὖν), 3:29 Is He not, 3:30 since there is only
(there is only `t`); 3:5 wrath on us (on us `t`); 3:22 this righteousness
(supplied demonstrative `t`).

Modal and person words on the wrong Greek word: 3:3 "Will their" → their =
αὐτῶν, Will + nullify = καταργήσει; 3:6 "could God" → could + judge =
κρινεῖ; 3:7 "am I" → I = κἀγώ, am + condemned = κρίνομαι; 3:31 "Do we,
then, nullify" → then = οὖν, Do we + nullify; 3:4 "Let God" → Let + be =
γινέσθω (jussive "let" on the imperative), God = Θεός.

Negatives split: 3:8 not say → not (say `t`); 3:17 they have not known → not
= οὐκ, they have + known. In 3:10-12 "no one" had been on οὐκ: no = οὐκ,
and "one" goes with the substantive (3:10 one righteous = δίκαιος; 3:11,
3:12 one who understands/seeks/does = article + participle joined, rule 3).
3:22 who + believe joined the same way.

μὴ γένοιτο: 3:4, 3:6, 3:31 "Certainly not" had been on γένοιτο alone; now
μὴ γένοιτο together.

## Readings and verse division

- 3:4: EVIDENCE shows one word differs. καθάπερ and English "As" were the
  only unlinked pair, so the different word is probably καθώς; linked As =
  καθάπερ with a same-meaning note.
- 3:25/3:26: the publisher's Greek puts ἐν τῇ ἀνοχῇ τοῦ Θεοῦ in 3:25 (BSB
  "because in His forbearance"), and probably reads διὰ τῆς πίστεως. Real
  verse-division difference: 3:26 has `m 0 1 2 3 4 > 3:25`; 3:25 links
  @3:26 ἐν = in, τῇ ἀνοχῇ = forbearance, τοῦ Θεοῦ = His. The extra article
  has no English counterpart, so nothing is claimed for it; ἱλαστήριον = as
  the atoning sacrifice (as 1 John 4:10), διὰ πίστεως = through faith.

## Notable decisions

- Theological terms are linked word for word, no interpretive notes:
  δικαιοσύνη = righteousness, δικαιόω = be justified / justify, πίστις =
  faith, νόμος = law (principle in 3:27), χάρις = grace, ἀπολύτρωσις =
  redemption, ἱλαστήριον = atoning sacrifice, καύχησις = boasting.
- Connectives: διότι = Therefore (3:20), ἐπεί = In that case (3:6), δέ =
  Now/But/However/And; unexpressed γάρ (3:2, 3:3, 3:22), μέν (3:2), ἤ (3:29)
  and 3:4 first δέ are `s`.
- 3:9 τε = alike, kept: "Jews and Greeks alike" renders τε … καί (Acts 18:4,
  19:10, 20:21).
- 3:27 On#0 + On#1 = διά (preposition understood again in the elliptical
  question); "that" of works `t`; "that" of faith = νόμου (the Greek has the
  noun there).
- 3:30 τῆς = that same and 3:31 τῆς = this kept (anaphoric article).
- 3:5 ὁ ἐπιφέρων = to inflict (article + participle).
- 3:13 ὑπό = on, 3:16 ὁδοῖς = wake, 3:19 ἐν = under: same role, shifted
  English idiom, linked with a short note.

## Doubts for the peer

- 3:7 "the increase of" is `t`; one could argue "increase" renders
  ἐπερίσσευσεν a second time. I kept one link (accentuates) because the noun
  has a different role.
- 3:10 one righteous = δίκαιος, and the "one" moved off οὐκ in 3:10-12; the
  publisher's "no one" on οὐκ is defensible as English idiom.
- 3:20 οὐ … πᾶσα joined for "no" (the negative also belongs to the verb
  syntactically).
- 3:27 linking On#1 to διά, while "that#0" is `t`.
- 3:8 "say#0" `t`, not a second link for λέγειν (different role).
