71 publisher rows were changed in 22 verses because they were wrong or

## Publisher rows corrected

- 18:2: "Jesus invited" → invited, plus `t Jesus` (the Greek has only the
  verb ending). "among them" on μέσῳ → ἐν μέσῳ = among, αὐτῶν = them.
- 18:3: "you will never enter" on the verb → οὐ μή = never, verb = you will +
  enter; εἰς is explained as absorbed in enter.
- 18:6: ὃς = anyone and ἄν = if → ὃς ἄν = if anyone (ἄν alone is not "if").
  "Me to stumble" → Me; "to stumble" goes with σκανδαλίσῃ (causes + to stumble).
  τῶν = who + πιστευόντων = believe → joined as article + participle.
  ἵνα = "to have" → ἵνα = to; "have" goes with κρεμασθῇ (have + hung).
- 18:8, 18:9: "causes you to sin" → causes + to sin, σε = you. "cut it off" /
  "gouge it out" → cut + off / gouge + out, αὐτόν = it + it (one object for
  both verbs). "throw it away" → throw, ἀπὸ σοῦ = away.
- 18:10: "See that" and "you do not look down on" → Ὁρᾶτε = See, μή = that +
  not (lest), verb = you do + look down on.
- 18:12: ὑμῖν = "do you" → ὑμῖν = you, δοκεῖ = do + think. "will he not
  leave" → οὐχί = not, verb = will he + leave. τὸ = "the one" + πλανώμενον =
  "that is lost" → joined.
- 18:15: "brother over" → over moves to the verb (have won + over).
- 18:16: "he will not listen" → μή = not, verb = he will + listen. "of two" on
  indeclinable δύο → δύο = two, "of" goes with μαρτύρων (of + witnesses).
- 18:17: ἔστω = "regard him" and σοι = "you would" → joined (σοι cannot carry
  "would"). τελώνης = "a tax collector" → the second ὁ = a, τελώνης = tax
  collector (as with the first ὁ = a).
- 18:20: ἐν = "with them" → ἐν μέσῳ = with, αὐτῶν = them.
- 18:24: δέ = "As" → As + began with the genitive absolute; δέ explained.
- 18:25: δέ = "Since" → Since + was unable with μὴ ἔχοντος; δέ explained.
  "to pay his debt" → to pay, plus `t his debt` (passive infinitive, no object).
- 18:26: σοι = "back" → "I will pay back" (ἀπο- of the verb); σοι explained.
- 18:27: "had compassion on him" → had compassion, plus `t on him`. ὁ = "His"
  → τοῦ δούλου ἐκείνου = His. "and released" → καί = and, released.
- 18:28: δέ = "But when" → δέ = But, when + went out. αὐτοῦ = "of his" →
  τῶν = of (partitive), αὐτοῦ = his. "and began to choke him" → him#2 goes to
  αὐτόν (him + him). Ἀπόδος = Pay and εἴ = "back" → Pay back; εἴ τι = what.
  "you owe me" → you owe, plus `t me`.
- 18:29: σοι = "you back" → σοι = you; back goes to the verb (I will pay + back).
- 18:30: "he refused" → ὁ (pronominal article) = he, οὐκ ἤθελεν = refused.
  "and had the man thrown" → αὐτόν = the man, verb = and had + thrown.
  τὸ = his + ὀφειλόμενον = debt → joined.
- 18:31: οὖν = "When" → When + saw with the participle; οὖν explained.
  "they were greatly distressed" → σφόδρα = greatly. τὰ = what +
  γενόμενα = had happened → joined.
- 18:32: ἐκείνην = "your" → σοι = your; ἐκείνην (that) explained.
- 18:33: "on your" / "on you" → on goes with the mercy verbs (have had mercy
  on; had + on); σου = your, σέ = you.
- 18:34: "turned him over" → turned + over, αὐτόν = him. τὸ = that +
  ὀφειλόμενον = he owed → joined.

## Notable decisions

- Joined units: ἐὰν μή → unless (18:3, 18:35); οὐ μή → never (18:3); ὃς ἐάν,
  ὅσα ἐάν → whoever/whatever (18:5, 18:18); ἐνενήκοντα ἐννέα (18:12);
  διὰ παντός → always (18:10); ἕως οὗ → until (18:34); ἑβδομηκοντάκις ἑπτά →
  seventy-seven times (18:22); μεταξὺ σοῦ καὶ αὐτοῦ μόνου → privately (18:15);
  παντὸς πράγματος → anything (18:19); εἰσιν συνηγμένοι → gather together
  (18:20); οὐκ ἔδει → Shouldn’t (18:33); article + participle in 18:6, 18:12,
  18:30, 18:31, 18:34.
- Genitive absolutes and temporal participles carry the English As / Since /
  When; the δέ or οὖν beside them is explained, not linked.
- Textual differences: 18:15 "against you" is `t` (BSB follows εἰς σέ, which
  N1904 lacks). 18:28 "me" is `t`; N1904 has no pronoun (the Textus Receptus
  reads Ἀπόδος μοι).
- Supplied English: Jesus (18:2), on him (18:27), his debt (18:25), me (18:28).
- Person shifts kept with notes: 18:19 αἰτήσωνται (they ask) → you ask,
  αὐτοῖς → for you; 18:35 αὐτοῦ (his) → your, ἕκαστος moved to "each of you".
- 18:23 ἀνθρώπῳ in ἀνθρώπῳ βασιλεῖ is explained (`s`); "a king" stays on βασιλεῖ.
- 18:14 ἔμπροσθεν is explained: BSB makes Father the subject of "is not willing".

## Doubts for the peer

- 18:6: ἵνα = to (in "to have"), but the second "to" ("to be drowned") stays
  with καταποντισθῇ as the publisher had it. An alternative is ἵνα = to + to.
- 18:28 links partitive "of" to τῶν. In 18:6, 18:10 and 18:14 the publisher's
  "of these" on τούτων was kept, because that demonstrative's genitive is
  itself partitive.
- 18:22: ἕως = "just" is loose; the second ἕως is explained as not repeated.
- 18:9: BSB reverses τὴν γέενναν τοῦ πυρός to "the fire of hell". The
  publisher links τὴν = the and τοῦ = of were kept, with a note on hell.
- 18:24: "owing" is linked to ὀφειλέτης (debtor + owing) instead of `t`.
- 18:2: αὐτό is explained (`s`). It is not anchored jointly with "a little
  child", which is how the earlier reviewer handled αὐτῷ in 18:21.
- Kept from the publisher: the English "and" bundled with the verb when a
  participle becomes a finite verb (and asked, and confront, and be thrown,
  and began to choke, and had, and recounted, and declared), and "tell it"
  (18:17).
