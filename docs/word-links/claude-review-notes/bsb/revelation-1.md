## Notable decisions

- 1:4 ἀπὸ ὁ ὤν: the note says only that the title stays in the nominative
  after ἀπό. 1:5 gets a similar plain note for the nominative titles after
  the genitive Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ. 1:15 πεπυρωμένης gets a note that it is
  genitive in the Greek and that BSB applies "refined" to the bronze.
  These notes do not interpret.
- 1:8 Alpha/Omega linked word by word: τό = "the", Ἄλφα = "Alpha", καί =
  "and", τό = "the", Ὦ = "Omega".
- 1:11: only the last καί carries "and#1", and only the first εἰς carries
  "to#1". BSB does not repeat these words in its list, so the other five
  καί and six εἰς are `s`.
- 1:20: the second article αἱ (28) keeps the publisher's "the#5", and the
  first αἱ (26) is `s`. The predicate "the#6 seven" stays on ἑπτά (30) as
  the publisher has it.
- Kept from the publisher: 1:1 διά = "by" ("by sending His angel"). 1:7

  "brightest", with a note. 1:17 νεκρός = "a dead man" (as 1 Timothy

## Doubts for the peer

- 1:13 περιεζωσμένον = "with" and πρός = "around". The other choice is
  the participle = "around" ("girded around") with πρός `s` and "with"
  `t`. I chose this split so it matches Revelation 15:6 (περιεζωσμένοι
  περὶ τὰ στήθη → "with … around").
- 1:1 διά = "by": in BSB "by" marks the means "sending". You could also
  put "by" on ἀποστείλας with διά `s`. I kept the publisher's row.
- 1:6 "who" on ἐποίησεν with καί `s`. The other choice is "who" `t` and
  ἐποίησεν = "has made".
- 1:14 "white#1" on λευκόν (which in the Greek describes the wool). The
  other choice is to treat "white#1" as λευκαί understood again and leave
  λευκόν `s`.
- 1:9 "of#0 Patmos": "of" is `t`. It could instead go on Πάτμῳ as the
  English naming construction.
