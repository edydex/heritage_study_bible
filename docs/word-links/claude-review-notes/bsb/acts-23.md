# BSB Acts 23: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: all 35 (23:1-35). No KEEP blocks in this chapter. Final
check: `fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 35, "problems": 0}`.

124 of the 532 publisher `g` rows changed; every verse has at least one
change. 17 rows only gained a token, with the English unchanged: Ἄνδρες
ἀδελφοί = Brothers (23:1, 23:6); ἓν μέρος = some of them (23:6); τὰ
ἀμφότερα = them all (23:8); Γενομένης … ἡμέρας = When daylight came (23:12,
as Acts 12:18); ἕως οὗ = until (23:12, 23:14, 23:21); ὡς μέλλοντας / ὡς
μέλλων = on the pretext (23:15, 23:20); πρὸ τοῦ ἐγγίσαι = on the way
(23:15); τοῦ + infinitive = to kill / to ask (23:15, 23:20, Acts 10:25);
κατ’ ἰδίαν = aside (23:19); τι ἀκριβέστερον = more information (23:20);
ἀπὸ σοῦ = your (23:21); ἔχουσαν τὸν τύπον τοῦτον = the following (23:25).
The other 107 were corrected, split or moved.

No parallel Gospel chapter. Conventions followed: Acts 15:23 for χαίρειν =
Greetings; Acts 10:4 for the article ὁ (μὲν οὖν) used as a pronoun and
named by BSB (23:18 "the centurion"); Acts 16:3 / 9:25 for one object shared
by a participle and its main verb (23:24); Acts 16:39 peer for a second
"him" left understood after a coordinated verb (23:10, `t`); Acts 4:32,
10:28, 11:28 for a supplied "that" before an infinitive; Acts 16:13 peer for
a supplied place word (23:3 "here"); Acts 18:21 peer and 14:19 for the καί
of a participle chain on BSB's closing "and" (23:16, 23:19, 23:33).

## Publisher rows corrected

Lone articles carrying a preposition or other word (the article keeps "the"
or a possessive, or is `s`; the preposition goes on the noun, all nouns
declinable):

- 23:1 τῷ "at the" (dative after ἀτενίσας; "at" now on συνεδρίῳ, as
  Acts 10:4, 14:9 "at him"); 23:2 τό "on the"; 23:5 τοῦ "of"; 23:7 τῶν
  "between the"; 23:9 τοῦ "from the" and τῶν "of the"; 23:10 ὁ "that the";
  23:14 τοῖς "to the"; 23:17 τῶν "of the"; 23:19 τῆς "by the"; 23:23 τῶν
  "of his" (now "his", "of" on ἑκατονταρχῶν) and τῆς "of the"; 23:26 τῷ
  "To" (now on κρατίστῳ: "To His Excellency"); 23:29 τοῦ "about"; 23:33
  τῷ "to the".
- 23:13 "of them" was on the number τεσσεράκοντα; now with the article +
  participle οἱ … ποιησάμενοι = "of them … involved in" (ταύτην = "this").

English-only words folded into a Greek word (now `t`):

- "and" joining a participle to the main verb with no καί there: 23:1,
  23:9, 23:10 (go down and remove), 23:11, 23:12, 23:14, 23:17, 23:18 (sent
  and asked), 23:22, 23:23, 23:31, 23:32; 23:25 "And" (new sentence for
  γράψας).
- Supplied "that" before an infinitive or as a that-clause: 23:3, 23:8,
  23:29, 23:30, 23:35; 23:10 "that" of "so … that".
- Supplied objects: 23:10 "him" (bring), 23:19 "him" (drew aside), 23:22
  "him" (instructed), 23:27 "him" (rescue; the Greek object is τὸν ἄνδρα
  τοῦτον = This man), 23:28 "him" (brought down), 23:30 "him" (sent),
  23:34 "the letter".
- Supplied names/subjects: 23:34 "The governor" (participle) and "Paul"
  (ἐστίν ending); first-person "I" on a participle clause where the person
  comes from a finite verb elsewhere: 23:27 "For I had learned", 23:28
  "since I wanted", 23:30 "I also instructed".
- Supplied copulas and frames: 23:6 "It is … that" (cleft); 23:34 "he
  was" (ὅτι ἀπὸ Κιλικίας has no verb; never linked to ἐστίν of the previous
  clause).
- Others: 23:3 "here" (sit here) and "yourself"; 23:6 "my" (hope); 23:35
  "Then".

Words on the wrong Greek word:

- 23:3 σε had the vocative "you" and Τύπτειν had "strike you": now Τύπτειν
  = strike, σε = you#0, τοῖχε = "you … wall" (vocative). The second person
  "you" of "you yourself violate" moved from the participle παρανομῶν to
  the finite κελεύεις ("you … by commanding"); με = "I" only.
- Negatives split off verbs: 23:5 Οὐκ = not, ᾔδειν = "I was … aware";
  23:5 οὐκ ἐρεῖς = not / "Do … speak"; 23:14 μηδενός = "not … anything",
  γεύσασθαι = "to eat"; 23:21 μή = not, πεισθῇς = "Do … let … persuade";
  23:22 μηδενί = "not … anyone", ἐκλαλῆσαι = "Do … tell".
- When/As soon as/since/For moved onto the participle or genitive absolute:
  23:7 "As soon as" (was on τοῦτο, with "this" on λαλοῦντος; now τοῦτο =
  this); 23:16 "when" (was on δέ, now δέ = But only); 23:27 "when … came"
  and "For … had learned"; 23:28 "since … wanted"; 23:30 "When" (was on δέ);
  23:33 "When" (was on οἵτινες, which keeps "the horsemen").
- Greek words hidden inside a neighbour's English: 23:10 Παῦλος (Paul was
  inside "would tear Paul to pieces") and αὐτόν (him inside "remove him by
  force"); 23:12 and 23:21 ἑαυτούς (themselves inside the oath verb); 23:15
  αὐτόν (him inside "bring him down"); 23:24 "him" now on τὸν Παῦλον
  (Paul + him), not on διασώσωσι.
- 23:9 "A" moved from μεγάλη to κραυγή ("a" goes with the noun).
- 23:15 τά had "his case": now τὰ περί = case, αὐτοῦ = his.
- 23:16 "of" moved from Παύλου to ἀδελφῆς ("of … sister", Παύλου = Paul's);
  "he went" moved from παραγενόμενος (now `s`) to εἰσελθών ("went into"),
  and the chain καί now carries "and" before told.
- 23:18 "took him": παραλαβὼν … ἤγαγεν joined = took, αὐτόν = him.
- 23:19 "and" moved from ἐπυνθάνετο to the chain καί.
- 23:24 ἵνα = to#0 (as ὅπως = to in 23:15, 23:20, 23:23); διασώσωσι =
  "take … safely".
- 23:31 "followed their orders" split: κατά = followed, τὸ διατεταγμένον =
  orders, αὐτοῖς = their; ἀναλαβόντες ("taking along") `s`, ἤγαγον =
  brought.
- 23:33 "and" moved from the adverbial καί ("also") before τὸν Παῦλον to
  the καί of the participle chain (see doubts).
- 23:35 "your" moved from Διακούσομαι to σου; the verb keeps "I will hear …
  case".

## Readings

- 23:20 (EVIDENCE: 1 N1904 word unmatched, 1 publisher word extra): ὡς
  μέλλων = "on the pretext" linked with a note that BSB's Greek has a
  different form of the participle with the same sense (probably μέλλον, as
  NA28).
- 23:30 (0 unmatched, 1 extra): "their case" `t` with a reading reason; BSB
  probably reads λέγειν τὰ πρὸς αὐτόν.
- 23:23 and 23:33 (1/1): Caesarea linked with a spelling note (probably
  Καισαρείας / Καισάρειαν).
- 23:6 and 23:8 (0 unmatched, 1 extra): no reading claimed, since every
  English word is covered by N1904. In 23:6 the extra word is probably ἐγώ
  before κρίνομαι ("I am on trial"), but the verb's first person already
  gives "I"; in 23:8 it is probably μέν, which BSB does not render.

## Notable decisions

- 23:26 letter address: τῷ `s`, κρατίστῳ = "To His Excellency", ἡγεμόνι =
  Governor, Φήλικι = Felix, χαίρειν = Greetings (15:23). "To" sits on the
  first dative word rather than on the article.
- 23:2 and 23:4 τοῖς παρεστῶσιν / οἱ παρεστῶτες joined (article +
  participle, rule 3); 23:2 αὐτῷ = Paul, αὐτοῦ = him (pronouns BSB moves
  into new roles); 23:11 αὐτῷ = Paul; 23:35 αὐτόν = Paul.
- 23:3 καί = "yet" (a connective BSB renders with another connective).
- 23:6 καί of "hope and resurrection" `s`; BSB's "hope in the resurrection"
  keeps "in" on ἀναστάσεως (publisher row). περί = "because of", with "It
  is … that" `t`.
- 23:10 μή after φοβηθείς `s` (it is "lest", not a negative); ἐκ μέσου
  αὐτῶν `s` (BSB's remove covers it); τε = "and" between the infinitives.
- 23:13 οἱ … ποιησάμενοι = "of them … involved in": BSB turns the article
  + participle subject ("those who made") into "of them … involved".
- 23:24 ἐπιβιβάσαντες = "for" kept (publisher row), with a note: the mounts
  are for Paul to ride.
- 23:27 ἀναιρεῖσθαι = "to kill him" (passive turned active keeps its
  patient, as "would stone them", "release you" in earlier chapters);
  ἐξειλάμην = "I … to rescue" (finite verb carries the person).
- 23:28 τε = "and" (τε alone, not τε … καί); ἐνεκάλουν = "against" kept
  (publisher) with a note; δι’ ἥν `s`.

## Doubts for the peer

- 23:33: "and" on the chain καί (εἰσελθόντες καὶ ἀναδόντες) per the 18:21
  peer rule, with παρέστησαν καὶ τὸν Παῦλον's καί ("also") `s`. The
  publisher linked the second καί; that is the alternative.
- First-person "I" in the letter: I placed one "I" on each finite verb
  (23:27 ἐξειλάμην, 23:28 κατήγαγον, 23:30 ἔπεμψα) and made the extra "I"
  of a participle clause `t` (23:27 "For I had learned", 23:28 "since I
  wanted", 23:30 "I also instructed"). Acts 10:29 instead left "when I was
  invited" on the participle; linking the extra "I" to the finite verb as
  well is another option.
- 23:10 "him#1" (bring him) `t` as a second object left understood after τε
  (16:39 peer). Linking αὐτόν to both "him"s (16:3 pattern) is arguable,
  since the two infinitives share one object.
- 23:16 παραγενόμενος `s` and εἰσελθών = "he went"; joining both participles
  to "he went" (publisher had παραγενόμενος) is the alternative. 23:18 I did
  join παραλαβὼν … ἤγαγεν = took, because "took" fits both verbs; 23:31
  ἀναλαβόντες `s` because "brought" renders only ἤγαγον.
- 23:24 ἐπιβιβάσαντες = "for" (kept) could instead be `s` with "for" `t`.
- 23:3 "yourself" `t` (emphasis) and "here" `t`; keeping "you yourself" as
  one unit on κελεύεις is possible.
- 23:30 "their case": `t` with a reading reason; if the peer doubts the
  reading, "English supplies their case" is the fallback reason.
