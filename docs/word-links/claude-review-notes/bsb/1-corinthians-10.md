## Notable decisions

- 10:4 ἡ πέτρα: the article ἡ = "that#1" (anaphoric article) is kept.
- 10:16, 10:17, 10:21, 10:31: the Lord's Supper terms (ποτήριον, εὐλογία,
  κοινωνία, ἄρτος, τράπεζα) and "glory" are linked word by word, with no
  interpretive notes. κοινωνία = "a participation" and its genitive = "in +
  noun".
- 10:7 (Exodus 32:6) and 10:26 (Psalm 24:1) quotations are linked word by
  word.
- 10:18 τὸν Ἰσραὴλ κατὰ σάρκα: τόν = "the#0", Ἰσραήλ = "of Israel" (the
  publisher row), κατὰ σάρκα joined = "people" (publisher had σάρκα = "the#0
  people" with κατά TODO). The note says "people" renders "according to the
  flesh".
- 10:24 τό (twice) = "good#0" and "the good" (publisher rows kept): τό stands
  alone as a pronoun ("the thing"), as τά = "the thoughts" in 1 Corinthians
  2:11. In 10:33 the noun σύμφορον is present, so "good#1" links to it there.
- 10:25 and 10:27 διά = "of" ("questions of conscience") is kept with a note.
- 10:30 οὗ = "that for which" is kept (the relative includes its
  antecedent).

## Doubts for the peer

- 10:24: τό = "good" follows the article-used-as-a-pronoun convention (1
  Corinthians 2:11). If the "men’s" rule (1 Corinthians 4:5) is read as
  covering this too, both τό would become `s`/"the" and "good" (twice) `t`.
- 10:1-3: "they/They" moved from πάντες to the finite verbs (Mark 14:23).
  Matthew 21:26 keeps πάντες = "they all", so the publisher rows in 10:1 and
  10:3 were defensible. I changed them so 10:1-3 are consistent with 10:2,
  where πάντες was TODO and "all" had been on the verb.
- 10:6: μή = "keep + from" and εἶναι ἐπιθυμητάς joined = "craving". Another
  option is εἶναι `s` and ἐπιθυμητάς = "craving" alone.
- 10:11: πρός = "as#1" and ἡμῶν = "for us" replace the publisher's πρός =
  "for" and νουθεσίαν = "as warnings". This is a meaning fix, but both
  layouts can be argued.
- 10:20: "No" is linked to ἀλλά (with "but"), as the publisher had it. The
  stricter option is "No" `t` (English supplies an answer).
- 10:33: "For" sits on the participle ζητῶν as its causal sense (Acts 15:9,
  13:12); "I#2" is `t`. Alternative: "For" `t`.
- 10:17: οἱ πολλοί = "who + many" with "are#0" `t`; "we#0" goes with ἐσμεν.
