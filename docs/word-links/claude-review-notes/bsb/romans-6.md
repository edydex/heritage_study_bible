## Publisher rows corrected

Lone articles carrying a preposition:

- 6:4 τοῦ "of the" → "the", "of" on Πατρός ("of + Father").
- 6:5 τῷ "in" → `s`; "in" moved to θανάτου ("in + death"), matching "in +
  resurrection" in the second clause.
- 6:19 τῆς "of" → `s`, σαρκός = "of + flesh"; τῇ "to" → `s`, "to" on
  ἀνομίᾳ (see 6:19 below).

μὴ γένοιτο joined as one unit: 6:2 and 6:15 (μή had been left out of
"Certainly not").

Negatives split from verbs:

- 6:6 μηκέτι = "no longer" (was "should no longer"); τοῦ δουλεύειν =
  "that + should + be slaves" (articular τοῦ joined; "that" was on ἡμᾶς,
  which now has "we").
- 6:9 οὐκέτι = "cannot + again", ἀποθνῄσκει = "He + die" (was all on the
  verb).
- 6:12 Μή = "not"; "do not let sin reign" was all on βασιλευέτω: now "do +
  let + reign" on the imperative, ἁμαρτία = "sin".
- 6:13 μηδέ = "not", "Do + present" on the verb.
- 6:14 οὐ = "not"; ὑμῶν = "your"; κυριεύσει = "shall + be + master" (was
  "shall not be your master").
- 6:16 οὐκ = "not", "Do you + know".

Person words off participles and infinitives (`t`):

- 6:6 "We" (γινώσκοντες); 6:9 "we" (εἰδότες; "For" stays on the causal
  participle as in Romans 5:3); 6:18 "You" (ἐλευθερωθέντες); 6:22 "you"
  (ἐλευθερωθέντες); 6:12 "you" (infinitive ὑπακούειν).

Supplied words that had been folded into Greek words (now `t`):

- Copulas: 6:17 "be" ("thanks be"), 6:21 "is" (death), 6:22 "is" (eternal),
  6:23 "is" twice.
- Objects: 6:5 "Him" twice and 6:6 "Him" (σύμφυτοι, συνεσταυρώθη have no
  pronoun; 6:4 and 6:8 have αὐτῷ and keep "Him").
- 6:13 "have been brought"; 6:17 "once" (added adverb); 6:18 "and" (joins the
  participle to the main verb; the δέ is `s`); 6:21 "from" (links fruit to
  "the things").

Wrong owners and over-claims:

- 6:2 πῶς carried "How can we": now πῶς = "How", ζήσομεν = "can we + live".
- 6:3 ὅσοι = "all of + who" (was "all of"), ἐβαπτίσθημεν = "us + were
  baptized" (was "us who were baptized").
- 6:4 οὖν = "therefore" (was inside "We were therefore buried").
- 6:5 ἐσόμεθα carried "we will certainly also be united with Him": now ἀλλά =
  "certainly", καί = "also", ἐσόμεθα = "we will + be", and σύμφυτοι =
  "united with + united with" (understood again). τῆς = "His" (article as
  possessive) in "in His resurrection".
- 6:7 ὁ + ἀποθανών joined = "anyone who has died" (rule 3).
- 6:9 ὅτι carried "since": ὅτι = "that", ἐγερθείς = "since + was raised".
- 6:12 εἰς τό joined = "so that".
- 6:13 ὑμῶν carried "of your body": μέλη = "parts of + body", ὑμῶν = "your"
  (both clauses). καί carried "and present": καί = "and", παραστήσατε =
  "present + present" (understood again). ὡσεί carried "as those who have
  been", ἐκ "brought from": now ὡσεί = "as", ἐκ = "from", ζῶντας = "those who
  + to life".
- 6:16 ἤτοι carried "whether you are slaves": the repeat now links to δοῦλοι
  ("slaves + slaves") and ἐστε ("you are + you are"), ἤτοι = "whether". εἰς
  carried "as": δούλους = "as + slaves", εἰς ὑπακοήν = "obedient" (joined).
  ᾧ "when" → `s` + `t` (see below).
- 6:17 ἦτε carried "though you once were": δέ (before ὑπηκούσατε) =
  "though", ἦτε = "you + were"; ὑπηκούσατε carried "wholeheartedly": ἐκ
  καρδίας = "wholeheartedly", verb = "you + obeyed".
- 6:18 ἐδουλώθητε carried "and" (now `t`).
- 6:19 ὑμῶν carried "your body": μέλη = "parts of + body", ὑμῶν = "your".
- 6:21 ἐφ’ carried "from the things", οἷς "of which": ἐφ’ = "of", οἷς = "the
  things + which"; ἐπαισχύνεσθε carried "now": νῦν = "now", verb = "you are +
  ashamed".
- 6:22 νυνί carried "now that": νυνί = "now", "that" goes with the participle
  ἐλευθερωθέντες, like "When/As" on a participle.

## Notable decisions

- Theological terms linked word by word, no interpretive notes: ἁμαρτία =
  "sin" throughout (dative "to/in sin", genitive "of/to sin"), βάπτισμα =
  "baptism" (6:4), βαπτίζω = "baptized" (6:3), θάνατος = "death", ἀνάστασις =
  "resurrection" (6:5), δοῦλος = "slaves" (6:16, 6:17, 6:20), δοῦλα = "in
  slavery" (6:19), δουλόω = "become slaves" (6:18, 6:22), δουλεύω = "be
  slaves" (6:6), δικαιοσύνη = "righteousness", ἀδικία = "wickedness",
  ἁγιασμός = "holiness" (6:19, 6:22), χάρισμα = "gift", ὀψώνια = "wages"
  (6:23), χάρις = "grace" (6:1, 6:14, 6:15) and "thanks" (6:17), δικαιόω =
  "has been freed" (6:7, with a note on the literal "justified").
- 6:8: the publisher's Greek spells the "live with" verb differently
  (probably συζήσομεν for N1904 συνζήσομεν). Same word, so linked with the
  note "Same word, spelled differently in BSB's Greek text"; καί = "also".
- 6:16 first ᾧ ("to whom"): BSB recasts the relative clause as "when you
  offer yourselves", so ᾧ is `s` and "when" `t` (different meaning, not a
  split pair). The second ᾧ keeps "to the one".
- 6:17 δέ = "though": BSB moves the contrast marked by the second clause's δέ
  into the first clause, so the connective is linked.
- 6:19 τῇ ἀνομίᾳ εἰς τὴν ἀνομίαν: ἀνομίᾳ = "to + wickedness", εἰς τὴν
  ἀνομίαν joined = "escalating". τὰ μέλη ὑμῶν in the second half: μέλη =
  "them", article and ὑμῶν `s`.
- 6:9 "cannot … again" on οὐκέτι: "cannot" is one English word holding the
  negative, so it goes with the negative and the verb keeps "He + die".
- 6:11 εἶναι and μέν `s`; 6:4 οὕτως `s` (answers "just as", no English word);
  6:6 τοῦτο `s` (points ahead to the that-clause); γάρ `s` in 6:10, 6:19,
  6:21; οὖν `s` in 6:21; ὑμῶν `s` in 6:22 ("the fruit you reap").
- 6:13 Θεῷ (second) = "to Him" (pronoun for the repeated noun), νεκρῶν =
  "death".

## Doubts for the peer

- 6:5 "in" (both clauses) is put on the genitive noun BSB governs with it
  ("in + death", "in + resurrection"). It could instead be read as the
  dative relation of τῷ ὁμοιώματι ("in the likeness"); "like this" stays on
  ὁμοιώματι as in the publisher row, though "this" is arguably a supplied
  demonstrative.
- 6:5 and 6:6 "Him" made `t` (no pronoun after σύμφυτοι / συνεσταυρώθη);
  the publisher had folded it into the συν- word.
- 6:9 "For" kept on the causal participle εἰδότες (Romans 5:3 precedent);
  it could be `t` if the peer reads it as a pure sentence connective.
- 6:20 "free of obligation" kept on ἐλεύθεροι (publisher row); "of
  obligation" could be `t` as an English expansion.
- 6:13 ζῶντας = "those who + to life" with "have been brought" `t`; an
  alternative would leave "those who" `t` as well.
- 6:22 "that" moved from νυνί to the participle; "now that" could be kept as
  one English conjunction on νυνί.
