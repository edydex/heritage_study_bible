## Notable decisions

- Vocatives: 5:8 τὸ πνεῦμα is linked to "you + spirit". 5:41 Τὸ κοράσιον is linked to "Little girl".
- Names: 5:22 Ἰάειρος and 5:37 Ἰωάνην are linked to Jairus and John. EVIDENCE shows the publisher's Greek differs in these verses, but only in the name's spelling (rule 8).
- Pronouns and titles that BSB renders as names are linked: αὐτοῦ/αὐτόν → Jesus (5:2, 5:10, 5:12, 5:17, 5:22); ἀρχισυναγώγου/ἀρχισυναγώγῳ → Jairus (5:35, 5:36).
- 5:14: BSB's one "in the" serves both εἰς τὴν πόλιν and εἰς τοὺς ἀγρούς, so both prepositions and both articles are linked to it.
- 5:3: οὐδείς and αὐτόν are `s`, because BSB makes "no one could bind him" passive.
- 5:21: καὶ ἦν ("and He was") is `s`. BSB folds "beside the sea" into the gathering clause.
- No different Greek reading is claimed anywhere.

## Doubts for the peer

- 5:15, 5:38: BSB subordinates the first of two finite clauses joined by καί. I kept the publisher's opening καί → When (with a note) and made the middle καί `s`. The alternative is to link the middle καί to When.
- 5:2: "As soon as" is linked to εὐθύς alone. It could also include the genitive absolute ἐξελθόντος.
- 5:40: ὅπου ἦν ("where [the child] was") → "to see". This is a free rendering. It is linked so that the pair is not split between `s` and `t`.
- 5:24: one "Him" is linked to both αὐτῷ (followed) and αὐτόν (pressed around), as a shared object.
- 5:43: αὐτοῖς is `s` and "them" (He told them) is `t`, because "them" is the agent of BSB's active "give", not the recipients of the orders. Linking αὐτοῖς → them is the alternative.
- 5:4: διὰ τό ("because") → "Though" follows BSB's concessive recast. "there was" is `t`, and ἴσχυεν → "with the strength".
- 5:36: the article τόν → "their" (article as possessive); λόγον λαλούμενον → conversation.
