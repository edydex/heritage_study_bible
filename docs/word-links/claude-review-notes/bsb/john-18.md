# BSB John 18: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: 39 (18:1-9, 18:11-40). Kept: 18:10 (KEEP block, already
reviewed by Codex), copied unchanged; the output block is identical to
`scripts/original-languages/reviewed-bsb/john-18.json`. Final check:
`fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 40, "problems": 0}` (and `check --target BSB`
gives the same).

502 of the 611 publisher `g` rows outside 18:10 are unchanged. The other 109
rows, in 32 verses, were corrected, split, joined or moved.

Parallels checked: Matthew 26:47-75, Mark 14:43-72 and Luke 22:47-62 for the
arrest and Peter's denials. John conventions followed from John 1-13: ὁ before
names `s`; unrendered αὐτῷ / αὐτοῖς after verbs of speaking `s`; ὅτι before
direct speech `s`; ἐγώ εἰμι = I + am with `t He` (John 8:24, 8:28, 13:19);
Μὴ … εἶ = Aren’t (John 7:52); οὐκ ἄν = not + would with the verb keeping
have (John 11:21); ἀπεκρίθησαν καὶ εἶπαν = they replied (John 7:52); "This
was to fulfill" = ἵνα to, πληρωθῇ fulfill, `t This was` (John 12:38); a
genitive or dative article takes only "the", and "of"/"to"/"by" goes with
the noun (John 3:1; the case word sits on the article only with indeclinable
names).

## Publisher rows corrected

ἐγώ εἰμι, linked word by word:

- 18:5, 18:6, 18:8 εἰμι "am He" → am, `t He`. 18:5 λέγει "Jesus said" and
  18:6 εἶπεν "Jesus said" → said, `t Jesus`.

Peter's denials, matched to the reviewed Synoptic rows:

- 18:17, 18:25 ἐκ "one of" → of, `t one` (as Matthew 26:73, Mark 14:69-70,
  Luke 22:58). Μή + εἶ joined as Aren’t (publisher had εἶ alone).
  Οὐκ εἰμί stays not + I am (Luke 22:58).
- 18:25, 18:27 ἠρνήσατο "denied it" → denied, `t it` (as Matthew 26:70, 72,
  Mark 14:68, 70, Luke 22:57).
- 18:25 δέ "still" → `s`; `t still` (δέ does not mean still).
- 18:26 kept οὐκ = Didn’t, εἶδον = see (as John 7:45 didn’t).

Pilate dialogue, negatives and auxiliaries split off verbs:

- 18:28 οὐκ εἰσῆλθον "did not" + "enter" → not, did + enter.
- 18:30 "we would not have handed Him over" (all on παρεδώκαμεν) → οὐκ = not,
  ἄν = would, παρεδώκαμεν = we + have handed + over, αὐτόν = Him.
- 18:31 "We are not permitted" → Ἡμῖν = We, οὐκ = not, ἔξεστιν = are +
  permitted; "to execute anyone" → to execute, οὐδένα = anyone. "You take"
  → ὑμεῖς = You (explicit pronoun), Λάβετε = take.
- 18:34 "Are you" / "did others" → σύ = you, λέγεις = Are + saying; ἄλλοι =
  others, εἶπον = did + tell.
- 18:35 "over to me" → παρέδωκαν = handed + over, ἐμοί = to me.
- 18:36 "would fight" → ἄν = would, ἠγωνίζοντο = fight.
- 18:9 "I have not lost one" (all on ἀπώλεσα) → οὐκ = not, ἀπώλεσα = I have
  + lost, οὐδένα = one. 18:11 "Shall I not drink" → οὐ μή = not, πίω = Shall
  I + drink.
- 18:2 πολλάκις "had often" → often, συνήχθη = had + met.

Articles carrying other words:

- 18:15 ὁ "Since" → `s`; Since moved to the καί between the clauses (the
  When-rule), δέ `s`.
- 18:16 ὁ "who was" (lone article with is/was) → ὁ γνωστός = who was known.
  18:23 τοῦ "what" → τοῦ κακοῦ = what was wrong.
- 18:31 τόν "own" and 18:35 τό "own" → `s`. 18:35 σόν = Your own (emphatic
  possessive adjective); 18:31 `t own` (the Greek is only "your law").
- 18:32 τοῦ "that" → `s`; that moved to the relative ὅν.
- "of the"/"to the"/"by the" on a lone article → the, case word with the noun:
  18:12, 18:15 (twice), 18:16 (twice), 18:20, 18:22, 18:33, 18:36, 18:37,
  18:39. 18:11 τῷ "to" → τῷ Πέτρῳ = to Peter (as Matthew 26:73).

English-only words folded into Greek words (now `t`):

- 18:1 Ἰησοῦς "After Jesus" → Jesus; After moved to the participle εἰπών.
- 18:5 εἱστήκει "was standing there" → `t there`. 18:18 εἱστήκεισαν "were
  standing around" → `t around`. 18:11 εἰς "back in" → in, `t back`.
- 18:13 ἤγαγον "They brought Him first" → They brought, `t Him`, πρῶτον = first.
- 18:9 and 18:32 ἵνα "This was" / πληρωθῇ "(was) to fulfill" → ἵνα = to,
  πληρωθῇ = fulfill, `t This was` (John 12:38).
- 18:22 Οὕτως "Is this how" → this how, `t Is`; εἰπών "and said" → said,
  `t and`. 18:39 ἕνα "one prisoner" → one, `t prisoner`.
- 18:28 ἀλλά "and unable" → and, `t unable`.

Moved or joined units:

- 18:1 the subject αὐτὸς καὶ οἱ μαθηταὶ αὐτοῦ = they (publisher had only
  μαθηταί). 18:2, 18:4, 18:5, 18:14, 18:21, 18:37 article + participle joined
  (betrayer; that was coming; the one who had advised; those who heard; who
  belongs). 18:17 ἡ θυρωρός = watching the door.
- 18:5, 18:7 τὸν Ναζωραῖον = of Nazareth (as Matthew 26:71).
- 18:6 ἀπῆλθαν εἰς τὰ ὀπίσω = they drew back (as John 6:66).
- 18:16 εἰσήγαγεν "brought" + Πέτρον "Peter in" → brought + in, Peter.
- 18:21 τί ἐλάλησα = My message; αὐτοῖς `s`.
- 18:22 genitive absolute: δέ "When" → `s`, εἰπόντος = When + had said;
  ἔδωκεν ῥάπισμα = slapped + in the face; τῷ Ἰησοῦ = Him (BSB names Jesus for
  αὐτοῦ above, as Mark 14:67).
- 18:23 ἐλάλησα = I said + I spoke (verb understood again in the second
  if-clause); καλῶς = correctly.
- 18:30 κακὸν ποιῶν = a criminal; ἀπεκρίθησαν καὶ εἶπαν = they replied.
- 18:36 ἡ βασιλεία ἡ ἐμή (second) = it; repeated ἐκ τοῦ κόσμου τούτου `s`;
  μή = prevent, παραδοθῶ = My arrest.
- 18:37 ἐγώ (17) was linked to the "I" of "I am a king"; it is the subject of
  γεγέννημαι, so ἐγώ = I#1 (I was born), εἰμι = I#0 am; repeated εἰς τοῦτο `s`.

## 18:10 (KEEP, not edited)

No link error found. Two small points for whoever next touches it: ἔχων
("having") is folded into "his sword" with μάχαιραν and αὐτήν (its own note
says having alone does not mean his; `s` for ἔχων would also be defensible),

not separately express Greek and there" for δέ).

## Doubts for the peer

- 18:15: Since put on the καί between the clauses and δέ `s` (When-rule);
  "also" left inside "he also went" for the σύν of συνεισῆλθεν rather than
  `t`. Alternative: δέ = Since, καί = also.
- 18:1: five tokens (αὐτὸς καὶ οἱ μαθηταὶ αὐτοῦ) on "they"; and ἦν / ὅν `s`
  for BSB's compression "where they entered a garden".
- 18:9: Οὕς = those with resumptive αὐτῶν `s`; αὐτῶν = those with Οὕς `s`
  is also possible.
- 18:26: "the" of "the high priest’s servants" left on τῶν (publisher row);
  it could equally go on τοῦ.
- 18:28: ἵνα = to, μή = avoid, ἀλλά = and, `t unable` for BSB's "to avoid being
  defiled and unable to eat".
- 18:36: μή = prevent and παραδοθῶ = My arrest are BSB's recast of "that I
  might not be handed over".
