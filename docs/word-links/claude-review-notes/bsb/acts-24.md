## Verse division (24:18-19)

The EVIDENCE lines show 6 publisher words extra in 24:18 and 6 N1904 words
missing in 24:19: BSB ends 24:18 with "But there are some Jews from the
province of Asia", which renders τινὲς δὲ ἀπὸ τῆς Ἀσίας Ἰουδαῖοι, printed at
the start of 24:19 in N1904. 24:19 now has `m 0 1 2 3 4 5 > 24:18`, and 24:18
links them with `g @24:19` (τινές = some, δέ = But, ἀπό = from, τῆς = the,
Ἀσίας = Asia, Ἰουδαῖοι = Jews; "there are" and "province of" `t`). 24:6 and
24:8 both match N1904 word for word, so no cross-verse link is used there.

(διαλεγομένου) and "When" (μεταλαβών), 24:27 "After" (πληρωθείσης).

Other moves:

- 24:2 and 24:16 "a" moved from the adjective to the noun (a … peace, a …
  conscience).
- 24:5 "among" was on πᾶσιν: now "among + Jews"; πᾶσιν = "all" and κατά =
  "over" (was "all over"); τε `s`.
- 24:10 εὐθύμως = "gladly" (was folded into ἀπολογοῦμαι = "I gladly make my
  defense"); τὰ περὶ ἐμαυτοῦ = "my"; ἀπολογοῦμαι = "I … make … defense".
- 24:12 negative split: οὔτε = "not", εὗρον = "did … find".
- 24:15 αὐτοί and οὗτοι were swapped: the intensive αὐτοί = "themselves",
  οὗτοι = "they" (as 24:20, where αὐτοί = "for themselves"). "of" moved from
  τε to δικαίων; τε = "both" (BSB says both … and).
- 24:17 ποιήσων = "to bring + to present" (it governs both alms and
  offerings); προσφοράς = "offerings" only.
- 24:18 the second μετά joined to the first: μετά … μετά = "inciting".
- 24:20 εἰπάτωσαν = "let … state", οὗτοι = "these men"; μου = "I" (subject
  of the genitive absolute στάντος = "when … stood"); "in me" `t`.
- 24:21 φωνῆς = "thing" (was on the relative ἧς, now `s`).
- 24:22 εἰδώς = "who was … informed", ἀκριβέστερον = "well".
- 24:23 αὐτόν = "Paul + him" (subject of both infinitives); τηρεῖσθαι =
  "to keep … under guard"; ἔχειν = "to allow".

## Notable decisions

- 24:1 "their case" `t` as a supplied object (Acts 10:10), but 24:22
  ἀνεβάλετο αὐτούς joined as the legal idiom "adjourned the hearing"
  (BDAG: "adjourn someone's case").
- 24:2 publisher's "to this" on τούτῳ kept (not a lone article); τῷ `s`.
- 24:6 the connecting relative ὅς keeps "and he"; καί = "so" kept.
- 24:8 παρά `s`, οὗ = "him" (BSB makes "from whom" the object of examining);
  τούτων and ὧν `s`.
- 24:9 ταῦτα = "these charges" and 24:16 τούτῳ = "this hope" kept (Acts 7:1,
  17:11 precedent).
- 24:12 the second οὔτε `s`; the third = "or".
- 24:15 καί = "the same" kept (the hope they also hold).
- 24:23 τε = "but" kept as a simple connective, not a τε … καί pair.

## Doubts for the peer

- 24:20 μου = "I" with "in me" `t`, versus the publisher's μου = "in me"
  with "I" inside "when I stood".
- 24:18 "inciting" on μετὰ … μετά: BSB's verb is an interpretation of
  "with"; `s` for both μετά with "inciting" `t` is the alternative.
- 24:17 linking "to present" to ποιήσων as a second rendering; `t` is the
  alternative.
- 24:5 πᾶσιν = "all" (from "all over") rather than joining πᾶσιν + κατά.
- 24:1 "their case" `t` versus keeping it on ἐνεφάνισαν (legal "present a
  case").
- 24:23 τε = "but": if read as τε … καί, τε would be `s` and "but" `t`.
- 24:6 "and he" on ὅς: "and" could be `t`.
