check: `fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 28, "problems": 0}`.

110 of the 426 publisher `g` rows changed, in 27 of the 28 verses (18:13
keeps every publisher row and only resolved TODO tokens). 10 rows only gained

below); Acts 9:25 and Luke 6:1 for ἐν νυκτί = One night; Acts 9:11, 12:15
for πρὸς αὐτόν/αὐτήν = him/her after told (18:6) and Acts 7:3 for πρός `s`

  "After" (ποιήσας), 18:26 "When" (ἀκούσαντες), 18:27 "When" (βουλομένου).
- 18:19 "When" moved from the opening δέ to κἀκείνους, whose καί joins the
  two clauses BSB turns into "When …, …" (Acts 11:22).
- Negatives split off verbs: 18:9 Μή = "not", the verb keeps "Do/do … be

- 18:9 "One" was on δέ: ἐν νυκτί = "One night", δέ `s`.
- 18:10 "you" in "lay a hand on you" moved from σε (object of the
  unrendered κακῶσαι) to σοι (object of ἐπιθήσεται); τοῦ κακῶσαί σε `s`.
- 18:12 "an" moved from ὁμοθυμαδόν to "attack"; 18:24 "an" from λόγιος to

- 18:28 "he" moved from εὐτόνως to διακατηλέγχετο.

## Notable decisions

- 18:2 Ποντικόν = "of Pontus", γένει = "a native", τῷ `s` (Acts 4:36 joined
  all three as "from Cyprus"; here BSB's two English parts map cleanly).
  18:24 Ἀλεξανδρεύς / γένει the same way.
- 18:3 publisher's διὰ τό = "because" kept (now with τό), ὁμότεχνον = "just
  as", εἶναι = "he was"; γάρ `s` because BSB folds the γάρ clause into its
  because clause.
- 18:4 τε = "alike" kept: BSB renders τε … καί ("both … and") as "Jews and
  Greeks alike", like "both" in Acts 5:14, 8:12.
- 18:7 ἐκεῖθεν `s` while τῇ συναγωγῇ keeps "the synagogue" (BSB moves the
  synagogue into the role of the place Paul left); οὗ ἡ οἰκία ἦν `s`;
  ὀνόματι `s` (no "named").
- 18:14 ὦ = "O", Ἰουδαῖοι = "Jews" (ὦ is an actual Greek word); κατὰ λόγον
  = "reasonable", ἄν = "would".
- 18:17 δέ = "At this", πάντες = "the crowd" (publisher rows kept, notes
  added).
- 18:24 ὤν = "He was" kept (BSB's He was also governs well versed).

## Doubts for the peer

- 18:19 "When" on the crasis κἀκείνους (καί + ἐκείνους) following Acts
  11:22; keeping the publisher's δέ = "When" is the alternative.
- 18:3 "because" on διὰ τό vs on γάρ: BSB's because-clause is the γάρ
  clause (they were tentmakers); I kept the publisher's διὰ τό.
- 18:10 "you" on σοι rather than σε (publisher); linking both or keeping σε
  is also defensible.
- 18:13 "in ways contrary to" kept whole on παρά; "in ways" could be `t`.
- 18:28 "in public debate" kept whole on δημοσίᾳ; "debate" could be `t` or
  go with διακατηλέγχετο.
