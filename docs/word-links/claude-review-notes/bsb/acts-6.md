# BSB Acts 6: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: all 15 (6:1-15). No KEEP blocks in this chapter. Final
check: `fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 15, "problems": 0}`.

47 of the 238 publisher `g` rows changed, in all 15 verses. Some only
gained a token, with the English unchanged: 6:2 οὐκ ἀρεστόν =
unacceptable, 6:9 τῆς λεγομένης = what was called, 6:14 ὁ Ναζωραῖος = of
Nazareth (as John 18:5, 18:7 and Luke 18:37), 6:15 οἱ καθεζόμενοι = who were
sitting. The rest were corrected, split or moved.

## Publisher rows corrected

English words with no Greek counterpart that had been folded into a Greek
word (now `t`):

- Supplied copulas: 6:3 "to be" (confirmed to be full), 6:8 "who was"
  (who was full), 6:15 "was" (was like; ὡσεί = like).
- Supplied objects and names: 6:12 "Stephen" (confronted Stephen; the
  participle ἐπιστάντες has no object) and "him" (brought him).
- BSB additions: 6:1 "among them" (the Grecian Jews among the disciples),
  6:6 "seven" (these seven; οὕς = these), 6:9 "the provinces of" (Cilicia
  and Asia), 6:15 "that" (saw that …).
- Participle "and": 6:2 "and said", 6:6 "and laid", 6:15 "and they saw":
  each "and" only joins a participle to the main verb.

Words on the wrong Greek word:

- Lone articles carrying a preposition: 6:4 τῇ "to", τῇ "to the", τοῦ "of
  the"; 6:7 τῶν "of" twice and τῇ "to the"; 6:9 τῆς "of the" and τῷ "with"
  (Στέφανος is declinable, so "with" goes on Στεφάνῳ); 6:13 τοῦ "against
  the"; 6:14 ὁ "of" (now joined with Ναζωραῖος). The articles keep "the"
  where English has it; the prepositions go on the nouns.
- 6:9 the synagogue phrase was scrambled (τῆς = "what", λεγομένης = "was
  called", τῆς = "of the"): now τῆς = the, συναγωγῆς = Synagogue, τῆς
  λεγομένης = what was called, Λιβερτίνων = of the Freedmen.
- 6:1 "when" moved from ἡμέραις ("days when") to the genitive absolute
  πληθυνόντων.
- 6:3 ἐξ ὑμῶν: "among" moved from ὑμῶν to ἐξ ("from among" / "you").
- 6:5 Πνεύματος Ἁγίου: "of the" moved from Ἁγίου to Πνεύματος, as Luke 4:1
  (Πνεύματος = of the + Spirit, Ἁγίου = Holy).
- 6:11 and 6:13: the repeated "against" before God / the law had been put
  on the noun or a lone article; it now joins the one εἰς / κατά that
  governs both nouns (precedent: "g = in#0 + in#1 // ἐν governs both").

Negatives split off verbs: 6:10 οὐκ (not / they could), 6:13 οὐ (never /
stops). In 6:2 οὐκ ἀρεστόν is one English word, unacceptable, so the two
tokens are joined.

## Readings

- 6:5 πλήρη: the publisher's Greek has this word in a different form
  (probably the indeclinable πλήρης); linked to "full" with a same-meaning
  note.
- 6:9 συνζητοῦντες: spelled differently in the publisher's Greek (probably
  συζητοῦντες); linked to "They began to argue" with a note.
- 6:13 "this" (this holy place): BSB follows a reading with an extra word
  (probably τούτου after ἁγίου); "this" is `t` with the reading reason.
- 6:3 is flagged as differing with 0/0 unmatched words, so no reading is
  claimed; δέ = Therefore is kept with a note.

## Notable decisions

- 6:5 and 6:9: every name of the Seven is linked (Stephen, Philip,
  Prochorus, Nicanor, Timon, Parmenas, Nicolas), and so are Freedmen
  (Λιβερτίνων), Cyrenians, Alexandrians, Cilicia and Asia. The list καί
  rendered only by commas are `s`; 6:5 καί before Φίλιππον = "as well as"
  and before Νικόλαον = "and" (publisher rows).
- 6:4 ἡμεῖς is `s`: BSB carries the "We" of verse 3 ("We will appoint …
  and will devote ourselves") and "ourselves" belongs to the verb
  προσκαρτερήσομεν, as "devoted themselves" in Acts 2:42.
- 6:3 οὓς καταστήσομεν ἐπὶ τῆς χρείας ταύτης: BSB makes the responsibility
  the object (appoint this responsibility to them), so οὕς = "to them",
  ἐπί and τῆς `s`.
- 6:6 καί = "who" and 6:13 τε = "where" kept as publisher rows with notes:
  BSB turns the connective into a relative, as Luke 8:5 and Mark 14:16
  (καί = where) and Luke 15:15 (καί = who).
- 6:9 τῶν before ἀπὸ Κιλικίας stands as a noun ("those") and keeps "men";
  τινες τῶν ("some of those") before ἐκ are `s` because BSB speaks of
  "resistance" arising (ἀνέστησαν = resistance arose).
- 6:13 ῥήματα is `s` (BSB: "never stops speaking against").
- 6:14 οὗτος after the name is `s` (BSB has no "this").
- Pronoun → name with a note: 6:11 αὐτοῦ = Stephen, 6:15 αὐτόν = Stephen.

## Doubts for the peer

- 6:9 καί = "including": BSB reads the Cyrenians and Alexandrians as part
  of the Freedmen synagogue. Kept as the publisher's link with a note; the
  alternative is καί `s` + "including" `t`.
- 6:9 "They began to argue" on the participle συνζητοῦντες: "began" could
  be `t` (the Greek participle is plain present); kept in the verb phrase.
- 6:2 "in order to wait on" on διακονεῖν: "in order" could be `t`, since
  BSB turns the infinitive subject into purpose; kept as publisher row.
- 6:1 ἐγένετο γογγυσμός = "began" / "to grumble" kept split as the publisher
  had it (alternative: join both tokens to "began to grumble").
