## Notable decisions

- 5:1 ἔχωμεν: the publisher's Greek has a different word here; BSB's "we
  have" is the indicative of the same verb (probably ἔχομεν), N1904 the
  hortatory subjunctive. Linked with the John 8:39 note "Same verb; BSB's
  Greek has another form of it here …" rather than `s`/`t`, since the
  English renders the same verb and person.
- Theological terms linked word by word, no interpretive notes:
  δικαιόω = "justified" (5:1, 5:9), εἰρήνη = "peace", χάρις = "grace" (5:2,
  5:15 twice, 5:17, 5:20, 5:21), καταλλάσσω/καταλλαγή = "reconciled" /
  "reconciliation" (5:10, 5:11), παράπτωμα = "trespass(es)" (5:15-18, 5:20),
  ἁμαρτία = "sin", χάρισμα = "gift" (5:15, 5:16), δωρεά / δώρημα = "gift",
  κρίμα = "judgment", κατάκριμα = "condemnation", δικαίωμα = "justification"
  (5:16) and "act of righteousness" (5:18), δικαίωσις = "justification",
  παράβασις = "transgressed" (5:14, noun as verb).
- Comparisons (5:12-21): ὥσπερ/ὡς = "just as"/"like", οὕτως = "so", καί =
  "also", εἰ = "if", γάρ = "For", πολλῷ = "how much", μᾶλλον = "more" (5:9,
  5:10, 5:15, 5:17). 5:12 καὶ οὕτως ("and so") = "so also" (καί → "also").
  5:15 οὕτως καί `s`: BSB folds "not as the trespass, so also the gift" into
  one sentence with no word for them.
- Connectives rendered with another connective stay linked: 5:7 γάρ =
  "though", 5:14 ἀλλά = "Nevertheless", 5:16 καί = "Again". Untranslated: δέ
  (5:3, 5:4 first, 5:11, 5:20 first), γάρ (5:7 first, 5:16), μέν (5:16), καί
  (5:2 "also", 5:7 "even").
- Prepositions BSB recasts kept on the preposition with a note: 5:16 δι’ =
  "the result of", ἐξ/ἐκ = "that followed", εἰς = "brought" (also 5:18), 5:21
  εἰς = "to bring". 5:18 δι’ (twice) `s` because "one trespass" / "one act of
  righteousness" become subjects.
- 5:6 the repeated ἔτι: the first = "still", the second `s` (BSB renders it
  once); "just" in "at just the right time" does not mean ἔτι, so `t`.
- 5:5 ἡ = "His" kept (article as possessive after BSB makes God the subject);
  ἐκκέχυται = "has poured out" with a note on the passive.
- 5:13 μὴ ὄντος = "no" + "when there is" kept (existential εἰμί carries
  "there is", majority convention).
- 5:15 τῇ (ἐν χάριτι τῇ τοῦ ἑνός) = the "the" before "grace"; χάριτι =
  "grace".
- 5:17 τοῦ = "that" kept (anaphoric article: "that one man").
- 5:18 ζωῆς = "and life" kept with a note (genitive rendered as BSB's "and
  life").

## Doubts for the peer

- 5:10 Θεῷ = "to Him" with "of God" (after "enemies") `t`. The publisher had
  "enemies of God" on ἐχθροί. Linking Θεῷ to "God" with "to Him" `t` is the
  alternative; I kept the dative role.
- 5:15, 5:17, 5:19 "of" on the substantive ἑνός ("of + one man") where there is
  no noun; ἑνός is the genitive head there, but it is a numeral. The
  alternative is "of" `t`.
- 5:1 ἔχωμεν linked with a form note versus `s` + "we have" `t` (the mood
  difference changes statement to exhortation).
- 5:6 which ἔτι carries "still" (publisher's first one kept).
- 5:14 ὁμοιώματι = "way that" kept; "that" `t` is the alternative.
- 5:16 δι’ = "the result of" and εἰς = "brought" (5:16, 5:18): supplied verbs
  `t` with the prepositions `s` is the alternative.
- 5:8 ὅτι = "in this" kept as one group.
