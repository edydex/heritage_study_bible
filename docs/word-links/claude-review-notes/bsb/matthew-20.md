# BSB Matthew 20: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: 34 of 34 (no verse was kept from an earlier reviewer).

Check: `fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 34, "problems": 0}`.

53 publisher rows in 26 verses were changed because they were wrong or
over-claimed. Another 26 rows only gained a TODO token or a word freed by a
neighbouring correction (for example ὃς + ἐάν → whoever).

## Publisher rows corrected

- 20:1: ἅμα = "early in", πρωῒ = "the morning" → joined: ἅμα πρωῒ = early in
  the morning (ἅμα alone does not mean "early").
- 20:2: μετά = "to pay" was wrong → μετὰ τῶν ἐργατῶν = them, plus `t to pay`.
  ἐκ = "for" was wrong: that "for" belongs to the accusative of time (for +
  day), and ἐκ joins δηναρίου (price). "and sent" → sent, `t and`.
- 20:3, 20:6, 20:9, 20:20, 20:25, 20:32: "and saw", "and found", "came and",
  "and knelt down", "and said", "and called" → the verb only; the "and" is
  `t` (a participle rendered as a finite verb). Same for "and did" in 20:5.
- 20:5: "they went" → οἱ = they (article as pronoun), ἀπῆλθον = went.
  "the sixth hour" over-claimed the first "hour" → the one ὥραν = hour + hour.
- 20:6: "eleventh hour" → eleventh, `t hour` (N1904 has no ὥραν here).
  "still others" → others, `t still`.
- 20:7: "So he told" → he told, `t So` (asyndeton).
- 20:8: δέ = "When" was wrong → When + came with the genitive absolute; δέ
  explained. "last ones hired" → last ones, `t hired`. ἕως = "and moving on",
  τῶν = "to the" → ἕως = and moving on to, τῶν = the.
- 20:9: οἱ = "The workers who were hired" (a lone article cannot carry
  "were") → οἱ = The workers, `t who were hired`.
- 20:10: καί = "So when" → So; "when" goes with the participle.
- 20:11: "On receiving their pay" → On receiving, `t their pay`.
- 20:12: οἱ = "men who were hired" → Οὗτοι = These men, οἱ explained,
  `t who were hired`. "only one" → one, `t only`. τοῖς = who and βαστάσασι =
  have borne → joined as article + participle.
- 20:13: "he answered" → ὁ = he, ἀποκριθεὶς + εἶπεν = answered.
  "I am not being unfair" and "Did you not agree" → οὐκ / οὐχί = not, verbs =
  I am + being unfair / Did you + agree.
- 20:14: τό = "pay" → τὸ σόν = your pay. καί = "I gave" was wrong → ὡς καί =
  the same as, `t I gave` (ellipsis).
- 20:15: "Do I not have the right" on ἔξεστιν → οὐκ = not, μοι = I, ἔξεστιν =
  Do + have the right. τοῖς = "what is" (article carrying "is") → τοῖς ἐμοῖς =
  what is mine. ἐστιν = "are you" → are; σου = you.
- 20:16: ἔσχατοι = "will be last" → last; the one ἔσονται = will be + will be.
- 20:19: εἰς = "to" before "the Gentiles" was wrong → that "to" is the dative
  (τοῖς = to the); εἰς τὸ ἐμπαῖξαι = to be mocked (purpose). "Him over" →
  Him; "over" goes with the verb (will deliver + over).
- 20:20: "of Zebedee’s" → Zebedee’s; "of" belongs to τῶν (of … sons).
- 20:21: "He inquired" → ὁ = He, εἶπεν = inquired. οὗτοι = "of these" →
  these; "of" goes with εἷς (one of).
- 20:22: "You do not know" → Οὐκ = not, verb = You do + know. "the brothers
  answered" → answered, `t the brothers` (verb ending only).
- 20:23: "Jesus said" → said, `t Jesus`. "You will indeed drink" → μέν =
  indeed, verb = You will + drink. ἀλλά = "These seats belong" was wrong →
  ἀλλά explained, `t These seats belong` (ellipsis).
- 20:24: καί = "When" was wrong → When + heard with the participle; καί
  explained. "heard about this" → `t about this`.
- 20:25: "called them aside" → called + aside, αὐτούς = them.
- 20:28: "did not come" → οὐκ = not, verb = did + come.
- 20:29: αὐτῶν = "As they" → they; As goes with the genitive absolute.
- 20:31: "they cried out" → οἱ = they, verb = cried out.

## Notable decisions

- Joint units: ἀνθρώπῳ οἰκοδεσπότῃ = a landowner (as in 13:52); ἅμα πρωῒ;
  κατ’ ἰδίαν = aside; ὃς ἐάν / ὃς ἄν = whoever; ὃ ἐάν = whatever;
  ἀποκριθεὶς … εἶπεν = answered / replied; ὁ ὀφθαλμὸς … πονηρός = envious
  (σου = you, ἐστιν = are); αἰτοῦσά τι = to make a request; ἵνα σιωπήσωσιν =
  to be silent; articular infinitive τὸ … καθίσαι = to sit.
- Articles used as pronouns are linked: ὁ δέ / οἱ δέ = he / He / they
  (20:5, 13, 21, 31); article as possessive kept: my (20:4, 20:7), their
  (20:8, 20:25).
- One Greek word, two English copies: ὥραν = hour + hour (20:5); ἔσονται =
  will be + will be (20:16).
- Textual differences from N1904:
  - 20:6 "hour": N1904 lacks ὥραν (the Byzantine text has it) → `t`.
  - 20:17 "disciples": BSB follows τοὺς δώδεκα μαθητάς → `t`. N1904 reads
    Μέλλων ἀναβαίνειν ("being about to go up"); BSB's wording matches
    ἀναβαίνων. Linked jointly as As + was going up, with a note.
  - 20:21 the middle "Your" (at Your right hand): N1904 has no σου after
    δεξιῶν → `t`.
  - 20:26 "It shall not be": N1904 has the present ἐστίν; linked to
    It shall + be, with a note (BSB matches ἔσται or reads a prescriptive present).
- Supplied English (`t`): to pay (20:2), them (20:8: pay them), their pay
  (20:11), who were hired (20:9, 20:12), hired (20:8), only (20:12),
  still (20:6), So (20:7), about this (20:24), These seats belong and Jesus
  (20:23), the brothers (20:22).
- Kept from the publisher on purpose: ἰδού = there were (20:30, presentational);
  ἐγερθήσεται = He will be raised to life (20:19, one verb); ἀργούς / ἀργοί =
  doing nothing; πρῶτοι = original workers; αὐτοί = of them (20:10).
- Left unexpressed (`s`): addressee datives after verbs of saying, λέγοντες
  before quotations (20:30, 20:31), ἐν τῇ ὁδῷ (20:17), resumptive τοῦτο and
  ὑπὸ τοῦ (passive turned active) in 20:23, ἀπό in "leaving Jericho" (20:29).

## Doubts for the peer

- 20:17: is the joint group Μέλλων ἀναβαίνειν = As + was going up right, or
  should Μέλλων be `s` (reading difference) with only ἀναβαίνειν = was going up?
- 20:2: "to pay" is `t` and ἐκ joins δηναρίου; linking ἐκ (price) to "to pay"
  is the alternative.
- 20:9: οἱ = "The workers" (article as "those"), or οἱ = The with "workers" as `t`?
- 20:21: middle "Your" as `t` (reading difference) rather than letting the
  one σου after εὐωνύμων cover both sides.
- 20:6 "still others" and 20:12 "only": I made the added words `t`; the peer
  may judge them English idiom for ἄλλους / μίαν and restore the publisher rows.
