draft had no KEEP blocks). Final check: `fromtext` →
`{"versesOk": 31, "problems": 0}`.

92 of the 502 publisher `g` rows changed, in 26 verses. 11 of them only
gained unlinked Greek tokens, with the English unchanged (ὃς εἶχεν = whose
16:1, τὰ ὑπάρχοντα = possessions 16:1, οὐ δύνῃ = you cannot 16:2, οὐκ ἰσχύω

## Publisher rows corrected

Words supplied by English that had been folded into a Greek word (now `t`):

- "Jesus" sat on Ἔλεγεν (16:1) and "Abraham" on εἶπεν (16:31); now `t`.
- 16:2: Τί "What is" → What; `t is`.
- 16:3: αἰσχύνομαι "and too ashamed" → too ashamed; `t and` (asyndeton).
- 16:13: Θεῷ "both God" → God; `t both` (as Matthew 6:24).
- 16:15: βδέλυγμα "is detestable" → detestable; `t is` (nominal clause).
- 16:16: προφῆται "Prophets were proclaimed" → Prophets; `t were proclaimed` (no Greek verb).
- 16:21: πιπτόντων "crumbs that fell" → τῶν πιπτόντων = the + that fell; `t crumbs` (EVIDENCE: publisher Greek = N1904, which has no word for crumbs).
- 16:21: ἐπέλειχον "and licked" → licked; 16:23 ὁρᾷ "and saw" → saw; `t and` (participle joined to the main verb).
- 16:26: ὅπως "so that even" → so that; `t even`.
- 16:29: ἀκουσάτωσαν "let your brothers listen to" → let + listen to; `t your brothers`.

Words on the wrong Greek word:

- 16:1: διεβλήθη "was accused of" → was accused; ὡς ("as", introducing the charge) = of.
- 16:2: αὐτόν "him in" → him; φωνήσας = he called + in. τόν "an", τῆς "of" → `s`; λόγον = an account, οἰκονομίας = of + management.
- 16:3: ἀπ’ "away" → ἀφαιρεῖται = is taking away; ἀπ’ ἐμοῦ = my (the person deprived).
- 16:5: ἑαυτοῦ "of his" → his; χρεοφειλετῶν = of + debtors (the partitive genitive).
- 16:6, 16:7, 16:30: in ὁ δέ the verb carried the pronoun ("he answered", "he replied", "he said"); now ὁ = he and the verb = answered/replied/said (as Luke 7:40, 10:27). 16:6: εἶπεν "said the manager" → said; ὁ = the manager, with a note.
- 16:6: γράψον "and write" → write; καί = and (was TODO).
- 16:7: Σύ "do you" → you; ὀφείλεις = do + owe.
- 16:8: τούτου "of this" → this; αἰῶνος = of + age. ὑπέρ "than are" → than; εἰσιν = are + are (verb understood again).
- 16:10: πιστός/ἄδικος "be faithful/dishonest" and ἐστιν "will" → ἐστιν = will + be, the adjective alone. ὁ πιστός = Whoever is faithful and ὁ … ἄδικος = whoever is dishonest are joined (article + adjective, like rule 3), instead of the copula sitting on the adjective.
- 16:11: τό carried "with" (true riches); now πιστεύσει = will entrust + with, τό `s`.
- 16:12: τῷ carried "the belongings"; now τῷ = the, ἀλλοτρίῳ ("belonging to another") = belongings of another.
- 16:16: ἡ carried "the gospel" and βασιλεία "of the kingdom". Now εὐαγγελίζεται = the gospel of + is being preached, ἡ = the (kingdom), βασιλεία = kingdom.
- 16:17: τοῦ carried "of the"; now τοῦ = the, νόμου = of + Law. εὐκοπώτερον "easier for" → easier; οὐρανόν = for heaven (as Mark 10:25).
- 16:19: εὐφραινόμενος "who lived each day in joyous" → who lived + joyous; καθ’ ἡμέραν = each day (was TODO); λαμπρῶς = in + splendor.
- 16:24: τοῦ "of" → `s`; δακτύλου = of + finger.
- 16:25: τά carried "things" and ὁμοίως "received". Now ἀγαθά = good things, τά `s`; ἀπέλαβες = you received + received (verb understood again), ὁμοίως `s`.
- 16:26: μέγα "a great" → great; χάσμα = a + chasm.

Negatives split off verbs: 16:11, 16:12 οὐκ = not, ἐγένεσθε = you have + been; 16:28 μή = not, καί = also, ἔλθωσιν = will + end up; 16:31 οὐκ = not, ἀκούουσιν = they do + listen to, οὐδέ = not + even, πεισθήσονται = they will + be persuaded. Where English has one word "cannot", οὐ/μή and the verb are joined (16:2, 16:13, 16:26).

## Notable decisions

- Readings: EVIDENCE shows a different publisher text in 16:12, 16:16, 16:23 and 16:31.
  - 16:12 differs in meaning: BSB "belongings of your own" (probably ὑμέτερον) against N1904 ἡμέτερον ("ours"). ἡμέτερον is `s` and "belongings of your own" is `t`, both with the reading reason; τό is `s`.
  - 16:16 Ἰωάνου = John and 16:23 Ἅιδῃ = Hades: same name, spelled differently (probably Ἰωάννου and ᾅδῃ), linked with the standard note.
  - 16:31: one word differs, but every English word is covered by a same-meaning link, so nothing is claimed. The difference is probably a spelling, for example οὐδ’ for οὐδέ.
- 16:1: καί and οὗτος (and this one) are `s`: BSB folds the second clause into "whose manager was accused". αὐτῷ (to him, the rich man) is `s`. τούς = His (article as possessive).
- 16:4: "people will welcome" kept on δέξωνται (the generic subject of a third-plural verb), unlike 16:29, where "your brothers" names the subject and is `t`.
- 16:9: ἐκ = use and ποιήσατε = to make (publisher rows kept, with notes); τῆς ἀδικίας = worldly with τῆς `s`, and ἀδίκῳ = worldly in 16:11.
- 16:11: τὸ ἀληθινόν: ἀληθινόν = true riches (publisher row kept, with a note on the substantive); τό `s`.
- 16:13: γάρ `s` and οὐ δύνασθε = You cannot, as Matthew 6:24.
- 16:14: αὐτόν = Jesus, with a note. ταῦτα = of this, πάντα = all (publisher rows kept).
- 16:18: ὁ ἀπολύων = who divorces (Matthew 5:32 kept ὁ and the participle apart; the rule-3 join is used here). ἀπὸ ἀνδρός is `s` (implicit in "divorced").
- 16:21: ἀλλὰ καί = Even, joined as one idiom.
- 16:22: ἐγένετο δέ = One day (Luke precedent); αὐτόν `s`; κόλπον = side with a note.
- 16:27: ἵνα is `s` (English uses a direct command); αὐτόν = Lazarus, with a note.
- 16:30: πορευθῇ ("goes") = is sent, publisher row kept with a note; EVIDENCE shows no different reading here.

## Doubts for the peer

- 16:12: the reading claim rests on the EVIDENCE line plus the publisher leaving "of your own" unaligned; the meaning difference (yours/ours) is inferred, not seen.
- 16:21: `t crumbs`. The alternative is to keep "crumbs" inside τῶν πιπτόντων as the noun of the substantive.
- 16:1: ὡς = of. The alternative is ὡς `s` with "of" inside διεβλήθη ("was accused of").
- 16:1: οὗτος `s`. One could instead link it to "manager" with οἰκονόμον, but that puts two Greek words on one English word.
- 16:10 and 16:15: article + adjective joined (Whoever is faithful, what is prized). The alternative is the article alone (Whoever/what) with the supplied "is" as `t`.
- 16:28: ὅπως διαμαρτύρηται = Let him warn as one group; the alternative is ὅπως `s`.
- 16:31: which word differs in BSB's Greek is not visible; if it is not a spelling difference, a reading note may be needed.
