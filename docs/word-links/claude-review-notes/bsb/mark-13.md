## Publisher rows corrected

- "When/As/While" on the wrong word: 13:1 καί = "As" and 13:3 καί = "While"
  → the genitive absolutes carry them (ἐκπορευομένου … ἐκ = As + was leaving;
  καθημένου = While + was sitting); the opening καί is `s`.
- Participle "and", now `t`: 13:11 ("and hand you over", παραδιδόντες =
  hand + over), 13:36 ("and find", εὕρῃ = find). 13:33 "and stay alert": no
  καί in the Greek, `t and`.
- Negatives folded into verbs: 13:7 μή = "do not" → not, θροεῖσθε = do + be
  alarmed; 13:11 μή = "do not" → not, προμεριμνᾶτε = do + worry beforehand;
  13:14 δεῖ = "it should not be" → οὐ = not; 13:18 γένηται = "this will not
  occur"; 13:20 ἐκολόβωσεν = "had not cut short" → μή = not; 13:21 πιστεύετε
  = "do not believe it"; 13:24 δώσει = "will not give"; 13:30 παρέλθῃ = "will
  not pass away" (οὐ μή = not); 13:31 "will never pass away" (οὐ = never);
  13:33 and 13:35 οἴδατε = "you do not know"; 13:11 ἐστε = "it will not be";
  13:2 οὐ = "Not one" (οὐ μή = Not, λίθος = one stone).
- Verb or helper on the wrong word: 13:4 πότε = "when will" (will + happen
  now on ἔσται) and τί = "what will be" (`t will be`, as Matthew 24:3);
  13:23 πάντα = "everything in advance" → προείρηκα = I have told + in
  advance; 13:16 καί = "And let", ἐπιστρεψάτω = "return for" → let + return
  (with εἰς τὰ ὀπίσω), ἆραι = for; 13:14 τότε = "then let", ὁ = "let the"
  → ὁ ἀναγινώσκων = the reader, νοείτω / φευγέτωσαν = let + understand / let

  → μή τις = that no one; 13:9 ἕνεκεν = "On My account" → On + account,
  ἐμοῦ = My; 13:13 ὁ = "the one who" joined with ὑπομείνας.
- English-only words claimed by Greek, now `t`: 13:1 "the" (ποταποί);

## Notable decisions

- Different readings, only where EVIDENCE allows:
  - 13:2: `t here` (BSB follows ὧδε; N1904 lacks it).
  - 13:22: δέ and ποιήσουσιν are `s`; "For" and "perform" are `t` (BSB
    follows γάρ and δώσουσιν). This is a deliberate split pair, required by
    the reading rule.
  - 13:31: οὐ = never, with a note that BSB's Greek has οὐ μή.
  - 13:3: Ἰωάνης = John (N1904 spelling only). 13:15 and 13:19: the counted
    difference is not rendered (13:15 probably δέ), so nothing is claimed.
- Words stated once for two Greek words: ποταποὶ … ποταπαί = magnificent
  (13:1); the two ἔσονται = There will be (13:8). One ὑμᾶς linked to both
  "you"s of "arrest you and hand you over" (13:11).
- Joined units: Βλέπετε … ἑαυτούς = be on your guard (13:9, ὑμεῖς `s`; also
  `s` in 13:23); καταβάτω … εἰσελθάτω = Let + go back inside (13:15);
  οἵα οὐ γέγονεν τοιαύτη = unmatched (13:19); πρὸς τὸ = that would (13:22);
  καὶ ἐπισυνάξει: καί `s`, verb = to gather (13:27).
- Articles kept as demonstratives with a note: τάς = those (13:20), τήν =
  this (13:28). Articles kept as "of" (13:1 τῶν, 13:14 τῆς) as earlier files do.

## Doubts for the peer

- 13:22: the `s`/`t` pairs for ποιήσουσιν/"perform" and δέ/"For".
- 13:6: "He" of "I am He" as `t` rather than on εἰμι.
- 13:11: ὑμᾶς linked to both "you"s; `t you#1` is the alternative.
- 13:15: whether "go back inside" should carry both καταβάτω and εἰσελθάτω.
- 13:29: ὑμεῖς = "you" in the when-clause (it is grammatically the subject
  of γινώσκετε, BSB "know").
- 13:9: ἑαυτούς joined into "be on your guard" while ὑμεῖς is `s`.
- 13:14: δεῖ = "it should" with `t be`.
