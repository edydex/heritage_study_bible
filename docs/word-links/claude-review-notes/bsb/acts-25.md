# BSB Acts 25: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: all 27 (25:1-27). No KEEP blocks in this chapter. Final
check: `fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 27, "problems": 0}`.

91 of the 439 publisher `g` rows changed, in 24 of the 27 verses (25:3,
25:10 and 25:27 kept every publisher row and only resolved TODO tokens and
gained notes). 10 of these only gained a token, with the English unchanged:
25:4 ἐν τάχει = soon; 25:5 ἐστιν ἐν = has done; 25:9 ἀποκριθεὶς … εἶπεν =
said (as 8:34); 25:11 τὸ ἀποθανεῖν = to die (articular infinitive); 25:16
πρὸς οὕς = them, πρὶν ἤ = before (as 7:2), ὁ κατηγορούμενος = he; 25:21
ἕως οὗ = until; 25:23 τοῖς κατ’ ἐξοχήν = leading; 25:26 οὐκ … τι =
nothing. The other 81 were corrected, split or moved.

No parallel Gospel chapter. Verse division matches N1904 throughout, so
there are no `m` or `g @` cross-verse links.

## Publisher rows corrected

Lone articles carrying a preposition (article keeps "the" or its
possessive/demonstrative sense; the preposition goes on the noun):

- 25:1 τῇ "in the" → "the", "in" on ἐπαρχείῳ; 25:8 τῶν, 25:15 τῶν, 25:21
  τοῦ and 25:23 τῆς "of the" → "the", "of" on Ἰουδαίων, Σεβαστοῦ, πόλεως;
  25:14 τῷ "before the" → "the", "before" on βασιλεῖ; 25:26 τῷ "to" → "our"
  (possessive article), "to" on κυρίῳ ("our sovereign" had been on κυρίῳ).

When/After/Since/While moved off δέ/οὖν to the participle or genitive
absolute: 25:6 "After" (Διατρίψας), 25:7 "When" (παραγενομένου), 25:13
"After" (διαγενομένων), 25:15 "While" (γενομένου; was on μου), 25:17 "when"
(συνελθόντων; was on οὖν), 25:18 "when" (σταθέντες; was on οὗ), 25:20
"Since" (ἀπορούμενος), 25:21 "when" (ἐπικαλεσαμένου), 25:25 "since"
(ἐπικαλεσαμένου; was on τούτου), 25:26 "after" (γενομένης). 25:14 "Since"
moved from δέ to ὡς.

English words with no Greek counterpart that had been folded into a Greek
word (now `t`):

- Supplied objects: 25:2 and 25:15 "their case" (ἐνεφάνισαν has no object;
  as the Acts 24:1 draft), 25:25 "him" (send).
- Supplied names: 25:6 "Festus" (participle καταβάς), 25:22 "Festus#1"
  (φησίν).
- Added adverbs and verbs: 25:4 and 25:5 "there", 25:25 "now", 25:18 "to
  speak" (σταθέντες means only "having stood"), 25:22 "said" (was on πρός;
  the Greek leaves the verb understood).
- Added contrasts and quantifiers: 25:18 "But", 25:19 "but" (was on ὅν),
  25:5 "some" (was on Οἱ with "let"), 25:11 "truth" and "to#1" (were on ὧν
  and οὗτοι).
- "and" joining a participle to the main verb with no καί: 25:5, 25:6,
  25:12, 25:15, 25:17. 25:24 "that" before the infinitive δεῖν.
- 25:17 "I#0" (participle ποιησάμενος; the first person comes from
  ἐκέλευσα, which now has "I#1 + ordered that", as 23:27 draft).

Other moves:

- 25:2 καί "They" → `s` (new sentence); "They" joins παρεκάλουν.
- 25:5 jussive "let" moved from Οἱ to κατηγορείτωσαν (let + accuse).
- 25:7 and 25:11 negatives split off the verbs (οὐκ = not, they could;
  οὐ = not, I do + refuse).
- 25:8 the first Οὔτε joined with τι = "no" (was all on ἥμαρτον, now "I
  have committed + offense").
- 25:9 "to do the Jews a favor" split as Acts 24:27 draft: τοῖς = the,
  Ἰουδαίοις = Jews, χάριν = a favor, καταθέσθαι = to do.
- 25:11 "however" moved from μέν to οὖν (μέν `s`, as 25:4); πέπραχα had
  "of" alone: ἀδικῶ καὶ … πέπραχα now jointly "I am guilty of"; με = me#1
  (was folded into "hand me over").
- 25:14 ὑπό "whom" → `s`; the passive participle carries "whom + left".
  τὰ κατά = case, Παῦλον = Paul’s (was "Paul’s case" on the name).
- 25:16 ἔθος = the + custom, Ῥωμαίοις = Roman (both had been on ἔθος);
  "over" moved from ἄνθρωπον to χαρίζεσθαι; κατὰ πρόσωπον ἔχοι = to face.
- 25:17 "with me" moved from ἐνθάδε to the συν- participle (as 25:5).
- 25:18 rebuilt: περὶ οὗ = him, οἱ = his, οὐδεμίαν = not + any, αἰτίαν …
  ἔφερον = they did + charge + with, ὧν `s`, πονηρῶν = of the crimes.
- 25:20 ἐγώ = I#0 + I#1 (explicit subject of both the participle and
  ἔλεγον, as 22:3); κἀκεῖ = and + there ("there" had been on κρίνεσθαι).
- 25:24 ἄνδρες "who" → `s`; οἱ συνπαρόντες = who are present with.
- 25:25 αὐτοῦ … τούτου = he#1; δέ = and.

## Readings

No different reading changes a link's meaning. Spelling-only differences
are linked with a note: ἐπαρχείῳ and Καισαρίας (25:1), Καισαρίαν (25:4,
25:6, 25:13), συνλαλήσας (25:12), συνπαρόντες (25:24). 25:10 (1/1) is
linked with a same-sense note in the οὐδὲν ἠδίκηκα clause (probably the
aorist ἠδίκησα). 25:17 (0/1): BSB's extra word is probably αὐτῶν (as NA28),
so "they" is `t` with the reading reason. 25:26 (0/0) is order or form
only.

## Notable decisions

- Names linked: Festus, Caesarea, Jerusalem, Paul, Agrippa, Bernice, Felix,
  Caesar, Jesus. Pronouns BSB renames are linked: 25:2 αὐτόν = Festus,
  25:3 and 25:7 αὐτοῦ = Paul. Σεβαστός = Emperor (25:21, 25:25).
- 25:2 τε = where kept (Acts 6:13); 25:3 ὅπως = by kept as a changed
  connective.
- 25:4 ἑαυτόν = I myself (reflexive of indirect speech, BSB direct
  speech); μέλλειν = am, ἐκπορεύεσθαι = going.
- 25:10 καί = yourself with σύ = you kept (as 22:29 καί = himself).
- 25:23 τε `s` (no "both"); 25:24 τε = both (BSB says both … and).
- Third-person subjects stay on participles rendered as finite verbs
  (25:3 "because they were preparing", 25:6 "he sat") by the 16:3
  convention.

## Doubts for the peer

- 25:3 αἰτούμενοι = "to grant them" (the publisher's row, a converse
  rendering of "asking for themselves"); the alternative is `s` with "to
  grant them" `t`.
- 25:19 δέ = "only" (contrast with 25:18); the alternative is δέ `s` and
  "only" `t`.
- 25:11 "truth" `t` and ὧν `s` with "to#1" `t`; οὐδέν could take "no
  truth" as a shifted rendering of "nothing".
- 25:2 and 25:15 "their case" `t` versus keeping it inside ἐνεφάνισαν as
  the legal "bring charges".
- 25:18 "to speak" `t` versus the publisher's "rose to speak" on σταθέντες;
  "with" placed in the charge group rather than `t`.
- 25:11 ἀδικῶ καὶ … πέπραχα joined; πέπραχα `s` with "of#0" `t` is the
  alternative.
- 25:8 "Then" `t` (genitive absolute opening); Οὔτε … τι = "no" joined.
