# BSB 2 Corinthians 12: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: 20 of 21 (12:1-14, 12:16-21). 12:15 is a KEEP block
(already reviewed by Codex) and is copied unchanged. Final check:
`fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 21, "problems": 0}`.

80 of the 320 publisher `g` rows outside the KEEP verse were changed, in
19 of the 20 reviewed verses. In 12:8 every publisher row stayed; only
ὑπὲρ τούτου needed a decision. Every publisher row was checked, including
the unchanged ones. After the changes, no "of the", "to the", "in the" or
"for" sits on a lone article or a number. Articles with no English "the"
are `s` with the standard reason (README rule 5). Names are linked:
Παράδεισον = "Paradise", Σατανᾶ = "of Satan", Τίτον/Τίτος = "Titus" (twice).
The terms are linked word by word, with no interpretive notes: ὀπτασίας =
"visions", ἀποκαλύψεις/ἀποκαλύψεων = "revelations", ἁρπαγέντα/ἡρπάγη = "was
caught up", σκόλοψ = "a thorn", ἄγγελος = "a messenger", χάρις = "grace",
δύναμις = "power" (twice), ἀσθένεια = "weakness(es)", σημεῖα = "marks",
σημείοις = "signs", δυνάμεσιν = "miracles".

Conventions followed: 1 Corinthians 5:3 and John 10:41 (μέν … δέ =
"Although"), 1 Corinthians 1:15 and 16:11 (μή "no" + τις "one"),
2 Corinthians 2:7 (μή πως joined), 1 Corinthians 10:33 (a participle keeps
"am" but not "I"), Acts 22:22 and 1 Corinthians 5:5 (τοιοῦτος rendered by a
pronoun or "this man"), Philippians 2:4 (τὰ + genitive joined as one unit),
2 Corinthians 4:17 and 1 Corinthians 5:4 (a possessive from an extra word in
BSB's Greek is `t`), Acts 15:24-25 (no cross-verse link when both texts
divide the verses at the same place), 1 Timothy 3:2 (δεῖ = "must" only).

## KEEP verse

- 12:15: I checked it and found no real error. All the links are sound: ἐγώ =
  "I", the joined δαπανήσω καὶ ἐκδαπανηθήσομαι, and the passive ἀγαπῶμαι
  given as "will … love me" with a `t` "you".

## Publisher rows corrected

Subjects, copulas and objects with no Greek owner (`t`):

- 12:1 δεῖ "I must" → "must"; "I#0" `t` (δεῖ is impersonal). "there is" `t`.
- 12:2 εἴτε "Whether it was" → "Whether"; "it was" `t`. Θεός "but God" →
  "God", "but" `t` (no connective); the same in 12:3.
- 12:11 ὑμεῖς "but you" → "you"; "but" `t`.
- 12:16 ὑπάρχων "as I am" → "as + am"; "I#1" `t` (participle).
- 12:18 Τίτον "Titus to visit you" → "Titus"; "to visit you" `t`. ἀδελφόν
  "brother with him" → "brother"; "with" goes to συναπέστειλα ("sent
  along with"), and "him" is `t`.
- 12:19 ὑπέρ "is" → "to" (purpose); "is" `t`.
- 12:20 ἐλθών "when I come" → "when + come"; "I#1" `t` (participle).
  πως "that there may be" → joined with μή as "that"; "there may be" `t`.
  ἀκαταστασίαι "and disorder" → "disorder"; list "and" `t`.
- 12:21 μή "I am afraid that" → "that"; "I am afraid" `t` (it repeats
  verse 20, which is the same verse division in both texts).

Lone articles and prepositions:

- 12:7 τῇ (1) "because of these" → `s`; "because of" goes on ὑπερβολῇ
  (causal dative), and "these" on τῶν (the revelations of verse 1). τῇ (12)
  "in my" → "my"; "in" goes on σαρκί.
- 12:11 τῶν "to those" → "those"; "to" goes on ὑπερλίαν ἀποστόλων (the
  genitive of comparison), which is joined as "super-apostles".
- 12:14 τοῖς "for their" (twice) → "their"; "for" goes on γονεῦσιν and
  τέκνοις. τά "possessions" (an article carrying a noun) → τὰ ὑμῶν joined as
  "your possessions".
- 12:18 τῷ/τοῖς "in the" → "the"; "in" goes on Πνεύματι and ἴχνεσιν.
- 12:19 τά "of this" → "this"; "of" goes with πάντα ("all of").

Wrong owners:

- 12:2 δεκατεσσάρων "who fourteen" → "fourteen"; "who" goes on τὸν
  τοιοῦτον, which takes up the man again. The first οὐκ οἶδα is `s`
  (BSB says "I do not know" once, for both alternatives).
- 12:6 γάρ "Even" → `s`; "Even" `t` (it does not mean "for").
- 12:10 καί "in#4" → `s` (comma); "in#4" is the one ἐν (8) understood
  again.
- 12:11 με "me to it" → "me"; "drove + to it" on ἠναγκάσατε. ὑφ’ ὑμῶν
  joined as "you" (the active subject). οὐδέν taken out of "I am in no way
  inferior": οὐδέν = "in no way", ὑστέρησα = "I am + inferior".
- 12:18 Τίτος "Did Titus" → "Titus"; "Did" goes on ἐπλεονέκτησεν. οὐ (15)
  "and follow" → `s` (BSB joins the two questions); "and" `t`; "follow" is
  περιεπατήσαμεν understood again.
- 12:19 οἰκοδομῆς "to build you up" → "build + up"; ὑμῶν = "you", "to" on
  ὑπέρ.
- 12:20 the second μή "I fear" → μή πως = "that"; "I fear" is φοβοῦμαι
  understood again. κἀγώ "and" → "and + me" (ἐγώ is the explicit pronoun),
  and εὑρεθῶ "may not find me" → "may + find".
- 12:21 ἐλθόντος "when I come" → "when + come"; the explicit μου gets "I".
  ᾗ ἔπραξαν joined as "their acts of".

Negatives split off verbs (οὐ/οὐκ/οὐχ → "not", and the verb keeps
do/will/would/am): 12:2, 12:3, 12:4, 12:5, 12:6, 12:13, 12:14 (three
times), 12:16, 12:18, 12:20 (twice), 12:21 (μή).

Joins: 12:1 μέν … δέ = "Although"; 12:5 and 12:13 εἰ μή = "except";
12:6 μή = "so + no" and τις = "one" (the publisher's "no one" on τις
over-claimed the negative), εἰς ἐμέ = "me"; 12:7 ἵνα μή = "to keep + from",
and ὑπεραίρωμαι = "me + becoming conceited"; 12:13 τί … ἐστιν ὅ = "In what
way"; 12:17 πρὸς ὑμᾶς = "you"; 12:21 τῶν προημαρτηκότων = "who have sinned
earlier".

## Notable decisions

- 12:4 ἄρρητα = "too sacred for words" and ῥήματα = "The things"; ἐξόν =
  "is + permitted" (an impersonal participle rendered as a passive verb, not
  a supplied copula); ἅ = "things that" (relative, publisher row kept).
- 12:7 the closing repeated ἵνα μὴ ὑπεραίρωμαι is `s`; BSB renders the
  clause once, where the first one stands.
- 12:9 "My#1" (power) is `t`: BSB follows a reading with an extra word,
  probably μου after δύναμις. The article ἡ (8) is `s`, following the
  precedent for possessives that come from an extra word.
- 12:12 "true" `t`; μέν, τε (no "both") and the first καί (comma) are `s`;
  τοῦ `s` with "of an apostle" on ἀποστόλου.
- 12:14 τρίτον τοῦτο: τοῦτο is `s` (BSB says "a third time").
- 12:17 μή (question) `s`; ὧν `s` (relative implicit); αὐτοῦ `s` (BSB does
  not repeat "him", and "by" stands before "anyone").
- Readings: 12:6 has an extra word in BSB's Greek, probably τι after ἀκούει.
  It has no English counterpart and nothing is claimed. 12:4 and 12:12 differ
  without an added or missing word (spelling or order), and no reading is
  claimed.
- Publisher rows kept, though they could be challenged: 12:6 ὑπέρ "with
  more than" and με "in me"; 12:10 ὑπέρ "for the sake" + Χριστοῦ "of
  Christ"; 12:11 εἰ "though" + καί "even"; 12:16 ὑμᾶς "to you" (English
  needs these prepositions because it recasts the verb).

## Doubts for the peer

- 12:3/12:4: BSB's "that" in 12:3 renders the ὅτι that begins 12:4, but both
  texts divide the verses at the same place. So I used `t` in 12:3 and `s` in
  12:4, with notes on both sides, and no cross-verse link. Check that this
  is the right reading of the rule.
- 12:18 μήτι = "in any way" (publisher row kept, as in John 4:29, where a
  rendered μήτι is linked). The alternative is μήτι `s` with "in any way" `t`.
  Also περιεπατήσαμεν = "walk + follow", treating "follow (in the
  footsteps)" as the same verb understood again, not as a `t`.
- 12:2 τὸν τοιοῦτον = "who". The alternative is `s` for τὸν τοιοῦτον and `t`
  for "who" (a relative supplied for the participle).
- 12:13 τί … ἐστιν ὅ joined as "In what way" (ἐστιν absorbed), rather than
  ἐστιν and ὅ `s`.
- 12:14 τὰ ὑμῶν joined as "your possessions", rather than keeping τά as a
  substantival article = "possessions", as 1 Corinthians 10:24 does.
