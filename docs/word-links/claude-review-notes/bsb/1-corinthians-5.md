# BSB 1 Corinthians 5: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: all 13 (5:1-13). No KEEP blocks in this chapter. Final
check: `fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 13, "problems": 0}`.

54 of the 188 publisher `g` rows changed, in 12 of the 13 verses (5:9 keeps
every publisher row). 2 of the 54 only joined tokens with the English
unchanged (listed under "Joined units"). Unchanged rows were checked too.

Readings: EVIDENCE shows the publisher's Greek differs from N1904 only in
5:4 and 5:8.

- 5:4: BSB's Greek has one extra word, probably ἡμῶν after the first
  Κυρίου ("our Lord Jesus"). "our" is `t` with the reading reason. BSB then
  gives "the Lord Jesus" without "our" in the second phrase, so N1904's
  ἡμῶν there is `s`.
- 5:8: BSB's Greek spells the word for sincerity differently (probably
  εἰλικρινείας for N1904 εἰλικρινίας). It has the same meaning, so it is
  linked ("of + sincerity") with a note.

No other reading is claimed.

Conventions followed: 1 Corinthians 3:16, Romans 6:16 for οὐκ οἴδατε (οὐκ =
"not", οἴδατε = "Do you + know"); John 10:41 for μέν … δέ = "Although";

## Publisher rows corrected

Lone articles carrying a case word:

- 5:4 τοῦ (first) "of" → `s`; Κυρίου = "of + Lord". τοῦ (second) "of the" →
  "the"; Κυρίου = "of + Lord".
- 5:5 τῆς "of the" → "the"; σαρκός = "of + flesh". τοῦ "of the" → "the";
  Κυρίου = "of + Lord".
- 5:10 τούτου "of this" → "this"; κόσμου = "of + world". τοῦ (4) `s`.
- 5:11 τῷ "With" → `s`; "With" joins συνεσθίειν ("With + do + eat", the
  συν- of "eat with").

Supplied words moved off Greek words (`t`):

- 5:1 Ὅλως "It is actually" → "actually"; ἀκούεται "reported that there is"
  → "It is + reported"; "that there is" and the copula "is" in "that is
  intolerable" are `t`.
- 5:3 "from you" off ἀπών and "with you" off παρών; "and" off κέκρικα
  (no καί).
- 5:4 "am with you" off ἐμοῦ, which keeps "I" (see doubts).
- 5:6 Οὐ "is not" → "not"; the copula is `t`.
- 5:7 "really" off ἐστε ("you + are").
- 5:10 Οὐ "I was not" → "not"; πάντως "including" → `s`; "I was" and
  "including" are `t` (the clause has no verb; see doubts).
- 5:11 ἐάν "but" → joined ἐάν τις = "anyone who"; "but" is `t` (no
  connective in the Greek); ὀνομαζόμενος "who claims to be" → "claims to
  be".
- 5:12 μοι "business of mine is it" → "of mine"; "business" and "is it"
  are `t`. ἔξω "outside the church" → "outside"; "the church" is `t`.

Person, modal, negatives and wrong owners:

- 5:1 τοιαύτη "that is" → "of a kind"; πορνεία (second) "of a kind" → `s`
  (BSB does not repeat the noun); ἥτις = "that".
- 5:2 οὐχί "Shouldn’t you" and ἐπενθήσατε "have been stricken with grief"
  joined as "Shouldn’t you + have been stricken with grief": "you" and the
  modal belong to the verb, and the contraction cannot be split (see
  doubts).
- 5:3 γάρ "Although" → `s`; μέν … δέ = "Although". ἐγώ "I" → "I + I + I +
  I". παρών (first) "I am present with you" → "am present"; παρών (second)
  "I were present" → "were present". κέκρικα "and I have already
  pronounced judgment" → "have + pronounced judgment on"; ἤδη = "already".
  οὕτως "who" → `s`; τόν "on the one" joins κατεργασάμενον ("the one who
  did").
- 5:4 ὑμῶν "When you" → "you"; "When" joins συναχθέντων ("When + are
  assembled").
- 5:5 "over" moves from τοιοῦτον ("this man") to παραδοῦναι ("hand +
  over").
- 5:7 νέον "a new" → "new"; φύραμα = "a + batch".
- 5:8 ζύμῃ (first) "bread, leavened" → `s`; "bread" is `t`; the second ζύμῃ
  = "leavened" (see doubts). μηδὲ ἐν (6, 7) are `s`.
- 5:10 ἄρα "In that case" → joined ἐπεὶ … ἄρα; ἐξελθεῖν "to leave" joins
  ἐκ.
- 5:11 μηδέ "do not even" → "not even"; "do" joins the verb.
- 5:12 ὑμεῖς "Are you" → "you"; κρίνετε = "Are + to judge".
- 5:13 ἐξ "from" → "from among"; ὑμῶν "among you" → ὑμῶν αὐτῶν = "you".

## Notable decisions

- Key terms are linked word by word with no interpretive notes: πορνεία =
  "sexual immorality" (5:1); πόρνος/πόρνοις = "sexually immoral (people)"
  (5:9, 5:10, 5:11); ζύμη = "leaven" (5:6, 5:7) and "leavened" (5:8);
  ζυμοῖ = "works through"; φύραμα = "batch of dough" (5:6) and "batch"
  (5:7); ἄζυμοι = "unleavened" (5:7, placed by BSB before "batch");
  ἀζύμοις = "the unleavened bread" (5:8); πάσχα = "Passover lamb" (5:7);
  ὄλεθρον = "the destruction" (5:5); Σατανᾷ = "to Satan".
- The quotation in 5:13 is linked word by word: ἐξάρατε = "Expel", τόν =
  "the", πονηρόν = "wicked man", ἐξ = "from among", ὑμῶν αὐτῶν = "you".
- 5:2 ἵνα = "and" with a note (a connective rendered by another
  connective, as Luke 20:14). καί (4) is `s` because BSB starts a new
  question. τὸ ἔργον is `s`; τοῦτο = "this". μέσου = "fellowship" and the
  passive ἀρθῇ = "have removed" have notes.
- 5:3 μέν … δέ joined for "Although"; γάρ is `s`. The two articles before
  σώματι and πνεύματι are `s`.
- 5:4 ἐμοῦ ("my") = "I" with a note: BSB recasts "my spirit" as "I … in
  spirit". "When" goes on the genitive absolute participle.
- 5:5 τό = "his" (article as possessive, majority convention); 5:9 τῇ =
  "my" likewise; 5:1 τοῦ = "his".
- 5:10 τοῦ (18) keeps "this" (article rendered "this", referring back to
  "this world"), now with a note.
- 5:11 ἤ (14, 18) are `s` where BSB uses commas.
- 5:13 δέ and ὁ are `s`.

## Doubts for the peer

- 5:8 ζύμῃ (first) is `s` and "bread#0" `t`: "bread" is not a word for
  leaven, and BSB gives leaven once, as "leavened" (from ζύμῃ κακίας).
  Alternatives: link the first ζύμῃ to "bread" with a note (a shifted
  rendering), or join both ζύμῃ to "leavened".
- 5:1 οὐδέ keeps the publisher's "intolerable even" with a note (the
  negative is carried by "intolerable"). The stricter alternative is οὐδέ =
  "even" with "intolerable" `t`.
- 5:2 οὐχί and ἐπενθήσατε are joined because "Shouldn’t" holds both the
  verb's modal and the negative (Matthew 18:33, Mark 12:24). The
  alternative is οὐχί = "Shouldn’t" alone (as James 4:1 "Don’t") with the
  verb = "you + have been stricken with grief".
- 5:4 "am with you" is `t`; it could instead link to συναχθέντων as a verb
  understood again for the second subject, though the wording differs.
- 5:10 πάντως is `s` and "I was … including" `t`; keeping the publisher's
  πάντως = "including" with a note ("not altogether" → "not including") is
  the looser alternative.
- 5:3 ἐγώ is linked to all four "I"s (Acts 22:3). The stricter alternative
  keeps ἐγώ on "I#2" (the subject of κέκρικα) and makes the participle
  clauses' "I"s `t`.
- 5:12 "business" is `t`; joining it to τί ("What business") is the
  alternative.
