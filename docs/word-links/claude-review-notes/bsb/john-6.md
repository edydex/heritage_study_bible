# BSB John 6: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: 71 (6:1-71). No verse was kept from an earlier review (the
draft had no KEEP blocks). Final check: `fromtext` →
`{"versesOk": 71, "problems": 0}`.

844 of the 1046 publisher `g` rows are unchanged. 35 more only gained
unlinked Greek tokens, with the English unchanged (for example ἐὰν μή =
unless 6:44, 6:53, 6:65; εἰ μή = except 6:46; ἀπεκρίθη … καὶ εἶπεν = replied
6:26, 6:29, 6:43; μή τι = nothing 6:12; ὃς ἔχει = with 6:9; εἰς ἥν = where
6:21; εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα = forever 6:51, 6:58). The other 167 rows, in 52 verses,
were corrected, split, joined or moved. About a third of them are article +
participle joins (rule 3).

Parallels checked for 6:1-21: Matthew 14:13-27, Mark 6:35-52, Luke 9:12-17.
John's Greek differs in most places (εὐχαριστήσας "gave thanks", κοφίνους
"baskets", ἐμβάντες εἰς). Where the wording is the same it is linked the same
way: "two hundred denarii" (Mark 6:37), "about five thousand" (Matthew 14:21),
and "But Jesus spoke up: It is I; do not be afraid" (Mark 6:50: ὁ = Jesus,
ἐγώ = I, εἰμι = It is, μή = not, φοβεῖσθε = do + be afraid).

## Publisher rows corrected

"When" on a connective instead of the participle (brief convention):

- 6:5 οὖν "When" → `s`; ἐπάρας τοὺς ὀφθαλμούς = When + looked up (one idiom,
  as Luke 6:20 and 16:23). 6:14 οὖν → `s`; ἰδόντες = When + saw. 6:19 οὖν →
  `s`; ἐληλακότες = When + had rowed. 6:25 καί → `s`; εὑρόντες = When + found.
- 6:23: τοῦ "after the" → the; the genitive absolute εὐχαριστήσαντος = after +
  had given thanks.

A lone article carried a case word or a content word:

- "of the" on the article: 6:1 and 6:22 (πέραν now = "to/on the other side
  of", τῆς = the), 6:4 (τό = the; "of" is `t`, see below), 6:25, 6:51 (τοῦ =
  the; κόσμου = of + world), 6:53 (τοῦ = the; Υἱοῦ = of + Son).
- "to"/"at": 6:12 τοῖς → `s`, μαθηταῖς = to + disciples; 6:33 τῷ = the,
  κόσμῳ = to + world; 6:54 τῇ = the, ἡμέρᾳ = at + day (dative of time; N1904
  has no ἐν here).
- 6:1: τῆς before Τιβεριάδος carried "that is"; the last article carried "the
  Sea of Tiberias". Now τῆς = the, Τιβεριάδος = of Tiberias, θαλάσσης = Sea +
  Sea (understood again), and "that is" is `t`.
- 6:38: τό carried "own". ἐμόν now = My own; both articles `s`.

Negatives and auxiliaries split off verbs: 6:17 (οὔπω = not yet, ἐληλύθει =
had + gone out), 6:20, 6:27 (μή = not, ἐργάζεσθε = Do + work for), 6:35 (οὐ
μή = never; πεινάσῃ = will + hunger; οὐ μή … πώποτε = never, διψήσει = will +
thirst), 6:36, 6:37 (οὐ μή = never, ἐκβάλω = I will + drive), 6:64 (both
clauses). Also 6:30 σύ "will You" → You, ποιεῖς = will + perform; 6:61 Τοῦτο
"Does this" → this, σκανδαλίζει = Does + offend; 6:67 ὑμεῖς "Do you" → you,
θέλετε = Do + want; 6:70 ἐγώ "Have I" → I, ἐξελεξάμην = Have + chosen.

English-only words that a Greek word had claimed (now `t`):

- Names: "Jesus" in 6:14 (ἐποίησεν), 6:59 and 6:65 (εἶπεν/ἔλεγεν). 6:20: ὁ δέ
  = Jesus, as Mark 6:50, and λέγει = spoke up.
- Subjects/objects: "the people" (6:23), "them" (6:13), "it" (6:30 we may see
  it, 6:60 On hearing it), "these" (6:26 these signs), "teaching" (6:61 this
  teaching), "Him" (6:45 learned from Him).
- Cleft frames: 6:26 "it is … that"; 6:32 "it was … who" and "it is … who".
- Others: "and" joining a participle to the main verb (6:17 and started);
  "as" in "as I stated" (6:36); "only" (6:46); "what will happen" (6:62, BSB
  completes the unfinished "if then you see …"); "although" and "was" (6:71,
  BSB makes the apposition "one of the Twelve" a concessive clause); "the"
  with an anarthrous noun (6:13 pieces, 6:59 synagogue, 6:64 beginning, 6:68
  words, as in John 3).
- 6:10: τὸν ἀριθμόν carried "of them". Now `s` ("in number" is not rendered as
  such) and "of them" is `t`. See doubts.

Other wrong anchors:

- 6:7: αὐτοῖς "for" → for + of them; ἕκαστος "each of them" → each; ἵνα = to
  (with λάβῃ = have); οὐκ = not and ἀρκοῦσιν = would + buy enough.
- 6:9: ταῦτα carried "will these make". Now ταῦτα = these and ἐστιν = will +
  make (BSB's "what difference will these make" for "what are these").
- 6:11: διέδωκεν carried "and". The first καί now = and (before the last verb
  of BSB's series); ὁμοίως καί = and (joins the fish to the loaves); ἐκ `s`.
- 6:22: αὐτοῦ carried "with His". Now συνεισῆλθεν … εἰς = had + boarded +
  with, αὐτοῦ = His, τὸ πλοῖον = it, οἱ μαθηταὶ αὐτοῦ = they, and πλοιάριον
  ἄλλο οὐκ … εἰ μὴ ἕν gives "only" (with ἕν = one).
- 6:27: ἀλλά carried "but for". The verb ἐργάζεσθε is understood again, so it
  takes both "for"s.
- 6:38: ἀλλά carried "but to do"; ἵνα and ποιῶ are understood again (to + to,
  do + do).
- 6:39, 6:40, 6:44, 6:54: ἀναστήσω carried the pronoun; αὐτό/αὐτόν now = them
  /him (6:39 notes that the singular αὐτό is rendered "them").
- 6:45: ἔσονται carried "all"; πάντες = all. παρά carried "from Him"; παρά =
  from (with a note), "Him" `t`.
- 6:64: οἱ carried "of them". Now τίνες = which of them and εἰσὶν οἱ …
  πιστεύοντες = did + believe; ἐστιν ὁ παραδώσων = would betray.
- 6:65: Διά carried "is why"; Διὰ τοῦτο is now one idiom = This is why.
- 6:68: αἰωνίου carried "of"; ζωῆς = of + life.
- 6:71: Ἰούδαν carried "about"; it now goes with ἔλεγεν ("was speaking about",
  λέγω + accusative = "speak of"). οὗτος = Judas (note), γάρ = For.
- 6:2, 6:5, 6:18, 6:60: English "a" moved from the adjective to the noun (A +
  crowd, A + wind, a + teaching), as the brief and the recent Luke reviews do.

Joined units: article + participle in 6:2, 6:11, 6:13, 6:14, 6:22, 6:27 (×2),
6:33, 6:35 (×2), 6:37, 6:38, 6:39, 6:40, 6:41, 6:44, 6:45, 6:46, 6:47, 6:50,
6:51 (×2), 6:54, 6:56, 6:57, 6:58 (×2). Other units: 6:19 σταδίους εἴκοσι
πέντε ἢ τριάκοντα = three or four miles (converted measure, as John 2:6);
6:19 ἐγγὺς … γινόμενον = approaching; 6:21 ἐγένετο … ἐπί = reached; 6:15
αὐτὸς μόνος = by Himself; 6:17 ἐμβάντες εἰς = got into; 6:58 οὐ καθώς =
Unlike; 6:61 εἰδὼς … ἐν ἑαυτῷ = Aware; 6:63 ἐστιν τὸ ζωοποιοῦν = gives life
and οὐκ … οὐδέν = nothing; 6:65 ᾖ δεδομένον = has granted it; 6:66 ἀπῆλθον
εἰς τὰ ὀπίσω = turned back; 6:51 καὶ … δέ = And.

## Readings

Only 6:2, 6:25, 6:29, 6:52 and 6:66 have EVIDENCE lines showing different
publisher Greek.

- 6:2: another verb of seeing (probably ἐθεώρουν for ἑώρων). Same meaning;
  linked with a note.
- 6:25: Rabbi, spelled differently (probably Ῥαββί). Linked with the usual
  note.
- 6:29: one extra publisher word (probably ὁ before Ἰησοῦς). Nothing in the
  English depends on it, so no claim.
- 6:52: one extra publisher word, and "His" was unlinked (probably αὐτοῦ after
  σάρκα). Same meaning as the N1904 article used as a possessive, so τήν = His
  with a reading note.
- 6:66: one extra publisher word, and "of" was unlinked (probably ἐκ before
  τῶν μαθητῶν). Same meaning as the N1904 partitive genitive, so μαθητῶν = of +
  disciples with a reading note.
- 6:58: EVIDENCE says N1904 word for word, so BSB's "your fathers … ate the
  manna" claims no reading: οἱ = your (article as possessive, with a note) and
  "the manna" is `t`.

## Notable decisions

- 6:1: θαλάσσης = Sea + Sea. The second name τῆς Τιβεριάδος is an apposition
  with the noun understood again ("the [Sea] of Tiberias").
- 6:4: τὸ πάσχα and ἡ ἑορτὴ τῶν Ἰουδαίων are appositives; BSB writes "the
  Jewish Feast of the Passover". "of" is `t`, τῶν `s`, Ἰουδαίων = Jewish (as
  John 2:6, 2:13).
- 6:33: ὁ καταβαίνων = He who comes down, with a note that the masculine
  could also be read "that which comes down" (the bread). No claim about which
  John means.
- 6:37: neuter Πᾶν = Everyone (note); the relative ὅ is `s`. 6:39: πᾶν ὅ =
  those, μή = none, ἐξ αὐτοῦ = of (resumptive).
- 6:12: "everyone was full" stays on ἐνεπλήσθησαν (BSB's word for the
  verb's "they").
- 6:42: νῦν = then (note). 6:66: Ἐκ τούτου = From that time on, with a note
  that it can also mean "for this reason".
- 6:51: the article ὁ before the second ἄρτος = this (publisher row kept).
- 6:53: αὐτοῦ before τὸ αἷμα is `s`; BSB's "of the Son of Man" covers flesh
  and blood.
- 6:67: the question particle Μή (expecting "no") is `s`; "Do" goes with
  θέλετε.

## Doubts for the peer

- 6:10: τὸν ἀριθμόν `s` + "of them" `t`. "of them" does not mean "number",
  but it is BSB's way of counting the men. The other option is to keep the
  publisher link ἀριθμόν = of them.
- 6:7: αὐτοῖς = for + of them and ἕκαστος = each. The other option is the
  publisher's αὐτοῖς = for, ἕκαστος = each of them.
- 6:22: εἰς is joined to συνεισῆλθεν (had + boarded + with) and τὸ πλοῖον =
  it. ἄλλο is inside the "only" group with οὐκ … εἰ μή. Either could be `s`.
- 6:36: ὅτι `s` + "as" `t`. "as" does not mean "that", so this is not a split
  pair, but the peer may prefer another split.
- 6:11: ὁμοίως καί = "and" (BSB joins "the loaves and the fish"). The other
  option is ὁμοίως καί `s` and "and" `t`.
- 6:65: Διὰ τοῦτο = "This is why" as one idiom, including the supplied "is".
  The other option is Διά = why, τοῦτο = This, "is" `t`.
- 6:67: Μή `s`. It could instead carry "Do", if the peer reads "Do …?" as
  BSB's rendering of the question particle.
