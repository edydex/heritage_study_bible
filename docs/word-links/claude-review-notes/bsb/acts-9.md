# BSB Acts 9: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: all 43 (9:1-43). No KEEP blocks in this chapter. Final
check: `fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 43, "problems": 0}`.

135 of the 653 publisher `g` rows changed, in 39 verses (9:12, 9:16, 9:19
and 9:23 keep every publisher row). Some only gained a token, with the
English unchanged: 9:6 ὅ τι = what, 9:7 οἱ συνοδεύοντες = traveling with,
9:15 τοῦ βαστάσαι = to carry, 9:21 Οὐχ … ἐστιν = Isn’t, 9:28
εἰσπορευόμενος καὶ ἐκπορευόμενος = moving about freely, 9:31 καθ’ ὅλης =
throughout, 9:32 τοὺς κατοικοῦντας = in, 9:38 ἐν αὐτῇ = there and πρὸς
αὐτόν = him, 9:40 θεὶς τὰ γόνατα = He knelt down. The rest were corrected,
split or moved.

## Publisher rows corrected

English words with no Greek counterpart that had been folded into a Greek
word (now `t`):

- Names supplied for a verb ending or participle: 9:5 "Saul" (Saul
  asked), 9:20 "Saul" (Saul promptly began), 9:26 "Saul" (Saul arrived),
  9:27 "Saul" twice (Saul had seen, Saul had spoken), 9:28 "Saul" (Saul
  stayed), 9:34 "Aeneas" (Aeneas got up), 9:41 "Peter" (Peter took), 9:43
  "Peter" (Peter stayed).
- Participle "and": 9:2 "and requested" (joins προσελθών of 9:1), 9:4 "and
  heard", 9:18 "and was baptized", 9:25 "and lowered", 9:37 "and died" and
  "and placed", 9:38 "and sent" (the "and" had been missing), 9:39 "and went
  with", 9:40 "and prayed", 9:41 "and helped" and "and presented". Each
  only joins a participle to the main verb.
- Other English-only words: 9:2 "them" (bring them; the Greek has only
  δεδεμένους), 9:9 "anything" (drink anything), 9:13 "and" (and all the
  harm; ὅσα has no connective), 9:14 "now" (And now), 9:17 "here" (were
  coming here), 9:18 "something" (something like scales), 9:21 "him" (who
  heard him), 9:28 "and" (and speaking boldly), 9:30 "of this", 9:31 "a
  time of" (peace), 9:33 "and" (and bedridden), 9:39 "other" (other
  clothing) and "still" (while she was still), 9:40 "of the room", 9:41 "to
  them", 9:42 "This", 9:43 "named" (no ὀνόματι here), 9:15 "their"
  (their kings), 9:25 "a window in" (the Greek says through the wall).
- Supplied copula: 9:10 "am" (Here I am; Ἰδοὺ ἐγώ has no verb).

Words on the wrong Greek word:

- 9:1 "was" moved from ἔτι to the participle ἐμπνέων (ἔτι = still, ἐμπνέων
  = was + breathing out).
- 9:3 "As" moved from ἐγένετο to τε, as BSB subordinates the first of the
  τε-joined clauses (convention for καί → When); ἐγένετο is the narrative
  formula (`s`), and τῷ πορεύεσθαι is joined as "his journey".
- 9:5 δέ had "He replied": ὁ = He, and the verbless ὁ δέ clause takes
  "replied" from εἶπεν (asked + replied).
- 9:6 εἴσελθε "go into" split (εἰς = into); λαληθήσεται "you will be told"
  split (σοι = you).
- 9:10 εἶπεν "he answered" split (ὁ = he). Same for 9:29 ἐπεχείρουν (οἱ =
  they) and 9:40 ἤνοιξεν (ἡ = She).
- 9:11 ἰδού had "he is": now `s`, and προσεύχεται = he is praying. πρός had
  "told": πρὸς αὐτόν = him (object of told, as in reviewed Luke), and
  "told" is `t` because the clause has no verb.
- 9:14 ὧδε "he is here" / ἔχει "with": now ὧδε = here, ἔχει = he is + with.
- 9:17 καί before ἐπιθείς carries "when" (BSB: "and when he arrived, he
  placed"); the first καί keeps "and". "with the Holy" moved: Πνεύματος =
  with the + Spirit, Ἁγίου = Holy (as Acts 6:5).
- 9:20 "Saul promptly began to proclaim" on ἐκήρυσσεν split: εὐθέως =
  promptly, ἐκήρυσσεν = began to proclaim, Saul `t`. ὅτι had "declaring":
  now `s`, with "declaring" `t` (it does not mean "that").
- 9:21 τοὺς "on those" and ὁ "the man who": article and participle joined
  (the man who wreaked havoc + on; those who call on).
- 9:25 λαβόντες "took him": the one αὐτόν now = him#0 + him#1 (Acts 6:12);
  καθῆκαν and χαλάσαντες joined to "lowered".
- 9:26, 9:30, 9:38: "When"/"Since" moved from δέ to the participle or
  genitive absolute. 9:32 "As" moved from Ἐγένετο to διερχόμενον.
- 9:27 ὁδῷ "road to Damascus" and Δαμασκῷ "that city": Δαμασκῷ now =
  Damascus + that city (BSB names the city with the road and refers back to
  it), "to" `t`. ἐπιλαβόμενος `s`; αὐτόν = him (brought him); καὶ ὅτι
  joined = who.
- 9:37 αὐτήν had "and": now αὐτήν = she, ἀσθενήσασαν = became sick.
- 9:39 ἀνήγαγον "they took him": the relative ὅν = him, ἀνήγαγον = they
  took. αὐτῷ = him#1 + him#2 (stood around him … showing him).
- 9:8 χειραγωγοῦντες "they led him by the hand": now εἰσήγαγον = they led,
  χειραγωγοῦντες = by the hand, αὐτόν = him.
- 9:15 ἐνώπιον "before#1 the people" on υἱῶν: the one ἐνώπιον = before#0 +
  before#1 (Acts 6:11, 6:13); τε before καί `s`.
- 9:24 τε "and" moved to the καί of ἡμέρας τε καὶ νυκτός.
- 9:34 "put away your mat": σεαυτῷ = your.
- 9:36 "which is translated as": λέγεται = is, διερμηνευομένη = translated
  as (John 1:41 precedent).
- 9:43 "Peter stayed for": μεῖναι = stayed, "for" with ἡμέρας (also 9:9 For
  + days).

Lone articles carrying a preposition: 9:1 τοῦ "of the", 9:2 τῆς "to the",
9:17 τὴν "to the", 9:25 τοῦ "in the", 9:28 τοῦ "of the", 9:31 τῷ "in the"
and τοῦ "of the" twice, 9:13 τοῖς "to". The articles keep "the" where
English has it; the prepositions go on the nouns.

Negatives split off verbs: 9:7 μηδένα (not + anyone / did + see), 9:8
οὐδέν (not + a thing / could + see), 9:9 οὐκ (not / did + eat).

## Readings

- 9:12 "In a vision": BSB follows a reading with extra words (probably ἐν
  ὁράματι); `t` with the reading reason. "his" (his hands) is `t`; the
  third extra word is probably τάς, which would give the possessive, but
  the review file only says BSB's text has extra words here.
- 9:15 and 9:22: one extra word in the publisher's Greek (probably τῶν
  before ἐθνῶν and τούς before Ἰουδαίους). No reading is claimed: "the
  Gentiles" stays with ἐθνῶν (as "the people" with υἱῶν in the same verse),
  and in 9:22 "the" links to N1904's own τοὺς κατοικοῦντας.
- 9:27: one extra word (probably τοῦ before Ἰησοῦ); no effect on the links.
- 9:37 "her body": BSB follows a reading with an extra word (probably
  αὐτήν with λούσαντες/ἔθηκαν); `t` with the reading reason.
- Spelling only: 9:30 Καισάριαν (Caesarea), 9:31 Σαμαρίας (Samaria), 9:36
  and 9:40 Ταβειθά (Tabitha), linked with "Same name, spelled differently".
- 9:38 is flagged as differing with 0/0 unmatched words, so no reading is
  claimed.

## Notable decisions

- Scene openers: 9:1 δέ = Meanwhile (returns to Saul after 8:3), 9:19b δέ =
  And, 9:43 δέ = And; 9:10 and 9:36 open new scenes with no English word
  (δέ `s`). 9:3, 9:32, 9:37, 9:43 ἐγένετο is the narrative formula (`s`);
  9:19 Ἐγένετο with the days keeps "he spent" (Saul is its subject).
- Vocatives: Σαοὺλ Σαούλ, Κύριε, Ἀνανία, Σαοὺλ ἀδελφέ (Brother Saul),
  Αἰνέα, Ταβειθά. BSB has no "O"/"You" before any of them.
- Consistency with the later tellings (drafts only, not yet reviewed): the
  identical Σαοὺλ Σαούλ, τί με διώκεις (9:4, 22:7, 26:14) and Τίς εἶ, Κύριε
  (9:5, 22:8, 26:15) have the same links word by word (are You on εἶ);
  δεδεμένους = as prisoners in both 9:2 and 9:21.
- 9:11 ἐπί = to and ἐν = on kept as publisher rows with notes: BSB makes
  the house the destination and places it on the street. Ταρσέα = a man
  from Tarsus (as Ἀντιοχέα = from Antioch, Acts 6:5). τὴν καλουμένην `s`
  (as Luke 19:29).
- 9:35 οἵτινες = and (relative turned into a connective; the reverse of
  Acts 6:6 καί = who), kept with a note.
- 9:36 ἐποίει = always and ὧν `s` kept (BSB: always occupied with);
  πλήρης = occupied with.
- 9:24 "city gates" kept on πύλας (compare "temple courts" for ἱερόν in
  Acts 3:2); 9:19 "some food" kept on τροφήν.

## Doubts for the peer

- 9:5 εἶπεν = asked + replied: treats the verbless ὁ δέ as the verb
  understood again. The alternative is "replied" `t`.
- 9:11 "told" `t` with πρὸς αὐτόν = him: the clause has no verb (the εἶπεν
  is in 9:10, so no cross-verse link). The publisher had πρός = told.
- 9:39 αὐτῷ = him#1 + him#2: the dative with παρέστησαν is taken as also
  the understood indirect object of ἐπιδεικνύμεναι. The alternative is
  "him#2" `t` (the middle may mean the widows showed garments they wore).
- 9:27 Δαμασκῷ = Damascus + that city (BSB moved the name to the road).
  The alternative is "to Damascus" `t` and Δαμασκῷ = that city only.
- 9:21 "hasn’t he come" on ἐληλύθει: the negative comes from the one Οὐχ,
  already joined with ἐστιν as Isn’t; kept on the verb with a note.
- 9:22 τοὺς κατοικοῦντας = the + living (N1904 has the article only with
  the participle); the alternative is Ἰουδαίους = the Jews and τοὺς `s`.
- 9:8 εἰσήγαγον = they led, χειραγωγοῦντες = by the hand: splits BSB's
  "led him by the hand into" by meaning; the alternative joins both verbs.
