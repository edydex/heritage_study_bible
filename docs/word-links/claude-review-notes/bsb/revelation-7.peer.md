# BSB Revelation 7: independent peer review (Claude)

Checked: 17 of 17 verses (7:1-17). This chapter has no KEEP blocks.
Final check: `fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 17, "problems": 0}`.

I started from the author's final file and exported every verse with
`totext --all`. I checked every `g`, `s` and `t` line against the Greek, the
BSB English and the publisher record. I compared the choices with the
registered Revelation 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 files and with the shared Revelation
choices. No lone article carries "of", "to" or "in", and none carries a
copula. Every "a", and every "the" with no Greek article, goes with its
noun. No person word sits on a participle. Every supplied copula, object,
possessive and noun is `t`. I found no wrong link and no over-claim. I made
one consistency change and improved one note. All other verses are
identical to the author's file.

## Changes

- 7:15 διά = "For + reason", τοῦτο = "this" → διὰ τοῦτο joined = "For this
  reason". The split was not wrong, but reviewed files join this idiom for
  this English phrase in 10 of 11 rows (Matthew 13:52, 24:44, Romans 1:26,
  Ephesians 1:15, Colossians 1:9 and others). Only John 12:39 splits it.
  The joined row is also the publisher's English with the TODO token
  added. Note: "διὰ τοῦτο ("because of this") is rendered For this reason."
- 7:9 note on οὐδεὶς ἐδύνατο = "too" (link unchanged). The old note ("BSB
  turns οὐδεὶς ἐδύνατο ("no one could") count it into …") was hard to read.
  It now reads: "BSB turns "which no one could count" into too large to
  count, so too carries οὐδεὶς ἐδύνατο ("no one could")."

## Answers to the author's doubts and the open points

- 7:9 οὐδεὶς ἐδύνατο = "too": **kept**. πολύς means "great, large", not
  "too large". Putting "too" on it, as the publisher row did, would claim
  an idea of excess that πολύς does not carry. English "too large to count"
  means "so large that no one could count it". The word that carries the
  impossibility is "too". Its "to" is the infinitive marker of ἀριθμῆσαι,
  so it cannot join "too". `s` + `t` would hide the Greek "no one could"
  from the reader who taps "too". So "too" on οὐδεὶς ἐδύνατο is the honest
  link. ὅν and αὐτόν `s` are right, because BSB's infinitive has no object.
  "They" `t` (participle) and "holding" `t` (no Greek verb with φοίνικες)
  are right. "were wearing" on περιβεβλημένους matches Mark 14:51.
- 7:3 αὐτῶν = "of#0": **kept**. This is consistent with 1:20, not against
  it. In 1:20 "of#1" stands before an accusative (λυχνίας), so no Greek
  word stands behind it, and it is `t`. Here αὐτῶν is a genitive ("their"),
  and BSB expresses exactly its possessive relation with "of", naming the
  servants in place of "them". The shared rule ("of" goes on the genitive
  word) puts it on αὐτῶν. `s` + `t` would be a split pair. ἐπί `s` and
  λέγων `s` (the colon) are right.
- 7:5 "were sealed" on ἐσφραγισμένοι: **kept**. The registered files link
  the auxiliary with a participle that BSB turns into a finite verb:
  Revelation 5:1 κατεσφραγισμένον = "was sealed" (the same verb), 5:12
  λέγοντες = "were saying", 4:1 λέγων = "was saying". Other books do the
  same (Jude 1:4, Colossians 1:26, Acts 22:3). Here "were" is the passive
  auxiliary of that finite rendering. It is not the copula of a verbless
  clause, as in 9:16 ("the number … was", `t`). 7:4 and 7:8 do not repeat
  "sealed", so those participles are `s`.
- 7:4-8 tribe list: all twelve lines follow one pattern. ἐκ = "From" or
  "from", φυλῆς = "the tribe", and the name = "of <Name>". δώδεκα
  χιλιάδες = "12,000" is joined. 7:4 is "144,000" (joined), with πάσης =
  "all", φυλῆς = "the#1 tribes" and υἱῶν `s`. I checked each line against
  the BSB English word by word. 7:8's "and" is `t`, because the Greek has
  no καί there.
- "the" on φυλῆς: φυλῆς has no Greek article, so this "the" is an English
  determiner, not a rendering of an article. Like "a", it goes with its
  noun, as with υἱόν "the Son" (1:13) and φωνήν "the voices" (5:11). It is
  not a copula or a preposition, so it does not need `t`. 5:5 has a real
  τῆς before φυλῆς, and that τῆς carries "the".
- 7:16 οὐδὲ … ἔτι = "and never": **kept**. BSB writes "never", not "not",
  because of ἔτι in both clauses ("Never again … and never …"). Without
  ἔτι it would be "nor will they thirst". So "never" carries the negative
  and the "any longer" of ἔτι together. `s` on the second ἔτι would hide
  that. οὐδὲ μή = "nor#0" and πέσῃ = "will#2 + beat down" are right. The
  publisher's "will the" on ὁ was rightly removed.
- 7:2 "of the + God" / ζῶντος = "living": **kept**. The registered
  Revelation 5:11 also puts "of" on the noun (ἀγγέλων = "of + angels",
  πολλῶν = "many"), as the shared rule says. Here the "the" has no Greek
  article, so it belongs with the noun God. On the participle ζῶντος,
  "the living" would read like an articular participle. 1:15 ("of many" on
  πολλῶν) is an older row that was kept from the publisher.
- 7:2 "power" `t`: **kept**. ἐδόθη + infinitive has no noun. Revelation
  2:27 "authority" and 2:7 "the right" are `t` in the same way.
- 7:11 "facedown" on ἐπὶ τὰ πρόσωπα αὐτῶν: **kept**, as in Matthew 17:6
  and 26:39. κύκλῳ = "around#0 + around#1" is right: the one κύκλῳ governs
  the throne and the elders, and BSB repeats it. The publisher's "and
  around" on καί claimed too much.
- 7:13 πόθεν = "where + from": **kept**. Reviewed files always do this
  (John 2:9, 3:8, 8:14, Luke 13:25, Matthew 13:27). "he" `t` on the
  participle λέγων is right.
- 7:14 Κύριέ μου joined = "Sir": **kept**. "Sir" is the polite address
  that "my lord" stands for, so it renders both words as one idiom
  (Κύριε alone = "Sir" in John 4:11 and Matthew 13:27). The publisher
  row put "Sir" on μου alone, which was wrong.
- 7:17 αὐτούς = "their#0": **kept**. "be their shepherd" renders "shepherd
  them". The pronoun and its referent are the same; only its grammatical
  role changes, so this is a shifted rendering, not a word of a different
  meaning. Unlike 7:15 "His#1" (`t`), it has a Greek owner. ζωῆς =
  "living" and ὑδάτων = "of#1 + water" are right.
- Other points checked: τῆς γῆς = "its" (7:1). φωνῇ = "in a + voice",
  μεγάλῃ = "loud" (7:2, 7:10). Μή = "not", ἀδικήσητε = "Do + harm" (7:3).
  εἶδον = "I looked", ἰδού = "saw", ταῦτα = "this" (7:9). τῷ καθημένῳ =
  "who sits", with τῷ = "the" and Ἀρνίῳ = "to + Lamb" (7:10, as 5:13).
  "forever" / "and ever" and "be" `t` (7:12). ἐλεύκαναν = "made + white",
  with αὐτάς = "them" (7:14). σκηνώσει = "will spread + tabernacle", with
  "His#1" `t` (7:15). ἀνά = "in", μέσον = "the center", θρόνου = "of +
  throne" (7:17, as 5:6). All match the registered Revelation files.

## Still uncertain

- 7:15 διὰ τοῦτο: the author's split is also defensible. I joined it only
  for consistency with the registered rows.
- 7:3 αὐτῶν = "of#0" is the least obvious link in the chapter. It is
  correct because "of" carries the genitive relation and the referent is
  named by "the servants". A later reviewer who prefers `s` + `t` would be
  creating a split pair, not fixing an error.
