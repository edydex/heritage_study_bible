## Notable decisions

- 8:3 αὐτοῦ is linked to "God#1" with a note; BSB names the referent.
- 8:4 "nothing at all" stays together on οὐδέν (emphatic negative, as
  Galatians 5:2).
- 8:6 αὐτόν and αὐτοῦ are linked to "whom#1"/"whom#3" with a grammatical
  note only; ἡμῖν = "for#0 us".
- 8:7 the article ἡ before γνῶσις = "this" (it points back to the knowledge
  of 8:4-6); τοῦ εἰδώλου: τοῦ `s`, εἰδώλου = "to#0 idols" with a note on the
  singular.
- 8:11 γάρ = "So" (a connective rendered with a different connective).
- 8:12 τούς = possessive "your" (majority convention).

## Doubts for the peer

- 8:5: the two πολλοί are joined to one "many", which covers both nouns.
  The other option is `s` for the second πολλοί.
- 8:6: τὰ πάντα is joined as "all things" (Romans 11:36). If "word by word"
  is read strictly, τά could be `s`, as in Colossians 1:16-20.
- 8:7 ἐν πᾶσιν: ἐν = "has", πᾶσιν = "everyone", with a note. Hebrews 3:12
  links "has" to ἔσται ἐν. Here there is no verb.
- 8:10: αὐτοῦ = "with" ("someone with … conscience"). ἀσθενοῦς describes the
  person, but BSB moves "weak" onto the conscience (noted). The verb's
  Greek subject is the conscience, but "will he" stays on the verb.
  Possible alternative: αὐτοῦ `s` and "with" `t`.
- 8:13: ἀδελφόν μου is joined to "him"; τόν is `s`.
