## Notable decisions

- Theological terms are linked word by word to BSB's English with no
  interpretive notes: εὐαγγέλιον = "gospel" (1:1, 1:9, 1:16; 1:2 relative ὅ =
  "the gospel"; 1:17 αὐτῷ = "the gospel"), χάρις = "grace" (1:5, 1:7), πίστις
  = "faith" (1:5, 1:8, 1:12, 1:17), δικαιοσύνη = "righteousness" (1:17), ὀργή
  = "wrath" (1:18), δικαίωμα = "righteous decree" (1:32).
- Connectives rendered with a different connective stay linked: 1:16 γάρ =
  "because", 1:19 διότι = "For" and γάρ = "because", 1:13 καί = "but", 1:16
  καί = "then", 1:28 καί = "Furthermore", 1:24 Διό = "Therefore". Untranslated
  γάρ (1:9, 1:16 first, 1:18, 1:26), δέ (1:12, 1:13, 1:17 in the quotation)
  and μέν (1:8) are `s`.
- 1:14 τε … καί twice = "both … and" (linked); 1:12, 1:16, 1:20, 1:26, 1:27 τε
  `s`.
- Pronouns or nouns BSB moves into a new role stay linked: 1:5 οὗ = "Him",
  1:12 ὑμῶν … ἐμοῦ = "you … I" (ἐν ὑμῖν `s`), 1:15 κατ’ ἐμέ = "I", 1:20 αὐτοῦ
  = "God's" and αὐτούς = "men", 1:25 and 1:32 οἵτινες = "They"/"they", 1:28
  Θεός = "He".
- 1:6 Ἰησοῦ = "to belong to Jesus" kept (genitive of belonging).
- 1:16 and 1:17 "it is" / "it is written" kept on the finite verb.
- 1:26 τήν = "ones" kept: the article stands for χρῆσιν.

## Doubts for the peer

- 1:17 εἰς πίστιν = "from start to finish": the phrase really renders the
  whole "from faith to faith" span; joining all four tokens to "by faith from
  start to finish" is the alternative.
- 1:5 εἰς = "to" (before "the obedience") with "to call" `t`, versus the
  publisher's εἰς = "to call".
- 1:26 "Even" `t` with τε and γάρ `s`; linking "Even" to γάρ or τε is the
  alternative.
- 1:7 τοῖς οὖσιν = "who are" although BSB attaches it to "loved"; `s` for τοῖς
  οὖσιν with "who are" `t` is the alternative.
- 1:6 οἷς = "those who" (publisher, minus "are"): "who" could be `t`, since
  BSB's relative clause belongs to κλητοί.
- 1:12 ὑμῶν = "you" with ἐν ὑμῖν `s`, versus ὑμῖν = "you" with ὑμῶν `s`.
- 1:13 "have" `t` in "I have had" (added perfect) with "I" and "had" linked to
  σχῶ; the whole of "I have had" `t` is the alternative.
- 1:15 οὕτως = "That is why" with "so" `t`; "so" could instead render οὕτως.
- 1:23 ὁμοιώματι εἰκόνος joined = "images" rather than ὁμοιώματι `s`.
- 1:30 "new" `t` in "invent new forms of evil".
