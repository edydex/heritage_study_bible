## Notable decisions

- 18:1: τὸ δεῖν joined = need (articular infinitive); αὐτούς = their kept, with a note (subject of προσεύχεσθαι).
- 18:3: the καί before ἤρχετο is `s`; ἤρχετο = who kept appealing (relative recast, as Mark 10:34 "who will mock"); λέγουσα `s` (introduces the quotation).
- 18:9: ὅτι εἰσίν `s`; δίκαιοι = righteousness, with a note on the recast "trusted in themselves that they were righteous".
- 18:18: ποιήσας = must I do and κληρονομήσω = to inherit kept (publisher), with notes on BSB's recast of participle and main verb.
- 18:25: "easier for" + "a camel" kept (publisher, as Matthew 19:24); Mark 10:25 splits "easier" + "for a camel". Both are accurate.
- 18:28: ἀφέντες = have left, ἠκολουθήσαμεν = to follow (Matthew 19:27 pattern).
- 18:29-30: as Mark 10:29-30: ἐστιν `s` ("no one who"); the second ὅς `s`; οὐχὶ μή = fail to; λάβῃ = will + receive.
- 18:34: αὐτοί = the disciples kept (pronoun named); τοῦτο `s` (BSB "The meaning").
- 18:40: "directed that" kept on ἐκέλευσεν (only "and" removed).

## Doubts for the peer

- 18:5: ἐρχομένη = with her + requests and εἰς τέλος = perpetual; the alternative is `s` for ἐρχομένη with `t requests`. ἵνα = Then and μή = stop are renderings of purpose and "not keep on", not literal.
- 18:6: τί … λέγει = the words + of; the alternative is τί = the words with λέγει `s` and `t of`.
- 18:7: ἐπ’ αὐτοῖς = their with `t help`; "for" moved from "justice" to ἐκλεκτῶν (the genitive). The publisher row "justice for" was defensible.
- 18:14: εἰς τὸν οἶκον αὐτοῦ = home (four tokens); the alternative is οἶκον = home with the rest `s`.
- 18:31-33: "to" / "about" / "on" are on the noun, not the article (brief rule). Mark 10:33 and Matthew 20:19 still put "to the" on τοῖς.
- 18:12 and 18:30: the reading is placed on the verb because the publisher left exactly those tokens unlinked; if the differing word is elsewhere, the note moves.
- 18:36: εἴη τοῦτο joined = was happening; the publisher's split ("was" / "happening") is the alternative.
