# BSB 1 Corinthians 3: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: all 23 (3:1-23). No KEEP blocks in this chapter. Final
check: `fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 23, "problems": 0}`.

65 of the 296 publisher `g` rows changed, in 21 of the 23 verses (3:9 and
3:21 keep every publisher row; 3:21 only gained `s` for γάρ). 12 of the 65
only joined a token with the English unchanged (listed under "Joined
units"). Unchanged rows were checked too.

Readings: EVIDENCE shows the publisher's Greek differs from N1904 only in
3:12, where BSB's Greek has a different form of the words for gold and
silver (probably χρυσόν, ἄργυρον for N1904 χρυσίον, ἀργύριον). Same
meaning, so both are linked with a note. No other reading is claimed.

Conventions followed: Romans 6:16, James 4:4 for οὐκ οἴδατε (οὐκ = "not",
οἴδατε = "Do you + know"); Galatians 3:29, 5:24 for a genitive of belonging
("to Christ" on the genitive, a supplied "belong" `t`); Galatians 6:3 for
εἶναι under δοκεῖ = "he is"; Romans 8:9 for εἰμί + genitive carrying the
English verb ("follow" here, as "belong" there); Hebrews 5:12 for γάλα /
"milk" and for "of" sitting on the head noun ("of + word"); Acts 23:33,
Luke 10:42, Galatians 4:26 for a relative rendered by "and" + its
antecedent; Acts 7:6, John 16:30 for one ὅτι linked to a repeated "that";
1 John 5:10 for an anaphoric article = "this"; Ephesians 5:6 for jussive
"Let" on the imperative; README rule 3 for article + participle units.

## Publisher rows corrected

- 3:13 τό "man’s" → `s`; ἑκάστου "of each" → "each man’s"; ἔργον "work" →

- 3:19 τούτου "of this" → "this"; "of" joins κόσμου ("of + world").
- 3:20 τῶν "of the" → "the#1"; "of" joins σοφῶν ("of + wise").

  "other than" → "than".

- 3:3 "there is" off ὅπου, which keeps "since" (verbless clause).

## Notable decisions

- The key terms are linked word by word with no interpretive notes:
  σάρκινος (3:1) and σαρκικός (3:3, twice) = "worldly"; πνευματικός =
  "spiritual"; νήπιος = "infants"; θεμέλιος = "foundation" (3:10, 3:11,
  3:12); ναός = "temple" (3:16, 3:17); σοφία = "wisdom", μωρία =
  "foolishness" (3:19); σοφός = "wise"/"expert"; μωρός = "a fool".
- Names are linked despite spelling: Ἀπολλῶ/Ἀπολλῶς = "Apollos", Παύλου/
  Παῦλος = "Paul", Κηφᾶς = "Cephas".
- The Job (3:19) and Psalm (3:20) quotations are linked word by word.
  3:19 γάρ = "As" (a connective BSB renders with another connective).
- 3:1 Κἀγώ = "I" with a note (as 1 Thessalonians 3:5, Acts 22:19).
- 3:4 εἰμι = "follow#0" with a note (εἰμί + genitive), as Romans 8:9
  ἔστιν = "does + belong". μέν is `s`.
- 3:12 the article τόν = "this" (anaphoric, as 1 John 5:10).
- 3:13 ἑκάστου (first) kept as "his" with a note; ὅτι, ἐστιν (inside "what
  sort it is") and αὐτό are `s`.
- 3:15 οὕτως is `s` and "only" `t` (see doubts); δέ (7) is `s` as BSB
  starts a new sentence.
- 3:18 εἶναι keeps "he#0 is" (Galatians 6:3), the infinitive's subject
  being the thinker; ἐν ὑμῖν = "of + you".
- 3:21 μηδείς = "stop" with a note (a third-person prohibition recast as a
  command); γάρ `s`.
- 3:8 τόν (second) = "his", ἴδιον = "own" kept (article as possessive,
  majority convention); the first τὸν ἴδιον is `s`, as BSB gives "his own"
  only with labor.

## Doubts for the peer

- 3:14 τὸ ἔργον … ὅ joined to "what" ("the work that"). The stricter
  alternative is ὅ = "what" with τὸ ἔργον `s`.
- 3:4 "follow#1" is `t` under the supplied-copula rule (the second clause
  has no verb, as Galatians 3:29 "belong"); linking it to εἰμι as a verb
  understood again is the alternative. "mere" is `t`; it could be read as
  part of BSB's rendering of ἄνθρωποι.
- 3:15 οὕτως ("so, in this way") `s` + "only" `t`; οὕτως = "only" (the
  restrictive "yet so as") is the looser alternative.
- 3:2 "for solid food" `t`; it could instead link to βρῶμα as understood
  again, though its role differs (complement of "ready", not object of
  "gave"). ἔτι νῦν joined to "still"; νῦν `s` is the alternative.
- 3:13 ἑκάστου = "his" (BSB refers back to "anyone" in 3:12); `s` + `t` is
  the stricter alternative.
- 3:3 ὅπου = "since" (causal use of "where"); 3:2 Ἀλλ’ = "In fact".
