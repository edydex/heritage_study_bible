# BSB Luke 24: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: all 53 (24:1-53). No KEEP blocks in this chapter. Final
check: `fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 53, "problems": 0}`.

130 of the 671 publisher `g` rows changed, in 46 verses. About 40 of them
only gained unlinked Greek tokens or were merged into one group, with the
English unchanged (for example 24:4/15/30/51 ἐν τῷ + infinitive = While/as;
24:5 τὸν ζῶντα = the living; 24:13 ᾗ ὄνομα = called; 24:14 and 24:44
article + participle; 24:17 ἀντιβάλλετε πρὸς ἀλλήλους = are you discussing so

= since; 24:22 ἀλλὰ καί = Furthermore; 24:24 τῶν σὺν ἡμῖν = of our

24:31 ἄφαντος ἐγένετο = disappeared; 24:36 ἐν μέσῳ = among; 24:37 ἔμφοβοι

πρός = as far as; 24:51 διέστη ἀπ’ = left; 24:53 διὰ παντός = continually).
The rest were corrected, split or moved.

Parallels followed: Mark 16:1-8 and Matthew 28:1-8. 24:1 uses the same
treatment of τῇ μιᾷ τῶν σαββάτων as Mark 16:2 (μιᾷ = first, `t day`), but
with the case words on the nouns (On + first, of + week) instead of on the
articles, following the rule that a lone article carries only "the" and the
later Luke chapters (Luke 9:22 "on + day"). 24:6 "He is not here; He has
risen" matches Matthew 28:6 / Mark 16:6 (not / He is, He has risen). 24:10
"Mary the mother of James" uses the Mark 16:1 note.

  "Jesus" (24:38 εἶπεν, 24:44 Εἶπεν, 24:50 Ἐξήγαγεν), "Jesus" as the one

- 24:18: εἷς "One of them" → One; `t of them` (precedent elsewhere).
- 24:27 τά "what was written" → what; `t was written`. 24:35 τά "what had
  happened" → what; `t had happened`.
- 24:31: αὐτῶν "their sight" → their; `t sight`.
- Supplied copulas: 24:36 "Peace be" → Peace + `t be`; 24:44 "These are" →
  These + `t are`; 24:46 "This is" → This + what, `t is`.
- "and" joining a participle to the main verb (now `t`): 24:12 (got up and

Words on the wrong Greek word, or missing links:

- When/As/While moved off δέ/καί to the participle, genitive absolute or ἐν
  τῷ: 24:5 (As + bowed), 24:36 (While + were describing), 24:41 (While +

  to παρακύψας; 24:40 "when" from καί to εἰπών.
- 24:28 and 24:50: BSB turns two καί-joined clauses into "As …, …" / "When
  …, …", so As/When now sit on the καί between the clauses; the opening καί
  (24:28) and δέ (24:50) are `s`.
- 24:20: "Our" was on the article οἱ; it now goes to ἡμῶν (was TODO), and
  both articles are `s`.
- 24:21: ταύτην had "it", τρίτην "the third": now ταύτην = the, τρίτην =
  third, ἄγει = it is. γε had "besides": ἀλλά γε καί = And, σύν = besides.
- 24:19: εἶπαν "they answered" → answered, with οἱ (δέ) = they; ἀνήρ "This
  man" → man, with the relative ὅς = This; τοῦ Ναζαρηνοῦ = of Nazareth (as
  Luke 18:37).
- 24:18: Σύ "Are You" → You; παροικεῖς = Are + visitor. καί "who" → `s`

- 24:32: the second ὡς had "and"; both ὡς now share "as", and "and" is `t`.
- 24:33: καί had "There"; now `s` + `t There`. αὐτῇ τῇ ὥρᾳ = very / that

- 24:34: ἠγέρθη "has indeed risen" → has + risen; ὄντως (TODO) = indeed.
- 24:38: "do doubts" → doubts; do + arise on the verb.
- 24:16: τοῦ μή (TODO) = from; ἐπιγνῶναι = recognizing.

- 24:53: καὶ ἦσαν … εὐλογοῦντες: ἦσαν joined to the participle = praising;

## Notable decisions

- 24:12 and 24:33: the Greek καί between the second and third actions is
  dropped by BSB, while English adds an "and" between a participle and its
  verb (bending down and seeing; got up and returned). I treated these as
  the participle-joining "and" (`t`) plus an unrendered καί (`s`), not as a
  shifted split pair.
- 24:13: ἀπέχουσαν σταδίους ἑξήκοντα = "about seven miles" as one group
  (unit conversion).
- 24:17: οἱ λόγοι οὗτοι οὕς `s` (BSB recasts "What are these words that you
  exchange" as "What are you discussing").
- 24:11: ταῦτα keeps the publisher's "their" (with note); the article τά is
  `s`.
- 24:5: "As" placed with κλινουσῶν (As + bowed); ἐμφόβων γενομένων = in
  terror.

## Doubts for the peer

- 24:47: "and" for εἰς ("repentance for/leading to forgiveness"). EVIDENCE
  says the publisher's Greek equals N1904, so I did not claim a καί reading;
  `s` εἰς + `t and`. A peer may judge "and" an acceptable shifted rendering.
- 24:50: linking "out" to Ἐξήγαγεν vs `t out` with the reading reason.
- 24:12 / 24:33: split pair or not (see above).
- 24:1: departs from Mark 16:2's "on the"/"of the" on the articles to follow
  the lone-article rule; the peer may prefer exact parallel wording.
- 24:21: ἀλλά γε καί = And as one group (vs ἀλλά = And with γε καί `s`).
- 24:24: publisher's οὕτως = just, καθώς = as kept (alternative: one group
  "just as").
