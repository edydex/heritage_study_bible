# BSB Acts 12: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: all 25 (12:1-25). No KEEP blocks in this chapter. Final
check: `fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 25, "problems": 0}`.

110 of the 388 publisher `g` rows changed, in 24 of the 25 verses (12:24
keeps every publisher row). Some only gained a token, with the English
unchanged: 12:1 ἐπέβαλεν τὰς χεῖρας = reached out (as Acts 4:3, 5:18);
12:3 ἀρεστόν ἐστιν = pleased; 12:5 ἦν … γινομένη = was; 12:7 καὶ ἰδού =
Suddenly (as Luke 9:30, 9:38) and ἐν τάχει = quickly; 12:9 οὐκ ᾔδει = was
unaware (as μὴ εἰδυῖα in 5:7) and τὸ γινόμενον = what … was doing; 12:10 τὴν
φέρουσαν = leading; 12:12 and 12:25 article + participle = also called (as
Acts 3:2, 3:11); 12:14 οὐκ ἤνοιξεν = she forgot to open; 12:19 καὶ μὴ εὑρών
= unsuccessfully; 12:20 τὸν ἐπὶ τοῦ κοιτῶνος = chamberlain, διὰ τό =
because, ὁμοθυμαδὸν … παρῆσαν = they convened; 12:23 ἀνθ’ ὧν = because. The
rest were corrected, split or moved.

No parallel Gospel chapter. Conventions followed: article as pronoun (οἱ δέ
/ ἡ δέ in 12:15), article as possessive (12:17, 12:21, 12:25), "The
preposition repeats the verb's prefix" (12:10 ἀπ’), Luke 22:1 for τῶν
ἀζύμων, John 1:37 and 10:12 for "when" on the connective joining two
clauses.

## Publisher rows corrected

English words with no Greek counterpart that had been folded into a Greek
word (now `t`):

- Supplied names for a verb or participle ending: "Herod" (12:3, 12:4,
  12:20, 12:23), "Peter" (12:8, 12:9, 12:17), "the angel" (12:8).
- Supplied objects: 12:9 "him", 12:12 "this",
  12:13 "it", 12:16 "the door".
- Supplied subjects/copulas: 12:3 "this" (had been on ἐστιν), 12:14 "she
  was", 12:19 "that they", 12:22 "This is".
- BSB additions: 12:9 "only", 12:15 "must" (It must be; the Greek says "it
  is"), 12:18 "as to", 12:19 "there", 12:21 "his" (royal robes).
- "and" joining a participle (or genitive absolute) to the main verb: 12:4,
  12:7, 12:10 (twice), 12:11, 12:13, 12:14, 12:16, 12:17, 12:19 (twice),
  12:23. 12:4 καί ("also") had carried this "and"; it is now `s`.

Words on the wrong Greek word:

- Lone articles carrying a preposition: 12:4 τῷ "to the", 12:6 τῇ "On" and
  τήν "to the", 12:7 τήν "on the", 12:12 τῆς "of" (Μαρίας is declinable),
  12:13 τήν "at the", 12:17 τῇ "with his" and τοῖς "to the", 12:20 τῆς "on
  the". The articles keep "the"/"his"; the prepositions go on the nouns.
- When/After/At moved off δέ to the participle or genitive absolute: 12:12
  "when" (τε → συνιδών), 12:18 "At" (δέ → Γενομένης ἡμέρας), 12:19 "After"
  (δέ → ἐπιζητήσας), 12:25 "When" (δέ → πληρώσαντες).
- When between two καί/δέ-joined clauses moved to the connective that joins
  them: 12:10 "When" from the καί opening the clause (now `s`, new sentence)
  to the καί before εὐθέως; 12:15 "when" from the first δέ ("But when") to
  the δέ of οἱ δὲ ἔλεγον; 12:16 "when" from ἀνοίξαντες to the καί before
  ἐξέστησαν.
- Pronouns put on the Greek pronoun or article instead of the verb or a
  noun: 12:2 "He had" from Ἰάκωβον to ἀνεῖλεν; 12:4 "him" from ἀναγαγεῖν
  to αὐτόν, and "him" after handing from παραδούς to αὐτόν (object of
  φυλάσσειν); 12:7 "him" from ἤγειρεν to αὐτόν, "his" from τῶν to αὐτοῦ;
  12:15 "they" (twice) and "she" onto οἱ / ἡ; 12:23 "down" from αὐτόν to
  ἐπάταξεν.
- 12:3 "Feast" from ἀζύμων to ἡμέραι ("days"); ἀζύμων = "of Unleavened
  Bread" as in Luke 22:1.
- 12:4 "each" from στρατιωτῶν to τετραδίοις (squads of four … each).
- 12:6 "to trial" kept on προαγαγεῖν, "him" moved to αὐτόν.
- 12:8 "around you" from ἱμάτιον to the middle Περιβαλοῦ.
- 12:10 "the length of" from προῆλθον to ῥύμην (accusative of extent).
- 12:11 "from" repeated before everything now links to the one ἐκ
  (understood again); "the" moved from τῶν (of Ἰουδαίων) to τοῦ (of λαοῦ).
- 12:13 θύραν τοῦ πυλῶνος joined as "outer gate" (θύραν had been
  unlinked); "and" removed from παιδίσκη.
- 12:14 "she was so overjoyed that" had been all on χαρᾶς: now ἀπὸ τῆς
  χαρᾶς = "so overjoyed that", "she was" `t`.
- 12:17 "for" from αὐτοῖς to σιγᾶν (for silence); αὐτοῖς twice `s`.
- 12:20 "depended" had been on ἀπό: now τρέφεσθαι = "depended … for food",
  ἀπό = "on".
- 12:21 "the people" now also carries πρός (πρὸς αὐτούς), as BSB names the
  people for the pronoun.

Negatives: 12:18 οὐκ = no, 12:22 οὐκ = not, 12:23 οὐκ = not (was inside
"Herod did not give"). 12:9 (unaware), 12:14 (forgot to open) and 12:19
(unsuccessfully) stay joined because BSB has no separate negative word.

## Readings

- 12:2 and 12:12 Ἰωάνου, 12:19 Καισάριαν, 12:25 Ἰωάνην: linked with a
  spelling note (probably Ἰωάννου, Καισάρειαν, Ἰωάννην).
- 12:25 συνπαραλαβόντες: linked with a spelling note (probably
  συμπαραλαβόντες).
- 12:25 ἐξ Ἱερουσαλήμ: BSB's "to Jerusalem" follows a different reading
  (probably εἰς); ἐξ is `s`, "to" is `t`, both with the reading reason.
- 12:24 τοῦ Κυρίου: BSB's "of God" follows a different reading (probably
  τοῦ θεοῦ); `s` + `t` as in John 4:1.
- 12:3: one extra publisher word and an unowned "the" before Feast: `t the`
  with the reading reason (probably αἱ before ἡμέραι).
- 12:21: one extra publisher word (probably καί before καθίσας). No English
  word needs it; the reading is mentioned in the `t and` reason.

## Notable decisions

- 12:3 ἦσαν = "during" (publisher row kept, with a note): BSB turns the
  clause "it was the days" into a during-phrase.
- 12:4 ὅν = "him" + "him": the relative is the object of both πιάσας and
  ἔθετο (as the one αὐτούς in 5:27).
- 12:6 τῇ νυκτὶ ἐκείνῃ: τῇ = "the", νυκτί = "On … night", ἐκείνῃ `s`. The
  publisher had "the" on ἐκείνῃ and "On" on τῇ.
- 12:6 τε = "with" (publisher row kept, note added): BSB turns "and guards
  … were guarding" into "with sentries standing guard".
- 12:20 Τυρίοις = "with the people of Tyre", Σιδωνίοις = "Sidon" (names
  linked despite the -ians form).
- 12:22 ὁ δῆμος: δῆμος = "they" (note), ὁ `s`.
- 12:4 "by four" and 12:6 "with two" stay on the numerals, and 12:21 "On
  the appointed" stays on τακτῇ: they are not lone articles.

## Doubts for the peer

- 12:10 and 12:16 put "When"/"when" on the καί between the two clauses,
  because BSB's when-clause includes a finite verb (προῆλθον, εἶδαν). The
  alternative is "when" on the participle (ἐξελθόντες, ἀνοίξαντες), with
  that καί `s`. 12:15 applies the same rule to δέ.
- 12:6 ἐκείνῃ `s` with "the" on τῇ; keeping the publisher's ἐκείνῃ = "the"
  (τῇ `s`) is also defensible.
- 12:13 θύραν τοῦ πυλῶνος joined as "outer gate"; the alternative is θύραν
  = "gate", πυλῶνος = "outer".
- 12:20 ὁμοθυμαδόν joined with παρῆσαν as "they convened"; the alternative
  is ὁμοθυμαδόν `s`.
- 12:18 "as to" made `t` (the publisher had "as to what" on τί).
