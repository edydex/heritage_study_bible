# BSB John 14: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: 31 (14:1-31). The draft had no KEEP blocks, so no verse was
kept from an earlier review. Final check: `fromtext` →
`{"versesOk": 31, "problems": 0}`.

431 of the 503 publisher `g` rows are unchanged. 72 rows in 24 verses were
extended with TODO tokens, joined, split or corrected, as listed below. Every
publisher `g` row in 14:3, 14:4, 14:8, 14:13, 14:15, 14:25 and 14:29 is kept
(some of these verses gained only notes or `s`/`t` lines).

No Synoptic parallel. Conventions followed: John 4:26, 6:35, 6:48 (Ἐγώ = I,
εἰμι = am), John 1:23 and 10:38 (a supplied copula is `t`), John 6:44 and 6:56
(κἀγώ = and I), John 6:57 (κἀκεῖνος = also), John 6:51 (εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα =
forever), John 1:48 and 3:3 (ἀπεκρίθη … καὶ εἶπεν = replied, one group),
John 4:10 and 5:46 (ἄν joined to the unreal verb), John 2:5 (ὅ τι ἄν =
whatever), John 7:7 (οὐ δύναται = cannot), Matthew 6:3 (μή = not; a
third-person command = do + let + verb), Mark 2:21 (εἰ δὲ μή as one unit).

## ἐγώ εἰμι, παράκλητος and the "in Me / in the Father" phrases

All linked word by word, with no interpretive notes:

- 14:6: Ἐγώ = I, εἰμι = am, each ἡ = the, each καί = and, ὁδός = way,
  ἀλήθεια = truth, ζωή = life; εἰ μή = except, δι’ = through, ἐμοῦ = Me.
- 14:16 Παράκλητον = Advocate (ἄλλον = another); 14:26 Παράκλητος = Advocate
  (ὁ = the, δέ = But).
- 14:10: ἐγώ = I, ἐν = in, τῷ = the, Πατρί = Father; ὁ = the, Πατήρ = Father,
  ἐν = in, ἐμοί = Me, ἐστιν = is. "am" is `t`. Later ἐν ἐμοί = in Me (with
  μένων = dwelling).
- 14:11: the same phrase without a verb: ἐγώ = I, ἐν = in (×2); "am" and "is"
  are `t`.
- 14:20: ἐγώ = I, ἐν = in, Πατρί = Father, μου = My; ὑμεῖς = you, ἐν = in,
  ἐμοί = Me; κἀγώ = and I, ἐν = in, ὑμῖν = you. "am", "are", "am" are `t`.

## Publisher rows corrected

Negatives and auxiliaries split off verbs:

- 14:1 and 14:27 Μή/μή carried "Do not let" → not; ταρασσέσθω = Do + let + be
  troubled. 14:27 μηδέ "do not" → not; δειλιάτω = do + be afraid.
- 14:5 οἴδαμεν "we do not know" → οὐκ = not, οἴδαμεν = we do + know.
- 14:9 ἔγνωκας "still you do not know" → οὐκ = not, ἔγνωκας = you do + know,
  "still" `t`.
- 14:10 πιστεύεις "Do you not believe" → οὐ = not, πιστεύεις = Do you +
  believe; λαλῶ "I do not speak" → οὐ = not, λαλῶ = I do + speak.
- 14:17 θεωρεῖ "it neither sees" → οὐ = neither, θεωρεῖ = it + sees.
- 14:18 ἀφήσω "I will not leave" → οὐκ = not, ἀφήσω = I will + leave.
- 14:24 μή and οὐ were TODO while the verbs carried "not" → μή = not,
  ὁ … ἀγαπῶν = Whoever does + love; οὐ = not, τηρεῖ = does + keep.
- 14:30 λαλήσω carried "I will not speak" and οὐκέτι only "longer" →
  οὐκέτι = not + longer, λαλήσω = I will + speak.
- 14:27 ἐγώ carried "I do" → ἐγώ = I, δίδωμι = do + give.

Particles on the wrong word:

- 14:2 ἄν carried "so" and the verb carried "would" → εἰ δὲ μή = If it were
  not so; εἶπον ἄν = would I have told.
- 14:7 ἄν carried "as well" while καί was TODO → καί = as well; ἂν ᾔδειτε =
  you would know.
- 14:12 ποιήσει carried "will also do" while κἀκεῖνος was TODO → κἀκεῖνος =
  also, ποιήσει = will + do.
- 14:28 ἄν (TODO) joined to "you would rejoice"; "than" moved from μείζων to
  μου (comparison by the genitive, as τούτων = than these in 14:12).

Lone articles carrying other words:

- 14:10 ὁ "it is the" → the; "it is" `t`.
- 14:22 τῷ "to the" → the; κόσμῳ = to + world.
- 14:24 τοῦ "it is from the" → the; Πατρός = from + Father; "it is" `t`.
- 14:30 τοῦ "of this" → this (with a note); κόσμου = of + world.

English-only words that a Greek word had claimed (now `t`):

- Supplied copulas: 14:10 "am" (on ἐγώ); 14:11 "am" (on ἐγώ), "is" (on ἐν);
  14:20 "am" (×2, on ἐγώ and κἀγώ), "are" (on ὑμεῖς).
- 14:2 "there" (on πορεύομαι); 14:5 "so" (on πῶς); 14:9 "can" (on σύ);
  14:14 "it" (on ποιήσω); 14:17 "Him" (on γινώσκει) and "But" (on ὑμεῖς; N1904
  has no δέ here); 14:23 "Our" (on μονήν); 14:28 "back" (on ἔρχομαι).

Units joined:

- Article + participle (rule 3): 14:9 ὁ ἑωρακώς = Anyone who has seen; 14:12
  ὁ πιστεύων = whoever believes; 14:21 ὁ ἔχων = Whoever has, ὁ ἀγαπῶν = the
  one who loves, ὁ (δέ) ἀγαπῶν = The one who loves; 14:24 as above.
- 14:19 ἔτι "In" + μικρόν "a little while" → ἔτι μικρόν = In a little while
  (ἔτι does not mean "in"); καί `s`.
- TODO tokens added with the English unchanged: εἰ μή = except 14:6; εἰ δὲ μή
  = or at least 14:11; εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα = forever 14:16; οὐ δύναται = cannot
  14:17; τί γέγονεν ὅτι = why 14:22; ἀπεκρίθη … καὶ εἶπεν = replied 14:23;
  οὐκ … οὐδέν = no claim 14:30; καθὼς … οὕτως = exactly what 14:31.
- 14:26 ὑπομνήσει "will remind you of" → will remind; ὑμᾶς (TODO) = you;
  πάντα = of everything (as τι = for anything in 14:14).

## Readings (EVIDENCE lines)

- 14:5: the second οἴδαμεν = we + know with a same-verb note; "can" `t`, BSB
  follows a reading with an extra word (probably δυνάμεθα … εἰδέναι).
- 14:7: the second "Him" is `t`, BSB follows a reading with an extra word
  (probably αὐτόν after ἑωράκατε).
- 14:9: Τοσοῦτον χρόνον = all this time with a same-sense note (probably the
  dative τοσούτῳ χρόνῳ).
- 14:13: ὅ τι ἄν = whatever with a same-sense note (probably ὅτι written as one
  word).
- 14:6: one extra publisher word (probably ὁ before Ἰησοῦς) with no English
  effect; not noted.

## Notable decisions

- 14:1: the first πιστεύετε keeps "You believe" (statement) with a note that the
  same form is a command later in the verse.
- 14:3: πάλιν = back and ἐμαυτόν = My presence kept, with notes.
- 14:4: opening καί `s`; ὅπου keeps the publisher's "to the place where" with a
  note (the relative carries its implied antecedent).
- 14:13 τοῦτο, 14:21 ἐκεῖνος and 14:26 ἐκεῖνος are `s` as resumptive pronouns;
  the relatives ἅ in 14:10 and 14:26 are `s`.
- 14:17: ὅ = Him (new sentence, with a note); the neuter αὐτό = Him without
  notes.
- 14:28: ὅτι and ὑμῖν are `s`; ἐγώ = Me (BSB turns "that I said to you" into
  "You heard Me say").
- 14:23, 14:24 and 14:31: καί `s` where BSB starts a new sentence or reorders.

## Doubts for the peer

- 14:30 τοῦ = "this" (the article read as a demonstrative, avoiding a split
  pair). The alternative is τοῦ `s` + "this" `t`; EVIDENCE shows no extra
  publisher word in this verse.
- 14:10 "am" `t` while ἐστιν = is. The alternative is ἐστιν = am + is (one
  verb serving both clauses); I chose `t` because ἐστιν is third person and
  stands in the second clause.
- 14:4 ὅπου = "to the place where" kept; the alternative is ὅπου = where with
  "to the place" `t`.
- 14:23 "Our" `t` and 14:28 "back" `t`: both were folded into a Greek word that
  does not mean them; the peer may prefer to keep the publisher rows.
- 14:5: "we know" linked to N1904 οἴδαμεν as the same verb, with "can" `t`. If
  "how can we know" is judged a different meaning from "how do we know", the
  verb becomes `s` and "we know" `t`.
