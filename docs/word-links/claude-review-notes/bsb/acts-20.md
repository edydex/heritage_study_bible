# BSB Acts 20: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: all 38 (20:1-38). No KEEP blocks in this chapter. Final
check: `fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 38, "problems": 0}` (also `check --target
BSB`).

Of the 554 publisher `g` rows, 20 only gained a token with the English
unchanged (20:1 τὸ παύσασθαι = had ended; 20:3 ἐγένετο γνώμης = he decided
and τοῦ ὑποστρέφειν = to go back; 20:6 ἤλθομεν πρός = we rejoined; 20:13
διατεταγμένος ἦν = He had arranged; 20:16 μὴ γένηται = avoid and γενέσθαι εἰς
= to reach; 20:19 τῶν συμβάντων = that came upon; 20:20 and 20:27 τοῦ μὴ
ἀναγγεῖλαι = from declaring (as Acts 14:18 τοῦ μή = from); 20:22 ἐν αὐτῇ =
there; 20:23 κατὰ πόλιν = in town after town; 20:24 οὐδενὸς λόγου = of no;
20:25 διῆλθον κηρύσσων = I have preached; 20:30 τοῦ ἀποσπᾶν = to draw away;
20:31 ἕνα ἕκαστον = each; 20:32 τὰ νῦν = now; 20:36 θεὶς τὰ γόνατα αὐτοῦ =
he knelt down (as Acts 9:40); 20:37 κλαυθμὸς ἐγένετο = wept and ἐπιπεσόντες
ἐπὶ τὸν τράχηλον = they embraced). About 110 rows changed their English, in
34 verses; 20:8, 20:21 and 20:26 kept every publisher row.

## Publisher rows corrected

English words with no Greek counterpart that had been folded into a Greek
word (now `t`):

- Supplied names: 20:11 "Paul" (ἀναβάς), 20:17 "Paul" (πέμψας), 20:36 "Paul"
  (εἰπών), 20:12 "the people" (was with "were greatly" on μετρίως). 20:4
  αὐτῷ = Paul and 20:10 αὐτῷ = the young man stay linked: there the Greek
  has a pronoun.
- Participle "and" with no καί: 20:1 "and left", 20:5 "and waited for",
  20:13 "and sailed", 20:14 "and went on", 20:29 "and will not spare", 20:30
  "and distort", 20:31 "and remember", 20:36 "and prayed", 20:37 "and
  kissed".
- Supplied objects: 20:1 "them" twice (after encouraging, said goodbye to),
  20:10 "him" (embraced), 20:32 "you" in "build you up" and "give you" (the
  second was on the article τήν).
- Other additions: 20:3 "And" (was "And when" on ὑπό, now `s`); 20:4 "son"
  (was on Πύρρου) and "the province of" (was on Ἀσιανοί); 20:16 and 20:18
  "province of" (was on Ἀσίᾳ/Ἀσίαν; the article keeps "the", as Acts 6:9 and
  the Acts 16:6 review); 20:7 "day" (μιᾷ = first, as John 20:1, Mark 16:2,
  Luke 24:1); 20:10 "still" (was on ψυχή); 20:12 "home" (was on ζῶντα);
  20:13 "there" (was on ἦν); 20:23 "I … know" (was on πλήν, which keeps
  "only"); 20:30 "number" (was on αὐτῶν, now own; ὑμῶν = your); 20:31 "of
  you" (was on ἕνα); 20:34 "own" (was on the article ταῖς), "those" (was on
  καί) and "of mine" (was on χεῖρες).

Words on the wrong Greek word:

- Lone articles carrying a preposition: 20:6 τῶν "of"; 20:7 τῶν "of the";
  20:15 τῇ "on the"; 20:16 τήν "by the"; 20:17 τῆς "of the"; 20:19 τῶν "of
  the"; 20:22 τῷ "by the"; 20:24 τό "to the" and τῆς "of"; 20:28 τήν "of
  the"; 20:32 τῷ "to the" and τῆς "of"; 20:35 τοῦ "of the". The articles
  keep "the" (or become `s`); the preposition goes on the noun (ἀζύμων = of
  Unleavened Bread, σαββάτων = of + week, ἐχομένῃ = on + following day,
  ἡμέραν = by + day, ἐκκλησίας/ἐκκλησίαν = of + church, Ἰουδαίων = of +
  Jews, πνεύματι = by + Spirit, εὐαγγέλιον = to + good news, χάριτος = of +
  grace, λόγῳ = to + word, Κυρίου = of + Lord).
- "When/as/after/since" on the wrong word: 20:2 "After" from δέ to
  διελθών; 20:3 "when" from ὑπό to γενομένης; 20:7 "Since" from Παῦλος to
  μέλλων; 20:9 "as" from Παύλου to διαλεγομένου; 20:36 "When" from the
  opening καί to εἰπών (as Acts 1:9).
- 20:13 ἦν had "because he was going there": ἦν joins διατεταγμένος; μέλλων
  = because + was going, αὐτός = he, "there" `t`. "aboard" moved from
  Παῦλον to ἀναλαμβάνειν (take + aboard); 20:14 "him" moved from
  ἀναλαβόντες to αὐτόν.
- Negatives split: 20:20 οὐδέν = not + anything, ὑπεστειλάμην = I did +
  shrink back (also 20:27); 20:31 οὐκ = never; 20:33 οὐδενός = not +
  anyone’s, ἐπεθύμησα = I have + coveted; 20:38 οὐκέτι = never + again (was
  "would never" on μέλλουσιν); 20:29 μή = not, φειδόμενοι = will + spare;
  20:12 οὐ μετρίως joined = greatly.
- 20:1 ἐξῆλθεν πορεύεσθαι joined = left (as Acts 12:17), εἰς = for (was on
  the infinitive).
- 20:2 παρακαλέσας had "speaking many words of encouragement": λόγῳ =
  words, πολλῷ = many; παρακαλέσας = speaking + of encouragement.
- 20:9 βαθεῖ had "a deep": "a" goes with the noun (ὕπνῳ = a + sleep);
  ἐπὶ πλεῖον joined = on and on.
- 20:10 "He is still alive": ψυχή = alive (was "still alive"), αὐτοῦ = He,
  ἐστιν = is, ἐν αὐτῷ `s`.
- 20:18 μεθ’ had "I was with": μεθ’ = with; ἐγενόμην = I lived + I was.
- 20:22 τὰ … συναντήσοντα joined = what will happen (article + participle);
  likewise joined 20:20 τῶν συμφερόντων = that was helpful, 20:32 τῷ
  δυναμένῳ = which can and τοῖς ἡγιασμένοις = who are sanctified, 20:35 τῶν
  ἀσθενούντων = the weak.
- 20:23 the first τό of τὸ Πνεῦμα τὸ Ἅγιον now has "the" and the second is
  `s` (as Acts 13:2); the publisher had it the other way.
- 20:24 "and complete" was on καί: καί = and, τελειώσω = I may finish +
  complete (the same verb understood again); the second "the ministry" was
  on διαμαρτύρασθαι: now τήν/διακονίαν understood again, and the infinitive
  = of testifying.
- 20:30 λαλοῦντες had "the truth" and διεστραμμένα "and distort": joined
  = distort the truth.
- 20:34 τοῖς had "my companions": τοῖς οὖσιν μετ’ = of + companions, ἐμοῦ
  = my.
- 20:38 εἰρήκει had "his statement": λόγῳ = statement, ᾧ εἰρήκει = his;
  μάλιστα = especially (was inside the ὀδυνώμενοι phrase).

## Readings

- 20:21 "Christ" `t`: BSB follows a reading with an extra word (probably
  Χριστόν).
- 20:22 ἐμοί: a different form of the pronoun (probably μοι); "to me"
  linked with a note.
- 20:24 τελειώσω: a different form of the verb (probably the infinitive
  τελειῶσαι); "I may finish" and "complete" linked with a note.
- 20:32 Κυρίῳ `s`, "to God" `t`: BSB follows a reading with God (probably
  Θεῷ). Lord and God differ, so no link.

## Notable decisions

- Names and places linked as spelled. City adjectives take "from"
  (Βεροιαῖος, Θεσσαλονικέων, Δερβαῖος, Ἀσιανοί), as Acts 6:5 Ἀντιοχέα;
  Πύρρου = of Pyrrhus with "son" `t` (as Acts 1:13). In 20:4 the δέ before
  Ἀσιανοί = the "and" before Tychicus; the other δέ and the καί before Gaius
  and Timothy are `s`.
- "We" passage: ἡμεῖς/ἡμᾶς/ἡμῖν/ἡμῶν are linked to we/us; first-person
  plural verbs carry their own "we".
- Connectives rendered with another connective, kept linked with a note:
  20:3 τε = where; 20:19 καί = especially; 20:20 and 20:37 καί = as; 20:4 δέ
  = and.
- 20:6 ἡμέρας = Feast kept (note); only "of" moved to ἀζύμων.
- 20:15 ἐπιούσῃ, ἑτέρᾳ, ἐχομένῃ keep next day / day after that / following
  day (the feminine substantive means "the next day", as the Acts 16:11
  review), unlike the numeral μιᾷ in 20:7.
- 20:21 τε = alike, as τε = "both" in Acts 2:11, 5:14 and 10:39.
- 20:24 οὐδενὸς λόγου = of no, τιμίαν = value (publisher row kept).
- 20:25 kept πάντες = none of and οὐκέτι = again, with notes.
- 20:17 μετεκαλέσατο = for (sent … for) kept with a note.
- 20:12 ἤγαγον = to take; the καί is `s` (BSB subordinates the clause).

## Doubts for the peer

- 20:21 τε = alike: the τε … καί rule normally gives `s`; I linked it
  because "alike" renders the "both". The alternative is τε `s` + "alike"
  `t`.
- 20:6 "Feast" on ἡμέρας, against the Acts 12:3 model (whole name on
  ἀζύμων, ἡμέρας elsewhere).
- 20:15 "day" in next day etc. linked to the feminine substantive, while
  20:7 "day" is `t`.
- 20:24 the repeated "the ministry" linked as understood again rather than
  `t` as an English appositive frame.
- 20:37 ἱκανός = openly and 20:19 καί = especially: both kept as BSB's
  renderings, but the English meaning shifts.
- 20:34 "of mine" `t` with αἱ `s`; the alternative is the article as a
  possessive.
- 20:38 ᾧ εἰρήκει = his; the alternative is the article τῷ = his with the
  relative clause `s`.
- 20:10 "still" `t` with ἐν αὐτῷ `s`.
- 20:18 ἐγενόμην linked to both "I lived" and "I was".
