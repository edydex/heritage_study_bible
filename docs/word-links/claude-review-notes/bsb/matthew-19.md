# BSB Matthew 19: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: 29 of 30 (19:1–21, 19:23–30). Kept unchanged from the
earlier reviewer: 19:22. Final check: `{"versesOk": 30, "problems": 0}`.

## Publisher rows corrected (43 rows)

- 19:1: "saying these things" was all on τούτους; now τούτους = these, λόγους = saying + things.
- 19:4: "Jesus answered" was all on ἀποκριθείς; now ὁ = Jesus, ἀποκριθεὶς εἶπεν = answered. "Have you not read" was on the verb alone; οὐκ = not.
- 19:5: "For this reason" was all on ἕνεκα; τούτου now owns "this".
- 19:6: "let man" was on ἄνθρωπος; "let" belongs to the imperative χωριζέτω (let + separate).
- 19:7: "did Moses" was on Μωϋσῆς ("did" moved to the verb). "a man", "his wife" and "her" were folded into the verbs; they are supplied English (`t`).
- 19:8: "Jesus replied" was on λέγει; the name is supplied (`t`).
- 19:10: the article τοῦ carried "between" and μετά carried "and"; now μετά = between + and, τοῦ is an unrendered article.
- 19:11: "He replied" was on εἶπεν; ὁ = He. "but only those" was on ἀλλά; now ἀλλά = but, οἷς = those to whom, "only" is supplied.
- 19:12: ὁ δυνάμενος joined as "The one who can"; "this" and "it" were folded into χωρεῖν/χωρείτω and are supplied objects.
- 19:13: "His hands" split (τάς = His); "pray for them" and "those who brought them" carried supplied words; "for them" and "who brought them" are now `t`.
- 19:14: "do not hinder" was on κωλύετε; μή = not.
- 19:16: "Just then" was on καί alone; now Καὶ ἰδού.
- 19:17: "Jesus replied" was on εἶπεν (ὁ = Jesus). Two lone articles carried "what is" and "who is"; now τοῦ ἀγαθοῦ = what + good and ὁ ἀγαθός = who + good, with the supplied copulas as `t`.
- 19:18: "the man asked" was on λέγει (name supplied). The four prohibitions were on the verbs alone; each Οὐ = not, verb = do + verb.
- 19:20: "All these" was on πάντα (ταῦτα = these); "do I still lack" was on the verb (ἔτι = still).
- 19:25: δέ carried "When" and "heard this" was on the participle; now ἀκούσαντες = When + heard, "this" supplied, δέ unrendered. σφόδρα carried "they were greatly"; now σφόδρα = greatly, the verb = they were + astonished.
- 19:26: "looked at them" was on ἐμβλέψας; αὐτοῖς now owns "them". "all things are" was on πάντα; "are" now joins ἐστιν (ellipsis).
- 19:28: οἱ ἀκολουθήσαντες joined as "who have followed"; "will also sit" was on the verb (καί = also).
- 19:30: "who are first" was on πρῶτοι (the relative clause is supplied); "will be first" was on πρῶτοι ("will be" now joins ἔσονται).

## Notable decisions

- Tokens joined as one unit: ὃς ἄν (whoever), Καὶ ἰδού (Just then), ἔσονται εἰς (will become, 19:5), ἀποκριθεὶς … εἶπεν (answered/replied, 19:4, 19:27), μετῆρεν ἀπό (He left), εἰσέρχομαι + εἰς (enter, 19:17, 19:23), εὐνούχισαν ἑαυτούς (live like eunuchs), and article + participle in 19:12 and 19:28.
- 19:12: BSB shortens "there are eunuchs who" to "others" / "still others", so εἰσίν + εὐνοῦχοι + οἵτινες link jointly; ἐκ κοιλίας μητρός is left implicit in "born" (`s`).
- Ellipsis: one Greek verb links to its repeated English form: εἰσελθεῖν (to pass + to enter, 19:24), ἐστιν (is + are, 19:26), ἔσονται (will be + will be, 19:30).
- Pronominal ὁ in ὁ δέ is linked to "Jesus"/"He" (19:4, 19:11, 19:17), as in earlier Matthew chapters. Where the Greek has only a verb ending, the name is `t` (19:8, 19:18). αὐτῷ → "to Jesus" is kept (19:13, 19:16).
- Article with possessive force is linked to his/His/your (19:5, 19:10, 19:13, 19:19).
- Textual differences between N1904 and BSB:
  - 19:29: "or wife" (ἢ γυναῖκα) is not in N1904 (`t`). BSB's "a hundredfold" follows ἑκατονταπλασίονα, while N1904 reads πολλαπλασίονα; no link is invented, so both sides are explained (`s`/`t`).
  - 19:10: "His disciples": N1904 has no αὐτοῦ (`t`).
  - 19:3 "for a man" and 19:7 "her": supplied English (`t`). The reasons note that other editions read ἀνθρώπῳ / αὐτήν.
- Supplied English (`t`): 19:7 a man / his wife / her; 19:11 only; 19:12 this / it; 19:13 the / for them / who brought them; 19:17 is (×2); 19:25 this; 19:30 who are.

## Doubts for the peer

- 19:10, 19:3, 19:7: I am fairly but not fully sure that the editions BSB follows read αὐτοῦ, ἀνθρώπῳ and αὐτήν here. The reasons are worded so that they stay true either way. Please check them.
- 19:29: should "a hundredfold" rather link to πολλαπλασίονα as the same slot? I chose not to, following rule 9.
- 19:26: αὐτοῖς ("to them" after εἶπεν) is linked to "them" in "looked at them". The alternative is `s` for αὐτοῖς, with "them" left on ἐμβλέψας.
- 19:14: the publisher row τῶν → "to" (in "belongs to such as these") is kept as a genitive marker, like τῶν → "of" elsewhere. It could instead be an unrendered article, with "to" on ἐστίν.
- 19:24: εἰς is `s` (absorbed by "enter"); it could instead join εἰσελθεῖν, but then "to pass" would also show εἰς.
- Kept publisher renderings that add a little English: "some Pharisees" (19:3), "only One" (19:17), "renewal of all things" (19:28), "the last" (19:30).
