# BSB Mark 12: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: 44 of 44 (no verse was kept from an earlier reviewer).

Check: `fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 44, "problems": 0}`.

73 publisher rows in 37 verses were changed because they were wrong or
over-claimed. Another 19 rows only took a word freed by a neighbouring
correction (for example "around" moving to περιέθηκεν in 12:1), and 33 rows
only gained a token that had no owner, or were merged into one unit with their
English unchanged (ἕως ἄν, ἐπ’ ἀληθείας, article + participle, article +
articular infinitive, οὐ … οὐδενός).

## Publisher rows corrected

- `καί` carrying the "When/While/As" of a participle: 12:34 (ἰδών = When +
  saw), 12:35 (διδάσκων = While + was teaching), 12:41 (καθίσας = As + was
  sitting). The opening καί is now `s`.
- Negatives folded into verbs, now linked on their own: "Have you never read"
  (12:10, οὐδέ = never), "have you not read" (12:26), "you do not know"
  (12:24), "they will neither marry" (12:25, οὔτε = neither). Joint units
  instead: Οὐ … πλανᾶσθε = Aren’t you mistaken (12:24); μὴ ἀφῇ = no (12:19);
  μὴ δῶμεν = not (12:14).
- Participle "and" now `t`, the verb keeps its own word: "and went away"
  (12:12), "and said" (12:15, 12:43), "and heard" (12:28), "and left"
  (12:21), "and put in" (12:42).
- Lone article or particle carrying other English: ὁ = "will the" (12:9);
  οἱ = "can the" (12:35); ὅτι = "the" (12:29); εἰς = "the" (12:10, now
  κεφαλὴν γωνίας = the cornerstone); ἡ = "whose" (12:16, now Τίνος = Whose +
  whose); ὅταν = "then" (12:23); τάς = "of their" (12:40); ἐκεῖνοι = "the"
  (12:7, "the" now on οἱ). Article + "of/to" moved to the noun as in Mark 6:
  τοῦ/τῶν/οἱ = "of the" (12:2, 12:8, 12:9, 12:13, 12:22, 12:28), τῷ = "to"
  (12:36), τῷ = "for" (12:19, now τῷ ἀδελφῷ αὐτοῦ = for him).
- Copula claimed by a word that lacks it, now `t is`: τά = "what is" (12:17
  twice), αὕτη = "is this" (12:16, 12:31), ἐστιν = "God is" (12:32, God `t`),
  Ἐγώ = "I am" (12:26, am `t`), Πρώτη ἐστίν → "This" `t` (12:29).
- English-only words claimed by a Greek word, now `t`: Jesus (12:1, 12:15 ←
  ὁ, 12:28, 12:38, 12:41, 12:43), the leaders (12:12), it (12:1, 12:16),
  them (12:14), His (12:13), his (12:2 "his share of"), burning (12:26),
  Speaking (12:36), to receive (12:38), to have (12:39), Now (12:20), one
  (12:20, 12:21), also and did (12:21).
- Object pronouns placed in the wrong group: 12:1 "he rented it out" (it =
  αὐτόν); 12:3 "beat him", "sent him away" (him = the one αὐτόν, which also
  gives "the servant"); 12:4 "struck him", "treated him" (him = κἀκεῖνον);
  12:8 "they seized the son" (the son = αὐτόν with him#0).
- Wrong tokens:
  - 12:5 καί = "He sent" (the second "He sent" now repeats ἀπέστειλεν).
  - 12:23 ἔσχον = "were" → ἔσχον … γυναῖκα = were married.
  - 12:25 ἀναστῶσιν = "the dead rise" → ἐκ νεκρῶν = the dead, ἀναστῶσιν = rise.
  - 12:27 πολύ was unlinked → badly; πλανᾶσθε = You are + mistaken.
  - 12:28 "Jesus had answered" → had answered; 12:34 ἀπεκρίθη = "the man had
    answered" → the proleptic αὐτόν = the man.
  - 12:35 Ἰησοῦς = "He", διδάσκων = "Jesus was teaching" → Ἰησοῦς = Jesus,
    ἀποκριθεὶς … ἔλεγεν = He asked.
  - 12:40 μακρά was unlinked inside "make lengthy prayers" → lengthy;
    οἱ κατεσθίοντες = They defraud + of; τῶν χηρῶν = widows.
  - 12:43 "Jesus called", "disciples to Him" → προσκαλεσάμενος = called + to
    Him (as Mark 6:7).

## Notable decisions

- Publisher Greek differences: 12:9 "then" `t` (BSB's Greek adds οὖν);
  12:26 the#4 and the#5 `t` (BSB's Greek has ὁ before θεὸς Ἰσαάκ and θεὸς
  Ἰακώβ); 12:28 συνζητούντων = debate (same verb as the publisher's
  συζητούντων), while N1904's εἰδώς ("knowing") is `s` and "Noticing" `t`
  (BSB's Greek has ἰδών, "seeing"). 12:33's EVIDENCE line reports a
  difference but no unmatched word, so nothing is claimed there.
- 12:23 "then" is `t` without a reading claim (EVIDENCE: publisher Greek =
  N1904); ὅταν ἀναστῶσιν is `s` as a BSB omission.
- Kept as connective renderings: Καί = At this (12:12), Later (12:13), In
  this way (12:22), also (12:38, as Mark 4:21); the adverbial καί of 12:22b =
  And.
- One Greek word rendered twice: αὐτόν = the servant + him + him (12:3),
  the son + him (12:8); κἀκεῖνον = and + him + him (12:4); ἀπέστειλεν =
  He sent + He sent (12:5); Τίνος = Whose + whose (12:16).
- Articles rendered as possessives kept: τῆς/τόν = your (12:33, no σου in
  the Greek); τάς = their (12:40); τήν = his brother’s (12:19).
- 12:14: οὐ μέλει σοι = seek favor, οὐ … οὐδενός = no one; οὐ βλέπεις εἰς
  πρόσωπον ἀνθρώπων = You are impartial as one unit.
- 12:44: ὅλον τὸν βίον αὐτῆς = to live on (BSB merges it into "all she had to
  live on"); πάντα ὅσα = all.

## Doubts for the peer

- 12:19: BSB recasts "his brother should take the wife" as "the man is to
  marry his brother’s widow". I linked ὁ ἀδελφὸς αὐτοῦ = the man and kept the
  publisher's τήν = his brother’s. Alternative: "his brother’s" `t` with τήν
  `s`.
- 12:5: the second "He sent" is linked to the one ἀπέστειλεν (ellipsis).
  Alternative: `t He#1 sent#1`.
- 12:13 Later and 12:22 In this way kept on καί, as "At this". Alternative:
  `t` with καί `s`.
- 12:21: καί = but, with "also" `t`. One could argue καί = also and "but" `t`.
- 12:28: is the s/t split for εἰδώς/"Noticing" right, given that εἰδώς could
  also be rendered "perceiving"? I followed the rule against linking across a
  different reading.
- 12:41: πῶς … βάλλει = putting (joined) rather than πῶς `s`.
