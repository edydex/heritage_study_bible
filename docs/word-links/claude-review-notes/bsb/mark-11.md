# BSB Mark 11: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: 31 (11:1-33; 11:26 is empty in both texts). 11:23 was already
reviewed and is kept unchanged. Final check: `{"versesOk": 32, "problems": 0}`.
84 of the 436 publisher `g` rows were changed. 72 of them were corrected, split
or joined. The other 12 only gained a Greek token from the TODO list.

## Publisher rows corrected

"When", "As" or "After" was on the wrong word:

- 11:15, 11:18, 11:27: BSB turns the first of two clauses joined by καί into a
  When- or After-clause. When/After now goes on the καί between the clauses.
  The opening καί, which only links to the previous verse, is `s`.
- 11:20: "As" moves from καί to the participle παραπορευόμενοι.
- 11:13: καί carried "But when". καί → But, and ἐλθὼν ἐπ’ → when He reached.
- 11:12: αὐτῶν carried "when they". Now the genitive absolute ἐξελθόντων ... ἀπό
  → when + had left, and αὐτῶν → they.
- 11:11: the article τῆς carried "but since". Now οὔσης → since + was, τῆς is
  `s`, and "but" is `t`.

A supplied "and" joining a participle or genitive absolute to the main verb is
now `t`: 11:15 ("and began"), 11:21 ("and said"), 11:27.

English-only words that a Greek word had claimed (now `t`):

- Subjects from a verb ending: Jesus (11:1, 11:11, 11:12, 11:15, 11:17),
  "the people" (11:6), "Jesus and His disciples" (11:19).
- Objects: him (11:3), them (11:6), it (11:2, 11:21, 11:24, 11:25), this
  (11:18), "this statement" (11:14).
- Other: "and came" (11:1) and "and went" (11:11), supplied for a second εἰς
  phrase; there/here (11:2); "in the crowd" and the repeated "spread" (11:8);
  "is" (11:9, 11:10), which had been on εὐλογημένος and on the article ἡ; "to
  see", "fruit" and the second "on it" (11:13); "its" (11:20).

Negatives folded into verbs: 11:16 οὐκ → not, ἤφιεν → He would + allow;
11:17 Οὐ → not, γέγραπται → Is it + written; 11:31 οὐκ → not; 11:33 Οὐκ → not,
οἴδαμεν → We do + know.

Other wrong anchors:

- 11:2: οὔπω → ever, taken out of "has ever sat". ἀνθρώπων is `s`.
- 11:5: the article τῶν carried "who were". Now it is joined to ἑστηκότων.
- 11:6: εἶπαν "The disciples answered": now οἱ (δέ) → The disciples and εἶπαν →
  answered. αὐτούς → them, taken out of "the people gave them permission".
- 11:10: ἡμῶν carried "of our". Now τοῦ → of and ἡμῶν → our.
- 11:13: ἄρα carried "there was". ἄρα is now `s`, and εὑρήσει → there was.
- 11:14: "again" was on καρπόν and "May" was on μηδείς. Now Μηκέτι εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα
  → ever + again, μηδείς → no one, and φάγοι → May + eat.
- 11:16: ἵνα → to, taken out of "to carry".
- 11:24: ἔσται → it will be. "it" had been on καί.
- 11:29: καί carried "and if". Now καί → and, and the second καί, after the
  imperative ἀποκρίθητε, → if.
- 11:32: the article τόν carried "that". Now ὅτι → that, and τόν is `s`.
- 11:33: "will I" → ἐγώ → I, and λέγω → will + tell.

Joined units (rule 3): verbs with εἰς rendered as transitive verbs (11:1,
11:2, 11:11, 11:15); article + participle (11:5, 11:9 three times, 11:15);
χρείαν ἔχει → needs and ἀποστέλλει πάλιν → will return (11:3); εἰ μή (11:13);
ἀποκριθείς with the speech verb (11:14, 11:22, 11:33); ἐν τῷ ἱερῷ → there
(11:15); διὰ τοῦτο and πάντα ὅσα (11:24); ἔρχονται πάλιν → their return
(11:27); Διὰ τί (11:31).

## Notable decisions

- 11:31: BSB's "what they should answer" is `t`. The Berean interlinear shows
  BSB's Greek adds τί εἴπωμεν ("what should we say"), which N1904 lacks.
  λέγοντες is `s`, because only the colon before the quotation represents it.
- 11:21, 11:30, 11:32: EVIDENCE shows the publisher Greek differs here, but only
  in the spelling of the name (Ῥαββί, Ἰωάννου, Ἰωάννην). The words are linked
  (rule 8).
- 11:3: the one extra publisher word is ὅτι before Ὁ Κύριος. BSB does not
  translate it, so no link changes. ὧδε is `s`, because "return" already implies
  "here".
- 11:17: αὐτοῖς belongs to ἔλεγεν, but BSB moves it to "teach them". It is
  linked so that the pair is not split between `s` and `t`.
- 11:14 αὐτῇ → "to the tree" and 11:27 αὐτοῦ → Jesus: these pronouns are
  rendered by nouns, and they are linked.

## Doubts for the peer

- 11:13: εὑρήσει "he would find" → "there was", and "to see" → `t`. The other
  option is εὑρήσει → "to see" with "there was" as `t`.
- 11:29: "if" is on the second καί, after the imperative. The first καί is
  "and". The Matthew 21:24 parallel has different Greek (ὃν ἐάν).
- 11:32: "if we say" is kept on the deliberative subjunctive εἴπωμεν. BSB's
  Greek has no ἐάν here, so "if" could be `t` instead.
- 11:11 and 11:13: "it" in "it was already late" is linked to ὥρας ("the hour").
  "it was not the season" is kept on ἦν. 11:30's "was it" is also kept on ἦν.
  In each case "it" could be `t` instead.
- 11:27: "After" is on the middle καί, and "and" before "the chief priests" is
  `t`. The other option is the middle καί → "and", with "After" as `t`.
