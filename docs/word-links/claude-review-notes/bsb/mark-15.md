# BSB Mark 15: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: 46 (15:1-47; 15:28 is empty in both texts and skipped). No
verse was kept from an earlier reviewer. Final check:
`{"versesOk": 46, "problems": 0}`.

Of the 548 publisher `g` rows, 450 are unchanged and 15 only gained a Greek
token from the TODO list (for example ὃν παρῃτοῦντο → of their choosing). The
English of 83 rows in 36 verses changed: 57 were wrong or over-claimed, and the
rest are rule-3 joins or rows that took a word freed by a neighbouring fix.

## Publisher rows corrected

"When", "After" or "From" was on the wrong word:

- 15:20: καί carried "After". Now ὅτε → After and the opening καί is `s`.
- 15:35: καί carried "When"; 15:39: δέ carried "When". The participles
  ἀκούσαντες and Ἰδών now carry When + heard / When + saw.
- 15:33: ἕκτης carried "From the sixth". The genitive absolute γενομένης now
  carries From.
- 15:45: γνούς carried "When Pilate had confirmed it". Now When + had
  confirmed; Pilate and it are `t`.

A supplied "and" joining a participle to the main verb is now `t`: 15:8 (and
began), 15:15 (and handed), 15:19 (and bowed), 15:21, 15:30 (and save), 15:36
(and held), 15:37 (and breathed).

Negatives folded into verbs: 15:4 Οὐκ … οὐδέν → no, ἀποκρίνῃ → Have You +
answer; 15:5 οὐκέτι οὐδέν → no further, ἀπεκρίθη → made + reply; 15:23 οὐκ →
not, ἔλαβεν → did + take.

English-only words that a Greek word had claimed (now `t`, or moved to the
Greek word they render):

- Objects: Him in "led Him away"/"handed Him over" (15:1, 15:15, 15:20) now
  goes with the one Greek object τὸν Ἰησοῦν/αὐτόν; Jesus in "led Jesus away"
  (15:16) and "had handed Jesus over" (15:10) now goes to αὐτόν; it (15:23,
  15:29, 15:36, 15:45); Him (15:31, 15:36 twice).
- Subjects: Jesus (15:2 → ὁ; 15:44 `t`), Pilate's (15:6), Pilate (15:8,
  15:45), the soldiers (15:21), Joseph (15:46), His body (15:47).
- Other: "it was" cleft (15:10); "together" moved from σπεῖραν to συνκαλοῦσιν
  (15:16); "head" (15:17); "back" (15:20); "called" (15:22); "There" (15:23,
  as in Mark 9:2); "to decide" had been on ἐπ’ (15:24); "were" had been on the
  καί of 15:40; "there were" (15:41); "to hear" (15:44); "already" moved from
  τέθνηκεν to ἤδη (15:44); "loud" moved from the noun to μεγάλῃ/μεγάλην
  (15:34, 15:37).

Other wrong anchors:

- 15:1: εὐθύς carried "Early". πρωΐ alone means early in the morning (as in
  Mark 1:35), so πρωΐ → Early in the morning and εὐθύς is `s`.
- 15:6: δέ carried "Now it was Pilate's custom". Now δέ → Now and the customary
  imperfect ἀπέλυεν → it was + custom + to release.
- 15:21: τινα carried "Now". τινα is `s`, the opening καί → Now, and Σίμωνα →
  Simon + him.
- 15:24: "also" moved from διαμερίζονται to the καί between the clauses.
- 15:27: Καί carried "Along". Now σύν → Along with and καί is `s`; αὐτοῦ →
  His + His (it applies to both sides).
- 15:32: ὁ carried "Let". Now καταβάτω → Let + come down.
- 15:15: τὸ ἱκανὸν ποιῆσαι is joined as one idiom → to satisfy.

Joined units (rule 3): article + participle in 15:7, 15:29 (twice), 15:32,
15:35, 15:39, 15:41; ἀποκριθείς/ἀπεκρίθη with the speech verb (15:2, 15:9,
15:12); τιθέντες τὰ γόνατα → knelt down (15:19); ἦν ἐπιγεγραμμένη → read
(15:26); ἐστιν μεθερμηνευόμενος/-ον → means (15:22, 15:34); εἰς τί → why
(15:34); ἐξ ἐναντίας and ὅτι οὕτως (15:39); οὐ δύναται → cannot (15:31).

## Notable decisions

- Different readings (EVIDENCE shows the publisher Greek differs): 15:12
  "do you want" is `t` (the publisher's Greek has θέλετε, N1904 lacks it).
  15:20 "own" is `t`: it is the one extra publisher word. Same-sense
  differences are linked with a note: 15:1 ἑτοιμάσαντες (publisher
  ποιήσαντες) → devised; 15:22 μεθερμηνευόμενος (publisher -ον); 15:34 λαμά →
  lema and σαβαχθανεί → sabachthani (spellings); 15:46 κατέθηκεν (publisher
  ἔθηκεν) → placed and μνήματι (publisher μνημείῳ) → tomb.
- Name spellings (Πειλᾶτος, Ἡλείας) are the other counted differences; they
  are linked without a note (rule 8).
- Repeated objects follow Matthew 27: one Greek object linked to every English
  repetition (15:1, 15:15, 15:17 crown + it, 15:21, 15:46 the body of Jesus +
  it).
- 15:13, 15:14: οἱ (δέ) used as a pronoun → they; the verb keeps shouted.
- 15:47: ἡ Ἰωσῆτος → "the mother of Joseph", with "the mother" on the
  article, as Mark 1:19 and 2:14 put "son" on the article.

## Doubts for the peer

- 15:20: "own" is `t` as the extra publisher word. I could not see the
  publisher Greek; Matthew 27:31 links "His own" to αὐτοῦ.
- 15:21: the opening καί → Now and BSB's "and" is `t`. The other option keeps
  καί → and, with Now as `t`.
- 15:23: "There" is `t` and καί `s`, following Mark 9:2. The publisher had
  καί → There.
- 15:43: ἐλθών … εἰσῆλθεν are joined → went. The other option is ἐλθών as `s`.
- 15:44: πάλαι ἀπέθανεν → "this was so" (a pro-form for "already dead");
  αὐτόν is `s`. πάλαι could be `s` instead.
- 15:8: καθὼς ἐποίει → to keep his custom; αὐτοῖς is `s`.
- 15:17: αὐτῷ → His, with head `t`. The other option is αὐτῷ → His head.
- 15:31: μετά → and (the scribes become a co-subject); 15:1 has μετά `s`
  because its "and" belongs to a real καί.
- 15:32: one καί → "And even".
