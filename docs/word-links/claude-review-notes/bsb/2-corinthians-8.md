Readings: EVIDENCE shows a word difference from N1904 only in 8:5 and
8:19 (the other "differs" lines show 0 unmatched words on both sides, so
no reading is claimed there).
- 8:5: the unmatched word is probably ἀλλά, elided in BSB's Greek (ἀλλ’
  before ἑαυτούς). Same word, so ἀλλά = "but" with a spelling note.
- 8:19: BSB's Greek probably has σὺν τῇ χάριτι where N1904 has ἐν τῇ
  χάριτι. "with" also renders N1904's ἐν here (as KJV's "with this grace"
  from the same text), so ἐν = "with" with a note that BSB's Greek has a
  different preposition with much the same sense.
No cross-verse links.

Conventions followed: 1 Corinthians 15:1 for Γνωρίζω with ὑμῖν = "you"
(8:1). README rule 3 and 2 Corinthians 1:4 for article + participle units
(8:1 τὴν δεδομένην, 8:16 τῷ διδόντι, 8:19 and 8:20 τῇ διακονουμένῃ). 2
Corinthians 3:3 for ὑφ’ ἡμῶν joined (8:19, 8:20). 2 Corinthians 1:3 for
"of + Lord" with ἡμῶν = "our" (8:9). 1 Corinthians 9 for participle ὤν =
"though + was" with the subject `t` (8:9). Romans 5:3, 8:23, 9:10 for
"that" after "Not only"/"More than" as `t` (8:19). Romans 12:17 for
προνοούμενοι καλά: καλά = "what + right", "is" `t` (8:21). 1 Corinthians
11:34 for a neuter pronoun carrying "matter(s)" (8:10). Acts 10:25 for an
articular infinitive's article joining its infinitive (8:6, 8:10, 8:11).

adverbs ("even", "then", "yet", "only") and participle subjects as `t`.

## Notable decisions

- Key terms linked word by word: χάρις = "grace" (8:1, 8:6, 8:7, 8:9),
  "privilege" (8:4), "offering + gracious gift" (8:19), "thanks" (8:16);
  ἁπλότης = "generosity" (8:2); περισσεία = "abundant" (8:2); πτωχεία =
  "poverty" (8:2, 8:9); προθυμία = "eagerly"/"eagerness" (8:11, 8:12,
  8:19); ἰσότης = "equality" (8:13, 8:14); ἀπόστολοι = "messengers"
  (8:23). Supplied expansions ("act of", "of giving", "to help") are `t`.
  No interpretive notes.
- Names: Μακεδονία = "of Macedonia" (8:1, article `s`); Τίτος = "Titus"
  (8:6, 8:16, 8:23); 8:18 αὐτοῦ ("him") = "Titus" with a note.
- 8:2 publisher "the terrible" kept on πολλῇ (an English "the" for an
  anarthrous phrase, not wrong).
- 8:12 πρόκειται keeps "is#0 there" ("is present"): the "there" renders
  the verb, not an added place word.
- 8:14 the first clause has no verb: εἰς = "meet#0", "will#0" `t`; in the
  second γένηται = "will#1", εἰς = "meet#1" (publisher).
- 8:15 Ὁ / ὁ (pronoun articles) = "He who" / "he who"; "gathered" is
  supplied (`t`), as in the Exodus 16:18 wording.
- 8:16 Χάρις = "thanks" ("be" `t`, as 2 Corinthians 1:3).
- 8:19 ταύτῃ `s` (BSB "the offering", not "this offering").
- 8:21 "taking great care" kept on προνοοῦμεν (idiom for the verb).

## KEEP block (8:17)

Unchanged. No real error. Only a style point: Codex has τήν `s` with
"our" `t`; the majority convention would let the article carry "our"
(article used as a possessive). Not changed.

## Doubts for the peer

- 8:11 τοῦ θέλειν ("of the wanting") `s` with "you#1 began" `t`, since
  "began" does not mean "want". Linking τοῦ θέλειν = "you#1 began" with a
  note (recalling 8:10) is the alternative. καθάπερ … οὕτως joined as
  "just as + as#1"; splitting (οὕτως = "just as#0", καθάπερ = "as#1") is
  possible but the English order makes it fragile.
- 8:19 χάριτι linked to both "offering" and "gracious gift" (same role,
  appositional repeat). χάριτι = "offering" only with "gracious gift" `t`
  is the stricter option. ἐν = "with" under the reading note; `s` + `t` is
  the alternative if the peer judges ἐν and σύν different in meaning.
- 8:10 δίδωμι = "is#0 my" (the finite verb carries BSB's verb and person);
  δίδωμι = "my" with "is#0" `t`, or γνώμην δίδωμι joined, are
  alternatives. τοῦτο = "this#0" kept from the publisher although BSB's
  "this" points forward.
- 8:24 "the#2 reason" `t`; linking ἔνδειξιν = "proof + reason" (understood
  again) is possible, but "reason" is not "proof".
- 8:13 ἐξ `s` ("from equality" recast as "that there may be equality");
  8:5 διά = "because" and 8:8 διά = "in comparison to" are linked as
  shifted renderings of the same role; `s` + `t` would be the strict
  reading of "different meaning".
- 8:23 εἴτε = "As" with ὑπέρ = "for#0 + for#1"; joining εἴτε ὑπέρ = "As#0
  for" and leaving the second εἴτε = "As#1 for" is the alternative.
