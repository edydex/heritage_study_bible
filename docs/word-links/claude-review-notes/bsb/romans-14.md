# BSB Romans 14: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: all 23 (14:1-23). No KEEP blocks in this chapter. Final
check: `fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 23, "problems": 0}`.

100 of the 315 publisher `g` rows changed, in 21 of the 23 verses. Every
publisher row was checked, including the two verses whose rows stay
unchanged (14:9 and 14:12, where only the TODO tokens were resolved). No
"of the"/"to the"/"in the"/"for"/"by" now sits on a lone article. Articles
used as possessives carry "his/your" (14:4, 14:5, 14:13); τὸ/τὰ that turn
an adjective or genitive into a noun carry "what" (14:16, 14:19).

No Gospel parallel. Precedent used: Romans 6 (μέν `s` with δέ answering;
τοῦτο pointing ahead `s`; supplied possessives and objects `t`), Acts 11/14
(τῷ Κυρίῳ: "the" on the article, "to + Lord" on the noun), John 18:37 (εἰς
τοῦτο "For this reason" with γάρ `s`, as 1 Timothy 4:10), Galatians 4:10
("special" days `t`), Romans 5:18 / 9:16 (ἄρα = "So", οὖν = "then"), Acts
10:25 (articular infinitive's article joined to its infinitive).

## Publisher rows corrected

Lone articles carrying a preposition or other word:

- 14:4 τῷ "To his" → "his" (article as possessive); "To + master" on κυρίῳ.
- 14:8 τῷ "to the" (twice) → "the"; "to + Lord" on Κυρίῳ.
- 14:15 τῷ "by" → `s`; "by + eating" on βρώματι.
- 14:19 τά "what leads to" → "what"; "leads" `t`, "to" on εἰρήνης; the
  second τά "to" → `s` (BSB does not repeat "what"), "to + edification".

Article + participle units joined (rule 3): 14:1 τὸν ἀσθενοῦντα "him whose +
is weak", 14:2 ὁ ἀσθενῶν "another + who is weak", 14:3 (four times: "The one
who eats", "the one who + does", "the one who + does + eat", "the one who +
does"), 14:4 ὁ κρίνων "to judge", 14:6 ὁ φρονῶν, ὁ ἐσθίων, ὁ μὴ ἐσθίων ("he
who abstains", negative included because "abstains" holds it), 14:14 τῷ
λογιζομένῳ "anyone + regards", 14:18 ὁ … δουλεύων "whoever + serves", 14:20
τῷ … ἐσθίοντι "his eating", 14:22 ὁ μὴ κρίνων "the one who + does +
condemn", 14:23 ὁ διακρινόμενος.

Negatives split from verbs (do/must/let stay on the verb): 14:3 four μή
(were inside "does not", "must not belittle", "does not eat", "must not
judge"), 14:10 "do" moved from σύ to the verbs, 14:13 μηκέτι = "stop" (was
in "let us stop judging"), 14:15 οὐκέτι = "no longer", μή = "not" ("Do" on
ἀπόλλυε), 14:16 μή = "not" ("Do … allow" on the imperative), 14:20 μή =
"not" ("Do" on κατάλυε).

Supplied words that had been folded into Greek words (now `t`):

- Copulas and subjects: 14:14 "is" (nothing is), "it is", 14:18 "is", 14:20
  "is" (All food is), "it is", "be", 14:21 "It is", 14:22 "is" (Blessed
  is), 14:23 "is" twice (his eating is, that is).
- Objects, adverbs, adjectives: 14:2 "only" (vegetables), 14:3 "everything"
  twice, 14:5 "certain", "alike", 14:6 "special", 14:7 "alone" twice, 14:14

  14:20 "to let", 14:21 "to do", 14:22 "about such matters".
- Possessives/persons: 14:1 "his" (opinions), 14:15 "you" (what you eat).

Other corrections:

- 14:2 μέν = "For" → `s`; "For" `t`. μέν only answers δέ ("while"); there is
  no γάρ in this verse, and no reviewed chapter links μέν to "For".
- 14:7 "of us" in the second clause was folded into οὐδείς; it now links to
  ἡμῶν as understood again ("of us + of us").
- 14:11 ἐγώ "As surely as I" → "I"; the oath formula "As surely as" goes on
  ζῶ ("As surely as + live"), ὅτι introducing the oath `s`.
- 14:15 σου (second) had "your" of "your brother" and ἐκεῖνον only
  "brother"; now σου = "your" (your eating) and ἐκεῖνον = "your brother".
- 14:21 μηδέ "or to do anything" → "or"; ἐν ᾧ (ᾧ had "to cause") = "anything
  + to cause"; "to do" `t`.
- 14:22 σύ "your" and ἥν "about such matters" → σὺ … ἣν ἔχεις jointly
  "your"; κατά "between" and ἐνώπιον "and" joined as "between + and".
- 14:23 ὅτι "because his eating is" → "because"; "his eating" links to φάγῃ
  as understood again, "is" `t`.

## Notable decisions

- 14:6: the EVIDENCE line says BSB's Greek lacks one N1904 word here; the
  likely word is probably the καί before ὁ ἐσθίων (BSB has no "and"), but the
  review file only says BSB leaves it untranslated. οὐκ ἐσθίει = "does so"
  (pointing back to "abstains").
- 14:19: BSB's "let us pursue" follows a reading of the verb with that sense
  (probably the subjunctive διώκωμεν; N1904 has the indicative διώκομεν).
  Same verb, mood only, so it is linked with a note, like a voice change.
- 14:8: ἐάν τε … ἐάν τε: first τε `s` in both pairs (no "both"); the second
  τε = "and" (kept), and in "whether … or" the second ἐάν τε joined = "or".
  ἐσμέν "we belong to" kept: "to" is the English verb's own preposition.
- 14:13: ἀλλὰ … μᾶλλον joined = "Instead"; τὸ … τιθέναι joined = "to put";
  "any stumbling block" kept on πρόσκομμα ("any" as the negative form of "a").
- 14:20: διὰ προσκόμματος joined = "a stumbling block"; "to let" and "be" `t`.
- 14:9 and 14:12 left as the publisher had them; γάρ in 14:9 `s` because
  "For" renders εἰς (εἰς τοῦτο "For this reason").

## Doubts for the peer

  faith"); I kept the publisher's "him whose" on the article + participle and
  τῇ `s`.
- 14:22: whether σύ should join "your" or stay `s` beside the imperative
  "Keep"; I joined it with ἣν ἔχεις.
- 14:19: "leads" `t` with "to" on the genitive noun; the alternative is "leads
  to" `t` and the second "to" `t` too.
- 14:21: "to cause" on ἐν ᾧ ("by which") versus `t`.
