# BSB John 7: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: 53 (7:1-53). No verse was kept from an earlier review (the
draft had no KEEP blocks). Final check: `fromtext` →
`{"versesOk": 53, "problems": 0}`.

595 of the 731 publisher `g` rows are unchanged. 19 more only gained Greek
tokens from the TODO list, with the English unchanged (οὐ δύναται/δύνασθε =
cannot 7:7, 7:34, 7:36; ἀπεκρίθη … καὶ εἶπεν = replied/answered 7:16, 7:21,
7:52; ἐὰν μή = without 7:51; Διὰ τί = Why 7:45; Οὐχ … ἐστιν = Isn’t 7:25;
Μή … εἶ = Aren’t 7:52; Μὴ γάρ = How can 7:41; ἐν παρρησίᾳ = publicly 7:4;
τὸν νόμον = it 7:19; οἱ Φαρισαῖοι = they 7:32; οὕτως … ὡς = like 7:46). 26
rows were joined in pairs into 13 groups (article + participle units, ἀπ’/ἀφ’
ἑαυτοῦ idioms). The other 91 rows, in 40 verses, were corrected, split or
moved.

No Synoptic parallel. Conventions followed from John 2 and 3 (supplied "the"
before an anarthrous noun is `t`; πόθεν = where + from; ἀπεκρίθη καὶ εἶπεν as
one group), Luke 5:30 and 8:19 (a pronoun rendered "Jesus" is linked with a
note), Mark 6:3 and 6:4 (negative + copula contractions joined; "his own" on
αὐτοῦ). 7:53 opens the passage about the adulterous woman; N1904 prints it
(its first token carries the opening bracket) and BSB translates it, so it is
linked normally.

## Publisher rows corrected

Negatives and auxiliaries split off verbs:

- οὔπω carried the auxiliary: 7:6 and 7:8 ("has not yet" → not yet; πάρεστιν /
  πεπλήρωται = has + come), 7:30 (had + come), 7:39 (×2: ἦν = had + been;
  ἐδοξάσθη = had + been glorified).
- 7:1 οὐ = not, ἤθελεν = He did + want. 7:5 οὐδέ = even + not, ἐπίστευον =
  did + believe. 7:23 μή = not, λυθῇ = will + be broken. 7:26 οὐδέν = not +
  anything, λέγουσιν = they are + saying. 7:28 οὐκ = not (×2), ἐλήλυθα = I
  have + come, οἴδατε = do + know. 7:34, 7:36 οὐχ = not, εὑρήσετε = you will
  + find. 7:35 οὐχ = not, εὑρήσομεν = will + find. 7:19 οὐ = not, ἔδωκεν =
  Has + given. 7:45 οὐκ = didn’t, ἠγάγετε = you bring + in (the "in" was on
  αὐτόν). 7:46 Οὐδέποτε = Never, ἐλάλησεν = has + spoken.
- Auxiliaries on the wrong word: 7:15 οὗτος "did this man" → this man, οἶδεν
  = did + attain. 7:26 οἱ "Have the" → the, ἔγνωσαν = Have + recognized.
  7:35 οὗτος "does He" → He, μέλλει = does + intend. 7:47 ὑμεῖς "Have you" →
  you, πεπλάνησθε = Have + been deceived. 7:48 μή "Have" → `s`, ἐπίστευσεν =
  Have + believed. 7:51 ἡμῶν "Does our" → our, κρίνει = Does + convict.

Lone articles carrying prepositions, nouns or connectives:

- 7:4 τῷ "to the" → the; κόσμῳ = to + world. 7:13 τῶν "of the" → the;
  Ἰουδαίων = of + Jews. 7:37 τῆς "of the" → the; ἑορτῆς = of + feast.
- 7:2 ἡ (before σκηνοπηγία) carried "of" → `s`; σκηνοπηγία = of Tabernacles.
- 7:25 τῶν "the people" → the; Ἱεροσολυμειτῶν = people of Jerusalem.
- 7:35 τήν "the Jews" and τῶν "among the" → τὴν Διασποράν = the Jews are
  dispersed; τῶν = the; Ἑλλήνων = among + Greeks.
- 7:37 the second τῇ carried "and" (last and greatest) → `s`; "and" is `t`.
- 7:5 οἱ carried "own" → `s`; αὐτοῦ = His own (as Mark 6:4).
- 7:32 οἱ carried "When the" → the; "When" now on the καί between "heard …
  and sent", which BSB turns into a When-clause.

English-only words that a Greek word had claimed (now `t`):

- "there" (7:1 on Ἰουδαῖοι; 7:3 on καί "also", which is now `s`), "Jesus"
  (7:9, on ἔμεινεν), "and" joining a participle (7:15 λέγοντες), "It comes"
  (7:16, on ἀλλά, now `s`), "But" (7:22, on διά), "why" (7:23, the Greek is a
  yes/no question), "still" (7:28, on διδάσκων), "but" (7:29, on ἐγώ; no
  connective in the Greek), "only" (7:33, on Ἔτι), "given" (7:39, on οὔπω;
  "the Spirit was not yet"), "and" + "himself" (7:50, on ὤν), "it" (7:52,
  on ἐραύνησον).
- Supplied "the" with anarthrous nouns (as John 3:5, 3:23): 7:22 and 7:23 (×2)
  "the Sabbath", 7:23 "the whole man", 7:39 "the Spirit".

Other wrong anchors:

- 7:3 σοῦ carried "the" → τά = the; σοῦ and the relative ἅ are `s`.
- 7:21, 7:24: θαυμάζετε "you are all amazed" → πάντες = all,
  θαυμάζετε = you are + amazed. ἀλλά "and start" → and; κρίνατε = start
  judging; κρίσιν joins δικαίαν = justly.
- 7:17 ἤ "or whether" → or; πότερον = whether + whether (English repeats it).
- 7:18 τήν (attributive) "his" → `s`; ἰδίαν = his own.
- 7:27, 7:28: ἐστίν/εἰμί carried "from"; πόθεν now = where + from (×3).
- 7:33 Ἔτι "only", χρόνον "longer", μικρόν "a little while" → Ἔτι = longer,
  χρόνον μικρόν = a little while, `t only`.
- 7:36 λόγος "does He mean", ὅν "by", εἶπεν "saying" → τίς ἐστιν ὁ λόγος
  οὗτος is recast: ἐστιν ὁ λόγος οὗτος = does He mean, ὃν εἶπεν = by saying.
- 7:38 ζῶντος "of living" → living; ὕδατος = of + water.
- 7:41 γάρ alone carried "How can" → Μὴ γάρ = How can.

Joined units (rule 3): article + participle 7:16 (τοῦ πέμψαντος = from Him
who sent), 7:18 (×3), 7:28, 7:33, 7:38, 7:39, 7:49 (ὁ … γινώσκων = that does
+ know), 7:50; ἀφ’ ἑαυτοῦ = on his own authority (7:18) and ἀπ’ ἐμαυτοῦ = of
My own accord (7:28), where BSB adds a noun; in 7:17 ("on My own") the
publisher's split stays.

## Readings (EVIDENCE lines)

- 7:8: N1904 οὔπω ("not yet"); BSB's "I am not going up" follows a reading
  with a plain negative (probably οὐκ). Different meaning, so οὔπω is `s` and
  "not" is `t` with the reading reason; "am" goes with ἀναβαίνω.
- 7:9: N1904 αὐτοῖς ("to them") has no English counterpart (BSB's Greek
  probably has αὐτός). αὐτοῖς is `s`; "Jesus" is `t`.
- 7:19: ἔδωκεν = Has + given, noted as another form of the same verb (probably
  perfect δέδωκεν).
- 7:24: κρίνατε = start judging, noted as another form (probably present
  κρίνετε).
- 7:25: Ἱεροσολυμειτῶν linked with a spelling note (probably
  Ἱεροσολυμιτῶν).
- 7:34, 7:36: the second "Me" is `t`: BSB follows a reading with an extra word
  (probably με after εὑρήσετε).
- 7:53: ἐπορεύθη = went, noted as another form (probably plural ἐπορεύθησαν).
- 7:16 and 7:50 each show one extra publisher word (probably ὁ before
  Ἰησοῦς; τό before πρότερον) with no effect on the English; nothing claimed.

## Notable decisions

- 7:4: αὐτός keeps the publisher's "who" (BSB turns "and seeks himself to be
  in the open" into "who wants to be known publicly"); καί is `s`. εἶναι keeps
  "to be known" with a note; ἐν παρρησίᾳ = publicly.
- 7:6: δέ = "Although": BSB reorders the contrast into a concessive clause.
- 7:8: emphatic ὑμεῖς = "on your own" (publisher row kept, note added).
- 7:12: πολύς ("much murmuring") keeps "Many", with a note on the recast.
- 7:22: διὰ τοῦτο = because (BSB's reading of the clause); καί before ἐν
  σαββάτῳ is `s`, and the opening "But" is `t`.
- 7:26: Μή ποτε `s` (a hesitant question rendered as a plain one); the
  publisher's ἴδε = here and λαλεῖ = "He is, speaking" stay.
- 7:46: λαλεῖ in "as this man speaks" is `s` (BSB: "like this man").
- 7:48: the repeated ἐκ τῶν before Φαρισαίων is `s` ("of the rulers or
  Pharisees").
- 7:51: καί before γνῷ is `s` (BSB's purpose "to determine"), following John
  3:23.
- Question particle μή/Μή is `s` in 7:31, 7:35, 7:47, 7:48, 7:51; it is joined
  where BSB's English carries it (Μὴ γάρ = How can 7:41; Μή … εἶ = Aren’t
  7:52).

## Doubts for the peer

- 7:8: οὔπω `s` + "not" `t`. The alternative is οὔπω → "not" with a reading
  note, if "not" vs "not yet" counts as the same meaning. I kept them apart
  because this is the known variant that changes the sense.
- 7:4: αὐτός → "who" (publisher) vs καί → "who" (the καί that BSB turns
  into a subordinate clause, as with "When").
- 7:22: "But" `t` with διὰ τοῦτο = because. The other option is καί → "But"
  and διὰ τοῦτο → "because", if the peer reads BSB's "But" as the καί moved
  forward.
- 7:52: Μή … εἶ joined as "Aren’t" although BSB turns a question expecting
  "no" into one expecting "yes"; the other option is Μή `s` and εἶ = Aren’t.
- 7:15: "such learning" stays on γράμματα; "such" could be `t`.
