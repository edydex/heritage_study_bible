## Notable decisions

- "We" passages: we/us link to verb endings and pronouns throughout
  (21:1 ἡμᾶς = we, the subject of ἀναχθῆναι = sailed; 21:5 ἡμᾶς = our; 21:7
  Ἡμεῖς; 21:12 ἡμεῖς; 21:16 ξενισθῶμεν = us to stay; 21:17 ἡμῶν, ἡμᾶς;
  21:18 ἡμῖν). Participles keep the "we" BSB gives them (21:2 we boarded,
  21:4 We sought out, 21:5 we set out), as Acts 16:11.
- Names and places linked: Cos, Rhodes, Patara, Phoenicia, Cyprus, Syria,
  Tyre, Ptolemais, Caesarea, Philip, Judea, Agabus, Mnason, Cypriot,
  Trophimus, Ephesian, Egyptian, Tarsus (Ταρσεύς = from Tarsus), Cilicia.
- Pronouns BSB renames are linked: 21:12 αὐτόν, 21:27 αὐτόν, 21:33 αὐτοῦ,
  21:34 αὐτόν = Paul; 21:20 αὐτῷ = to Paul; 21:32 αὐτούς = the crowd;
  21:34 αὐτοῦ = the commander.
- Connectives BSB renders with another connective are linked: 21:7 καί =
  where, 21:24 καί = Then, 21:33 καί = Then, 21:26 τότε = So.
- 21:26 ἕως οὗ ("until") `s` and the "and" `t`: BSB coordinates the
  offering clause, which changes the meaning of the conjunction.
- 21:3 γάρ `s`: BSB turns the explanation into a where-clause; ἐκεῖσε =
  where.
- 21:8 εἰσελθόντες and παρ’ αὐτῷ `s`: "stayed at the home of Philip" covers
  both; the publisher's εἰς = at is kept.
- 21:40 αὐτοῦ `s` (BSB recasts "he having permitted" as Having received
  permission); κατέσεισεν τῇ χειρί = motioned; λέγων `s` (colon).

## Doubts for the peer

- 21:1 ἤλθομεν `s` ("sailed directly to Cos" absorbs "we came"); the
  alternative is ἤλθομεν = we (main clause) with ἡμᾶς = we of the
  participle clause.
- 21:11 "him" in "hand him over" is linked to τὸν ἄνδρα οὗ ἐστιν (owner of +
  him), as Acts 12:4; the alternative is `t`.
- 21:22 ἐστιν = should we do (publisher row kept, with a note on the idiom
  τί οὖν ἐστιν); the alternative is `s` + `t`.
- 21:27 οἱ `s` and "some" `t` (BSB makes "the Jews from Asia" indefinite);
  the publisher had οἱ = some.
- 21:34 δέ `s` and "and" `t`: the "and" joins the halves of the some …
  another idiom, not this sentence to 21:33.
- 21:35 συνέβη ("it came about") = had to, linked with a note rather than
  `s` + `t`.
- 21:32 οἱ = the people: an article used as a pronoun that BSB names (Acts
  3:5 precedent), not a lone article carrying a noun.
