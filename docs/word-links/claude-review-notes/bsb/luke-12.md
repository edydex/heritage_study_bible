# BSB Luke 12: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: 59 (12:1-59). No verse was kept from an earlier review (the
draft had no KEEP blocks). Final check: `fromtext` →
`{"versesOk": 59, "problems": 0}`.

165 of the 874 publisher `g` rows changed. 20 of them only gained unlinked
Greek tokens, with the English unchanged (for example Προσέχετε ἑαυτοῖς =
Beware 12:1, Διὰ τοῦτο = Therefore 12:22, οὐκ … ποῦ = nowhere 12:17, ἕως ὅτου
= until 12:50, ἀλλ’ ἤ = but 12:51, μή ποτε = Otherwise 12:58, οὐ μή = not
12:59). The other 145 rows, in 46 verses, were corrected, split or moved.
Matthew 10:19-20 and 10:26-36 were followed for "fear not" and the division
sayings (12:2-12, 12:51-53), Matthew 12:31-32 for the blasphemy saying
(12:10), Matthew 6:19-34 for "do not worry" and treasure (12:22-34),
Matthew 24:43-51 for the thief and the faithful servant (12:39-46), Matthew
16:2-3 for the weather signs (12:54-56) and Matthew 5:25-26 for the adversary
(12:58-59).

## Publisher rows corrected

Words supplied by English that had been folded into a Greek word (now `t`):

- "Jesus" sat on ἤρξατο (12:1), Εἶπεν (12:22) and Ἔλεγεν (12:54); now `t Jesus`. In 12:14 ὁ (the article as pronoun) = Jesus, with a note.
- 12:5: τό (of τὸ ἀποκτεῖναι) carried "you"; ἐμβαλεῖν carried "to throw you". Now τὸ ἀποκτεῖναι = have been killed, ἐμβαλεῖν = to throw; `t you` twice.
- 12:12: δεῖ "you should" → should; `t you` (δεῖ is impersonal).
- 12:18: Τοῦτο "This is what" → This; `t is what`. 12:21: οὕτως "This is how it will be for" → how; `t This is`, `t it will be for` (as Matthew 6:30 `t that is`).
- 12:19: εὐφραίνου "and be merry" → be merry; `t and` (the Greek list has no καί).
- 12:20: τῇ "very" (This very night) → `s`; `t very`. 12:7: καί "very" → even; ἀλλά "And even" → And; `t very` (as Matthew 10:30).
- 12:25: πῆχυν "a single hour" → a + hour; `t single` (Luke has no ἕνα, unlike Matthew 6:27).
- 12:29: μετεωρίζεσθε "worry about it" → Do + worry about; `t it`.
- 12:36: ὅμοιοι "will be like" → like, `t will be`; ἀνοίξωσιν "they can open the door" → they can open, `t the door`.
- 12:37, 12:43: μακάριοι/μακάριος "Blessed are/is" → Blessed; `t are`/`t is` (as Matthew 24:46). 12:37: παρελθών "he himself will come" → come; διακονήσει = he + will + wait on; `t himself`, `t and` (participle joined to the main verb).
- 12:38: εὕρῃ "finds them" → finds, `t them`; ἐκεῖνοι "those servants" → those, `t servants`.
- 12:42: διδόναι "to give them" → τοῦ διδόναι = to give; `t them`.

Words on the wrong Greek word:

- 12:2: δέ carried "There"; now ἐστίν = There is, δέ is `s`. οὐδέν = nothing + nothing (as Matthew 10:26); καί no longer carries "nothing".
- 12:4: τι carried "no"; now μή = no, περισσότερόν τι = more.
- 12:6: δύο carried "for two"; now ἀσσαρίων = for + pennies, δύο = two.
- 12:15: ἐστιν "does not consist" → does + consist; οὐκ = not.
- 12:23: τοῦ carried "more than" (body … clothes). Now πλεῖον = more + more, τροφῆς = than + food, ἐνδύματος = than + clothes (as Matthew 6:25).
- 12:24: διαφέρετε "valuable you are" → valuable + are; ὑμεῖς = you.
- 12:28: ὄντα "which is here" → which is, `t here`; οὕτως "that is how" → how, `t that is`; μᾶλλον "more will He clothe" → more. Θεός = God + He, ἀμφιέζει = clothes + will + clothe (as Matthew 6:30).
- 12:35: ὑμῶν carried "dressed for service" and οἱ carried "your". Now αἱ ὀσφύες περιεζωσμέναι = dressed for service, ὑμῶν = your, Ἔστωσαν = Be + keep, οἱ is `s`.
- 12:42: τῆς carried "servants" and θεραπείας carried "in charge". Now καταστήσει = puts + in charge, θεραπείας = servants, τῆς is `s`.
- 12:46: τό carried "a" (a place) → `s`; μέρος = a place (as Matthew 24:51).
- 12:48: δέ carried "From", and παρ’ αὐτοῦ carried the second clause's "from him". Now παρ’ = From (moved forward), δέ is `s`, αὐτοῦ is `s`, and αὐτόν (the one asked) = from him.
- 12:56: τοῦτον carried "the" and καιρόν "present time". Now τόν = the, καιρόν = time, τοῦτον = present.
- 12:57: καί carried "And"; now δέ = And, καί ("even") is `s`. τό carried "what is"; now τὸ δίκαιον = what is right.
- 12:58: ἀπ’ carried "with"; now μετά = with (your adversary), ἀπηλλάχθαι ἀπ’ αὐτοῦ = to reconcile. ὑπάγεις "you are on your way" → you are; ἐν τῇ ὁδῷ = on + your (article as possessive) + way.

Negatives and auxiliaries split off verbs:

- 12:4 μή "do not" → not, φοβηθῆτε = do + be afraid. 12:6 οὐχί "Are not" → not, πωλοῦνται = Are + sold. 12:10 οὐκ = not, ἀφεθήσεται = will + be forgiven. 12:11, 12:22, 12:29, 12:32: μή "do not" → not, verb = do + worry/be concerned/be afraid. 12:33 μή "that will not" → not, παλαιούμενα = that will + wear out. 12:39 οὐκ = not, ἂν ἀφῆκεν = he would + have let. 12:40, 12:46 οὐ = not, verb = you/he does + expect/anticipate. 12:2 ἀποκαλυφθήσεται/γνωσθήσεται = will + be disclosed/made known.

Prepositions on lone articles, "a" with its noun, rule 3 units:

- "of the"/"to the"/"than the" on an article → the; the preposition moved to the noun: 12:1 τῶν (Pharisees), 12:7 τῆς (of + head), 12:24 τῶν (than + birds), 12:30 τοῦ (of + world), 12:54 τοῖς (to + crowds), 12:56 τῆς (of + earth), 12:58 τῷ (to + officer). 12:1 τῶν "of" (thousands) → `s`; μυριάδων = of many thousands. 12:19 τῇ "to" → τῇ ψυχῇ μου = to myself.
- 12:16: τινός "of a certain" → a certain; Ἀνθρώπου = of + man. 12:33: "an inexhaustible" → an + treasure.
- Article + participle joined: 12:5 τὸν … ἔχοντα = the One who + has, 12:4 τῶν ἀποκτεννόντων = those who kill, 12:9 ὁ ἀρνησάμενος = whoever denies, 12:10 τῷ … βλασφημήσαντι = whoever blasphemes, 12:21 ὁ θησαυρίζων, 12:33 τὰ ὑπάρχοντα and 12:44 τοῖς ὑπάρχουσιν = possessions, 12:47 ὁ γνούς = who knows, 12:48 ὁ … ποιήσας = the one who + does.
- Other joins: Ἐν οἷς = In the meantime (12:1), πρὸς τὸ οὖς ἐλαλήσατε = you have whispered (12:3), ὃς ἄν = who (12:8), πότε ἀναλύσῃ = to return (12:36), ᾗ ὥρᾳ = at an hour (12:40, as Matthew 24:44), ὃν … αὐτοῦ = whose + him (12:43, as Matthew 24:46), ποιήσας πρός = follow (12:47), μὴ γνούς = unknowingly (12:48), Ὅταν … εὐθέως = As soon as (12:54).

## Notable decisions

- Readings: EVIDENCE shows a different publisher text in 12:4, 12:21, 12:22, 12:24, 12:27, 12:53, 12:54 and 12:56.
  - Same meaning, linked with a note: 12:4 ἀποκτεννόντων (probably a spelling of the same participle), 12:21 αὑτῷ (probably ἑαυτῷ), 12:24 οὔτε … οὔτε (probably οὐ … οὐδέ), 12:56 οὐ (probably οὐκ) and δοκιμάζετε = you + to interpret (probably οἴδατε δοκιμάζειν; "know how" is `t` as the extra word).
  - 12:27 differs in meaning: BSB "how the lilies grow: They do not labor or spin" (probably αὐξάνει· οὐ κοπιᾷ οὐδὲ νήθει) against N1904 "neither spin nor weave". νήθει = They do + spin, οὔτε … οὔτε = not + or, ὑφαίνει is `s`, and "grow" and "labor" are `t`, all with a reading reason.
  - 12:22: the publisher left the second "your" (body) unaligned and put "your life" on ψυχῇ, so the extra word is probably ὑμῶν after σώματι. τῇ = your (article as possessive), `t your#1` with the reading reason, τῷ is `s`.
  - 12:53 (probably τὴν before θυγατέρα) and 12:54 (probably τὴν before νεφέλην) add only an article; nothing is claimed. In 12:54 "a" goes with "cloud".
- Articles as possessives (majority convention): τῇ = your (12:22 life, 12:58 way), τό = their (12:42 portion, as Matthew 24:45). In 12:18 the final μου = my + my (grain and goods), and τόν/τά are `s`; in 12:35 the leading ὑμῶν = your (lamps).
- 12:8: ἐν (twice) is `s` and καί = also, ὁμολογήσει = will + confess (as Matthew 10:32).
- 12:15: τινί = one’s (publisher row kept), ἡ ζωὴ αὐτοῦ's αὐτοῦ is `s` ("English names the possessor of life once"), αὐτῷ = his.
- 12:19: Ψυχή (vocative "Soul") = You, publisher row kept with a note.
- 12:26: οὐδὲ … δύνασθε = you cannot do, as one group ("cannot" is one word; as Matthew 10:28 μὴ δυναμένων = cannot).
- 12:37: περιζώσεται = he will dress himself to serve and ἀνακλινεῖ = will have + recline at the table: the English expansions are kept inside the verb groups.
- 12:38: φυλακῇ = watch of the night (as Matthew 24:43); οὕτως = alert; the repeated ἐν τῇ is `s`.
- 12:42: the second article (ὁ φρόνιμος, "the wise one") keeps the publisher's "and", with a note, rather than an `s` + `t and` split pair.
- 12:45: τε = and#2, καί = and#3 (publisher rows kept).
- 12:52: ἀπό = From, τοῦ `s`, νῦν = now on (as Luke 1:48, 5:10).
- 12:59: οὐ μή = not, ἐξέλθῃς = you will + get out, ἐκεῖθεν `s`, καί = very (as Matthew 5:26).

## Doubts for the peer

- 12:22 and 12:27: the reading claims rest on the EVIDENCE line plus the publisher's alignment; which "your" in 12:22 renders the extra word is inferred, not seen.
- 12:42: ὁ (second article) = and. The alternative is `s` for ὁ and `t and`, which creates a split pair.
- 12:48: παρ’ = From with αὐτοῦ `s`. The alternative is to join παντί + αὐτοῦ = everyone.
- 12:15: τινί = one’s with αὐτοῦ `s`. One could instead link αὐτοῦ = one’s and make τινί `s`, or join both.
- 12:35: ὑμῶν = your on lamps, though in Greek it stands first and belongs at least to ὀσφύες; οἱ = your (article as possessive) with ὑμῶν inside the idiom is the alternative.
- 12:56: πῶς = Why (publisher row kept).
