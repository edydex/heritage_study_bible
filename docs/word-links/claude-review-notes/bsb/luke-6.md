# BSB Luke 6: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: 49 (6:1-49). No verse was kept from an earlier review (the
draft had no KEEP blocks). Final check: `fromtext` →
`{"versesOk": 49, "problems": 0}`.

176 of the 763 publisher `g` rows changed. 18 of them only gained unlinked
Greek tokens, with the English unchanged (for example ἐν σαββάτῳ = One Sabbath
6:1, ἀποκριθεὶς … εἶπεν = replied 6:3, οὐκ ἔξεστιν = is unlawful 6:2,
ἄν + optative = they might do 6:11, οὐ μή = not 6:37). The other 158 rows, in
42 verses, were corrected, split or moved. Mark 2:23-3:6 and Matthew 12:1-14
were followed for the Sabbath scenes (6:1-11), Mark 3:13-19 and Matthew
10:2-4 for the Twelve (6:12-16), and Matthew 5-7 for the Sermon (6:20-49).

## Publisher rows corrected

Words supplied by English that had been folded into a Greek word (now `t`):

- 6:1: ἤσθιον "eat them", ψώχοντες "rub them" → eat, rub; `t them` twice (τοὺς στάχυας is printed once).
- 6:4: ἔδωκεν "gave it" → gave; `t it`. 6:30: ἀπαίτει "demand it back" → do + demand + back; `t it`.
- 6:5: ἔλεγεν "Jesus declared" → declared; `t Jesus` (as Mark 2:27). 6:39: Εἶπεν "Jesus also told" → told; καί = also; `t Jesus`.
- 6:7: εὕρωσιν "Looking for a reason" → ἵνα εὕρωσιν = Looking for; `t a reason`; `t to see` (as Mark 3:2).
- 6:8: ἔστη "and stood there" → stood; `t and`, `t there`.
- 6:13: μαθητάς "disciples to Him" → disciples; "to Him" moved to προσεφώνησεν (called to Himself). ὠνόμασεν "He also designated as" → He + designated as; καί = also.
- 6:15, 6:16: "son" sat on Ἀλφαίου and Ἰούδαν; now `t son` (the genitive leaves the relationship unstated).
- 6:17: μαθητῶν "disciples was there" → of + disciples; `t was there` (the Greek lists the crowds without a verb); `t and` before stood; `t Jesus`.
- 6:20, 6:21: μακάριοι "Blessed are" → Blessed; `t are` (as Matthew 5:3-10).
- 6:32-6:34: ἐστίν "is that" → is; `t that`. 6:34: δανίσητε "you lend to those" → you lend; `t to those` (the relative ὧν has no antecedent).
- 6:35: "do good to them", "lend to them" → do good, lend; `t to them` twice.
- 6:38: ὑπερεκχυννόμενον "and running over" → running over; `t and` (no καί in the Greek list).
- 6:40: τόν "his" → `s`; `t his` (as Matthew 10:24). πᾶς "everyone who is" → everyone; κατηρτισμένος = who is fully trained.
- 6:45: πονηροῦ "evil treasure of his heart" → evil; `t treasure of his heart` (the Greek says only "the evil").
- 6:48: θεμέλιον "his foundation" → foundation; `t his`. 6:49: οἰκίαν "his house" → house; `t his`; ἀκούσας "hears My words" → hears, ποιήσας "act on them" → does + act on; `t My words`, `t them`.

Words on the wrong Greek word:

- 6:23, 6:26: κατὰ τὰ αὐτά was split, with αὐτά = "their" and αὐτῶν = "in the same way". Now κατὰ τὰ αὐτά = in the same way, αὐτῶν = their.
- 6:42: τό (second article) carried "out" of "take the speck out of your eye". Now ἐκβάλω = me take + out, ἐν = of. τοῦ carried "own" in "your own eye"; now σοῦ = your own (as Matthew 7:4-5).
- 6:43: πάλιν carried "does" in "nor does a bad tree bear". Now ποιοῦν = does + bear, and πάλιν ("again") is `s`. Οὐ … ἐστιν = No.
- 6:45: τό carried "things" and τῆς carried "of". Now ἀγαθόν = good things, καρδίας = of + heart.
- 6:47: ἐστίν "he is" → is; Πᾶς ("everyone") = he.
- 6:48: "dug down deep": καί is `s`, ἐβάθυνεν = deep. The genitive absolute γενομένης = When + came (δέ is `s`).

Negatives and auxiliaries:

- 6:3: Οὐδέ = not; ἀνέγνωτε = Have you + read; τοῦτο is `s`.
- 6:29: μή "do not" → not; κωλύσῃς = do + withhold. 6:30 the same.
- 6:37: μή "Do not" → not; κρίνετε/καταδικάζετε = Do + judge/condemn; οὐ μή = not; the verb keeps you will + be judged/condemned.
- 6:39: οὐχί "Will they not" → not; ἐμπεσοῦνται = Will they + fall. Μήτι is `s` (as Matthew 7:16).
- 6:44: συλλέγουσιν "are not gathered" → are + gathered; οὐ = not.
- 6:46: οὐ = not (it was unlinked).

Prepositions and "the" on a lone article, "a" with its noun, and rule 3 units:

- 6:2 τῶν, 6:5 τοῦ, 6:8 τῷ, 6:9 τῷ: "of the"/"on the"/"to the" → the; the preposition moved to the noun.
- 6:2 and 6:9: τοῖς/τῷ σαββάτῳ = the + on … Sabbath.
- 6:17: τοῦ λαοῦ "of" → `s`; λαοῦ = of + people. "a level", "A large", "a great" → "a" with place, crowd, number.
- 6:43: "a bad" → a + tree; bad.
- 6:1: ταῖς χερσίν "in their" + hands → ταῖς = their (the article as possessive), χερσίν = in + hands.
- 6:4: τῆς προθέσεως was folded into "consecrated bread" → ἄρτους = bread, προθέσεως = consecrated (as Mark 2:26).
- 6:33: τούς "to those who" → τοὺς ἀγαθοποιοῦντας = those who do good to; ἀγαθοποιῆτε = you do good to.
- Article + participle or adjective joined: 6:8 τῷ … ἔχοντι = with (as Mark 3:3), 6:15 τὸν καλούμενον = called, 6:18 οἱ ἐνοχλούμενοι, 6:20 οἱ πτωχοί = you who are poor, 6:21 οἱ πεινῶντες/κλαίοντες, 6:24 τοῖς πλουσίοις = who are rich, 6:25, 6:27 τοῖς ἀκούουσιν = those … who will listen and τοῖς μισοῦσιν, 6:28 (both), 6:29-6:30 τοῦ αἴροντος = if someone/anyone takes, 6:32 (both), 6:47 ὁ ἐρχόμενος = who comes, 6:49 ὁ ἀκούσας = the one who hears. No lone article now carries is/are or a preposition.

## Notable decisions

- Readings: EVIDENCE shows a different publisher text in 6:14, 6:17, 6:36, 6:45 and 6:46. 6:14 is a spelling of John (Ἰωάνην), linked under rule 8. 6:36 "just" and 6:45 the first "his" (heart) are `t` with "BSB follows a reading with an extra word here"; probably καθὼς καί in 6:36 and τῆς καρδίας αὐτοῦ in 6:45. 6:17 and 6:46 differ only in order or form, so no reading is claimed. In 6:45, N1904's final αὐτοῦ ("his mouth") is `s` because BSB says "the mouth".
- Sabbath scenes follow Mark/Matthew: ἐγένετο δέ frames `s` (6:1, 6:6, 6:12, as Luke 2), pronouns rendered "Jesus" linked with a note (6:1, 6:6, 6:7, 6:8, 6:12, 6:20), ψυχήν = life + it (6:9, as Mark 3:4), ἡ χεὶρ αὐτοῦ = it (6:10, as Mark 3:5), οἱ μετ’ αὐτοῦ (ὄντες) = his companions (6:3-4), οὐκ … εἰ μὴ μόνους = only (6:4, as Mark 2:26).
- 6:6: καὶ … αὐτοῦ = whose (the καί becomes the relative link). 6:10: ὁ δέ = He.
- 6:22: ὑμᾶς = you + you, the shared object of exclude and insult (as Matthew 5:11).
- 6:25: the second woe has no ὑμῖν in N1904; οἱ γελῶντες = you who laugh, `t to`. 6:26 has no ὑμῖν either: `t to you`.
- 6:31: ἄνθρωποι = them and αὐτοῖς = to others, exactly as Matthew 7:12; ἵνα and ὁμοίως are `s`.
- 6:41-42 follow Matthew 7:3-5: οὐ = fail, σοῦ = your own; ἰδίῳ = your own (6:41) and ἰδίου = its own (6:44). 6:42: τῷ ἀδελφῷ σου is `s` (this EN has no "to your brother"); "while" goes on the participle βλέπων.
- 6:48: διὰ τό (with the infinitive) = because; οἰκοδομῆσθαι = was + built. 6:49: ᾗ = that house (relative recast as a new sentence); τῆς οἰκίας ἐκείνης = its.

## Doubts for the peer

- 6:1 and 6:29 link an article as a possessive (ταῖς = their, τόν = your), but 6:40 follows Matthew 10:24 with `s` + `t his` for τὸν διδάσκαλον. Making all three `s` + `t` would be the uniform alternative. The publisher row τήν = "one" (6:29, "one cheek … the other") was kept.
- 6:13: "to Him" is linked to προσεφώνησεν as the sense "called to Himself"; `t to Him` is the alternative.
- 6:15-16: "son" is `t`; the publisher had it inside the name or genitive. 6:16 Ἰούδαν Ἰακώβου could be "brother of James", but BSB says "son".
- 6:47: Πᾶς = he. The alternative is `s` for Πᾶς and "he is" on ἐστίν.
- 6:4: καί 7 = "and" before ate, and καί 14 = "and" before gave, following BSB's reordered list.
- 6:42: ἐν = of (after take … out) and ἐν = from (after remove) are linked with notes; `s` is the alternative.
