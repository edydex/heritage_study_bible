# BSB Mark 1: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: 45 of 45 (1:1-45; the draft had no KEEP blocks). Final check:
`fromtext` → `{"versesOk": 45, "problems": 0}`.

48 publisher rows in 29 verses were changed because they were wrong or
over-claimed. Another 16 rows only gained a TODO token under rule 3 (article +
participle, periphrastic ἦν, fixed phrases), and 4 rows were reshaped only
because a neighbouring correction moved a word.

## Publisher rows corrected

Names and nouns supplied by English that had been folded into a Greek word (now `t`):

- 1:10, 1:16, 1:20, 1:21 (twice), 1:29, 1:35, 1:38, 1:41, 1:43: "Jesus" (or "Jesus and His companions") sat on a verb or participle; the Greek gives the subject only in the ending.
- 1:5: ἐξεπορεύετο "People went out" → went out; πάντες "from all" → all of. BSB turns the Greek subjects (Judean countryside, Jerusalemites) into "from …", so People and from are `t`.
- 1:22: ἐξεπλήσσοντο "The people were astonished" → were astonished.
- 1:16: ἀμφιβάλλοντας "They were casting a net" → were casting a net, `t They` (accusative participle with no pronoun; as in Matthew 4:18).
- 1:19: ἐν "were in" → in, `t were` (αὐτούς is still the object of εἶδεν).
- 1:31: προσελθών "He went to her" → He went to, `t her` (no object).
- 1:34: αὐτόν "who He was" → He; `t who`, `t was` (English recasts "knew Him" as an indirect question).
- 1:41: ἥψατο "and touched the man" → touched; `t the man`.
- 1:1: Ἀρχή "This is the beginning" → the beginning, `t This is`.

Words that render another Greek word:

- 1:2: πρό "ahead of You" → πρὸ προσώπου = ahead of; σου = You.
- 1:12: ἐκβάλλει "drove Jesus" → drove; αὐτόν = Jesus.
- 1:26: σπαράξαν "threw the man into convulsions" → threw + into convulsions; αὐτόν = the man. φωνῇ "with a loud shriek" → φωνῆσαν φωνῇ = with a … shriek; μεγάλῃ = loud. ἐξῆλθεν "and came" + ἐξ "out" → ἐξῆλθεν = came out; καί (between the participles) = and; ἐξ αὐτοῦ `s`.
- 1:30: λέγουσιν "they promptly told" → they + told; εὐθύς = promptly.
- 1:34: ἤφιεν "He would not allow" → He would + allow; οὐκ = not.
- 1:36: οἱ "his companions" → οἱ μετ’ = companions; αὐτοῦ = his.
- 1:40: καθαρίσαι "make me clean" → make + clean; με = me.
- 1:43: ἐξέβαλεν "Jesus promptly sent him away" → sent + away; εὐθύς = promptly; αὐτόν = him; `t Jesus`.
- 1:44: εἴπῃς "that you don’t tell" → you + tell; μηδενὶ μηδέν = don’t + anyone; `t that`. προσένεγκε "present the offering" → present; ἅ = the offering.
- 1:45: μηκέτι "could no longer" → no longer; δύνασθαι = could.
- 1:17: Δεῦτε "Come, follow" → Come; ὀπίσω = follow.
- 1:35: λίαν "while it was" and ἔννυχα "still dark" → ἔννυχα λίαν ("very much at night") = while it was still dark.
- 1:19: τῷ "a" → `s`; πλοίῳ = a boat (an article cannot render "a").
- 1:24: σοί "do You want" → the idiom Τί ἡμῖν καὶ σοί = What + You + with us, with study heads; `t do`, `t want` (as in Matthew 8:29).

Supplied "and" after a participle (now `t`, verb keeps its own words):

- 1:7 κύψας … λῦσαι ("to stoop down and untie": λῦσαι = to + untie), 1:14 κηρύσσων, 1:18 ἠκολούθησαν, 1:20 ἀπῆλθον (now ἀπῆλθον ὀπίσω = followed), 1:21 ἐδίδασκεν, 1:31 ἤγειρεν (now helped + up; αὐτήν = her), 1:35 (after ἀναστάς), 1:41 ἥψατο, 1:45 πολλά "and openly" → openly.

Wrong anchors for temporal words:

- 1:16: καί "As" → `s`; παράγων = As + was walking.
- 1:29: καί "As soon as" → `s`; εὐθύς = As soon as; ἐξελθόντες ἐκ = had left.
- 1:10: εὐθύς "As soon as Jesus" → As soon as (with the participle).
- 1:37: εὗρον "when they found" → they found; the second καί = when (BSB subordinates "found Him and said", as Matthew 26 does with "which").

## Notable decisions

- Rule-3 joins: ὁ βαπτίζων = the Baptist (1:4); τοὺς … ἔχοντας = who were, τοὺς δαιμονιζομένους = demon-possessed (1:32); ἦν διδάσκων = He taught (1:22); ἦν ἐπισυνηγμένη = gathered (1:33); Ὀψίας γενομένης = That evening, ἔδυσεν ὁ ἥλιος = sunset (1:32); ἐν τῇ ἐρήμῳ = there (1:13); εἰσελθὼν εἰς / εἰσελθεῖν εἰς = entered/enter (1:21, 1:45); ἀπῆλθεν ἀπ’ = left (1:42); πανταχοῦ εἰς = through (1:28); εἰς ὅλην = throughout (1:39); εἰς τοῦτο = that is why (1:38); ποιήσω … γενέσθαι = I will make (1:17); the resumptive οὗ … αὐτοῦ = whose (1:7); ἐξῆλθεν καὶ ἀπῆλθεν = slipped out (1:35).
- Pronoun rendered by a noun is linked: αὐτόν/αὐτῷ = Jesus (1:12, 1:30, 1:32, 1:40, 1:45), the spirit (1:25), the man (1:26); ὁ δέ = the man (1:45); Σίμωνος = his (1:16).
- Spelling differences linked as the same word (rule 8): Ἰωάνης/Ἰωάνην/Ἰωάνου = John, Ἱεροσολυμεῖται = Jerusalem, ἁλεεῖς = fishermen/fishers, ἐκαθερίσθη = was cleansed.
- Textual differences (only where EVIDENCE counts publisher words): 1:25 `t He said` (BSB follows a reading with λέγων). 1:27 `t one another` (BSB follows πρὸς ἑαυτούς), and N1904's αὐτούς is `s`. In the other verses where EVIDENCE counts publisher words, the BSB English is fully covered by N1904 words (several differences are only spellings), so no other reading was claimed.
- 1:19: τόν = son is linked (τὸν τοῦ Ζεβεδαίου, "the (son) of Zebedee") instead of `s` + `t son` as in Matthew 4:21, to avoid a split pair.
- Kept publisher rows that are free renderings but not wrong: ἔσθων = His food was (1:6), καί = with (1:6), ἀλλαχοῦ = on (1:38), τῆς = her and χειρός = by the hand (1:31), τά = their (1:18, 1:19).

## Doubts for the peer

- 1:8: "with" before the Holy Spirit is linked to N1904's plain dative Πνεύματι (as "with water" is to ὕδατι), although the publisher's Greek has ἐν there. `t with` is the alternative.
- 1:5: one English "all" is linked to both πᾶσα and πάντες. Linking only πάντες and giving πᾶσα an `s` is the alternative.
- 1:27: συνζητεῖν's συν- already means "with one another"; I still gave "one another" a `t` because the words come from the publisher's πρὸς ἑαυτούς.
- 1:41: αὐτοῦ is taken with χεῖρα (His hand), as the publisher row does; some read it as the object of ἥψατο ("touched him"), which would make "the man" linked and "His" `t`.
- 1:37: καί = when is a structural link; keeping "when" with εὗρον and giving καί an `s` is the alternative.
