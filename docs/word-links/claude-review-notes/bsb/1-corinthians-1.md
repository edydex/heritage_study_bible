Verses reviewed: all 31 (1:1-31). No KEEP blocks in this chapter. Final

110 of the 419 publisher `g` rows changed, in 26 verses. About a dozen of
these only absorb a TODO token into a joined unit (εἰ μή, ἵνα μή, ἐν παντὶ
τόπῳ, δι’ οὗ, κατὰ σάρκα, πᾶσα σάρξ, τὸ αὐτὸ λέγητε, article + participle).
Every publisher row was checked, including the five verses whose rows stay

Theological terms (κλητός, ἁγιάζω, χάρις, χάρισμα, σοφία, μωρία, σταυρός,
δύναμις, κήρυγμα, δικαιοσύνη, ἁγιασμός, ἀπολύτρωσις) are linked word by word
with no interpretive notes. The quotations in 1:19 and 1:31 are linked word
by word.

EVIDENCE differs in four verses, and only there is a reading claimed: 1:4
"my" `t` (BSB's Greek has an extra word, probably μου); 1:14 "God" `t`
(probably τῷ Θεῷ); 1:28 "and" before "the things that are not" `t`
(probably καί); 1:20 συνζητητής linked to "philosopher" with a
same-word-spelled-differently note (probably συζητητής). No cross-verse links.

Conventions followed: Romans 1:1 and 1:7 (κλητός = "called", "to be" `t`;
"and" with no καί `t`), Acts 20:32 (dative article: τῷ = "the", "to + word"
on the noun), Luke 16:8 and 20:34 ("of + age" on αἰῶνος, "this" on
τούτου), Acts 13–28 and Romans 5–8 (article as possessive = "our"/"its"),
Acts 22:3 (an explicit pronoun links to the repeated English pronoun),
Acts 16:33 and 13:13 (article standing for "those of X" carries BSB's
supplied head noun), Romans 12:16 and Philippians 4:2 (τὸ αὐτό + verb
joined), Philippians 1:18 (a discourse particle rendered "Yes" is linked),
John 15:2/15:4 (words understood again), and the brief's rules on
participles (no person), τε … καί, negatives and supplied copulas.

## Notable decisions

- 1:1 ὁ ἀδελφός: ὁ = "our" (article as possessive, majority convention);
  1:6 τό = "our", 1:21 τῆς = "its" likewise.
- 1:4 Θεοῦ = "He" and 1:21 Θεόν = "Him", 1:28 Θεός = "He", 1:29 Θεοῦ =
  "His": nouns BSB turns into pronouns stay linked.
- 1:9 δι’ οὗ = "who" and ἐκλήθητε = "has called you": BSB makes the passive
  active with God as the subject.
- 1:10 τὸ αὐτὸ λέγητε = "you agree together" (joined); καί = "so that"
  (connective rendered with a different connective); τῷ αὐτῷ and τῇ αὐτῇ
  `s`; the second ἐν `s`.
- 1:11 ἐδηλώθη = "have informed" (passive made active).
- 1:16 δέ = "Yes" (discourse particle rendered by a discourse marker).
- 1:17 κενωθῇ = "be emptied of its power" kept (publisher row; "of its
  power" is how BSB renders the verb's sense, not a separate object).
- 1:22 ἐπειδή `s` and the first καί `s` (no "both"); 1:24 τε … καί = "both
  … and" linked; 1:30 τε and the first καί `s` (no "both"; BSB uses a
  comma).
- 1:31 ἵνα = "Therefore" kept (connective rendered with a different
  connective).

## Doubts for the peer

- 1:2 "Lord#1" linked to Κυρίου as a word understood again; `t` is the
  alternative if "their Lord" is read as a separate appositive.
- 1:7 ὑμᾶς = "you + you": the second "you" could be `t` if the participle
  clause is read as having no owner of its own.
- 1:11 τῶν = "some from + household", Χλόης = "Chloe’s", ὑπό `s`.
  Alternatives: ὑπό = "from", or τῶν Χλόης joined as one unit (Acts 16:33
  style).
- 1:12 εἰμι = "follow" ×4: "follow" renders εἰμί + genitive of belonging.
  If that is judged a different meaning, εἰμι `s` and "follow" ×4 `t`.
- 1:17 λόγου = "words of" versus the publisher's σοφίᾳ = "of wisdom".
- 1:26 τήν `s` with "the time of" `t`; τήν = "the" is the alternative.
- 1:31 ἵνα = "Therefore" and 1:10 καί = "so that": both kept as
  connectives rendered differently; `s` + `t` is the alternative.
