## Formulas of the letters (the same in 3:1, 3:7, 3:14 and in 3:6, 3:13, 3:22)

- Καὶ τῷ ἀγγέλῳ τῆς ἐν X ἐκκλησίας γράψον: Καί = `s` (it only links the
  letter to the one before). τῷ = "the#0". ἀγγέλῳ = "To + angel" (the
  dative supplies To, as Revelation 1:4 ἐκκλησίαις and 1 Corinthians
  1:2). τῆς = "the#1". ἐκκλησίας = "of#0 + church". ἐν = "in". X is the
  name. γράψον = "write".
- Τάδε λέγει: Τάδε = "These". λέγει = "the#2 words of", with a note that
  "says" is rendered as a noun phrase. "are" = `t` (supplied copula).
  Putting "of" on λέγει keeps it off the subject in every letter, so it
  never lands on a lone article (3:14 ὁ Ἀμήν).
- Οἶδά σου τὰ ἔργα: Οἶδα = "I know", σου = "your", τά = `s`, ἔργα =
  "deeds". The explanatory ὅτι that follows in 3:1 and 3:15 is `s`.
- Ὁ ἔχων οὖς ἀκουσάτω …: ὁ ἔχων = "He who has" (joined, as Luke 14:35).
  οὖς = "an ear". ἀκουσάτω = "let him hear". ταῖς = "the#1".
  ἐκκλησίαις = "to + churches".
- Ὁ νικῶν is always joined: "he who overcomes" in 3:5, "The one who
  overcomes" in 3:12, and "the#0 one who overcomes" in 3:21.

## Publisher rows corrected

Lone articles carrying a preposition or "To":
- 3:1, 3:7, 3:14 τῷ "To the" (3:7: τῷ "To", ἀγγέλῳ "the#0 angel") →
  "the#0", and ἀγγέλῳ = "To + angel". τῆς "of#0 the" → "the#1", and
  ἐκκλησίας = "of#0 + church".
- 3:1, 3:7, 3:14 ὁ "of#1 the (One who)" → "of" moved to λέγει (see
  formulas).
- 3:6, 3:13, 3:22 ταῖς "to the" → "the#1"; ἐκκλησίαις = "to + churches".
- 3:10 τοῦ "of" → `s`; πειρασμοῦ = "of testing".
- 3:12 τοῦ (8, 23, 31) "of#0/#1/#3" → `s`, and each Θεοῦ = "of + God".
  τῆς (29) "of#2 the" → "the#3"; πόλεως = "of#2 + city".

Supplied words moved off Greek words (`t`):
- "are" in the "These are the words of" formula (3:1, 3:7, 3:14), off
  Τάδε. "is" in 3:7 ("the One who is holy"), off ὁ.
- "it" in 3:3 (off τήρει) and 3:12 (off the verb). "only" in 3:8 (off
  μικράν). "the#1 right" in 3:21 (off δώσω: "I will give to sit").
- 3:7 "What"/"what": these are supplied objects, so they are `t`. ὁ =
  "What" was wrong. ὁ ἀνοίγων = "He#0 opens". κλείων = "shuts", and its
  "He#1" is `t`, because a participle has no person.
- 3:7 "and#0" (holy and true) was on the second article ὁ. It is now `t`,
  and ὁ ἀληθινός = "true".

Over-claims and wrong owners:

- 3:9 καί "but", εἰσίν "are", ἀλλά "instead", ψεύδονται "liars" →

- 3:10 τηρήσω "I will also keep" → "will + keep"; κἀγώ = "I + also".

- 3:21 ὁ "who" and αὐτῷ "To the one" → ὁ νικῶν = "the#0 one who

## Notable decisions

- 3:9: διδῶ is broken off and taken up again by ποιήσω, so διδῶ is `s`.
  ἐκ (partitive "some from") keeps the publisher's "those who belong to".
  ἰδού = "Look at". The second ἰδού is `s`. ἵνα after make is `s` (bare
  infinitive "make them come"). The aorist ἠγάπησα = "love" has a note.
- 3:12: the resumptive αὐτόν (3) is `s`, because BSB makes "The one who
  overcomes" the object of make. ἡ καταβαίνουσα is nominative after the
  genitive Jerusalem, and its note says only that and how BSB renders it.
- 3:5 οὕτως = "Like them" (kept, with a note pointing back to verse 4).
  ἱματίοις is `s` ("in white"). The second ἐνώπιον is `s`.
- 3:15 ψυχρός = "one" and ζεστός = "the other" (kept, with a note).
  ὄφελον = "How I wish" (kept).
- 3:17: the opening causal ὅτι (it leads into verse 18) and the
  quotation ὅτι are `s`. So are the list καί before pitiful, poor, blind,
  and ὁ before wretched.
- Kept from the publisher: 3:1 ὅτι = "for" ("for being alive"), 3:3 πῶς =
  "what" and ποίαν = "when" with ὥραν = "the hour", 3:4 ὀνόματα =
  "people", 3:10 λόγον = "command" and ὑπομονῆς = "to persevere", and 3:18
  αἰσχύνη = "shameful". Each has a note.

## Doubts for the peer

- Τάδε λέγει: I split it into Τάδε = "These", λέγει = "the#2 words of",
  and "are" `t`. The other choice is to join Τάδε λέγει as a fixed formula
  = "These are the words of". Whichever you pick, it should be the same in
  all seven letters (Revelation 2 is not reviewed yet).
- 3:7 κλείων: I gave it "shuts" with "He#1" `t`, following Revelation 1:16
  ("He" `t` on a participle). But κλείων shares the article of ὁ ἀνοίγων,
  so "He#1 shuts" on κλείων could also be defended.
- 3:21 "the#1 right" `t`. The other choice is δώσω = "I#0 will grant the
  right", reading δίδωμι with an infinitive as "grant the right"
  (compare Matthew 20:15, where ἔξεστιν = "have the right").
- 3:9 καὶ οὐκ εἰσίν `s`. The other choice is καί = "but" (and "but" taken
  off ἀλλά), but then nothing renders the negative.
- 3:12 "it" `t` after "leave". You could instead treat it as the "out of
  (it)" that ἔξω implies.
