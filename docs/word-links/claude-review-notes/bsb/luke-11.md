# BSB Luke 11: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: 54 (11:1-54). No verse was kept from an earlier review (the
draft had no KEEP blocks). Final check: `fromtext` →
`{"versesOk": 54, "problems": 0}`.

181 of the 814 publisher `g` rows changed. 21 of them only gained unlinked
Greek tokens, with the English unchanged (for example Καὶ ἐγένετο = One day
11:1, ὡς ἐπαύσατο = had just finished 11:1, οὐκ … ὅ = nothing 11:6, ἐν εἰρήνῃ =
secure 11:21, σημεῖον οὐ = none and εἰ μή = except 11:29, Ἀποκριθεὶς … λέγει =
told 11:45). The other 160 rows, in 50 verses, were corrected, split or moved.
Matthew 6:9-13 was followed for the Lord's Prayer (11:2-4), Matthew 7:7-11 for
ask/seek/knock (11:9-13), Matthew 12:22-30 and 12:38-45 for Beelzebul, the
sign of Jonah and the returning spirit (11:14-26, 11:29-32), Matthew 6:22-23
for the lamp of the body (11:34-35) and Matthew 23 for the woes (11:39-52).

## Publisher rows corrected

Names and words supplied by English that had been folded into a Greek word (now `t`):

- "Jesus" sat on εἶπεν (11:2, 11:5), ἦν (11:14), λέγειν (11:29), λαλῆσαι (11:37), ἐβαπτίσθη (11:38); now `t Jesus`. Where Greek has a pronoun, the name stays linked to it with a note: αὐτόν (11:1, 11:27), αὐτός (11:17), αὐτοῦ (11:53).
- 11:7: "you anything" → you, `t anything`; κἀκεῖνος "And suppose the one" → And + the one, `t suppose`. 11:22: "the man trusted" → trusted, `t the man`; "and then" → and, `t then`. 11:44: "even noticing" → noticing, `t even`. 11:48: "build their tombs" → build, `t their tombs`. 11:51: "all of it will be charged" → it will be charged, `t all of`.
- "the door" (11:9, 11:10) is `t`, as in Matthew 7:7-8. "it finds the house" (11:25) → it finds, `t the house`, because the noun οἶκον is in 11:24.
- Clefts: 11:15 "It is by … that He drives out" → by + He drives out, `t It is`, `t that`; 11:47 "it was your … who killed" → your + killed, `t it was`, `t who`. 11:18: "you say that" → you say, `t that`.
- Supplied copulas: 11:27 "is the" (on ἡ) and "and blessed are" (on καί) → the + and; `t is`, `t are`. 11:28 οἱ "are those who" → `t are`. 11:31, 11:32 ὧδε "is here" → here, `t is`. 11:34 "is full of darkness" → full of darkness, `t is`. 11:36 "is full" → `t is`.

Negatives and auxiliaries split off verbs:

- 11:7 Μή "Do not" → not, κόπους πάρεχε = Do + bother; οὐ δύναμαι joined = I cannot. 11:8 "he will not get up" (on ἀναστάς) → οὐ = not, δώσει = he will + to provide, ἀναστάς = get up. 11:18 "can his" (on αὐτοῦ) → his, σταθήσεται = can + stand. 11:19 "do your" → your, ἐκβάλλουσιν = do + drive + out. 11:24 "does not find it" → μή = not, εὑρίσκον = does + find. 11:38 "Jesus did not first wash" → οὐ = not, πρῶτον = first, ἐβαπτίσθη = did + wash. 11:40 οὐχ "Did not" → not, ἐποίησεν = Did + make. 11:46 "will not lift" → οὐ = not, προσψαύετε = will + lift + to lighten. 11:52 "have not entered" → οὐκ = not, εἰσήλθατε = have + entered.

"When", "As" and supplied "and" moved off δέ/καί:

- 11:14 δέ "And when" → And; ἐξελθόντος = when + was gone. 11:27 δέ "As" → `s` (with Ἐγένετο); ἐν τῷ = As. 11:29 δέ "As" → `s`; ἐπαθροιζομένων = As + were increasing. 11:53 αὐτοῦ "As Jesus" → Jesus; ἐξελθόντος = As + went on.
- Supplied "and" after a participle (now `t`): 11:8 ἐγερθείς, 11:22 ἐπελθών, 11:26 εἰσελθόντα, 11:27 ἐπάρασα, 11:33 ἅψας, 11:37 εἰσελθών.

## Notable decisions

- Readings (only where EVIDENCE shows a difference): 11:1 Ἰωάνης = John and 11:30 Νινευείταις = Ninevites, 11:32 Νινευεῖται = of Nineveh: same name spelled differently. 11:2 ἐλθάτω = come and 11:15 εἶπαν = said: same form spelled differently (probably ἐλθέτω and εἶπον). 11:8 ἀναιδίαν = persistence: same word spelled differently (probably ἀναίδειαν). 11:11: μή is `s` with "BSB's Greek has a different word here" (probably καί, "and"); the English question renders neither. 11:24: `t Then` with "BSB follows a reading with an extra word here" (probably τότε before λέγει). 11:39, 11:41-43, 11:46-47 show "differs" with 0 unmatched words, so no reading is claimed there.
- Article + participle joined (rule 3): 11:10 (who asks, he who seeks, to him who knocks), 11:13 (to those who ask), 11:23 (He who is, he who does … gather), 11:27 (that bore), 11:28, 11:33, 11:40, 11:44, 11:50, 11:51, 11:52. Matthew 7:8, 7:11 and 12:30 split these; Luke follows the rule instead.
- Generic article rendered "a/an" is linked to the article, as Matthew 12:43 does for the same Greek (11:21 ὁ ἰσχυρός, 11:24 τὸ … πνεῦμα and τοῦ ἀνθρώπου, 11:36 ὁ λύχνος). Article rendered as a possessive is linked with a note (11:7 My, 11:11 his, 11:13 your, 11:34 your, 11:46 their).
- 11:14: the episode-opening Καί stays linked to "One day" (publisher row) with a note; leaving it `s` with `t One day` would be a split pair.
- 11:29: "This is a wicked generation": αὕτη = This; the subject's Ἡ γενεά is `s`, and the second γενεά = a + generation.
- 11:24: μου and ὅθεν are `s` ("the house I left"), as in Matthew 12:44; "rest + it" also follows Matthew 12:43.
- 11:42 follows Matthew 23:23 for the tithe woe (ἔδει = You should have; μή = without; παρεῖναι = neglecting); the opening ἀλλά is `s`.
- 11:8: δώσει = he will + to provide, because the future and the subject belong to δώσει, not to the participle ἀναστάς (= get up).
- No cross-verse links were needed.

## Doubts for the peer

- 11:1: ἐν τῷ εἶναι = "where" (BSB's "in a place where Jesus had just finished praying"). The alternative is `s` for ἐν τῷ εἶναι with `t where`.
- 11:46: προσψαύετε ("you touch") = will + lift + to lighten. The alternative is to leave "to lighten" as an English expansion (`t`), as Matthew 23:4 does with "to lift".
- 11:5: Τίς = "Suppose one" kept from the publisher; "Suppose" could instead be `t` for the recast question.
- 11:7: "suppose" is `t`; linking it to the subjunctive εἴπῃ (with ἀποκριθεὶς … = answers) is the alternative.
- 11:36: ἔσται = "you will be" (BSB turns "it [the body] will be" into "you"); φωτίζῃ … τῇ ἀστραπῇ joined = were shining (τῇ `s`).
- 11:49: ἐξ = some of + others (one partitive divided by BSB); `t others` is the alternative.
- 11:39: δέ = "then" in "Now then" kept from the publisher (Νῦν = Now); joining Νῦν = Now then with δέ `s` is the alternative.
