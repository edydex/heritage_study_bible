## Publisher rows corrected

English-only words folded into Greek words (now `t`):

- 9:1 "Jesus was passing by", 9:6 "When Jesus had said": `t Jesus`; the participles keep as/When + the verb. 9:7 ἀπῆλθεν "the man went" → went, `t the man`.
- 9:3 ἵνα "this happened so that" → so that; `t this happened` (elided main clause after ἀλλ’ ἵνα).
- 9:9 "but others" → others, `t but`; "he just looks" → he + looks, `t just`; "But the man" → the man, `t But`; εἰμι "am the one" → am, `t the one`.
- 9:15 καί "and now" → and, `t now`. 9:17 σου "it was your" → your, `t it was` (cleft). 9:19 πῶς "how is it that" → how, `t is it that` (as Galatians 4:9).
- 9:22 Χριστόν "as the Christ" → Christ, `t as the`. 9:23 διὰ τοῦτο "That was why" → That + why, `t was`. 9:25 ἕν "There is one thing" → one thing, `t There is`; ἄρτι "but now" → now, `t but` (the "but" joins the concessive participle ὤν to the main verb).
- 9:27 "to hear it" → to hear, `t it`. 9:35 εἶπεν "and said" → said, `t and` (participle εὑρών + main verb). 9:36 ἵνα "Tell me so that" → so that, `t Tell me`. 9:37 "You have already seen" → You have + seen, `t already`.
- 9:11 "and received my sight" → received my sight, `t and` (participle clause + main verb).
- Supplied "the" with an anarthrous noun (as John 3): 9:5 "the light", 9:14 "the day", 9:32 "the eyes".

Negatives, auxiliaries and pronouns split off verbs:

- 9:17 σύ "do you" → you, λέγεις = do + say. 9:27 ὑμεῖς "Do you" → you, θέλετε = Do + want. 9:35 σύ "Do you" → you, πιστεύεις = Do + believe.
- 9:10 σου "were your" → your, ἠνεῴχθησαν = were + opened. 9:19, 9:21 βλέπει "he can now see" → he can + see, νῦν/ἄρτι = now.
- 9:22 ἤδη = already split from "had already determined"; 9:27 ἤδη split from "I already told".
- 9:32 τις "has anyone" → anyone; "has" moved to ἠκούσθη (has + heard of).
- 9:41 εἴχετε "you would not be" → οὐκ = not, ἄν = would, εἴχετε = you + be guilty of, ἁμαρτίαν = sin.

Articles, prepositions and connectives on the wrong word:

## Readings

- Only the EVIDENCE lines of 9:2, 9:6, 9:16, 9:26, 9:28 and 9:40 show publisher Greek different from N1904.
- 9:2: Rabbi linked with a spelling note (probably Ῥαββί).
- 9:6: applied linked to ἐπέθηκεν ("put on") with a note that BSB's Greek has a different verb of similar meaning (probably ἐπέχρισεν, "smeared on", as in 9:11).
- 9:16: `t But` with the reading reason (one extra publisher word, probably δέ after ἄλλοι).
- 9:26, 9:28, 9:40: "they asked" / "said" linked to εἶπαν with a note that BSB's Greek has another form of the verb (probably εἶπον).

## Notable decisions

- 9:6: ἐκ τοῦ πτύσματος ("from the saliva") `s`: BSB says only "made some mud". τὸν πηλόν joined = it.
- 9:9: ἀλλά after Οὐχί `s` and "just" `t`; "just" does not mean "but".
- 9:13: ποτε = had been, noted as "once, formerly" expressed by the pluperfect; αὐτόν = the man, τόν = who (publisher rows kept).
- 9:21: the first οὐκ οἴδαμεν `s` (BSB joins both questions and says "we do not know" once); αὐτός = He kept (explicit pronoun).
- 9:22: ἀποσυνάγωγος γένηται kept as "put out of the synagogue" + "would be", noted as one Greek adjective.
- 9:32: τις = anyone, noted that in Greek it is the one who opens while BSB makes anyone the hearer.
- 9:33: οὐκ … οὐδέν = no such thing (double negative, one English negative).
- 9:36: Κύριε = Sir, noted that BSB has Lord in 9:38.
- 9:39: οἱ μὴ βλέποντες = the blind (one unit; English has no negative word).
- 9:41: νῦν = since (logical "as it is"), ἁμαρτία = guilt, both noted.

## Doubts for the peer

- 9:31: I moved "the one" to τούτου and made ἐάν τις = who. The publisher had τούτου = the and τις = one who; that alternative is defensible.
- 9:37: "already" `t` and both καί `s`. "already" might be BSB's way of rendering the first καί ("indeed") or the perfect ἑώρακας.
- 9:41: εἴχετε = "be guilty of", ἁμαρτίαν = sin. The idiom could instead be one group, εἴχετε ἁμαρτίαν = "be guilty of sin".
- 9:33: "such" is inside the joined group "no such thing"; a stricter reading would make it `t`.
- 9:13: ποτε = "had been"; an alternative is `t had been` and ποτε `s`.
- 9:32: ὅτι `s`, with "of" kept in "heard of"; ὅτι could instead take "of".
- 9:16: οὖν = "Because of this" kept from the publisher; it is a strong causal rendering of a plain connective.
