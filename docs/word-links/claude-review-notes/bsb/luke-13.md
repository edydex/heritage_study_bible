# BSB Luke 13: author review (Claude)

Verses reviewed: 35 (13:1-35). No verse was kept from an earlier review (the
draft had no KEEP blocks). Final check: `fromtext` →
`{"versesOk": 35, "problems": 0}`.

120 of the 534 publisher `g` rows changed. 18 of them only gained unlinked
Greek tokens, with the English unchanged (for example ἀποκριθεὶς εἶπεν = He
replied 13:2; ἐὰν μή = unless 13:3, 13:5; δέκα ὀκτώ = eighteen 13:4; ἵνα τί =
Why 13:7; ἕως ὅτου / ἕως οὗ = until 13:8, 13:21; ἀπεκρίθη … καὶ εἶπεν =
replied 13:15; ἐγένετο εἰς = became 13:19; ἀφ’ οὗ ἄν = After 13:25; ὃν τρόπον
= as 13:34). The other 102 rows, in 32 verses, were corrected, split or
moved.

Parallels followed: Matthew 7:13-14, 7:22-23 and 25:11-12 for the narrow door
(13:24-27); Matthew 8:11-12 for 13:28-29; Matthew 19:30, 20:16 and Mark 10:31
for 13:30; Mark 4:30-32 and Matthew 13:31-32 for the mustard seed (13:18-19);
Matthew 13:33 for the leaven (13:20-21); Matthew 23:37-39 for the lament
(13:34-35).

## Publisher rows corrected

Names and words supplied by English that had been folded into a Greek word (now `t`):

- "Jesus" sat on Ἔλεγεν (13:6, 13:18), Ἦν (13:10), διεπορεύετο (13:22), εἶπεν (13:32) and προσῆλθαν "came to Jesus" (13:31); now `t Jesus`. 13:23: εἶπεν "Jesus answered" → answered; ὁ (of ὁ δέ) = Jesus.
- 13:29: ἥξουσιν "People will come" → will come; `t People`. 13:2: Γαλιλαίους "other Galileans" → Galileans; `t other`. 13:22: πόλεις "the towns" → towns; `t the` (no Greek article).
- Objects: 13:6 εὗρεν "did not find any" and 13:7 εὑρίσκω "haven’t found any" → `t any`. 13:7 ἔκκοψον, 13:9 ἐκκόψεις "cut it down" → cut + down, αὐτήν = it. 13:8 ἄφες "leave it alone" → leave + alone, αὐτήν = it; κόπρια "fertilize it" → βάλω κόπρια = fertilize, `t it`. 13:15 ἀπαγαγών "lead it" → lead, `t it`. 13:32 ἰάσεις "healing people" → ἰάσεις ἀποτελῶ = healing, `t people`.
- 13:35: ἀφίεται "is left to you desolate" → is left; ὑμῖν = to you; `t desolate`. με "Me again" → Me, `t again`; ὑμῖν "you that" → you, `t that`; Εὐλογημένος "Blessed is" → Blessed, `t is` (as Matthew 23:38-39).

Negatives and auxiliaries split off verbs:

- 13:6: οὐχ = not, εὗρεν = did + find. 13:7: οὐχ εὑρίσκω = haven’t found (the negative is inside the contraction).
- 13:15: οὐ = not, λύει = Does + untie. 13:24: οὐκ = not, ἰσχύσουσιν = will + be able. 13:25, 13:27: Οὐκ = not, οἶδα = I do + know. 13:33: οὐκ = not, ἐνδέχεται = it is + admissible. 13:35: οὐ μή = not, ἴδητε = you will + see (as Matthew 23:39).
- 13:3, 13:5: ἀπολεῖσθε "you too will all perish" → you + will + perish; πάντες = all; ὁμοίως / ὡσαύτως = too.

"When" on the wrong word:

- 13:12: δέ "When" → `s`; ἰδών = When + saw. 13:17: καί "When" → `s`; λέγοντος = When + said.
- 13:4: ὁ "when the" → the; "when" now sits on the καί between ἔπεσεν and ἀπέκτεινεν, the two clauses BSB recasts as "were killed when … collapsed".

Prepositions on articles or on the wrong word:

- 13:10 τῶν "of the" → the, συναγωγῶν = of + synagogues. 13:19 τοῦ "of the" → the, οὐρανοῦ = of + air. 13:28 τῶν "of" → `s`, ὀδόντων = of teeth (as Matthew 8:12).
- 13:14 τῷ and τῇ "on the" → the; σαββάτῳ = on + Sabbath; τῇ ἡμέρᾳ τοῦ σαββάτου → ἡμέρᾳ τοῦ σαββάτου = on + Sabbath. 13:15 τῷ "on the" → the, σαββάτῳ = on + Sabbath. 13:16 τῇ "on the" → the, ἡμέρᾳ = on + day. 13:32 τῇ "on the" → the, τρίτῃ = on + third day.
- 13:11, 13:16: δέκα "for eighteen" → δέκα (καὶ) ὀκτώ = eighteen; ἔτη = for + years (accusative of time).
- 13:12: σου "from your" → your; ἀσθενείας = from + disability.
- 13:2, 13:4: πάντας "than all" → all; παρά = worse / more + than.
- 13:25, 13:27: ἐστέ "are from" → are; πόθεν = where + from.
- 13:26: σου "with you" → you; ἐνώπιον = with.
- 13:33: ἐνδέχεται "it is not admissible for" → it is + admissible; προφήτην = for a prophet.
- 13:34: ὑπό "under her" → under; τάς = her (as Matthew 23:37).

Other words on the wrong Greek word:

- 13:9: τό "next year" and μέλλον "fine" → εἰς τὸ μέλλον = next year; `t fine` (the Greek leaves the "if it bears fruit" clause unfinished).
- 13:10: δέ "One" → `s`; ἐν τοῖς σάββασιν = One Sabbath.
- 13:12: προσεφώνησεν "He called her over" → He called + over; αὐτῇ (the dative of εἶπεν, which BSB moves to "called over") = her.
- 13:8: λέγει "the man replied" → ὁ = the man; ἀποκριθεὶς λέγει = replied.
- Supplied "and" after a participle (now `t`): 13:14 θεραπεύεσθε "and be healed", 13:21 ἔκρυψεν "and mixed", 13:25 λέγοντες "and saying", 13:31 λέγοντες "and told".
- 13:21: οὗ "of it" → ἕως οὗ = until; `t of it` (as Matthew 13:33).
- 13:19: σινάπεως "a mustard seed" → mustard; κόκκῳ = a + seed (as Mark 4:31; see doubts).
- 13:22: πορείαν "His way" and ποιούμενος "as He made" → πορείαν ποιούμενος = He made His way; the καί between the participles = as.
- 13:23: εἰ "will" → `s` (it only marks the question); οἱ σῳζόμενοι = will + people be saved.
- 13:25: ἄνοιξον "open the door" → open; τὴν θύραν (after κρούειν) = the door.
- 13:27: πάντες "all you" → all; ἐργάται ἀδικίας = you evildoers (vocative "you"); ἐστέ = you are (see Readings).
- 13:30: εἰσίν "some who are" (×2) → some + are; οἵ = who (×2).
- 13:34: ἡ ἀποκτείνουσα = who kills and τοὺς ἀπεσταλμένους = those sent joined (as Matthew 23:37); τέκνα "children together" → children, ἐπισυνάξαι = to gather + together + gathers; ὄρνις "a hen gathers" → a hen.
- 13:35: ὁ ἐρχόμενος joined = He who comes (as Matthew 23:39).
- 13:7: ἀφ’ οὗ joined = for the past.

## Readings

- Only the EVIDENCE lines of 13:1, 13:2, 13:5, 13:7, 13:13 and 13:27 show publisher Greek different from N1904.
- Name spelling (rule 8): Pilate 13:1.
- Same meaning, linked with a note (rule 9): 13:5 μετανοήσητε = you repent (probably μετανοῆτε in BSB's Greek); 13:13 ἀνορθώθη = she straightened up (probably the augmented spelling ἀνωρθώθη).
- Longer readings: 13:7 "Therefore" `t` (one extra publisher word, probably οὖν). 13:27 has one extra publisher word, probably ὑμᾶς as in 13:25. The English "you" in "where you are from" is still the subject of ἐστέ, so it is linked there with a note instead of `t` (see doubts).
- 13:2 differs without unmatched words (probably order); no reading is claimed.
- 13:35 "desolate" is `t` without a reading claim: the EVIDENCE line says the publisher's Greek equals N1904 here (unlike Matthew 23:38).

## Notable decisions

- Pronouns rendered by names, linked with a note: αὐτῷ = Jesus (13:1), αὐτοῦ = Jesus (13:17), ὁ (δέ) = Jesus (13:23); ὁ = the man (13:8).
- ἰδού kept as BSB renders it: there (13:11), long (13:16), indeed (13:30), Look (13:7, 13:32, 13:35).
- ὀφειλέται = sinful and ἀνθρώπους = others (13:4); ταῦτα = this fate (13:2); αὐταῖς = those days (13:14); τούτου = her (13:16); ποτίζει = to water (13:15).
- 13:14: ἐν αἷς = for, with δεῖ `s` (BSB "six days for work"); ἀποκριθείς and recitative ὅτι `s`.
- 13:21 follows Matthew 13:33 row for row.
- 13:34: Luke has no second verb (Matthew 23:37 has ἐπισυνάγει), so English "gathers" is linked to ἐπισυνάξαι as the verb understood again. ἑαυτῆς = her, νοσσιάν = chicks, τήν `s`.
- 13:35: ἕως = until; ἥξει ὅτε `s` (BSB condenses "until the time comes when").
- 13:25, 13:26: ἄρξησθε / ἄρξεσθε ("begin") = you will; BSB does not render "begin".

## Doubts for the peer

- 13:19: κόκκῳ = a + seed, σινάπεως = mustard (as Mark 4:31 and the "a goes with the noun" convention). Matthew 13:31 and 17:20 put "a mustard" on σινάπεως; one of the two should probably be aligned.
- 13:25: τὴν θύραν, the object of κρούειν, is linked to "the door" after "open" to avoid a split pair. Matthew 25:11, where the Greek has no door, uses `t the door`. The alternative is `s` plus `t`.
- 13:11: εἰς τὸ παντελές = straight (publisher's choice; "fully upright"), with ἀνακύψαι = stand up. The alternative is ἀνακύψαι = stand up straight and εἰς τὸ παντελές `s`.
- 13:27: "you#1 are" on ἐστέ despite the extra publisher word. The alternative is `t you#1` with the reading reason.
- 13:34: "gathers" on ἐπισυνάξαι (understood again). The alternative is `t gathers`.
- 13:35: ἥξει ὅτε `s`. The alternative is joining ἕως ἥξει ὅτε = until.
- 13:4: οὓς = who and αὐτούς = them kept from the publisher, although BSB's passive recast swaps their roles ("who were killed … on them").
- 13:30: εἰσίν = some + are and οἵ = who; BSB's "who" attaches to "are last", the Greek οἵ to "will be first".
- 13:23: οἱ σῳζόμενοι = will + people be saved. The alternative is `t will`.
- 13:1: τινες = "some of those" kept from the publisher; "of those" could be `t`.
