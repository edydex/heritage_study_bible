## Publisher rows corrected

- Participle "and": the English "and" only joins a participle to the main
  verb, so it is now `t` and the verb keeps its own word: "and heal them"
  (6:5), "and preached" (6:12), "and asked" (6:24), "and saw" (6:34),
  "and said" (6:35), "and buy" (6:36), "and spend" (6:37), "and looking up"
  (6:41, between two participles), "and ran through" (6:55). Also "and see"
  (6:38, two imperatives without καί) and "and imprisoned" (6:17).
- Negatives folded into verbs, now linked on their own: "He could not" (6:5),
  "will not welcome" (6:11), "It is not lawful" (6:18, οὐκ = not, ἔξεστιν =
  It is + lawful), "he did not want" (6:26), "they did not even have time"
  (6:31, οὐδέ = not even), "they had not understood" (6:52), "Do not be
  afraid" (6:50). Joint units instead: οὐχ … ἐστιν = Isn’t and οὐκ εἰσίν =
  Aren’t (6:3), Οὐκ … εἰ μή = Only (6:4), μὴ ἔχοντα = without (6:34),
  οὐκ ἠδύνατο = had been unable (6:19).
- καί/δέ carrying "When" of a participle or genitive absolute: 6:2, 6:16
  ("But when"), 6:20, 6:22, 6:29, 6:34, 6:47, 6:53. "When" now goes with
  the participle. 6:49: οἱ δέ = they, ἰδόντες = when + saw.
- Lone articles carrying a preposition: τῶν = "over" (6:7), τοῖς = "On" and
  "for" (6:21), τῷ = "to the" (6:22, 6:28), τοῖς = "to" (6:41), τῶν/τῆς =
  "of the" (6:15, 6:47, 6:48). The preposition now goes with the dative or
  genitive noun. 6:17 τόν = "that" → "that" goes with "had ordered"; 6:4
  τῇ = "own" → "his own" = αὐτοῦ; 6:35 οἱ = "So the" → the; 6:28 τήν =
  "John’s" → αὐτοῦ = John’s; 6:28 and 6:56 αὐτῆς/αὐτοῦ = "to her" / "of
  His" → her / His, with to/of on the noun.
- English-only words claimed by a Greek word, now `t`: Jesus (6:1, 6:7, 6:34,
  6:39, 6:41, 6:45, 6:48), Herod (6:17), the man (6:28), the girl (6:25),
  the people (6:12, 6:54), the disciples (6:43, 6:51), Him (6:2), them
  (6:5, 6:13, 6:33, 6:41 twice), it (6:22), this / about this (6:14, 6:16,
  6:29), the people as object (6:6), something (6:37), all of (6:37), of
  Him (6:45), the boat (6:53), with Me (6:31), her (6:21), words (6:20),
  of old (6:15), of bread (6:43), from the dead (6:16).
- Supplied copula or verb, now `t`: did, get, is, how can (6:2); is in
  "That is why" (6:14); He is (6:15); was in "knowing that he was" (6:20);
  This, is (6:35).
- Wrong tokens:

    and διὰ τῶν χειρῶν … γινόμεναι = perform, instead of γινόμεναι = "He

  - 6:7 προσκαλεῖται = called + to Him, instead of δώδεκα = "Twelve to Him";

  - 6:20 πολλά = greatly (was part of "he was greatly perplexed").
  - 6:21 ἡμέρας εὐκαίρου = opportunity (ἡμέρας was unlinked; "her" is `t`).
  - 6:24 ἡ (ἡ δέ, "and she") = her mother; εἶπεν = answered.
  - 6:25 εἰσελθοῦσα μετὰ σπουδῆς = hurried back.
  - 6:26 καί = but (the contrast), διά = because of.
  - 6:30 αὐτῷ = Him (was inside "brought Him news").
  - 6:31 κατ’ = "with Me" and αὐτοί = "privately" → κατ’ ἰδίαν = privately;

  - 6:37 ὁ = Jesus, ἀποκριθεὶς εἶπεν = told; ἀγοράσωμεν = Should we + spend.

## Notable decisions

- 6:27–28: BSB ends its 6:27 with "who went and beheaded him in the prison",
  which renders the first seven words of N1904 6:28. They are linked from
  6:27 (`g @6:28`) and marked moved (`m … > 6:27`) in 6:28. καί = who (as
  καὶ τὸ κοράσιον = who later in 6:28).
- Publisher Greek differences: Ἰωάννης spellings (6:14–6:25), Ἠλίας (6:15),
  ἐθαύμαζεν (6:6), ἦλθον (6:29), εἶδον (6:50) are linked as the same word.
  6:39: BSB "to have the people sit" renders the active ἀνακλῖναι; N1904 has
  the passive ἀνακλιθῆναι, linked with a note. 6:41: "His" is `t` (BSB's
  Greek adds αὐτοῦ). 6:23: the publisher's Greek has one word more than
  N1904; no English word depends on it.
- 6:23: Ὅτι ἐάν = Whatever (read as ὅ τι ἐάν).
- Pronoun → noun renderings kept as links: αὐτοῦ = Jesus’ / John’s / Herod’s
  (6:14, 6:20, 6:21, 6:27, 6:28, 6:29); αὐτῷ = John / Jesus (6:19, 6:35);
  αὐτούς = the crowd / the disciples (6:36, 6:48); αὐτοῖς = the people
  (6:41); πάντας = the people (6:39).
- Article + participle joined: ὁ Βαπτίζων = the Baptist (6:14, 6:24),
  τοῖς συνανακειμένοις / τοὺς ἀνακειμένους = his guests (6:22, 6:26),
  οἱ ἐρχόμενοι / οἱ ὑπάγοντες (6:31), οἱ φαγόντες = who had eaten (6:44),
  the sick (6:55, 6:56). Other units: συμπόσια συμπόσια and πρασιαὶ πρασιαί
  = in groups, κοφίνων πληρώματα = basketfuls, ἐν τῷ ἐλαύνειν = to row,
  ἐπὶ τὴν γῆν ἦλθον = landed, ἤρξαντο … περιφέρειν = carrying, ἵνα κἂν …
  ἅψωνται = to let them touch, ὅσοι ἄν = all who.
- 6:54: εὐθύς = "As soon as" (it merges with the genitive absolute).
- Left unexpressed (`s`): ἐν ἑαυτοῖς (6:51), μετ’ αὐτῶν and καὶ λέγει
  αὐτοῖς (6:50), εἰς τὸ πέραν (6:45), μόνον (6:8), the second ἐνδύσησθε
  (6:9), resumptive οὗτος (6:16), intensive αὐτῆς (6:22), ἐκεῖ (6:33),
  recitative ὅτι.

## Doubts for the peer

- 6:2: ἡ = "this" (this wisdom) is kept, but 6:35 "This is a desolate place"
  is `t` (as Matthew 14:15). Is the difference justified?
- 6:14 "people were saying" is kept as the verb’s impersonal subject, while
  6:12 "the people should repent" is `t`. Consistent enough?
- 6:20: αὐτοῦ = John’s with `t words`, or "John’s words" as one rendering of
  ἀκούσας αὐτοῦ?
- 6:11: τόπος = place (in "that place") and ἐκεῖθεν = that, kept from the
  publisher. Alternative: ἐκεῖθεν = that place, τόπος folded into "If
  anyone".
- 6:35: ἤδη = By now + already#0 (one word rendered twice), with καί = So.
  Alternative: `t By now`, καί `s`.
- 6:39: I assume the publisher's differing word is ἀνακλῖναι (NA28); please
  confirm against the publisher Greek if available.
- 6:41 "among them all" and 6:37 "all of them": I split off the English-only
  part (`t them`, `t all of`). The peer may prefer one group each.
