## Publisher rows corrected

"When", "While", "After" or "Along the way" had been put on δέ, καί or τότε. These now go to the participle or genitive absolute, and the connective becomes `s`:

- 27:1, 27:57: Πρωΐας/Ὀψίας γενομένης → When + came / When + it was.
- 27:3, 27:24, 27:35, 27:47, 27:50, 27:54: ἰδών, σταυρώσαντες, ἀκούσαντες, κράξας, ἰδόντες → When + verb.

- 27:17, 27:19: συνηγμένων αὐτῶν → when … had assembled + the crowd; Καθημένου αὐτοῦ → While … was sitting + Pilate.
- 27:31: καί "After" → s; ὅτε → After.
- 27:32: δέ "Along the way" → s; Ἐξερχόμενοι → Along the way.
- 27:33: Καί "And when" → And; when goes to ἐλθόντες.

## Notable decisions

- 27:40: Ὁ καταλύων is a form of address. "You who are going to destroy" is linked as one group, since a vocative "You" goes with its vocative word.
- Pronouns that BSB renders as names are linked: αὐτοῦ → Pilate (27:19) and Jesus (27:32, 27:53); αὐτόν → Jesus (27:18, 27:48); ὁ → Pilate (27:23). A name that comes only from a verb ending is `t`.
- Articles rendered as possessives are linked: τάς → his (27:24), τό → His (27:50), οἱ → His (27:64).
- 27:9: τοῦ τετιμημένου → "on Him"; ὃν ἐτιμήσαντο → "set"; λέγοντος and the quotation's καί → `s`.
- 27:43: N1904 has αὐτόν once, as the object of θέλει, so the Him after deliver is `t`. I did not claim a different reading for BSB.
- No BSB/N1904 textual difference needed special handling. N1904 matches BSB at οἶνον (27:34), Βασιλεὺς Ἰσραήλ ἐστιν without εἰ (27:42), λεμά (27:46), Ἰωσήφ (27:56), and οἱ μαθηταί without νυκτός (27:64).

## Doubts for the peer

- 27:9: the split between "on Him" and "set" is a judgment call. "Him" could instead go to ὅν, and "set" to τετιμημένου.
- 27:64: οἱ → His (the article as a possessive) versus `t His`.
- 27:48: καί (between filled and put) is linked to the "and" that ends BSB's series ("and held"). "He filled" keeps "He" with the participle.
- 27:60 (τῇ → "across the"), 27:7 (τοῖς → "for"), 27:66 (μετά → "and posting"): the publisher's interpretive anchors are kept as not clearly wrong.
- 27:11: ὁ ἡγεμών → "who", and καί is `s`. καί could join the "who" group.
- 27:23: γάρ → "Why" (the retort use of γάρ) is kept from the publisher.
