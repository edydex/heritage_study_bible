Two ways to add a slide

Both keep your service order visible, start in Sermon when you’re within a sermon, and show where the new material will go.

  • A · Library workspace — recommended. Categories, choices and an output preview stay visible together. Fewer clicks when building a service with many songs and passages.
  • B · Focused steps. A calm chooser leads to one task at a time. More space for each form, with a Back button to change type. Inside a sermon it skips the chooser and opens sermon templates.

Try searching songs, changing outputs, adding a Scripture passage, choosing a prepared sermon and inserting a blank. This file uses sample content and does not edit your service.
