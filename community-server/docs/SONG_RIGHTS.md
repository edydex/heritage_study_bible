# WOTBC starter song rights review

Reviewed 2026-07-23. This is an operational record, not legal advice.

The public Grace Songs catalog was used to confirm English/Russian title pairs and to understand its bilingual data model. Its records do not contain copyright, license, or permission fields. Publicly readable data is not, by itself, permission to republish the lyrics, chords, scores, recordings, or Russian translations.

The Community editor therefore keeps rights information advisory. It does not block a church from publishing material the church has determined it may use.

## Publication practice

- A title-only listing can be published without copying the song text or media.
- A CCLI number identifies a song; it is not itself a license. The church should record the license it actually holds.
- Russian translations are reviewed independently from the English text.
- Uploaded scores, arrangements, and recordings can carry rights separate from the underlying hymn.
- The Rights tab is a record for the church. It intentionally does not act as a publishing lock.
