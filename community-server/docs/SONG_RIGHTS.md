# WOTBC starter song rights review

Reviewed 2026-07-23. This is an operational record, not legal advice.

The public Grace Songs catalog was used to confirm English/Russian title pairs and to understand its bilingual data model. Its records do not contain copyright, license, or permission fields. Publicly readable data is not, by itself, permission to republish the lyrics, chords, scores, recordings, or Russian translations.

The Community editor therefore keeps rights information advisory. It does not block a church from publishing material the church has determined it may use.

## Working interpretation used by Heritage

- For a church with an active
  [CCLI Church Copyright License](https://ccli.com/us/en/church-copyright-license-summary),
  Heritage treats an
  unlisted, phone-friendly lyric sheet used during that church's services and
  home groups as the same practical congregational-singing use as a projected
  lyric, service handout, or church-compiled songbook. This is the church's
  good-faith reading of its agreement, not a license granted by Heritage. The
  church remains responsible for song coverage, attribution, and reporting.
- [Sovereign Grace Music expressly permits](https://sovereigngracemusic.com/about/permissions/)
  copying its free sheet music for
  individual and family worship, churches under 50, and small, care, community,
  home, and Bible-study groups. That permission applies to material it owns; a
  recording or page can still identify an outside or co-published copyright
  whose permission must come from its administrator.
- Community song catalogs and exact song links require a signed-in church
  member. Private and not-yet-public scheduled songs remain visible only to
  owners, admins, and leaders. Song responses also ask search engines not to
  index them.
- Every song page identifies the publishing church's rights record and offers a
  rights-holder correction or takedown contact.

## Publication practice

- A title-only listing can be published without copying the song text or media.
- A CCLI number identifies a song; it is not itself the church's license number.
  The server setup records the church license number once and displays it
  separately on covered song pages.
- For CCLI-covered words, use the copyright line required by CCLI and report the
  church's copying or projection through its CCLI account.
- Russian translations are reviewed independently from the English text.
- Uploaded scores, arrangements, and recordings can carry rights separate from the underlying hymn.
- The Rights tab is a record for the church. It intentionally does not act as a publishing lock.
